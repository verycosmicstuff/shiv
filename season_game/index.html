Poem Unlocked 1965

ਪਿਤਾ ਜੇ ਪੁੱਤ ਦੀ ਸੇਜ ਤੇ ਬੈਠੇ, ਜੱਗ ਉਸਨੂੰ ਨਾ ਦੇਂਦਾ ਦੋਸ਼!

Pita je putt di sej te baithe, jagg usnu na denda daush!

"If an old father beds his son’s youthful bride, the world finds no fault!
Yet if a youth kisses his stepmother’s eyes, earth and heavens shake with wrath!"

Historical Revelation • Marxist Critique:

Marxist critic Sant Singh Sekhon condemned Shiv for framing Loona’s rebellion in sexual agony rather than class struggle. Yet working people wept to her lines across Punjab.

Citation: SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI
