# Aag Di Nadi

### **ਅੱਗ ਦੀ ਨਦੀ** • **اگ دی ندی**

#### *"The River of Living Flame"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Birha Tu Sultan (1964)](../05_birha_tu_sultan_1964.md)
- **Original Title**: ਅੱਗ ਦੀ ਨਦੀ (اگ دی ندی)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1964
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Fire` `#River` `#Ordeal` `#SufiAgony` `#Purification`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: Love is portrayed as a raging river not of water, but of molten volcanic fire through which the lover must swim barefoot.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala during an intense bout of high fever and poetic writing.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Invokes the classic Persian metaphor of love as a river of molten flame that must be crossed to attain divine union.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਹ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਨਹੀਂ ਇੱਕ ਨਦੀ ਏ ਅੱਗ ਦੀ,  
> ਜਿਸ ਵਿੱਚ ਤਰਨਾ ਪੈਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਡਰ ਜਾਵੇ ਇਸ ਸੇਕ ਕੋਲੋਂ,  
> ਉਹਨੂੰ ਜਿਊਂਦੇ ਜੀਅ ਮਰਨਾ ਪੈਂਦਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ایہہ عشق نہیں اک ندی اے اگ دی،  
> جس وچ ترنا پیندا اے!  
> جو ڈر جاوے اس سیک کولوں،  
> اوہنوں جیوندے جی مرنا پیندا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Eh ishq nahin ikk nadi ae agg di,  
> Jis vich tarna painda ae!  
> Jo dar jaave iss sek kolon,  
> Ohnu jiyunde jee marna painda ae!  

#### English Translation
> This love is no gentle stream—it is a roaring river of fire,  
> Across whose molten waves one must swim with bare breast!  
> Whosoever shudders in fear before its searing heat  
> Must endure the living death of spiritual cowardice!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The fiery baptism required of the authentic lover.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਸਾਂ ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਪੈਰ ਧਰੇ ਇਸ ਪਾਣੀ,  
> ਜੋ ਲਹੂ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਖੌਲਦਾ ਸੀ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਹੱਡੀ-ਹੱਡੀ ਸੜ ਕੇ ਕੋਲਾ ਹੋਈ,  
> ਪਰ ਦਿਲ ਤੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ ਹੀ ਬੋਲਦਾ ਸੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اساں ہس کے پیر دھرے اس پانی،  
> جو لہو وانگوں کھولدا سی!  
> ساڈی ہڈی ہڈی سڑ کے کولا ہوئی،  
> پر دل تیرا نام ہی بولدا سی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Asaan hass ke pair dhare iss paani,  
> Jo lahu vaangu kholda si!  
> Saadi haddi-haddi sarh ke kola hoyi,  
> Par dil tera naam hi bolda si!  

#### English Translation
> With a serene smile, we plunged our feet into these burning waters,  
> Which bubbled and boiled like molten blood!  
> Every marrow-bone of our frame was scorched to cinders,  
> Yet our steadfast heart continued chanting your sacred name!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Mortal body incinerated while love remains intact.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਪਾਰ ਲੰਘ ਗਿਆ ਅੱਗ ਦੀ ਨਦੀਓਂ,  
> ਤੇ ਅਮਰ ਕਹਾਣੀ ਬਣ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਡੁੱਬੇ ਸੀ ਇਸ ਦਰਿਆ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਉਹਦਾ ਗੀਤ ਸਦਾ ਲਈ ਤਣ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو پار لنگھ گیا اگ دی ندیوں،  
> تے امر کہانی بن گیا اے!  
> جو ڈبے سی اس دریا اندر،  
> اوہدا گیت سدا لئی تن گیا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv paar langh gaya agg di nadiyon,  
> Te amar kahaani ban gaya ae!  
> Jo dubbe si iss dariya andar,  
> Ohda geet sada layi tan gaya ae!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv has crossed through to the far bank of that river of flame,  
> And his ordeal has transformed into an immortal saga!  
> For those who dared to submerge in this fiery torrent,  
> His ringing song shall stand as an enduring canopy forever!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Triumphant emergence upon the other shore.*
