# Pehli Chithi

### **ਪਹਿਲੀ ਚਿੱਠੀ** • **پہلی چٹھی**

#### *"The First Unopened Letter"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Piran Da Paraga (1960)](../01_piran_da_paraga_1960.md)
- **Original Title**: ਪਹਿਲੀ ਚਿੱਠੀ (پہلی چٹھی)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1960
- **Thematic Tags**: `#PirandaParaga` `#Epistolary` `#UnsentLetter` `#FirstLove`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: The poet treasures the first letter received from his muse, now yellowed with age and stained with saltwater tears.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv preserved unsent letters and notes written on blue paper in a small tin trunk throughout his nomadic youth.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Inspired by Shiv's legendary unfulfilled romance with a village girl from Batala whose family married her far away.

**Source**: *Nagmani (Special Memorial Edition on Shiv Kumar)* by Amrita Pritam (1973, Nagmani Press, New Delhi, Vol. 85, June 1973). [Primary Memoir]

> "Amrita Pritam writes: "Death did not steal Shiv; Shiv courted death as his final bride." She catalogs his uncollected verses and reflects on the intellectual hostility he faced from dogmatic critics."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਪਹਿਲੀ ਚਿੱਠੀ ਆਈ ਤੇਰੀ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਹਿੱਕ ਨਾਲ ਲਾ ਕੇ ਰੋਇਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ ਤੇਰੇ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਦਾ,  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਦਰਦ ਦਾ ਗਵਾਹ ਹੋਇਆ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> پہلی چٹھی آئی تیری،  
> میں ہک نال لا کے رویا نی!  
> کاغذ تیرے ہنجھواں دا،  
> میرے درد دا گواہ ہویا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Pehli chithi aayi teri,  
> Main hikk naal laa ke roya ni!  
> Kaagaz tere hanjhuwan da,  
> Mere dard da gawaah hoyea ni!  

#### English Translation
> When your very first letter arrived,  
> I pressed it against my weeping breast!  
> The paper dampened by your secret tears  
> Became the silent witness to my affliction!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The written paper becomes a physical sacrament connecting separated lovers.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅੱਖਰ ਅੱਖਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਬੋਲੇ,  
> ਸਤਰ ਸਤਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਾਏ ਵੇ!  
> ਕਿਵੇਂ ਮੈਂ ਪੜ੍ਹਾਂ ਉਹ ਦਰਦ ਕਹਾਣੀ,  
> ਜੋ ਨੈਣਾਂ 'ਚੋਂ ਨਾ ਜਾਏ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اکھر اکھر وچ برہا بولے،  
> سطر سطر وچ ہائے وے!  
> کویں میں پڑھاں اوہ درد کہانی،  
> جو نیناں چوں نہ جائے وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Akkhar akkhar vich birha bole,  
> Satar satar vich haaye ve!  
> Kiven main parhaan oh dard kahaani,  
> Jo nainaan chon na jaaye ve!  

#### English Translation
> In every written syllable speaks separation,  
> Along every line resounds an unuttered groan!  
> How can I endure reading that tragic tale  
> Which refuses to vacate my brimming eyes?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The physical text dissolves into the weeping eye.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਉਹ ਚਿੱਠੀ ਪੀਲੀ ਪੈ ਗਈ,  
> ਸਿਆਹੀ ਵੀ ਉੱਡ ਗਈ ਹਵਾ ਵਿੱਚ!  
> ਪਰ ਉਹ ਦਰਦ ਅਜੇ ਵੀ ਤਾਜ਼ਾ ਏ,  
> ਜੋ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਸੀ ਉਸ ਵਫ਼ਾ ਵਿੱਚ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن اوہ چٹھی پیلی پے گئی،  
> سیاہی وی اڈ گئی ہوا وچ!  
> پر اوہ درد اجے وی تازہ اے،  
> جو لکھیا سی اس وفا وچ!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun oh chithi peeli pai gayi,  
> Siaahi vi udd gayi hawa vich!  
> Par oh dard aje vi taaza ae,  
> Jo likhiya si oss wafa vich!  

#### English Translation
> Now that sacred letter has turned pale yellow,  
> Its dark ink has faded into the dry air!  
> Yet the original wound remains freshly bleeding,  
> Just as it was inscribed in that eternal pledge!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Contrast between the material deterioration of the paper and the immortality of the grief.*
