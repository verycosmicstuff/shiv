# Parag Chithi

### **ਪਰਾਗਾ ਚਿੱਠੀ** • **پراگا چٹھی**

#### *"A Bundle of Scented Letters"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Piran Da Paraga (1960)](../01_piran_da_paraga_1960.md)
- **Original Title**: ਪਰਾਗਾ ਚਿੱਠੀ (پراگا چٹھی)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1960
- **Thematic Tags**: `#PirandaParaga` `#Letters` `#PerfumeofLoss` `#Memory`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `village_monsoon`

**Synopsis**: A batch of love letters is roasted in the furnace of grief, releasing a fragrance of dried jasmine and burnt paper.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv reportedly burnt packets of old correspondence in an earthenware saucer when overwhelmed by memories.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Puns on the anthology's title 'Paraga' (a baker's batch), here referring to a batch of letters burnt to ash.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਚਿੱਠੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਪਰਾਗਾ ਬੰਨ੍ਹ ਕੇ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਅੱਗ ਦੇ ਹਵਾਲੇ ਕੀਤਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਸੀ ਅੱਖਰ ਅੱਖਰ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਘੁੱਟ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਦਾ ਪੀਤਾ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> چٹھیاں دا پراگا بنھ کے،  
> میں اگ دے حوالے کیتا نی!  
> جو لکھیا سی اکھر اکھر،  
> میں گھٹ زہر دا پیتا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Chithiyaan da paraga bannh ke,  
> Main agg de hawaale keeta ni!  
> Jo likhiya si akkhar akkhar,  
> Main ghutt zeher da peeta ni!  

#### English Translation
> Binding that batch of old letters together,  
> I surrendered them to the consuming fire!  
> Every tender syllable she had once inscribed  
> Became a bitter draught of hemlock I had to swallow!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Connects back to the title motif: burning letters in place of parching grain.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਧੂੰਆਂ ਉੱਠਿਆ ਕਾਲਾ-ਸਿਆਹ,  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਛਾ ਗਿਆ ਵੇ!  
> ਉਹਨਾਂ ਸੜਦੇ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ਾਂ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ,  
> ਤੇਰਾ ਹਾਸਾ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਆ ਗਿਆ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> دھواں اٹھیا کالا سیاہ،  
> میرے کمرے وچ چھا گیا وے!  
> اوہناں سڑدے کاغذاں وچوں،  
> تیرا ہاسا مینوں آ گیا وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Dhuan utheya kaala-siyaah,  
> Mere kamre vich chhaa gaya ve!  
> Ohnaan sarhde kaagzaan vichon,  
> Tera haasa mainu aa gaya ve!  

#### English Translation
> Pitch-black smoke billowed high,  
> Enveloping my dim room in suffocating haze!  
> From within those curling, incinerating sheets,  
> Your spectral laughter echoed once again!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The burning paper produces an auditory hallucination of the beloved's laughter.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਰਾਖ ਬਚੀ ਬੱਸ ਕੋਲੇ ਵਰਗੀ,  
> ਕੁਝ ਨਾ ਰਿਹਾ ਸੰਸਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਲੁੱਟੇ ਗਏ ਹਾਂ ਦੋਸਤੋ,  
> ਇਸ ਝੂਠੇ ਇਤਬਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> راکھ بچی بس کولے ورگی،  
> کجھ نہ رہا سنسار وچ!  
> اسیں لٹے گئے ہاں دوستو،  
> اس جھوٹھے اعتبار وچ!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Raakh bachi bass kole vargi,  
> Kujh na reha sansaar vich!  
> Aseen lutte gaye haan dosto,  
> Iss jhoothe aitbaar vich!  

#### English Translation
> Only charcoal-black ash remained behind,  
> Nothing else survived in this empty universe!  
> We were utterly plundered, O friends,  
> By putting our faith in a deceitful pledge!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Ash (raakh) as the final residue of romantic idealism.*
