# Sandhya Taara

### **ਸੰਧਿਆ ਤਾਰਾ** • **سندھیا تارا**

#### *"The Evening Star of Despair"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Piran Da Paraga (1960)](../01_piran_da_paraga_1960.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸੰਧਿਆ ਤਾਰਾ (سندھیا تارا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1960
- **Thematic Tags**: `#PirandaParaga` `#EveningStar` `#Dusk` `#CosmicLoneliness`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: The solitary evening star arises in the purple dusk, finding its mirror in the solitary poet below.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv would sit on the rooftop of his family home in Batala at twilight, fixing his gaze on the evening star.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Reflects the romantic Keatsian influence on young Shiv, gazing at Venus at dusk.

**Source**: *Punjabi Kavi Ate Kavitriyan (Marxist Critical Essays)* by Sant Singh Sekhon (1972, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, pp. 89–112). [Literary Criticism]

> "Sant Singh Sekhon termed Shiv the "Keats of Punjabi poetry" for his meter and folk metaphor, but charged him with "palayanwad" (reactionary escapism), arguing that romanticizing youth-suicide was reactionary in post-Partition agrarian Punjab."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸੰਧਿਆ ਤਾਰਾ ਚੜ੍ਹਿਆ ਅੰਬਰ,  
> ਇਕੱਲਾ ਚਮਕੇ ਦੂਰ ਵੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੀ ਕਿਸਮਤ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਉਹ ਵੀ,  
> ਦਰਦ 'ਚ ਚੂਰੋ-ਚੂਰ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> سندھیا تارا چڑھیا انبر،  
> اکلا چمکے دور وے!  
> میری قسمت وانگوں اوہ وی،  
> درد چ چورو چور وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Sandhya taara charheya ambar,  
> Ikalla chamke door ve!  
> Meri kismat vaangoo oh vi,  
> Dard ch chooro-choor ve!  

#### English Translation
> The evening star has ascended the twilight firmament,  
> Glittering in lonely majesty far above!  
> Like my own solitary, doomed destiny,  
> It too stands fractured by cosmic sorrow!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Establishes kinship between the celestial body and human solitude.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸਾਰੇ ਤਾਰੇ ਰਲ ਕੇ ਹੱਸਣ,  
> ਇਹ ਕਿਉਂ ਕੱਲਾ ਰੋਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਸੁੰਞੇ ਅੰਬਰ ਦੀ ਹਿੱਕ ਉੱਤੇ,  
> ਕਿਹੜਾ ਦਰਦ ਪਰੋਂਦਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> سارے تارے رل کے ہسن،  
> ایہ کیوں کلا روندا اے!  
> سنجے انبر دی ہک اتے،  
> کہڑا درد پروندا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Saare taare ral ke hassan,  
> Eh kyon kalla ronda ae!  
> Sunje ambar di hikk utte,  
> Kehda dard paronda ae!  

#### English Translation
> All other stars cluster together in joyful light,  
> Why does this solitary wanderer weep apart?  
> Upon the barren breast of the twilight sky,  
> What unspeakable grief does it thread into verse?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The outsider status of the star reflects the outsider status of the lyric poet.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜਦ ਕਾਲੀ ਰਾਤ ਪਸਰ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਇਹ ਵੀ ਗੁਆਚ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਇਹਦਾ ਦੀਵਾ ਵੀ,  
> ਚੁੱਪ-ਚਾਪ ਬੁੱਝ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جد کالی رات پسر جاوے گی،  
> ایہ وی گواچ جاوے گا!  
> میرے وانگوں ایہدا دیوا وی،  
> چپ چاپ بجھ جاوے گا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Jad kaali raat pasar jaavegi,  
> Eh vi guaach jaavega!  
> Mere vaangoo ehda deeva vi,  
> Chup-chaap bujh jaavega!  

#### English Translation
> When the pitch-black night spreads her absolute mantle,  
> This fragile star too will drown in the abyss!  
> Just like my own flickering heart,  
> Its pale wick shall be snuffed out in silence!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The inevitability of darkness consuming even celestial beauty.*
