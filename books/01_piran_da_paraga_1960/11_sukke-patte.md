# Sukke Patte

### **ਸੁੱਕੇ ਪੱਤੇ** • **سکے پتے**

#### *"Dry Leaves of Autumn"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Piran Da Paraga (1960)](../01_piran_da_paraga_1960.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸੁੱਕੇ ਪੱਤੇ (سکے پتے)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1960
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Autumn` `#FallenLeaves` `#Mortality` `#Wind`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The human soul compared to dry autumn leaves that rustle under the indifferent boots of passersby.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in early winter as trees shed their leaves along the Gurdaspur-Batala highway.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Emphasizes Shiv's lifelong premonition that he would wither and fall before reaching middle age.

**Source**: *Shiv Batalvi: A Solitary and Passionate Singer* by Dr. O.P. Sharma (1979, Sterling Publishers, New Delhi, pp. 12–45, 180–205). [Primary Memoir]

> "Authoritative academic biography documenting his childhood in Bara Pind Lohtian (now Pakistan), the trauma of the 1947 Partition flight, his clerkship at the State Bank of India Chandigarh, and his final collapse in Kir Mangyal."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਸੀਂ ਸੁੱਕੇ ਪੱਤਿਆਂ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਝੜ ਗਏ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਰੁੱਖ ਦੀ ਸ਼ਾਖ਼ ਤੋਂ ਟੁੱਟ ਕੇ!  
> ਹਵਾਵਾਂ ਨੇ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਰੋਲ ਦਿੱਤਾ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਸਾਰਾ ਨੂਰ ਹੀ ਲੁੱਟ ਕੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اسیں سکے پتیاں وانگوں جھڑ گئے،  
> کسے رخ دی شاخ توں ٹٹ کے!  
> ہواواں نے سانوں رول دتا،  
> ساڈا سارا نور ہی لٹ کے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Aseen sukke pattiyan waangoon jharh gaye,  
> Kise rukkh di shaakh ton tutt ke!  
> Hawaawaan ne saanu rol ditta,  
> Saada saara noor hi lutt ke!  

#### English Translation
> We tumbled down like parched and dry autumn leaves,  
> Snapped forever from the branch of our parent tree!  
> The reckless winds have rolled and trampled us in the dust,  
> Robbing us of all our radiant, youthful luster!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Separation from the cosmic source portrayed as seasonal shedding.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜੋ ਕਦੇ ਛਾਂ ਕਰਦੇ ਸੀ ਰਾਹੀਆਂ ਤੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਪੈਰਾਂ ਹੇਠਾਂ ਮਧੋਲੇ ਗਏ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਚੀਕ ਨਾ ਸੁਣੀ ਕਿਸੇ ਪਾਂਧੀ ਨੇ,  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੀ ਰਲ ਗਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جو کدے چھاں کردے سی راہیاں تے،  
> اوہ پیراں ہیٹھاں مدھولے گئے!  
> ساڈی چیک نہ سنی کسے پاندھی نے،  
> اسیں مٹی وچ ہی رل گئے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Jo kade chhaan karde si raahiyaan te,  
> Oh pairaan hethan madhole gaye!  
> Saadi cheek na suni kise paandhi ne,  
> Aseen mitti vich hi ral gaye!  

#### English Translation
> We who once cast soothing shade upon passing travelers  
> Are now crushed and pulverized beneath their careless feet!  
> Not a single wanderer halted to hear our dying crackle;  
> We have dissolved back into the anonymous clods of clay!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The irony of generous souls being trampled when their strength fades.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਪਤਝੜ ਮੁੱਕਣ ਤੇ ਕੀ ਆਉਣਾ,  
> ਜਦ ਸਾਡੀਆਂ ਸ਼ਾਖ਼ਾਂ ਸੜ ਗਈਆਂ!  
> ਉਹ ਰੁੱਤਾਂ ਮੁੜ ਕੇ ਨਾ ਆਈਆਂ,  
> ਜੋ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੜ੍ਹ ਗਈਆਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن پتجھڑ مکن تے کی آونا،  
> جد ساڈیاں شاخاں سڑ گئیاں!  
> اوہ رتاں مڑ کے نہ آئیاں،  
> جو گیتاں دے وچ مڑھ گئیاں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun patjharh mukkan te ki aauna,  
> Jad saadiyan shaakhaan sarh gaiyan!  
> Oh ruttaan murh ke na aaiyan,  
> Jo geetaan de vich marhh gaiyan!  

#### English Translation
> What consolation can spring bring when autumn departs,  
> When our very branches have charred and turned to charcoal?  
> Those luminous seasons of love can never return,  
> Though they remain forever immortalized in my verses!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Poetry as the only preservative of lost seasons.*
