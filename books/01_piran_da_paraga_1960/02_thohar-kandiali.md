# Main Kandiali Thohar Ve Sajjna

### **ਮੈਂ ਕੰਡਿਆਲੀ ਥੋਹਰ ਵੇ ਸੱਜਣਾ** • **میں کنڈیالی تھوہر وے سجنا**

#### *"I Am a Thorny Cactus, O Beloved"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Piran Da Paraga (1960)](../01_piran_da_paraga_1960.md)
- **Original Title**: ਮੈਂ ਕੰਡਿਆਲੀ ਥੋਹਰ ਵੇ ਸੱਜਣਾ (میں کنڈیالی تھوہر وے سجنا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1960
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Desert` `#Thorns` `#UnrequitedLove` `#AgrarianImagery`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `barren_mountain_loona`

**Synopsis**: The poet identifies not with a fragrant garden rose, but with a bitter, thorny desert cactus that blossoms without care and stabs anyone who draws near.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in reaction to social judgment regarding his reckless, prickly personality.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> One of Shiv's most quoted botanical metaphors, converting Punjab's unloved desert scrub into a majestic symbol of solitary, self-protective pride.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਕੰਡਿਆਲੀ ਥੋਹਰ ਵੇ ਸੱਜਣਾ,  
> ਉੱਗੀ ਕਿਤੇ ਕੁਰਾਹੇ!  
> ਨਾ ਕਿਸੇ ਮਾਲੀ ਸਿੰਜਿਆ ਮੈਨੂੰ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਸਿੰਜਣਾ ਚਾਹੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں کنڈیالی تھوہر وے سجنا،  
> اگی کتے کُراہے!  
> نہ کسے مالی سنجیا مینوں،  
> نہ کوئی سنجنا چاہے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main kandiali thohar ve sajjna,  
> Uggi kite kuraahe!  
> Na kise maali sinjiya mainu,  
> Na koyi sinjna chaahe!  

#### English Translation
> I am a prickly, thorny cactus, O my beloved,  
> Sprouted along some forgotten, lonely byway!  
> No gardener ever nurtured me with sweet water,  
> Nor does any soul desire to water my roots!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The opening confession establishes emotional self-sufficiency born of permanent neglect.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਯਾਦ ਤੇਰੀ ਦਾ ਸੁਲਗਦਾ ਕੋਲਾ,  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਅੰਦਰ ਬਲੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਕੋਈ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਛੋਹਣਾ ਚਾਹੇ,  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਕੰਡਿਆਂ ਨਾਲ ਛਲੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> یاد تیری دا سلگدا کولا،  
> میرے سینے اندر بلے!  
> جو کوئی مینوں چھوہنا چاہے،  
> میرے کنڈیاں نال چھلے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Yaad teri da sulagda kola,  
> Mere seene andar bale!  
> Jo koyi mainu chhohna chaahe,  
> Mere kandiyan naal chhale!  

#### English Translation
> The smoldering ember of your memory  
> Burns deep within my hollow breast!  
> Whosoever dares reach out to touch my stems  
> Is pierced and wounded by my sharp thorns!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The thorns are not malice, but scars that ward off superficial affection.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਨਾ ਮੇਰੇ ਪੱਤੇ, ਨਾ ਮੇਰੀ ਛਾਂ ਵੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਪੰਛੀ ਆਣ ਬਹੇ!  
> ਇੱਕ ਇਕੱਲੀ ਜਿੰਦ ਨਿਮਾਣੀ,  
> ਧੁੱਪਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਈ ਸਹੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> نہ میرے پتے، نہ میری چھاں وے،  
> نہ کوئی پنچھی آن بہے!  
> اک اکلی جند نمانی،  
> دھپاں دے وچ پئی سہے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Na mere patte, na meri chhaan ve,  
> Na koyi panchhi aan bahe!  
> Ikk ikalli jind nimaani,  
> Dhuppan de vich payi sahe!  

#### English Translation
> I bear no sheltering leaves, nor do I cast soothing shade;  
> No singing bird ever alights upon my brittle branches!  
> A lone, humble life stranded upon the dunes,  
> Enduring the scorching glare of relentless noons!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *A complete rejection of the romantic trope of being a blooming, fragrant flower.*
