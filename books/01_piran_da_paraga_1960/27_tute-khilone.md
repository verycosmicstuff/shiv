# Tutte Khilone

### **ਟੁੱਟੇ ਖਿਡੌਣੇ** • **ٹٹے کھڈونے**

#### *"Broken Toys of Memory"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Piran Da Paraga (1960)](../01_piran_da_paraga_1960.md)
- **Original Title**: ਟੁੱਟੇ ਖਿਡੌਣੇ (ٹٹے کھڈونے)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1960
- **Thematic Tags**: `#PirandaParaga` `#Childhood` `#InnocenceLost` `#ClayDolls`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `village_monsoon`

**Synopsis**: The broken toys of childhood become emblems of adult hearts that cannot be mended once dropped.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's poignant remembrance of village melas (fairs) where cheap painted toys brought unalloyed joy.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Draws on village potters' earthenware figurines sold at local Punjab melas.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੇਲੇ 'ਚੋਂ ਲਿਆਂਦੇ ਸੀ ਖਿਡੌਣੇ,  
> ਜੋ ਹੱਥੋਂ ਛੁੱਟ ਕੇ ਟੁੱਟ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦੇ ਉਹ ਸੋਹਣੇ ਮੂਰਤ,  
> ਪਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਹੋ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میلے چوں لیاندے سی کھڈونے،  
> جو ہتھوں چھٹ کے ٹٹ گئے نی!  
> مٹی دے اوہ سوہنے مورت،  
> پل وچ مٹی ہو گئے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Mele chon liyande si khilone,  
> Jo hatthon chhutt ke tutt gaye ni!  
> Mitti de oh sohne moorat,  
> Pal vich mitti ho gaye ni!  

#### English Translation
> From the village fair we purchased painted toys,  
> Which slipped from careless hands and shattered!  
> Those radiant figurines sculpted of common clay  
> Returned to dust in the fraction of an instant!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Clay figurines (moorat) returning to dust echo the mortal human condition.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਰੰਗ-ਬਰੰਗੇ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਸਾਡੇ,  
> ਉਹਨਾਂ ਟੁਕੜਿਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੁਲ ਗਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਹਸਦੇ ਸੀ ਕਦੇ ਨਾਲ ਸਾਡੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਸਾਰੇ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਭੁੱਲ ਗਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> رنگ برنگے سپنے ساڈے،  
> اوہناں ٹکڑیاں وچ رل گئے!  
> جو ہسدے سی کدے نال ساڈے،  
> اوہ سارے سانوں بھل گئے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Rang-barange supne saade,  
> Ohnaan tukrhiyaan vich rul gaye!  
> Jo hasde si kade naal saade,  
> Oh saare saanu bhull gaye!  

#### English Translation
> Our vibrant, multi-colored dreams  
> Lie trampled amidst those earthen shards!  
> Those who once laughed in companionship beside us  
> Have long consigned our name to oblivion!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The shattering of toys represents the disillusionment of first love.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਕੌਣ ਜੋੜੇ ਇਹ ਟੁਕੜੇ,  
> ਕੌਣ ਇਹਨਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਰੂਪ ਦਵੇ!  
> ਟੁੱਟੇ ਹੋਏ ਇਸ ਦਿਲ ਨੂੰ ਹੁਣ,  
> ਕੌਣ ਜਿਊਣ ਦੀ ਧੂਪ ਦਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن کون جوڑے ایہ ٹکڑے،  
> کون ایہناں نوں روپ دوے!  
> ٹٹے ہوئے اس دل نوں ہن،  
> کون جیون دی دھوپ دوے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun kaun joreh eh tukrhe,  
> Kaun ehnaan nu roop dawe!  
> Tutte hoye iss dil nu hun,  
> Kaun jiyun di dhoop dawe!  

#### English Translation
> Who now possesses the skill to reassemble these fragments?  
> Who can restore their vanished form and grace?  
> To this irreparably broken heart of mine,  
> Who can offer the warming sun of renewed life?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Conclusion: an earthen heart, once fractured, cannot be repaired.*
