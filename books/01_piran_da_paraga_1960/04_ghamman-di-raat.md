# Ghamman Di Raat Lammi Ae

### **ਗ਼ਮਾਂ ਦੀ ਰਾਤ ਲੰਮੀ ਏ** • **غم دی رات لمی اے**

#### *"The Night of Sorrows is Endlessly Long"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Piran Da Paraga (1960)](../01_piran_da_paraga_1960.md)
- **Original Title**: ਗ਼ਮਾਂ ਦੀ ਰਾਤ ਲੰਮੀ ਏ (غم دی رات لمی اے)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1960
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Night` `#Sorrow` `#Insomnia` `#ClassicLyric`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `snowy_cabin_night`

**Synopsis**: An exquisite nocturne capturing the solitary wakefulness of the wounded soul when the entire cosmos sleeps.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Written during nights of insomnia in Batala when Shiv wandered along the railway tracks.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Sang by legendary singers like Hans Raj Hans and Jagjit Singh, this poem set the benchmark for Punjabi ghazal structure.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਗ਼ਮਾਂ ਦੀ ਰਾਤ ਲੰਮੀ ਏ,  
> ਜਾਂ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤ ਲੰਮੇ ਨੇ?  
> ਨਾ ਭੇਤੀ ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਕੋਈ,  
> ਨਾ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਦਰਦ ਥੰਮ੍ਹੇ ਨੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> غم دی رات لمی اے،  
> جاں میرے گیت لمے نے؟  
> نہ بھیتی اکھیاں دا کوئی،  
> نہ دل دے درد تھمے نے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Ghamman di raat lammi ae,  
> Jaan mere geet lamme ne?  
> Na bheti akhiyan da koyi,  
> Na dil de dard thamme ne!  

#### English Translation
> Is the night of sorrows endlessly long,  
> Or are my tragic songs stretching without bound?  
> There is no confidant who comprehends the secrets of my weeping eyes,  
> Nor have the raging sorrows of my heart ever paused!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Posing the timeless aesthetic question: does sorrow prolong the song, or does the song prolong the night?*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕੋਈ ਤਾਰਾ ਚਮਕਦਾ ਏ ਤੇ ਬੁੱਝ ਜਾਂਦਾ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਹੰਝੂ ਪਲਕਾਂ ਤੇ ਸੁੱਕ ਜਾਂਦਾ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਸਾਹਾਂ ਦੀ ਡੋਰੀ ਟੁੱਟਣ ਲੱਗੀ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਦਰਦ ਨਾ ਪਲ ਭਰ ਮੁੱਕ ਜਾਂਦਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کوئی تارا چمکدا اے تے بجھ جاندا،  
> کوئی ہنجھو پلکاں تے سک جاندا!  
> میرے ساہاں دی ڈوری ٹٹن لگی،  
> میرا درد نہ پل بھر مک جاندا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Koyi taara chamakda ae te bujh jaanda,  
> Koyi hanjhu palkan te sukk jaanda!  
> Mere saahaan di dori tuttan laggi,  
> Mera dard na pal bhar mukk jaanda!  

#### English Translation
> A distant star glimmers and is abruptly snuffed out;  
> A solitary tear dries upon the eyelashes before it falls!  
> The thread of my breath begins to fray and snap,  
> Yet my internal agony ceases not for a single heartbeat!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Microcosm of the eye mirroring the macrocosm of dying stars.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਤੁਸੀਂ ਸੌਂ ਜਾਓ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਦੇ ਸੁੱਖੀਓ,  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਜਾਗਣ ਦਿਓ!  
> ਮੇਰੀ ਚਿਖ਼ਾ ਦੇ ਕੋਲ ਬੈਠ ਕੇ,  
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਅੱਗ ਦੇ ਭਾਂਬੜ ਬਾਲਣ ਦਿਓ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> تسیں سون جاؤ دنیا دے سکھیو،  
> میرے گیتاں نوں جاگن دیو!  
> میری چکھا دے کول بیٹھ کے،  
> مینوں اگ دے بھانبڑ بالن دیو!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Tuseen saun jaao duniya de sukkhiyo,  
> Mere geetaan nu jaagan diyo!  
> Meri chikha de kol baith ke,  
> Mainu agg de bhaambar baalan diyo!  

#### English Translation
> Go forth and sleep, O comfortable children of the world!  
> Leave my wakeful songs to keep vigil through the dark!  
> Sitting beside the rim of my own funeral pyre,  
> Permit me to stoke the towering flames of my despair!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Disdain for the comfortable populace who sleep while the poet stands night guard over human grief.*
