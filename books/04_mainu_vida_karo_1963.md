# Mainu Vida Karo (1963)

## ਮੈਨੂੰ ਵਿਦਾ ਕਰੋ • مینوں وداع کرو
### *"Bid Me Farewell"*

---

## 📚 Anthology Overview

- **Author**: Shiv Kumar Batalvi (ਸਿ਼ਵ ਕੁਮਾਰ ਬਟਾਲਵੀ / شیو کمار بٹالوی)
- **Publication Year**: 1963
- **Original Publisher**: Navyug Publishers, Delhi
- **Total Master Poems in this Edition**: 28

### Literary & Historical Background
> A hauntingly nocturnal collection steeped in premonitions of premature death, shroud metaphors, funeral pyres, and the urge for release from physical torment.

**Critical Reception**: Features 'Kujh Rukh' and 'Maut Da Suneha', showcasing Shiv's mastery of animism—trees as grieving human relatives.

## 1. Mainu Vida Karo

### **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਵਿਦਾ ਕਰੋ (ਸਿਰਲੇਖ ਕਵਿਤਾ)** • **مینوں وداع کرو**

#### *"Bid Me Farewell: Title Poem"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Departure` `#Farewell` `#Mortality` `#Punjab` `#Altar`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The poet asks his companions and his native land to release him from life's obligations and bid him a solemn farewell.
- **Genesis**: Written during a period of acute sickness and hospitalization in Chandigarh.
- **Archival Context**: The title poem of his 1963 collection, composed in Chandigarh during a period of acute physical exhaustion and liver complications. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Regarded as one of his most haunting valedictory anthems.
- **Musical Recitation**: Sung in deep, sorrowful tarannum cadence.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਵਿਦਾ ਕਰੋ ਮੇਰੇ ਦੋਸਤੋ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਜਾਣ ਦਾ ਵੇਲਾ ਆ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਗੀਤ ਮੈਂ ਗਾਉਣੇ ਆਇਆ ਸਾਂ,  
> ਉਹ ਗ਼ਮ ਦੀ ਭੇਟ ਚੜ੍ਹਾ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> مینوں وداع کرو میرے دوستو،  
> میرا جان دا ویلا آ گیا اے!  
> جو گیت میں گاؤنے آیا ساں،  
> اوہ غم دی بھینٹ چڑھا گیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Mainu vida karo mere dosto,  
> Mera jaan da vela aa gaya ae!  
> Jo geet main gaaune aaya saan,  
> Oh gham di bhent charha gaya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Bid me farewell, my beloved comrades,  
> The hour of my final departure has arrived!  
> Those celestial melodies I came to sing upon this earth  
> I have laid as an unblemished sacrifice upon the altar of grief!  

💡 *Commentary*: The opening couplet presents the poet's life as a concluded mission of emotional offering.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਪਾਓ ਮੇਰੇ ਗਲ਼ ਹਾਰ ਕੋਈ,  
> ਨਾ ਰੋਵੋ ਮੇਰੇ ਜਾਣ ਪਿੱਛੋਂ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਤਾਂ ਇੱਕ ਬੱਦਲ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਸਾਂ,  
> ਜੋ ਵਰ੍ਹ ਕੇ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਿਆ ਸਾਵਣ 'ਚੋਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ پاؤ میرے گل ہار کوئی،  
> نہ رووو میرے جان پچھوں!  
> میں تاں اک بادل وانگوں ساں،  
> جو ورھ کے مک گیا ساون چوں!  

**Romanized**:
> Na paao mere gal haar koyi,  
> Na rovo mere jaan pichhon!  
> Main taan ikk baddal vaangu saan,  
> Jo varh ke mukk gaya Saavan chon!  

**English Translation**:
> Place no celebratory garlands around my neck,  
> Nor shed bitter tears after I have departed!  
> For I was merely like a wandering raincloud  
> That spent itself in torrential rain and vanished from the monsoon sky!  

💡 *Commentary*: The cloud metaphor: the poet exhausts his entire substance in tears and disappears.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦੀ ਧਰਤੀ ਤੋਂ,  
> ਜੋ ਪੀੜਾਂ ਦਾ ਵਰਦਾਨ ਲਿਆ!  
> ਉਹ ਸ਼ਬਦਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਢਾਲ਼ ਕੇ ਮੈਂ,  
> ਹਰ ਦਰਦੀਲੇ ਨੂੰ ਦਾਨ ਦਿੱਤਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں پنجاب دی دھرتی توں،  
> جو پیڑاں دا وردان لیا!  
> اوہ شبداں دے وچ ڈھال کے میں،  
> ہر دردیلے نوں دان دتا!  

**Romanized**:
> Main Punjab di dharti ton,  
> Jo peerran da vardaan liya!  
> Oh shabdaan de vich dhaal ke main,  
> Har dardeele nu daan ditta!  

**English Translation**:
> From the sacred soil of Punjab,  
> Whatever blessing of agony I was granted,  
> I forged into immortal living words  
> And distributed freely as alms to every suffering soul!  

💡 *Commentary*: Suffering as a spiritual blessing (vardaan) shared generously with humanity.

##### Stanza 4

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜੇ ਕੋਈ ਭੁੱਲ-ਚੁੱਕ ਹੋਈ ਹੋਵੇ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਬਖ਼ਸ਼ ਦੇਣਾ ਲੋਕੋ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਤਾਂ ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਕੀਤਾ,  
> ਹੋਰ ਨਾ ਕੁਝ ਜਾਣਿਆ ਲੋਕੋ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جے کوئی بھل-چک ہوئی ہووے،  
> تاں مینوں بخش دینا لوگو!  
> میں تاں صرف عشق کیتا،  
> ہور نہ کچھ جانیا لوگو!  

**Romanized**:
> Je koyi bhull-chukk hoyi hove,  
> Taan mainu bakhsh dena loko!  
> Main taan sirf ishq keeta,  
> Hor na kujh jaaneya loko!  

**English Translation**:
> If any transgression was committed by my hand,  
> Then grant me forgiveness, O people of Punjab!  
> For I knew only how to love without measure,  
> And understood nothing else in this calculating world!  

💡 *Commentary*: Humility before his audience, asking forgiveness for living solely through passion.

## 2. Kujh Rukh (Rukh)

### **ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ (ਰੁੱਖਾਂ ਦੀ ਕਵਿਤਾ)** • **کجھ رخ**

#### *"Some Trees: The Tree Elegies"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Trees` `#Kinship` `#Ecology` `#Mothers` `#Reincarnation`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: Shiv personifies trees as family members—some as sons, some as mothers, some as grandfathers—longing to be reincarnated as a tree.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala while resting beneath old neem and banyan trees.
- **Archival Context**: One of the most anthologized and revered ecological poems in South Asian literature, establishing tree kinship as spiritual ethics. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: One of the most celebrated ecological poems in the Punjabi language.
- **Musical Recitation**: Lyrical, flowing rhythm capturing the sway of boughs in wind.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਪੁੱਤ ਲੱਗਦੇ ਨੇ,  
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਲੱਗਦੇ ਮਾਵਾਂ!  
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਨੂੰਹਾਂ ਧੀਆਂ ਲੱਗਦੇ,  
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਵਾਂਗ ਭਰਾਵਾਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کجھ رخ مینوں پت لگدے نے،  
> کجھ رخ لگدے ماواں!  
> کجھ رخ نونہاں دھیاں لگدے،  
> کجھ رخ وانگ بھراواں!  

**Romanized**:
> Kujh rukh mainu putt lagde ne,  
> Kujh rukh lagde maavan!  
> Kujh rukh noohaan dhiyaan lagde,  
> Kujh rukh vaang bharaavan!  

**English Translation**:
> Some trees appear to me as beloved sons,  
> Some trees appear as nurturing mothers!  
> Some trees seem like daughters and brides,  
> And some stand sturdy like protective brothers!  

💡 *Commentary*: Kinship with nature: trees form an entire family network replacing human estrangement.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਮੇਰੇ ਬਾਬੇ ਵਾਕਣ,  
> ਪੱਤਰ ਟਾਵਾਂ ਟਾਵਾਂ!  
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਮੇਰੀ ਦਾਦੀ ਵਰਗੇ,  
> ਚੂਰੀ ਪਾਵਣ ਕਾਵਾਂ!  
> ਇੱਕ ਮੇਰੀ ਮਹਿਬੂਬਾ ਵਾਕਣ,  
> ਮਿੱਠਾ ਅੰਬ ਦਾ ਬੂਟਾ!  
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਮੇਰਾ ਦਿਲ ਕਰਦਾ ਏ,  
> ਮੋਢੇ ਚੁੱਕ ਖਿਡਾਵਾਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کجھ رخ میرے بابے واکن،  
> پتر تاواں تاواں!  
> کجھ رخ میری دادی ورگے،  
> چوری پاون کاواں!  
> اک میری محبوبہ واکن،  
> مٹھا انب دا بوٹا!  
> کجھ رخ میرا دل کردا اے،  
> موڈھے چک کھڈاواں!  

**Romanized**:
> Kujh rukh mere baabe vaakan,  
> Pattar taavaan taavaan!  
> Kujh rukh meri daadi varge,  
> Choori paavan kaavan!  
> Ikk meri mehbooba vaakan,  
> Mittha amb da boota!  
> Kujh rukh mera dil karda ae,  
> Modhe chukk khidaavaan!  

**English Translation**:
> Some trees are like my venerable grandfather,  
> Bearing only sparse, weathered leaves upon their crown!  
> Some trees are gentle like my grandmother,  
> Crumbling sweet bread to feed the morning crows!  
> One tree is tender like my beloved—  
> A sweet, fragrant sapling of mango!  
> And some young saplings my heart yearns  
> To hoist upon my shoulders and bounce in play!  

💡 *Commentary*: Intergenerational lineage mirrored in trees from withered elders to playful saplings.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੇਰਾ ਵੀ ਇਹ ਦਿਲ ਕਰਦਾ ਏ,  
> ਰੁੱਖ ਦੀ ਜੂਨੇ ਆਵਾਂ!  
> ਜੋ ਤੁਸਾਂ ਮੇਰਾ ਗੀਤ ਹੈ ਸੁਣਨਾ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਰੁੱਖਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਗਾਵਾਂ!  
> ਰੁੱਖ ਤਾਂ ਮੇਰੀ ਮਾਂ ਵਰਗੇ ਨੇ,  
> ਜੀਵਣ ਰੁੱਖਾਂ ਦੀਆਂ ਛਾਵਾਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میرا وی ایہہ دل کردا اے،  
> رخ دی جونے آواں!  
> جو تساں میرا گیت ہے سننا،  
> میں رخاں وچ گاواں!  
> رخ تاں میری ماں ورگے نے،  
> جیون رخاں دیاں چھاواں!  

**Romanized**:
> Mera vi eh dil karda ae,  
> Rukh di joone aavan!  
> Jo tusaan mera geet hai sunna,  
> Main rukkhaan vich gaavan!  
> Rukh taan meri maan varge ne,  
> Jeevan rukkhaan diyan chaavan!  

**English Translation**:
> My heart longs with deepest desire  
> To be reborn into the incarnation of a tree!  
> If you ever wish to hear my song again,  
> I shall sing to you from within the rustling leaves!  
> For trees are gentle like my own mother—  
> Long live the compassionate shelter of trees!  

💡 *Commentary*: The ultimate longing to dissolve human suffering into photosynthetic life.

##### Cultural Glossary

## 3. Zehar Da Pyaala

### **ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਦਾ ਪਿਆਲਾ** • **زہر دا پیالہ**

#### *"The Goblet of Poison"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Poison` `#Goblet` `#Tragedy` `#Socrates`
- **Theme / Biome**: `rebellion` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: The poet drinks from the poisoned goblet of life, finding that pain is the only authentic companion that never betrays.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala during late-night gatherings with fellow poets.
- **Archival Context**: Often cited by biographer Mohan Bhandari as an allegory for Shiv's escalating dependence on country liquor to numb grief. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਦਾ ਪਿਆਲਾ ਪੀ ਲਿਆ ਏ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਦੀ ਹੁਣ ਲੋੜ ਨਹੀਂ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਦਰਦ ਨੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਸਾਥ ਦਿੱਤਾ,  
> ਉਸ ਦਰਦ ਦੀ ਕੋਈ ਤੋੜ ਨਹੀਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں زہر دا پیالہ پی لیا اے،  
> کوئی امرت دی ہن لوڑ نہیں!  
> جس درد نے میرا ساتھ دتا،  
> اس درد دی کوئی توڑ نہیں!  

**Romanized**:
> Main zehar da pyaala pee liya ae,  
> Koyi amrit di hun lorh nahin!  
> Jis dard ne mera saath ditta,  
> Us dard di koyi torh nahin!  

**English Translation**:
> I have drained the poisoned goblet to its bitter dregs;  
> There is no longer any yearning for heavenly nectar!  
> That loyal agony which stood steadfast by my side  
> Possesses no antidote in all the realms of existence!  

💡 *Commentary*: Acceptance of poison over false promises of healing.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਲੋਕੀਂ ਕਹਿੰਦੇ ਨੇ ਇਹ ਸ਼ਰਾਬੀ ਏ,  
> ਜੋ ਰਾਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਕੁਰਲਾਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਉਹ ਕੀ ਜਾਨਣ ਇਸ ਪਿਆਲੇ 'ਚੋਂ,  
> ਉਹ ਆਪਣਾ ਲਹੂ ਲੰਘਾਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> لوکی کہندے نے ایہہ شرابی اے،  
> جو راتاں نوں کرلاؤندا اے!  
> اوہ کی جانن اس پیالے چوں،  
> اوہ اپنا لہو لنگھاؤندا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Loki kehnde ne eh sharaabi ae,  
> Jo raataan nu kurlaaunda ae!  
> Oh ki jaanan iss pyaale chon,  
> Oh aapna lahu langhaaunda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> The righteous declare: 'He is merely an unhinged drunkard  
> Who wails into the shadows of the lonely midnight!'  
> How could they ever perceive that from this chalice  
> He is swallowing the hot, sacrificial blood of his own heart?  

💡 *Commentary*: Subverting the bourgeois moral condemnation of the bohemian.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਪਚਾ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਇਹ ਜੱਗ ਵੇਖ ਕੇ ਡਰਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਮਰ ਕੇ ਵੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਮਰ ਸਕਿਆ,  
> ਉਹ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਖੜ੍ਹਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو تاں ہس کے زہر پچا گیا،  
> ایہہ جگ ویکھ کے ڈردا اے!  
> جو مر کے وی نہیں مر سکیا،  
> اوہ گیتاں دے وچ کھڑدا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv taan hass ke zehar pachaa gaya,  
> Eh jagg vekh ke darda ae!  
> Jo mar ke vi nahin mar sakeya,  
> Oh geetan de vich kharhda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv digested the deadly poison with an untroubled smile;  
> This cowardly world shudders merely to look upon him!  
> He who could not be slain even by death itself  
> Stands resurrected forever in the fortress of his songs!  

💡 *Commentary*: The invincible immortality of the poetic soul.

## 4. Maut Da Suneha

### **ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਸੁਨੇਹਾ** • **موت دا سُنیہا**

#### *"The Tidings of Approaching Death"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Death` `#Premonition` `#Messenger` `#Shadows`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: Death arrives not as a grim reaper, but as an intimate bridegroom delivering an overdue invitation to peace.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala during a high fever.
- **Archival Context**: Reflects Shiv's lifelong premonition that he would die young, echoing Keats and Shelley. (*Citation: `SHARMA-1979-SOLITARY`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਸੁਨੇਹਾ ਆਇਆ ਏ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਬੂਹਾ ਖੜਕਾ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਬਲ਼ਦੇ ਹੋਏ ਮੱਥੇ 'ਤੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਠੰਢਾ ਹੱਥ ਰੱਖ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> موت دا سُنیہا آیا اے،  
> کوئی بوہا کھڑکایا گیا نی!  
> میرے بلدے ہوئے متھے تے،  
> کوئی ٹھنڈا ہتھ رکھ گیا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Maut da suneha aaya ae,  
> Koyi booha kharhkaa gaya ni!  
> Mere balhde hoye matthe te,  
> Koyi thanddha hath rakh gaya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The solemn tidings of approaching death have arrived;  
> Someone has knocked softly upon my darkened door!  
> Upon my burning, fever-struck forehead  
> A soothing, cold palm has rested with maternal tenderness!  

💡 *Commentary*: Death as a gentle, relieving touch cooling existential fever.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਡਰ ਏ, ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਝੋਰਾ ਏ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਤਾਂ ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ੀ ਨਾਲ਼ ਤੁਰਨਾ ਏ!  
> ਇਸ ਜੀਵਨ ਦੇ ਝੂਠੇ ਝੰਮੇਲਿਆਂ 'ਚੋਂ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਹੁਣ ਰੱਬ ਵੱਲ ਮੁੜਨਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ کوئی ڈر اے، نہ کوئی جھورا اے،  
> میں تاں خوشی نال ترنا اے!  
> اس جیون دے جھوٹھے جھمیلیاں چوں،  
> میں ہن رب ول مڑنا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Na koyi darr ae, na koyi jhora ae,  
> Main taan khushi naal turna ae!  
> Iss jeevan de jhoothe jhameliyaan chon,  
> Main hun Rabb vall murhna ae!  

**English Translation**:
> There is no terror lingering in my heart, nor any sorrow;  
> I prepare to set forth upon this journey with profound relief!  
> From the exhausting entanglements of this deceitful world,  
> I now turn my face back toward the eternal Divine!  

💡 *Commentary*: Freedom from worldly entanglement.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਨੂੰ ਮੌਤ ਨਾ ਡਰਾ ਸਕੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਤਾਂ ਮੌਤ ਨੂੰ ਗਲ਼ ਲਾਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਸੂਰਜ ਡੁੱਬੂਗਾ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਦਾ,  
> ਉਹ ਇੱਕ ਨਵਾਂ ਗੀਤ ਗਾਵੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو نوں موت نہ ڈرا سکی،  
> اوہ تاں موت نوں گل لاوےگا!  
> جد سورج ڈبوگا شام دا،  
> اوہ اک نواں گیت گاوےگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv nu maut na daraa saki,  
> Oh taan maut nu gal laavega!  
> Jad suraj dubbooga shaam da,  
> Oh ikk nawaan geet gaavega!  

**English Translation**:
> Death could never strike terror into the heart of Shiv;  
> He shall clasp death warmly in an embrace like a bride!  
> When the evening sun sinks into the western horizon,  
> His lips shall burst into a triumphant, immortal hymn!  

💡 *Commentary*: Eros and Thanatos unite as death is embraced as a bride.

## 5. Aakhri Salaam

### **ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਸਲਾਮ** • **آخری سلام**

#### *"The Final Salutation to the World"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Salutation` `#Farewell` `#Peace` `#Sunset`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: A dignified, gracious bow to friends and foes alike, concluding the poet's earthly dialogue.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala as Shiv prepared for his move to Chandigarh in late 1963.
- **Archival Context**: Composed as a formal farewell poem to the literary societies of Jalandhar and Ludhiana before his move to Chandigarh. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੇਰਾ ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਸਲਾਮ ਕਬੂਲ ਕਰੋ,  
> ਇਸ ਜੱਗ ਦੇ ਵਸਣ ਵਾਲ਼ਿਓ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਵੇਖ ਕੇ,  
> ਆਪਣਾ ਹਾਸਾ ਹੱਸਣ ਵਾਲ਼ਿਓ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میرا آخری سلام قبول کرو،  
> اس جگ دے وسن والیو!  
> میرے دل دے زخماں نوں ویکھ کے،  
> اپنا ہاسا ہسن والیو!  

**Romanized**:
> Mera aakhri salaam kabool karo,  
> Iss jagg de vasan waaliyo!  
> Mere dil de zakhmaan nu vekh ke,  
> Aapna haasa hassan waaliyo!  

**English Translation**:
> Accept my final salutation and bow,  
> O residents of this crowded, bustling earth!  
> Ye who gazed upon the raw wounds of my heart  
> And burst into mocking laughter and derision!  

💡 *Commentary*: Gracious farewell addressing detractors without bitterness.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਤੁਹਾਥੋਂ ਕੁਝ ਨਾ ਮੰਗਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਥੋੜ੍ਹਾ ਪਿਆਰ ਹੀ ਮੰਗਿਆ ਸੀ!  
> ਪਰ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਤਾਂ ਮੇਰੀ ਰੂਹ ਨੂੰ ਵੀ,  
> ਕੰਡਿਆਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਟੰਗਿਆ ਸੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں تہاڈھوں کچھ نہ منگیا سی،  
> صرف تھوڑا پیار ہی منگیا سی!  
> پر تسیں تاں میری روح نوں وی،  
> کنڈیاں دے وچ ٹنگیا سی!  

**Romanized**:
> Main tuhaathon kujh na mangeya si,  
> Sirf thohrha pyaar hi mangeya si!  
> Par tuseen taan meri rooh nu vi,  
> Kandiyan de vich tangeya si!  

**English Translation**:
> I had sought no treasure or land from your hands;  
> I had asked only for a humble measure of love!  
> Yet you took my tender, defenseless spirit  
> And suspended it upon a cross of cruel thorns!  

💡 *Commentary*: The modest request of the artist met with social crucifixion.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਚੱਲਿਆ ਏ ਦੂਰ ਕਿਤੇ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਕੋਈ ਕੰਡਾ ਨਾ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਉਸ ਰੱਬ ਦੇ ਚਰਨਾਂ 'ਚ ਬਹਿ ਕੇ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਗੀਤ ਸੁਖੀ ਹੋ ਸੋਵੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن شیو چلیا اے دور کتے،  
> جتھے کوئی کنڈا نہ ہووےگا!  
> اس رب دے چرناں چ بہہ کے،  
> میرا گیت سکھی ہو سووےگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun Shiv challeya ae door kite,  
> Jitthe koyi kanda na hovega!  
> Us Rabb de charnaan ch beh ke,  
> Mera geet sukhi ho sovega!  

**English Translation**:
> Now Shiv departs for a distant, unmapped realm  
> Where no thorn of cruelty shall ever pierce the flesh!  
> Resting beneath the tranquil feet of the Divine,  
> My weary song shall slumber in perpetual peace!  

💡 *Commentary*: Slumber at the divine feet as ultimate sanctuary.

## 6. Kore Kagaz Te Dastkhat

### **ਕੋਰੇ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ 'ਤੇ ਦਸਤਖ਼ਤ** • **کورے کاغذ تے دستخط**

- **Genesis**: Penned in Chandigarh after signing publishing contracts that yielded little financial return.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਕੋਰੇ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ 'ਤੇ ਦਸਤਖ਼ਤ ਕਰ ਦਿੱਤੇ,  
> ਹੋਣੀ ਨੂੰ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹੀ ਛੁੱਟੀ ਦੇ ਦਿੱਤੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਲਿਖਣਾ ਏ ਉਹ ਲਿਖ ਲਵੇ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਦਿਲ ਦੀ ਦੌਲਤ ਲੁਟਾ ਦਿੱਤੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں کورے کاغذ تے دستخط کر دتے،  
> ہونی نوں کھلی چھٹی دے دتی!  
> جو لکھنا اے اوہ لکھ لوے،  
> میں دل دی دولت لٹا دتی!  

**Romanized**:
> Main kore kaghaz te dastkhat kar ditte,  
> Honi nu khullhi chhutti de ditti!  
> Jo likhna ae oh likh lave,  
> Main dil di daulat lutaa ditti!  

**English Translation**:
> I affixed my signature upon a blank deed of parchment,  
> Granting arbitrary fate complete, unchecked license!  
> Whatever decree it desires to write, let it write;  
> I have already surrendered the entire wealth of my heart!  

💡 *Commentary*: The ultimate existential gamble: total surrender to destiny.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਮੈਂ ਸ਼ਰਤਾਂ ਪੜ੍ਹੀਆਂ, ਨਾ ਹੱਕ ਮੰਗੇ,  
> ਸਭ ਕੁਝ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਦੇ ਨਾਮ ਕੀਤਾ!  
> ਇਸ ਜੱਗ ਦੇ ਝੂਠੇ ਮੁਕੱਦਮੇ 'ਚੋਂ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣਾ ਆਪ ਬਰੀ ਕੀਤਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ میں شرطاں پڑھیاں، نہ حق منگے،  
> سبھ کچھ عشق دے نام کیتا!  
> اس جگ دے جھوٹھے مقدمے چوں،  
> میں اپنا آپ بری کیتا!  

**Romanized**:
> Na main shartaan parhiyan, na hakk mange,  
> Sabh kujh ishq de naam keeta!  
> Iss jagg de jhoothe mukaddme chon,  
> Main aapna aap bari keeta!  

**English Translation**:
> I neither scrutinized the clauses nor demanded legal rights;  
> I signed every condition away in the holy name of love!  
> From the fraudulent, corrupt courtroom of this world  
> I have acquitted and emancipated my own soul!  

💡 *Commentary*: Rejecting legalistic bargaining in human affection.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਇਹ ਦਸਤਖ਼ਤ ਅਮਰ ਰਹੂ,  
> ਜਦ ਤੱਕ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਇਹ ਰਹੇਗੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਪੜ੍ਹੇਗਾ ਇਸ ਬੇ-ਜ਼ੁਬਾਨ ਦਰਦ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਉਹਦੀ ਅੱਖ 'ਚੋਂ ਗੰਗਾ ਵਹੇਗੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دا ایہہ دستخط امر رہو،  
> جد تک دنیا ایہہ رہےگی!  
> جو پڑھےگا اس بے-زبان درد نوں،  
> اوہدی اکھ چوں گنگا وہےگی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv da eh dastkhat amar rahu,  
> Jad takk duniya eh rahegi!  
> Jo parhega iss be-zubaan dard nu,  
> Ohdi akh chon Ganga vahegi!  

**English Translation**:
> This solitary signature of Shiv's shall remain immortal  
> As long as this earth continues to revolve!  
> Whosoever reads this wordless, articulate suffering  
> Shall find the sacred river Ganga streaming from their eyes!  

💡 *Commentary*: The holy tear as the river Ganga washing away worldly sins.

## 7. Alvida Punjab

### **ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਪੰਜਾਬ** • **الوداع پنجاب**

#### *"Farewell to the Soil of Punjab"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Punjab` `#Rivers` `#Farewell` `#Patriotism` `#Tragedy`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The poet takes his leave from the five rivers, the wheat fields, and the historic soil of Punjab that birthed his sorrow.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala as Shiv's health began to decline severely in the mid-1960s.
- **Archival Context**: Recited with immense emotional resonance at the Punjabi Sahit Akademi in Ludhiana, bringing audiences to tears. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਓ ਮੇਰੇ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦੀ ਧਰਤੀਏ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਤੇਰੇ ਚਰਨਾਂ 'ਤੇ ਸੀਸ ਧਰਿਆ!  
> ਜੋ ਪਿਆਰ ਤੂੰ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਬਖ਼ਸ਼ਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ 'ਚ ਸਦਾ ਤਰਿਆ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> الوداع او میرے پنجاب دی دھرتیئے،  
> میں تیرے چرناں تے سیس دھریا!  
> جو پیار توں مینوں بخشیا سی،  
> اوہ میرے گیتاں چ سدا تریا!  

**Romanized**:
> Alvida o mere Punjab di dhartiye,  
> Main tere charnaan te sees dhareya!  
> Jo pyaar toon mainu bakhsheya si,  
> Oh mere geetan ch sada tareya!  

**English Translation**:
> Farewell, O sacred soil of my beloved Punjab;  
> I lay my weary head in veneration upon your feet!  
> That unconditional love you bestowed upon my spirit  
> Has floated immortal upon the currents of my verses!  

💡 *Commentary*: The ultimate act of filial devotion to the motherland.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਤੇਰੀਆਂ ਪੰਜਾਂ ਨਦੀਆਂ ਦੇ ਪਾਣੀਆਂ 'ਚੋਂ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਵਹਾ ਦਿੱਤੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਫੁੱਲ ਸੀ ਸੀਨੇ ਮੇਰੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਤੇਰੀ ਜੂਹ 'ਤੇ ਵਿਛਾ ਦਿੱਤੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> تیریاں پنجاں ندیاں دے پانیاں چوں،  
> میں اپنے ہنجھو وہا دتے!  
> جو برہا دے پھل سی سینے میرے،  
> اوہ تیری جوہ تے وچھا دتے!  

**Romanized**:
> Teriyan panjaan nadiyan de paaniyan chon,  
> Main aapne hanjhu vahaa ditte!  
> Jo birha de phull si seene mere,  
> Oh teri jooh te vichhaa ditte!  

**English Translation**:
> Into the sacred waters of your five roaring rivers  
> I have poured the overflowing flood of my tears!  
> Those blossoms of holy separation blooming in my chest  
> I have scattered across the boundaries of your village paths!  

💡 *Commentary*: Merging of personal tears with the five rivers of Punjab.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਦ ਮੇਰੀ ਰਾਖ਼ ਨੂੰ ਰਾਵੀ 'ਚ ਪਾਓ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਇੱਕ ਗੀਤ ਮੇਰਾ ਗਾ ਲੈਣਾ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਮਰ ਕੇ ਵੀ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦਾ ਰਹੂ,  
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਆਪਣੇ ਦਿਲ 'ਚ ਵਸਾ ਲੈਣਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جد میری راکھ نوں راوی چ پاؤ،  
> تاں اک گیت میرا گا لینا!  
> شیو تاں مر کے وی پنجاب دا رہو،  
> مینوں اپنے دل چ وسا لینا!  

**Romanized**:
> Jad meri raakh nu Ravi ch paao,  
> Taan ikk geet mera gaa laina!  
> Shiv taan mar ke vi Punjab da rahu,  
> Mainu aapne dil ch vasaa laina!  

**English Translation**:
> When you consign my mortal ashes to the currents of the Ravi,  
> Sing just one of my gentle melodies in remembrance!  
> For Shiv, even in death, remains the eternal child of Punjab;  
> Enshrine my memory forever inside the chamber of your heart!  

💡 *Commentary*: Final resting place in the waters of the River Ravi.

## 8. Kafan Da Libaas

### **ਕਫ਼ਨ ਦਾ ਲਿਬਾਸ** • **کفن دا لباس**

#### *"The White Shroud as My Only Garment"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Shroud` `#Burial` `#White` `#Purity` `#Mortality`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The white unstitched shroud is described as the only honest robe that fits the shivering, naked soul of the poet without hypocrisy.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala after attending the cremation of a young companion.
- **Archival Context**: Shiv's stark meditation on unstitched white cloth (Kafan), stripping away all poetic ornament in the face of death. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਕਫ਼ਨ ਦਾ ਲਿਬਾਸ ਪੁਆ ਦੇਵੋ,  
> ਇਹ ਰੇਸ਼ਮ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਾੜਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਇਸ ਚਿੱਟੀ ਚਾਦਰ ਦੇ ਹੇਠਾਂ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਦਿਲ ਰੱਬ ਨੂੰ ਪੁਕਾਰਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> مینوں کفن دا لباس پوا دیوو،  
> ایہہ ریشم مینوں ساڑدا اے!  
> اس چٹی چادر دے ہیٹھاں،  
> میرا دل رب نوں پکاردا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Mainu kafan da libaas puaa devo,  
> Eh resham mainu saarhda ae!  
> Iss chitti chaadar de hethan,  
> Mera dil Rabb nu pukaarda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Drape upon my body the unstitched robe of the white shroud;  
> This worldly silk scorches and suffocates my skin!  
> Beneath this pristine white sheet of mortality,  
> My naked heart calls out directly to the Divine!  

💡 *Commentary*: The white shroud as spiritual relief from hypocritical luxury.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੋਈ ਜੇਬ ਨਹੀਂ ਇਸ ਕਫ਼ਨ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਲਾਲਚ ਨਾਲ਼ ਨਾ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਜੋ ਕਮਾਇਆ ਸੀ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ ਧਨ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਰੂਹ ਨੂੰ ਰੁਸ਼ਨਾਵੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کوئی جیب نہیں اس کفن دے وچ،  
> کوئی لالچ نال نہ جاوےگا!  
> جو کمایا سی برہا دا دھن،  
> اوہی روح نوں روشناوےگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Koyi jeb nahin iss kafan de vich,  
> Koyi laalach naal na jaavega!  
> Jo kamaaya si birha da dhan,  
> Uhi rooh nu rushnaavega!  

**English Translation**:
> There are no pockets sewn into this white shroud;  
> No worldly greed or gold can accompany me on this voyage!  
> Only that wealth of holy grief which I gathered in life  
> Shall illuminate the journey of my departing spirit!  

💡 *Commentary*: The classic folk adage of the pocketless shroud.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਤੁਰ ਚੱਲਿਆ ਸਫ਼ੈਦ ਹੋ ਕੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਦਾਗ਼ ਨਾ ਲਾਇਆ ਦਾਮਨ 'ਤੇ!  
> ਜਦ ਧਰਤੀ ਰੋਊਗੀ ਮੇਰੇ ਜਾਣ ਪਿੱਛੋਂ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਫੁੱਲ ਖਿੜਨਗੇ ਚਮਨ 'ਤੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو تاں تر چلیا سفید ہو کے،  
> کوئی داغ نہ لایا دامن تے!  
> جد دھرتی روؤگی میرے جان پچھوں،  
> تاں پھل کھڑنگے چمن تے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv taan tur challeya safaid ho ke,  
> Koyi daagh na laaya daaman te!  
> Jad dharti rougi mere jaan pichhon,  
> Taan phull khirhnge chaman te!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv departs cloaked in untainted, spotless white,  
> Leaving not a single stain of deceit upon his robe!  
> When this sacred soil weeps after my departure,  
> Fragrant wild blossoms shall burst forth across the gardens!  

💡 *Commentary*: The immaculate poet leaving the garden fertilized by his tears.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਕਫ਼ਨ (Kafan)** | *Ka-fan* | Burial shroud / Unstitched white cloth | Universal symbol of final renunciation and equality before the divine tribunal. |

## 9. Shamshaan Di Raakh

### **ਸ਼ਮਸ਼ਾਨ ਦੀ ਰਾਖ਼** • **شمشاں دی راکھ**

#### *"The Holy Ashes of the Cremation Ground"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Cremation` `#Ashes` `#Pyre` `#Mortality` `#Transcendence`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The cremation ground (Shamshaan) is portrayed as the only place of true equality, where all pride and power turn to cooling grey ash.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala during solitary walks past the cremation ground on the town outskirts.
- **Archival Context**: Draws on the funeral ghats along the Ravi and Beas where Shiv spent nocturnal hours contemplating mortality. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਸ਼ਮਸ਼ਾਨ ਦੀ ਰਾਖ਼ 'ਚ ਵੇਖਿਆ,  
> ਸਭ ਹੰਕਾਰ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਹੋ ਗਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਕਹਿੰਦੇ ਸੀ ਅਸੀਂ ਬਾਦਸ਼ਾਹ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਉਹ ਵੀ ਇੱਕੋ ਚਿਤਾ 'ਚ ਸੋ ਗਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں شمشاں دی راکھ چ ویکھیا،  
> سبھ ہنکار مٹی ہو گئے!  
> جو کہندے سی اسیں بادشاہ ہاں،  
> اوہ وی اکو چتا چ سو گئے!  

**Romanized**:
> Main shamshaan di raakh ch vekheya,  
> Sabh hankaar mitti ho gaye!  
> Jo kehnde si aseen baadshah haan,  
> Oh vi ikko chita ch so gaye!  

**English Translation**:
> Looking into the cooling ashes of the cremation ground,  
> I saw all human arrogance and vanity dissolved into dust!  
> Those who proclaimed themselves emperors and monarchs  
> Slept quietly within the very same heap of gray cinders!  

💡 *Commentary*: The great leveller: the pyre erases all worldly hierarchy.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਸ ਰਾਖ਼ 'ਚ ਕੋਈ ਜਾਤ ਨਾ ਦਿਸਦੀ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਅਮੀਰ ਨਾ ਗ਼ਰੀਬ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਅੱਗ ਨੇ ਸਾੜ ਕੇ ਪਾਕ ਕੀਤਾ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਰੂਹ ਦਾ ਨਸੀਬ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اس راکھ چ کوئی ذات نہ دسدی،  
> نہ کوئی امیر نہ غریب نی!  
> جو اگ نے ساڑ کے پاک کیتا،  
> اوہی روح دا نصیب نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Iss raakh ch koyi jaat na dissdi,  
> Na koyi ameer na gareeb ni!  
> Jo agg ne saarh ke paak keeta,  
> Uhi rooh da naseeb ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Within these ashes, no trace of caste or lineage remains;  
> Neither the billionaire nor the destitute pauper can be told apart!  
> That which the sacred fire purified and reduced to ash  
> Alone represents the true, unburdened destiny of the spirit!  

💡 *Commentary*: Fire as the ultimate purifier of social falsehoods.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਵੀ ਇਸੇ ਰਾਖ਼ 'ਚ ਰਲ਼ ਜਾਊ,  
> ਇਹ ਸੋਚ ਕੇ ਦਿਲ ਮੁਸਕਾਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੀ ਅੱਗ 'ਚ ਸੜਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਅਮਰ ਸੁੱਖ ਹੁਣ ਪਾਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو وی اسے راکھ چ رل جاؤ،  
> ایہہ سوچ کے دل مسکاوندا اے!  
> جو برہا دی اگ چ سڑیا سی،  
> اوہ امر سکھ ہن پاوندا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv vi isse raakh ch ral jaau,  
> Eh soch ke dil muskaaunda ae!  
> Jo birha di agg ch sarheya si,  
> Oh amar sukkh hun paaunda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv too shall dissolve into these very gray ashes;  
> Pondering this final grace, his weary heart smiles in peace!  
> He who burned through an entire lifetime in the fire of separation  
> Now attains at last the everlasting sanctuary of silence!  

💡 *Commentary*: Peace found in total dissolution.

|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਸ਼ਮਸ਼ਾਨ (Shamshaan)** | *Sham-shaan* | Hindu / Sikh cremation ground | Sacred space of detachment (Vairagya) where the mortal vessel is consigned to fire. |

## 10. Rukh Te Parinda

### **ਰੁੱਖ ਤੇ ਪਰਿੰਦਾ** • **رخ تے پرندہ**

#### *"The Tree and the Dying Bird"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Tree` `#Bird` `#Dialogue` `#Mortality` `#Dusk`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: A philosophical dialogue between an ancient tree that cannot move and a wounded bird that cannot fly anymore.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala as Shiv watched a wounded sparrow take shelter in an old Shisham tree.
- **Archival Context**: A poignant allegorical dialogue composed when Shiv stayed at his father's village house during a convalescence period. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਰੁੱਖ ਨੇ ਪੁੱਛਿਆ ਪੰਛੀ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਤੂੰ ਕਿਉਂ ਅੱਜ ਗੀਤ ਨਾ ਗਾਵੇਂ ਨੀ?  
> ਤੇਰੇ ਖੰਭਾਂ 'ਚੋਂ ਲਹੂ ਕਿਉਂ ਸਿੰਮਦਾ,  
> ਤੂੰ ਉੱਡ ਕੇ ਕਿਉਂ ਨਾ ਜਾਵੇਂ ਨੀ?  

**Shahmukhi**:
> رخ نے پچھیا پنچھی نوں،  
> توں کیوں اج گیت نہ گاویں نی؟  
> تیرے کھنبھاں چوں لہو کیوں سمدا،  
> توں اڈ کے کیوں نہ جاویں نی؟  

**Romanized**:
> Rukkh ne puchheya panchhi nu,  
> Toon kyon ajj geet na gaaven ni?  
> Tere khambhaan chon lahu kyon simmda,  
> Toon udd ke kyon na jaaven ni?  

**English Translation**:
> The ancient tree asked of the little bird:  
> 'Why do you utter no melody today, gentle singer?  
> Why does crimson blood seep from between your feathers?  
> Why do you not take wing and soar across the blue horizon?'  

💡 *Commentary*: The tree's inquiry into the bird's sudden silence.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪੰਛੀ ਰੋ ਕੇ ਆਖਣ ਲੱਗਾ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਸ਼ਿਕਾਰੀ ਨੇ ਖੰਭ ਤੋੜਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਆਇਆ ਸਾਂ ਤੇਰੀ ਛਾਂ ਦੇ ਉਹਲੇ,  
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਮੌਤ ਨੇ ਅੱਜ ਮੋੜਿਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پنچھی رو کے آکھن لگا،  
> میرا شکاری نے کھنبھ توڑیا اے!  
> میں آیا ساں تیری چھاں دے اوہلے،  
> مینوں موت نے اج موڑیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Panchhi ro ke aakhan lagga,  
> Mera shikaari ne khambh toreya ae!  
> Main aaya saan teri chhaan de ohle,  
> Mainu maut ne ajj morheya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Through weeping breaths, the bird replied:  
> 'A cruel hunter has shattered my flying wing!  
> I have come to take refuge beneath your maternal shade,  
> For death has summoned me back from life's journey today!'  

💡 *Commentary*: The wounded bird seeking final sanctuary in nature.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਰੁੱਖ ਨੇ ਗਲ਼ ਨਾਲ ਲਾਇਆ ਪੰਛੀ,  
> ਤੇ ਪੱਤਿਆਂ ਦੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਕੇਰੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਦੋਵੇਂ ਰੋ ਪਏ,  
> ਇਸ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਦੇ ਘੋਰ ਹਨੇਰੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> رخ نے گل نال لایا پنچھی،  
> تے پتیاں دے ہنجھو کیرے نی!  
> شیو کہندا دوویں رو پئے،  
> اس شام دے گھور ہنیرے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Rukkh ne gal naal laaya panchhi,  
> Te pattiyan de hanjhu kere ni!  
> Shiv kehnda dovein ro paye,  
> Iss shaam de ghor hanere ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The ancient tree embraced the dying bird within its boughs,  
> Shedding dew-like tears of leaves in communion!  
> Shiv says: 'Both broke into unutterable weeping together  
> Within the gathering, terrifying gloom of this twilight!'  

💡 *Commentary*: Universal solidarity in shared suffering.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਸ਼ਿਕਾਰੀ (Shikaari)** | *Shi-kaa-ree* | Hunter | Classic metaphor for inexorable time (Kaal) or society hunting down the vulnerable poet. |

## 11. Chup Da Shor

### **ਚੁੱਪ ਦਾ ਸ਼ੋਰ** • **چپ دا شور**

#### *"The Deafening Clamor of Absolute Silence"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Silence` `#Noise` `#Solitude` `#Echo` `#Void`
- **Theme / Biome**: `modernism` / `snowy_cabin_night`
- **Synopsis**: In the deserted house after loved ones have departed, the unbroken silence rings louder in the ears than an artillery barrage.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Chandigarh after moving into an empty bachelor flat.
- **Archival Context**: Pioneering Punjabi modernism by treating silence not as the absence of sound, but as an unbearable psychological roar. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਸ ਸੁੰਞੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਦੇ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਚੁੱਪ ਦਾ ਸ਼ੋਰ ਸੁਣਾਈ ਦਿੰਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਚਿਹਰਾ ਕਦੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਸ਼ੀਸ਼ੇ 'ਚੋਂ ਨਾ ਦਿਸਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اس سنجے کمرے دے اندر،  
> چپ دا شور سنائی دیندا اے!  
> جو چہرہ کدے میرا سی،  
> اوہ شیشے چوں نہ دسدا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Iss sunjne kamre de andar,  
> Chup da shor sunayi dinda ae!  
> Jo chehra kade mera si,  
> Oh sheeshe chon na dissda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Within the hollow walls of this deserted room,  
> The deafening clamor of absolute silence rings out!  
> That vibrant countenance which once belonged to me  
> Is no longer visible in the cold reflection of the mirror!  

💡 *Commentary*: The paradox of deafening silence and vanishing identity.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੰਧਾਂ ਵੀ ਗੱਲਾਂ ਕਰਦੀਆਂ ਨੇ,  
> ਪਰ ਕੋਈ ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਨਾ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਏ!  
> ਮੇਰੀ ਆਪਣੀ ਹੀ ਧੜਕਣ ਹੁਣ,  
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਮੌਤ ਵੱਲ ਬੁਲਾਉਂਦੀ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کندھاں وی گلاں کردیاں نے،  
> پر کوئی آواز نہ آوندی اے!  
> میری اپنی ہی دھڑکن ہن،  
> مینوں موت ول بلاوندی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kandhaan vi gallan kardiyaan ne,  
> Par koyi aawaaz na aaundi ae!  
> Meri aapni hi dhadkan hun,  
> Mainu maut vall bulaaundi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Even the bare walls whisper among themselves,  
> Yet no articulate human sound reaches my ears!  
> The desperate throbbing of my own heartbeat now  
> Summons me inexorably toward the gates of death!  

💡 *Commentary*: Heartbeat counting down mortal time.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਇਸ ਚੁੱਪ 'ਚੋਂ ਗੀਤ ਬਣਾਊ,  
> ਜੋ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਨੂੰ ਹਿਲਾ ਦੇਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਸਾਰੀ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਸੌਂ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਇਹ ਸ਼ੋਰ ਜਗ੍ਹਾ ਦੇਵੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو اس چپ چوں گیت بناؤ،  
> جو دنیا نوں ہلا دیوےگا!  
> جد ساری دنیا سوں جاوےگی،  
> ایہہ شور جگا دیوےگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv iss chup chon geet banaau,  
> Jo duniya nu hila devega!  
> Jad saari duniya saun jaavegi,  
> Eh shor jaghaa devega!  

**English Translation**:
> From the marrow of this silence, Shiv shall forge a song  
> That will shake the foundations of the sleeping world!  
> When all of humanity has sunk into complacent slumber,  
> This roaring silence shall awaken them to truth!  

💡 *Commentary*: Silence transmuted into prophetic poetry.

|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਚੁੱਪ (Chup)** | *Chupp* | Silence / Mutelessness | In Punjabi mystic poetry, the ultimate state beyond speech where truth resides. |

## 12. Maut Di Boli

### **ਮੌਤ ਦੀ ਬੋਲੀ** • **موت دی بولی**

#### *"The Gentle Dialect of Death"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MotherDeath` `#Consolation` `#Rest` `#Lullaby`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: Death speaks not as an executioner, but as a tender, forgiving mother inviting an exhausted child to sleep in her lap.
- **Genesis**: Composed during a quiet evening walk in Chandigarh.
- **Archival Context**: Inverts the frightening figure of Yamraj (death god) into a tender Punjabi mother singing a soothing bedtime lullaby (Lori). (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੌਤ ਨੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਹੌਲੀ ਜਿਹੇ ਕਿਹਾ,  
> ਪੁੱਤਰਾ ਹੁਣ ਸੌਂ ਜਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਬਹੁਤ ਰੋ ਲਿਆ ਤੂੰ ਦੁਨੀਆ 'ਚ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਮੇਰੀ ਗੋਦ 'ਚ ਖੋ ਜਾ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> موت نے مینوں ہولی جہے کہیا،  
> پترا ہن سوں جا نی!  
> بہت رو لیا توں دنیا چ،  
> ہن میری گود چ کھو جا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Maut ne mainu hauli jihe keha,  
> Puttra hun saun jaa ni!  
> Bahut ro leya toon duniya ch,  
> Hun meri godd ch kho jaa ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Death whispered softly into my ear:  
> 'O my exhausted son, sleep now in peace!  
> You have wept long and bitterly in this unyielding world;  
> Now dissolve safely into the shelter of my lap!'  

💡 *Commentary*: Death personified as the ultimate consoling mother.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇੱਥੇ ਕੋਈ ਤੈਨੂੰ ਦੁੱਖ ਨਾ ਦੇਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਤੋੜੇਗਾ ਤੇਰਾ ਦਿਲ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਆਲ੍ਹਣੇ 'ਚ ਆਣ ਕੇ ਬਹਿ ਜਾ,  
> ਸਭ ਪੀੜਾਂ ਦਾ ਹੋਵੇ ਹੱਲ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ایتھے کوئی تینوں دکھ نہ دیوےگا،  
> نہ کوئی توڑےگا تیرا دل نی!  
> میرے آلہنے چ آن کے بہہ جا،  
> سبھ پیڑاں دا ہووے حل نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Itthe koyi tainu dukkh na devega,  
> Na koyi torega tera dil ni!  
> Mere aalhane ch aan ke beh ja,  
> Sabh peerran da hove hall ni!  

**English Translation**:
> 'Here no cruel hand shall inflict wounds upon you;  
> No soul shall shatter your fragile heart again!  
> Come and rest within the safety of my quiet nest,  
> Where all human suffering finds its final resolution!'  

💡 *Commentary*: The grave as sanctuary from human cruelty.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਨੇ ਮਾਂ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਮੌਤ ਨੂੰ ਚੁੰਮਿਆ,  
> ਤੇ ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਮੀਚ ਲਈਆਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਸੀ ਸਾਰੀ ਉਮਰ ਦਾ,  
> ਉਸ ਨੇ ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਪੀ ਲਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو نے ماں وانگوں موت نوں چمیا،  
> تے اکھیاں میچ لئیاں نی!  
> جو درد سی ساری عمر دا،  
> اس نے ہس کے پی لیا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv ne maan vaangu maut nu chummeya,  
> Te akhiyan meech laiyan ni!  
> Jo dard si saari umar da,  
> Us ne hass ke pee leya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv kissed the forehead of Death as one kisses a mother,  
> And gently closed his weary, tear-worn eyes!  
> That vast agony which had burdened his entire lifetime  
> He drank down with a serene, triumphant smile!  

💡 *Commentary*: Tender reconciliation between the poet and mortal rest.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਗੋਦ (Godd)** | *Godd* | Maternal lap | Sacred haven of ultimate emotional security and tender reassurance. |

## 13. Pardesi Dhola

### **ਪਰਦੇਸੀ ਢੋਲਾ** • **پردیسی ڈھولا**

#### *"The Beloved Who Sailed Across the Black Waters"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Diaspora` `#Dhola` `#Ocean` `#Migration` `#ForgottenVows`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The separated bride addresses her lover who journeyed to foreign shores (Pardes), forgetting the sacred vows made by the village well.
- **Genesis**: Composed after visits to Doaba villages nicknamed 'villages of waiting wives'.
- **Archival Context**: Reflects the mass emigration from Doaba and Majha to England and Canada in the 1960s, leaving thousands of young brides abandoned. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪਰਦੇਸੀ ਢੋਲਾ ਤੂੰ ਕੀ ਕੀਤਾ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਪਿਆਰ ਵਿਸਾਰ ਦਿੱਤਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਸਮੁੰਦਰੋਂ ਪਾਰ ਜਾ ਕੇ ਬੈਠ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਸਾਨੂੰ ਰੋਣੇ ਲਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پردیسی ڈھولا توں کی کیتا،  
> ساڈا پیار وسار دتا نی!  
> سمندروں پار جا کے بیٹھ گیا،  
> سانوں رونے لا دتا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Pardesi dhola toon ki keeta,  
> Saada pyaar visaar ditta ni!  
> Samundron paar jaa ke baith gaya,  
> Saanu rone laa ditta ni!  

**English Translation**:
> O beloved who journeyed to foreign shores, what have you done?  
> You cast our sacred devotion into total oblivion!  
> Sitting in comfort far across the turbulent black oceans,  
> You left us here to drown in endless weeping!  

💡 *Commentary*: The trauma of the left-behind Punjabi bride.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਖੂਹਾਂ 'ਤੇ ਪਾਣੀ ਭਰਦੀਆਂ ਕੁੜੀਆਂ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਮਜ਼ਾਕ ਉਡਾਉਂਦੀਆਂ ਨੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਵਾਅਦੇ ਤੂੰ ਕੀਤੇ ਸੀ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਤੱਕ,  
> ਉਹ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਯਾਦ ਕਰਾਉਂਦੀਆਂ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کھوہاں تے پانی بھردیاں کڑیاں،  
> میرا مذاق اڈاوندیاں نے!  
> جو وعدے توں کیتے سی کلھ تک،  
> اوہ مینوں یاد کراوندییاں نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Khoohan te paani bhardiyaan kurhiyan,  
> Mera mazaak udaaundiyaan ne!  
> Jo vaade toon keete si kallh takk,  
> Oh mainu yaad karaaundiyaan ne!  

**English Translation**:
> The village maidens drawing water at the communal well  
> Mock and ridicule my solitary, abandoned condition!  
> Those fervent promises you whispered until yesterday  
> They maliciously bring back to torture my memory!  

💡 *Commentary*: The village well as community space turned theater of social humiliation.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਆਖੇ ਨੀ ਕਮਲੀਏ ਕੁੜੀਏ,  
> ਪਰਦੇਸੀ ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਮੁੜਦੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਤੋੜ ਗਏ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਤਾਰਾਂ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਉਹ ਮੁੜ ਕੇ ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਜੁੜਦੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو آکھے نی کملیئے کڑیئے،  
> پردیسی کدے نہ مڑدے نی!  
> جو توڑ گئے دل دے تاراں نوں،  
> اوہ مڑ کے کدے نہ جڑدے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv aakhe ni kamliye kurhiye,  
> Pardesi kade na murhde ni!  
> Jo torh gaye dil de taaraan nu,  
> Oh murh ke kade na jurhde ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv says to you, O innocent, heartbroken maiden:  
> Those who cross the black oceans never return home!  
> Those who recklessly severed the delicate strings of the heart  
> Can never mend or restore the music again!  

💡 *Commentary*: The irreversible rupture caused by overseas migration.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਢੋਲਾ (Dhola)** | *Dho-laa* | Beloved / Sweetheart | Classic romantic folk address in Western Punjabi poetry (especially Saraiki and Majhi). |

## 14. Batti Bujha De Maye

### **ਬੱਤੀ ਬੁਝਾ ਦੇ ਮਾਏ** • **بتی بجھا دے مائے**

#### *"Extinguish the Lamp, O Mother"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lamp` `#Darkness` `#Mother` `#Insomnia` `#Sleep`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The poet pleads with his mother to extinguish the bedroom lamp, as light hurts his burning eyes and darkness is the only remaining shelter.
- **Genesis**: Composed during his severe depressive episodes in Batala.
- **Archival Context**: Composed in Batala as Shiv's mother sat beside his sickbed during a season of relentless insomnia. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਬੱਤੀ ਬੁਝਾ ਦੇ ਮਾਏ ਨੀ ਮਾਏ,  
> ਮੇਰੀਆਂ ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਥੱਕੀਆਂ ਨੇ!  
> ਇਸ ਚਾਨਣ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੇਰੇ ਦਰਦਾਂ ਨੇ,  
> ਕਈ ਰਾਤਾਂ ਕੱਟੀਆਂ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> بتی بجھا دے مائے نی مائے،  
> میریاں اکھیاں تھکیاں نے!  
> اس چانن دے وچ میرے درداں نے،  
> کئی راتاں کٹیاں نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Batti bujhaa de maye ni maye,  
> Meriyaan akhiyaan thakkiyaan ne!  
> Iss chaanan de vich mere dardaan ne,  
> Kayi raataan kattiyaan ne!  

**English Translation**:
> Extinguish the burning lamp, O mother, my mother;  
> My weeping eyes are exhausted unto death!  
> Beneath the glaring intrusion of this light  
> My wounds have endured countless agonizing nights!  

💡 *Commentary*: Light experienced as pain; darkness sought as healing balm.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹਨੇਰਾ ਹੀ ਮੇਰਾ ਸਾਥੀ ਬਣਿਆ,  
> ਜੋ ਮੇਰੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਛੁਪਾਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਇਸ ਕਾਲ਼ੀ ਰਾਤ ਦੇ ਪਰਦੇ ਉਹਲੇ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਦਿਲ ਚੈਨ ਕੁਝ ਪਾਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہنیرا ہی میرا ساتھی بنیا،  
> جو میرے ہنجھو چھپاؤندا اے!  
> اس کالی رات دے پردے اوہلے،  
> میرا دل چین کچھ پاؤندا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Hanera hi mera saathi baneya,  
> Jo mere hanjhu chhupaaunda ae!  
> Iss kaali raat de parde ohle,  
> Mera dil chain kujh paaunda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Darkness alone has remained my faithful companion,  
> Sheltering and concealing my tears from mocking eyes!  
> Behind the dark curtain of this pitch-black night,  
> My writhing heart finds a brief measure of peace!  

💡 *Commentary*: Darkness as sanctuary against the gaze of society.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਦ ਮੇਰਾ ਦੀਵਾ ਬੁੱਝ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਤੂੰ ਰੋਈਂ ਨਾ ਮਾਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਸੌਂ ਗਿਆ ਲੰਮੀ ਨੀਂਦੇ,  
> ਉਸਨੂੰ ਜਗਾਈਂ ਨਾ ਮਾਏ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جد میرا دیوا بجھ جاوےگا،  
> تاں توں روئیں نہ مائے نی!  
> شیو تاں سوں گیا لمبی نیندے،  
> اس نوں جگائیں نہ مائے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Jad mera deeva bujjh jaavega,  
> Taan toon royin na maye ni!  
> Shiv taan saun gaya lammi neende,  
> Usnu jagaayin na maye ni!  

**English Translation**:
> When the frail earthen lamp of my life flickers and dies,  
> Shed not bitter tears, O mother!  
> For Shiv has finally fallen into that long, dreamless sleep;  
> Do not disturb or awaken him again, O mother!  

💡 *Commentary*: Epitaph addressed directly to the grieving mother.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਦੀਵਾ (Deeva)** | *Dee-vaa* | Clay oil lamp | Sacred folk symbol for human life and consciousness that flickers against cosmic winds. |

## 15. Dard Da Khel

### **ਦਰਦ ਦਾ ਖੇਲ** • **درد دا کھیل**

#### *"The Staged Game of Suffering"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Game` `#Dice` `#Gambling` `#TragicGame` `#Illusion`
- **Theme / Biome**: `modernism` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: Life is a rigged gambling game where the player wagers his heart and the house of fate always wins.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Chandigarh during nocturnal card games with artists.
- **Archival Context**: Evokes the ancient Mahabharata motif of Chaupar (dice game), depicting human life as a rigged gambling match. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਸਾਂ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਨਾਲ਼ ਜੁਆ ਖੇਡਿਆ,  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਹਰ ਇੱਕ ਬਾਜ਼ੀ ਹਾਰ ਗਈ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਦਿਲ 'ਤੇ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਮਾਣ ਬੜਾ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਹੋਣੀ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਮਾਰ ਗਈ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اساں زندگی نال جوا کھیڈیا،  
> ساڈی ہر اک بازی ہار گئی!  
> جس دل تے سانوں مان بڑا سی،  
> اوہی ہونی سانوں مار گئی!  

**Romanized**:
> Asaan zindagi naal juaa khediya,  
> Saadi har ikk baazi haar gayi!  
> Jis dil te saanu maan barha si,  
> Uhi honi saanu maar gayi!  

**English Translation**:
> We played a reckless game of dice with life itself,  
> And every single wager ended in total defeat!  
> That passionate heart of which we were so immensely proud  
> Was the very stake upon which fate delivered death!  

💡 *Commentary*: Life as a high-stakes gambling match lost to fate.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਡੇ ਪਾਸੇ ਪੁੱਠੇ ਪੈ ਗਏ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਦੁਸ਼ਮਣ ਜਿੱਤ ਕੇ ਹੱਸ ਪਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਉਮਰ ਦਾ ਸਾਰਾ ਸਰਮਾਇਆ,  
> ਪਲਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੀ ਖੱਸ ਪਿਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ساڈے پاسے پٹھے پے گئے اڑیو،  
> دشمن جت کے ہس پیا اے!  
> ساڈی عمر دا سارا سرمایہ،  
> پلاں دے وچ ہی کھس پیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Saade paase putthe pai gaye arhiyo,  
> Dushman jitt ke hass peya ae!  
> Saadi umar da saara sarmaaya,  
> Palaan de vich hi khass peya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> The rolling dice fell contrary to our prayers, friends;  
> The callous adversary won and laughed in triumphant mockery!  
> The entire accumulated capital of our youthful years  
> Was plundered and snatched away in a heartbeat!  

💡 *Commentary*: Total loss of artistic and emotional capital.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਹਾਰ ਕੇ ਵੀ ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਰੋਇਆ,  
> ਉਸ ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਬਾਜ਼ੀ ਛੱਡ ਦਿੱਤੀ!  
> ਇਸ ਜੱਗ ਦੇ ਝੂਠੇ ਖੇਲ 'ਚੋਂ,  
> ਉਸ ਆਪਣੀ ਹਸਤੀ ਕੱਢ ਲਿੱਤੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو ہار کے وی کدے نہ رویا،  
> اس ہس کے بازی چھڈ دتی!  
> اس جگ دے جھوٹھے کھیل چوں،  
> اس اپنی ہستی کڈھ لتی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv haar ke vi kade na royea,  
> Us hass ke baazi chhadd ditti!  
> Iss jagg de jhoothe khel chon,  
> Us aapni hasti kaddh litti!  

**English Translation**:
> Even in defeat, Shiv shed not a single humiliated tear;  
> With a serene smile, he vacated the gambling table forever!  
> From this fraudulent, rigged game of the mortal world,  
> He withdrew his sovereign existence and walked free!  

💡 *Commentary*: Victory achieved by walking away from the rigged game.

## 16. Vidaai Geet

### **ਵਿਦਾਈ ਗੀਤ** • **وداعئی گیت**

- **Synopsis**: A grand valedictory song addressing the earth, the sky, the rivers, and future generations of Punjabi lovers.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala as the final concluding poem for the 1963 manuscript.
- **Archival Context**: Closes the architectural arc of Mainu Vida Karo (1963), solidifying Shiv's status as the poet of eternal farewell. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:

> ਜੋ ਦਿਲ 'ਚ ਸੀ ਉਹ ਕਹਿ ਦਿੱਤਾ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਜਾਣ ਦੀ ਰੁੱਤ ਆਈ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میرا گیت سنو میرے ہانیو،  
> ایہہ آخری تار ہلائی اے!  
> جو دل چ سی اوہ کہہ دتا،  
> ہن جان دی رت آئی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Mera geet suno mere haaniyo,  
> Eh aakhri taar hilaayi ae!  
> Jo dil ch si oh keh ditta,  
> Hun jaan di rutt aayi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Listen to my song, O companions of my youth;  
> I have plucked the final resonating string of my instrument!  
> Whatever was concealed within my heart I have poured out;  
> Now the unsparing season of departure has arrived!  

💡 *Commentary*: The final chord played upon the instrument of life.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਿਸੇ ਯਾਰ ਨੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਕਦਰ ਨਾ ਜਾਣੀ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਦਿਲ ਨੇ ਭੇਤ ਨਾ ਪਾਇਆ ਨੀ!

> ਜੋ ਧਰਤੀ 'ਤੇ ਢਹਿ ਆਇਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کسے یار نے میری قدر نہ جانی،  
> کسے دل نے بھیت نہ پایا نی!  
> میں درداں دا اک پہاڑ ساں،  
> جو دھرتی تے ڈھہہ آیا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Kise yaar ne meri kadar na jaani,  
> Kise dil ne bhet na paaya ni!  
> Main dardaan da ikk pahaar saan,  
> Jo dharti te dheh aaya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Not a single companion recognized the sacred worth of my love;  
> Not a single living soul penetrated the mystery of my breast!  
> I was a colossal mountain of holy sorrows  
> That collapsed in devastating landslide upon the plains!  

💡 *Commentary*: The mountain of sorrow collapsing onto the plain.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਮੇਰੀ ਇਸ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਨੂੰ,

**Shahmukhi**:
> الوداع میری اس زندگی نوں،  
> الوداع میرے سنسار نوں!  
> شیو تاں ہس کے تر چلیا،  
> سونپ کے اپنے پیار نوں!  

**Romanized**:
> Alvida meri iss zindagi nu,  
> Alvida mere sansaar nu!  
> Shiv taan hass ke tur challeya,  
> Saump ke aapne pyaar nu!  

**English Translation**:
> Farewell to this brief, agonized incarnation!  
> Farewell to this bustling, uncaring universe!  
> Shiv sets forth upon his journey with a serene smile,  
> Entrusting his immortal love to the keeping of time!  

💡 *Commentary*: The peaceful, triumphant conclusion of Mainu Vida Karo.

## 17. Kafan Di Chaadar

### **ਕਫ਼ਨ ਦੀ ਚਾਦਰ** • **کفن دی چادر**

#### *"The Shroud of White Mist"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#Mortality` `#Shroud` `#NocturnalDeparture`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The poet contemplates the white shroud of mortality not with horror, but as the only pure, unsoiled garment awaiting his tired body.
- **Genesis**: Shiv suffered chronic respiratory illness and frequently spoke of the shroud as his true wedding attire.
- **Archival Context**: Marks Shiv's deepening obsession with the shroud (*kafan*) as a bridal garment, composed during his hospitalizations for pleurisy. (*Citation: `SHARMA-1979-SOLITARY`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਚਿੱਟੀ ਚਾਦਰ ਕਫ਼ਨ ਦੀ ਲੈ ਕੇ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਸੌਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੁੱਕ ਜਾਣਗੇ ਸਾਰੇ ਝੋਰੇ ਸਾਡੇ,  
> ਭੁੱਲ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ ਮੁਲਾਕਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> چٹی چادر کفن دی لے کے،  
> میں سون جاواں گا رات نوں نی!  
> مک جان گے سارے جھورے ساڈے،  
> بھل جاواں گے ملاقات نوں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Chitti chaadar kafan di lai ke,  
> Main saun jaawaanga raat nu ni!  
> Mukk jaange saare jhore saade,  
> Bhull jaawaange mulaakaat nu ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Draping the pure white sheet of the shroud around my shoulders,  
> I shall lay down to sleep beneath the stars tonight!  
> All the gnawing worries of our tortured existence will cease,  
> And we shall forget every bittersweet meeting of the past!  

💡 *Commentary*: The shroud viewed as an instrument of peaceful oblivion.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਸ ਚਾਦਰ ਤੇ ਦਾਗ਼ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ,  
> ਜਿਵੇਂ ਸਾਡੇ ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਪਿਆਰ ਸੀ ਵੇ!  
> ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਨੇ ਤਾਂ ਮੈਲਾ ਕੀਤਾ,  
> ਜੋ ਵੀ ਸਾਡਾ ਇਤਬਾਰ ਸੀ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اس چادر تے داغ نہ کوئی،  
> جیویں ساڈے دل دا پیار سی وے!  
> دنیا نے تاں میلا کیتا،  
> جو وی ساڈا اعتبار سی وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Iss chaadar te daagh na koyi,  
> Jiven saade dil da pyaar si ve!  
> Duniya ne taan maila keeta,  
> Jo vi saada aitbaar si ve!  

**English Translation**:
> Upon this linen shroud rests not a single blemish or stain,  
> Pure as the unuttered love that once dwelt in our heart!  
> While this callous, transactional world stained with mud  
> Every tender faith that we had placed in human pledges!  

💡 *Commentary*: The immaculate purity of the grave vs. the contamination of living society.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਰੋਵੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਿਰਹਾਣੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਹੰਝੂ ਕੇਰੇ ਵੇ!  
> ਬੱਸ ਕਫ਼ਨ ਦੀ ਓਟ 'ਚ ਸੌਣ ਦਿਓ,  
> ਮਿਟ ਗਏ ਸਭ ਝੇੜੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کوئی نہ رووے میرے سرہانے،  
> نہ کوئی ہنجھو کیرے وے!  
> بس کفن دی اوٹ چ سون دیو،  
> مٹ گئے سبھ جھیڑے میرے وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Koyi na rove mere sirhaane,  
> Na koyi hanjhu kere ve!  
> Bass kafan di oat ch saun diyo,  
> Mitt gaye sabh jherhe mere ve!  

**English Translation**:
> Let no mourner wail beside my quiet pillow,  
> Nor shed a single counterfeit tear upon my brow!  
> Only permit me to slumber undisturbed beneath the shroud's veil,  
> For all my bitter disputes with this world have been settled!  

💡 *Commentary*: Rejection of funeral mourning: silence is the only appropriate response.

## 18. Maut Di Sej

### **ਮੌਤ ਦੀ ਸੇਜ** • **موت دی سیج**

#### *"The Nuptial Bed of Death"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#DeathasBride` `#ErosandThanatos` `#Nuptials`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The funeral bier is envisioned as a bridal bed (*sej*), where the poet at last consummates his eternal union with Death.
- **Genesis**: Amrita Pritam famously observed: 'Death did not steal Shiv; Shiv courted death as his final bride.'
- **Archival Context**: Direct manifestation of Shiv's core metaphysical conceit: Death is not an executioner, but the true and faithful bride (*vahuuti*). (*Citation: `AMRITA-1974-NAGMANI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੌਤ ਦੀ ਸੇਜ ਸਜਾਈ ਏ ਅਸੀਂ,  
> ਫੁੱਲਾਂ ਦੀ ਥਾਂ ਕੰਡੇ ਧਰ ਕੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਅੱਜ ਸਾਡਾ ਵਿਆਹ ਹੋਣਾ ਏ,  
> ਇਸ ਜੀਵਨ ਤੋਂ ਪਰੇ ਖੜ੍ਹ ਕੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> موت دی سیج سجائی اے اسیں،  
> پھلاں دی تھاں کنڈے دھر کے نی!  
> اج ساڈا ویاہ ہونا اے،  
> اس جیون توں پرے کھڑھ کے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Maut di sej sajaayi ae aseen,  
> Phullaan di thaan kande dhar ke ni!  
> Ajj saada viah hona ae,  
> Iss jeevan ton pare kharh ke ni!  

**English Translation**:
> We have adorned the nuptial bed of Death with our own hands,  
> Spreading sharp thorns in place of scented rose petals!  
> Today our sacred wedding shall at last be solemnized,  
> Standing proudly beyond the frontier of this earthly life!  

💡 *Commentary*: Inverts the traditional wedding bed (*suhag sej*) into an altar of mortal sacrifice.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੌਤ ਸਾਡੀ ਲਾੜੀ ਬਣ ਕੇ ਆਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਘੁੰਡ ਕੱਢ ਕੇ ਚੁੱਪ-ਚਾਪ ਵੇ!  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਸ਼ਹਿਨਾਈ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਢੋਲਕ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਦਾ ਜਾਪ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> موت ساڈی لاڑی بن کے آوے گی،  
> گھنڈ کڈھ کے چپ چاپ وے!  
> نہ کوئی شہنائی نہ کوئی ڈھولک،  
> نہ کوئی گیتاں دا جاپ وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Maut saadi laarhi ban ke aavegi,  
> Ghundd kaddh ke chup-chaap ve!  
> Na koyi shehnaayi na koyi dholak,  
> Na koyi geetaan da jaap ve!  

**English Translation**:
> Death shall arrive as our veiled, faithful bride,  
> Gliding in majestic, absolute silence through the dusk!  
> No festive oboe of shehnai nor beating drums will attend us,  
> Nor any chanting of cheerful wedding melodies!  

💡 *Commentary*: The silence of death replaces noisy Indian matrimonial celebrations.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਉਸ ਸੇਜ ਤੇ ਜਾ ਕੇ ਸੌਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ,  
> ਮੁੜ ਕੇ ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਜਾਗਾਂਗੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਇਸ ਜੱਗ ਨੇ ਦਿੱਤਾ ਏ,  
> ਉਸ ਤੋਂ ਸਦਾ ਲਈ ਭੱਜਾਂਗੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اس سیج تے جا کے سون جاواں گے،  
> مڑ کے کدے نہ جاگاں گے نی!  
> جو درد اس جگ نے دتا اے،  
> اس توں سدا لئی بھجاں گے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Oss sej te jaa ke saun jaawaange,  
> Murh ke kade na jaagaange ni!  
> Jo dard iss jagg ne ditta ae,  
> Uss ton sadaa layi bhajjaange ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Upon that tranquil nuptial couch we shall lay our head,  
> Never again to awaken into this agonizing daylight!  
> From that bitter torment which this callous world inflicted,  
> We shall make our eternal, inviolable escape!  

💡 *Commentary*: Eternity as absolute refuge from human cruelty.

## 19. Chikha Di Agg

### **ਚਿਖ਼ਾ ਦੀ ਅੱਗ** • **چکھا دی اگ**

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#Cremation` `#Fire` `#Purification`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The leaping flames of the cremation pyre are welcomed as the only fire capable of consuming the unquenchable fire of separation.
- **Genesis**: Balwant Gargi recorded that Shiv found cremation grounds peaceful and free of hypocritical social posturing.
- **Archival Context**: Shiv regularly visited the cremation grounds (*sive*) on the outskirts of Batala and Chandigarh to meditate on pyres. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਚਿਖ਼ਾ ਦੀ ਅੱਗ ਬਲ ਉੱਠੀ ਏ,  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਜਿੰਦ ਨੂੰ ਸਾੜਨ ਲਈ!  
> ਇਸ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਲੰਮੇ ਦਰਦ ਤੋਂ,  
> ਸਦਾ ਲਈ ਛੁਡਾਵਣ ਲਈ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> چکھا دی اگ بل اٹھی اے،  
> ساڈی جند نوں ساڑن لئی!  
> اس برہا دے لمبے درد توں،  
> سدا لئی چھڈاون لئی!  

**Romanized**:
> Chikha di agg bal utthi ae,  
> Saadi jind nu saardan layi!  
> Iss birha de lamme dard ton,  
> Sadaa layi chhudaawan layi!  

**English Translation**:
> The fierce flames of the funeral pyre have leaped into the sky,  
> To consume the fragile flesh of our mortal existence!  
> And from this lifelong, agonizing torment of separation,  
> To grant us eternal emancipation at last!  

💡 *Commentary*: Fire as the purifier and liberator from emotional anguish.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਲੱਕੜਾਂ ਸੜਦੀਆਂ ਚਟਖ ਚਟਖ ਕੇ,  
> ਜਿਵੇਂ ਸਾਡੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਟੁਕੜੇ ਵੇ!  
> ਅੰਬਰ ਤੇ ਧੂੰਆਂ ਫੈਲ ਗਿਆ ਏ,  
> ਮਿਟ ਗਏ ਸਾਡੇ ਸਭ ਦੁਖੜੇ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> لکڑاں سڑدیاں چٹخ چٹخ کے،  
> جیویں ساڈے دل دے ٹکڑے وے!  
> انبر تے دھواں پھیل گیا اے،  
> مٹ گئے ساڈے سبھ دکھڑے وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Lakkrhaan sarhdiyaan chatakh chatakh ke,  
> Jiven saade dil de tukrhe ve!  
> Ambar te dhuan phail gaya ae,  
> Mitt gaye saade sabh dukhrhe ve!  

**English Translation**:
> The dry logs crackle and snap in the white heat,  
> Like the breaking shards of our own fractured heart!  
> Pallid smoke billows upwards into the vault of heaven,  
> And all our terrestrial afflictions dissolve into air!  

💡 *Commentary*: Onomatopoeic crackling (*chatakh chatakh*) of burning wood as physical agony.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਦ ਇਹ ਅੱਗ ਸ਼ਾਂਤ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਬੱਸ ਰਾਖ ਬਾਕੀ ਰਹਿ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ!  
> ਉਹ ਰਾਖ ਦਰਿਆ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੁੜ੍ਹ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਤੇ ਦਰਦ ਦੀ ਬਾਤ ਕਹਿ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جد ایہ اگ شانت ہووے گی،  
> بس راکھ باقی رہ جاوے گی!  
> اوہ راکھ دریا وچ رڑھ جاوے گی،  
> تے درد دی بات کہہ جاوے گی!  

**Romanized**:
> Jad eh agg shaant hovegi,  
> Bass raakh baaki reh jaavegi!  
> Oh raakh dariya vich rurh jaavegi,  
> Te dard di baat keh jaavegi!  

**English Translation**:
> When this consuming fire subsides into gentle silence,  
> Only a handful of pale grey ash shall remain behind!  
> Those ashes will be scattered upon the running river,  
> Whispering the eternal chronicle of our sorrow to the sea!  

💡 *Commentary*: The dispersion of ashes (*asthi visarjan*) as the transmission of song to nature.

## 20. Tutte Dastkhat

### **ਟੁੱਟੇ ਦਸਤਖ਼ਤ** • **ٹٹے دستخط**

#### *"Broken Signature on Destiny"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#Destiny` `#Signatures` `#Fate`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The poet's signature upon the deed of life was written in trembling ink that smudged and broke before the contract of joy could be sealed.
- **Genesis**: Shiv hated his brief stint in bureaucratic administrative offices where human passion was reduced to stamps and signatures.
- **Archival Context**: Shiv worked as a patwari (village revenue clerk) where signatures on land deeds decided human fortunes, inspiring this legal metaphor. (*Citation: `SHARMA-1979-SOLITARY`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਿਸਮਤ ਦੇ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ ਉੱਤੇ ਸਾਡੇ,  
> ਦਸਤਖ਼ਤ ਟੁੱਟ ਕੇ ਰਹਿ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਸੁੱਖ ਅਸੀਂ ਲਿਖਵਾਏ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਦੁੱਖਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਵਹਿ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> قسمت دے کاغذ اتے ساڈے،  
> دستخط ٹٹ کے رہ گئے نی!  
> جو سکھ اسیں لکھوائے سی،  
> اوہ دکھاں دے وچ وہہ گئے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Kismat de kaagaz utte saade,  
> Dastkhat tutt ke reh gaye ni!  
> Jo sukh aseen likhvaaye si,  
> Oh dukkhaan de vich veh gaye ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Upon the solemn parchment deed of Destiny  
> Our trembling signature fractured before it was complete!  
> Those peaceful fortunes we had hoped to record  
> Were washed away in the rising flood of affliction!  

💡 *Commentary*: Bureaucratic and legal metaphor: a broken signature invalidating the covenant of happiness.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਿਆਹੀ ਫੈਲ ਗਈ ਕਾਲੀ ਹੋ ਕੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਅੱਖਰ ਨਾ ਪੜ੍ਹਿਆ ਜਾਵੇ ਵੇ!  
> ਇਸ ਬੇਵਫ਼ਾ ਜਹਾਨ ਦੇ ਦਫ਼ਤਰ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਦਾਅਵਾ ਕੌਣ ਸੁਣਾਵੇ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> سیاہی پھیل گئی کالی ہو کے،  
> کوئی اکھر نہ پڑھیا جاوے وے!  
> اس بےوفا جہان دے دفتر وچ،  
> ساڈا دعویٰ کون سناوے وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Siaahi phail gayi kaali ho ke,  
> Koyi akkhar na parhiya jaave ve!  
> Iss bewafa jahaan de daftar vich,  
> Saada daawa kaun sunaave ve!  

**English Translation**:
> The black ink bled and smudged across the page,  
> Rendering every noble covenant unreadable to the eye!  
> In the cold, indifferent chancery of this false universe,  
> Who will plead the petition of our wounded soul?  

💡 *Commentary*: Kafkaesque legal imagery applied to existential alienation.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਕਲਮ ਵੀ ਟੁੱਟ ਗਈ ਹੱਥੋਂ ਸਾਡੇ,  
> ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ ਵੀ ਪਾਟ ਕੇ ਲੀਰਾਂ ਹੋਇਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੌਤ ਨੇ ਆ ਕੇ ਫ਼ੈਸਲਾ ਸੁਣਾਇਆ,  
> ਜੋ ਸਾਡੇ ਹੱਕ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੀਰਾਂ ਹੋਇਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن قلم وی ٹٹ گئی ہتھوں ساڈے،  
> کاغذ وی پاٹ کے لیراں ہویا نی!  
> موت نے آ کے فیصلہ سنایا،  
> جو ساڈے حق وچ ہیراں ہویا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun kalam vi tutt gayi hatthon saade,  
> Kaagaz vi paat ke leeraan hoyea ni!  
> Maut ne aa ke faisla sunaaya,  
> Jo saade hakk vich heeraan hoyea ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Now the reed pen has snapped in our exhausted hand,  
> The legal deed has shredded into useless tattered rags!  
> Until Death herself arrived to pronounce the final verdict,  
> Which shone like a liberating diamond in our defense!  

💡 *Commentary*: Death as the benevolent supreme judge who dismisses the suit.

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਦਸਤਖ਼ਤ (Dastkhat)** | *Dast-khat* | Handwritten signature | Persian-Urdu term for formal signature validating contracts and court deeds. |

## 21. Siveyaan Di Raakh

### **ਸਿਵਿਆਂ ਦੀ ਰਾਖ** • **سویاں دی راکھ**

#### *"Ashes of the Smoldering Pyre"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#Ashes` `#Cremation` `#Nihilism`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: Cold ashes remaining after the pyre has died down: all beauty, fame, and tragic agony reduced to a handful of grey dust.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's ongoing confrontation with the absolute physical finality of cremation.
- **Archival Context**: Written after Shiv attended the cremation of a close friend in Batala, profoundly shaken by the swiftness of incineration. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਿਵਿਆਂ ਦੀ ਰਾਖ ਫਰੋਲ ਕੇ ਵੇਖੋ,  
> ਕੀ ਬਚਿਆ ਇਸ ਜੀਵਨ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਕਦੇ ਸੋਹਣਾ ਮੁੱਖੜਾ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਮਿਲ ਗਿਆ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> سویاں دی راکھ پھول کے ویکھو،  
> کی بچیا اس جیون وچوں نی!  
> جو کدے سوہنا مکھڑا سی،  
> اوہ مل گیا مٹی وچوں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Siveyaan di raakh farol ke vekho,  
> Ki bacheya iss jeevan vichon ni!  
> Jo kade sohna mukhrha si,  
> Oh mil gaya mitti vichon ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Stir the cold ashes of the cremation ground and behold  
> What residue survives of this proud, turbulent life!  
> That face which was once radiant beyond earthly comparison  
> Has merged indistinguishably with the common soil!  

💡 *Commentary*: The stirring of cold ashes (*raakh farol ke*) as the ultimate memento mori.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹਵਾ ਉਡਾਵੇ ਇਸ ਰਾਖ ਨੂੰ ਹੁਣ,  
> ਖੇਤਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਖਿਲਾਰਦੀ ਏ!  
> ਜਿਵੇਂ ਕੋਈ ਰੋਂਦੀ ਮਾਂ ਆਪਣੇ,  
> ਪੁੱਤ ਨੂੰ ਦੂਰੋਂ ਤਾੜਦੀ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہوا اڈاوے اس راکھ نوں ہن،  
> کھیتاں وچ کھلارد ی اے!  
> جیویں کوئی روندی ماں اپنے،  
> پت نوں دوروں تاڑدی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Hawa udaave iss raakh nu hun,  
> Khetaan vich khilaardi ae!  
> Jiven koyi rondi maan apne,  
> Putt nu dooron taardi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> The restless wind lifts these ashes upon its breath,  
> Scattering them gently across the harvested wheat fields!  
> Just as a grieving mother watches from the courtyard gate  
> As her departing son disappears into the distance!  

💡 *Commentary*: Ashes scattering over farmland: returning nutrients to the earth.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਸੀਂ ਵੀ ਇੱਕ ਦਿਨ ਰਾਖ ਬਣਾਂਗੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਯਾਦ ਕਰੇਗਾ ਵੇ!  
> ਬੱਸ ਇਹ ਗੀਤ ਹਵਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੂੰਜਣਗੇ,  
> ਜਦ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ ਕੋਈ ਦੀਵਾ ਬਲੇਗਾ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اسیں وی اک دن راکھ بناں گے،  
> کوئی نہ سانوں یاد کرے گا وے!  
> بس ایہ گیت ہوا وچ گونجن گے،  
> جد برہا دا کوئی دیوا بلے گا وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Aseen vi ikk din raakh banaange,  
> Koyi na saanu yaad karega ve!  
> Bass eh geet hawa vich goonjange,  
> Jad birha da koyi deeva balega ve!  

**English Translation**:
> We too shall one day be reduced to identical grey ash,  
> And none in this uncaring world will preserve our name!  
> Only these tragic songs shall echo upon the nocturnal winds  
> Whenever the sacred lamp of longing is kindled in the dark!  

💡 *Commentary*: Immortality conceded only to the song, not the singer.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਰਾਖ (Raakh)** | *Raakh* | Ashes of cremation | Sacred post-cremation ash collected in copper urns for immersion in the river. |

## 22. Akhri Manzil

### **ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ** • **آخری منزل**

#### *"The Terminal Destination"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#Destination` `#Journey'sEnd` `#Grave`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: All journeys across roads, fields, and cities terminate at the same quiet mound of earth where the traveler finally sleeps.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Chandigarh as Shiv's bohemian lifestyle accelerated toward physical collapse.
- **Archival Context**: A philosophical culmination of Shiv's 'Mainu Vida Karo' volume, bidding farewell to all earthly companions. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ ਆ ਗਈ ਸਾਡੀ,  
> ਰਾਹ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਏ ਨੇ ਸਾਰੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਹੁਣ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਪੈਂਡਾ ਬਾਕੀ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਦਿਸਣ ਕਿਨਾਰੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> آخری منزل آ گئی ساڈی،  
> راہ مک گئے نے سارے نی!  
> ہن نہ کوئی پینڈا باقی،  
> نہ کوئی دسن کنارے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Akhri manzil aa gayi saadi,  
> Raah mukk gaye ne saare ni!  
> Hun na koyi painda baaki,  
> Na koyi disan kinaare ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Our terminal destination has appeared upon the horizon,  
> All earthly roads have reached their ultimate end!  
> No further journey remains for our weary feet to tread,  
> Nor do any shores beckon across the dark expanse!  

💡 *Commentary*: Exhaustion of the road (*painda*): the completion of life's forced march.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇੱਥੇ ਸਭ ਬਰਾਬਰ ਹੋ ਗਏ,  
> ਰਾਜਾ ਤੇ ਕੰਗਾਲ ਵੇ!  
> ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦੀ ਚਾਦਰ ਹੇਠਾਂ ਸੌਂ ਗਏ,  
> ਮਿਟ ਗਏ ਸਭ ਸਵਾਲ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ایتھے سبھ برابر ہو گئے،  
> راجہ تے کنگال وے!  
> مٹی دی چادر ہیٹھاں سون گئے،  
> مٹ گئے سبھ سوال وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Itthe sabh baraabar ho gaye,  
> Raaja te kangaal ve!  
> Mitti di chaadar hethaan saun gaye,  
> Mitt gaye sabh sawaal ve!  

**English Translation**:
> Here in this quiet soil all mortals achieve equal rank,  
> The emperor and the destitute beggar sleep as peers!  
> Slumbering together beneath the common earthen blanket,  
> All worldly disputes and bitter questions are erased!  

💡 *Commentary*: Egalitarianism of the grave: the tomb dissolves social hierarchy.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਓ ਦੋਸਤੋ ਮੇਰਿਓ,  
> ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਓ ਜੱਗ ਵਾਲਿਓ!  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਗਲ ਨਾਲ ਲਾ ਲਿਓ,  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਕਬਰ ਤੇ ਦੀਵਾ ਬਾਲਿਓ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> الوداع او دوستو مہریو،  
> الوداع او جگ والیو!  
> ساڈے گیتاں نوں گل نال لا لیو،  
> ساڈی قبر تے دیوا بالیو!  

**Romanized**:
> Alvida o dosto mereyo,  
> Alvida o jagg vaaliyo!  
> Saade geetaan nu gal naal laa liyo,  
> Saadi kabr te deeva baaliyo!  

**English Translation**:
> Adieu, O companions of my brief and stormy youth,  
> Adieu, O dwellers of this unsparing, beautiful world!  
> Gather my tragic songs and hold them close to your heart,  
> And kindle a solitary clay lamp upon my silent grave!  

💡 *Commentary*: The final testament: the bequest of songs to posterity.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਪੈਂਡਾ (Painda)** | *Pain-da* | Long footpath / Journey | The rustic Punjabi term for foot journeys between distant villages; metaphor for lifespan. |

## 23. Chup De Vass

### **ਚੁੱਪ ਦੇ ਵੱਸ** • **چپ دے وس**

#### *"Under the Dominion of Silence"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#Silence` `#Submission` `#Despair`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The poet surrenders unconditionally to silence, recognizing that words have lost their potency against the immensity of grief.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's acknowledgment that the deepest layer of human agony exists beyond the reach of lyric vocabulary.
- **Archival Context**: Reflects Shiv's periods of depressive withdrawal where he refused to speak or recite for weeks at a time. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਸੀਂ ਚੁੱਪ ਦੇ ਵੱਸ ਪੈ ਗਏ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਬੋਲ ਨਾ ਸਾਥੋਂ ਹੋਵੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਅਸੀਂ ਸੀਨੇ ਸੰਭਾਲਿਆ,  
> ਉਹ ਅੰਦਰੋਂ ਅੰਦਰੀਂ ਰੋਵੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اسیں چپ دے وس پے گئے ہاں،  
> ہن بول نہ ساتھوں ہووے نی!  
> جو درد اسیں سینے سنبھالیا،  
> اوہ اندروں اندریں رووے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Aseen chup de vass pai gaye haan,  
> Hun bol na saathon hove ni!  
> Jo dard aseen seene sambhaaleya,  
> Oh andron andreen rove ni!  

**English Translation**:
> We have fallen under the absolute dominion of Silence,  
> No words can cross the threshold of our lips anymore!  
> That colossal agony we treasured within our breast  
> Weeps in solitary darkness deep inside our soul!  

💡 *Commentary*: Silence (*chup*) personified as an authoritarian master.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਲੋਕੀਂ ਕਹਿਣ ਇਹ ਗੁੰਗਾ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਗੀਤ ਇਹਦੇ ਸਭ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਏ ਨੇ!  
> ਕੀ ਜਾਣਨ ਉਹ ਪੱਥਰ ਦਿਲ ਵਾਲੇ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਅੰਦਰੋਂ ਸੁੱਕ ਗਏ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> لوکی کہن ایہ گنگا ہویا،  
> گیت ایہدے سبھ مک گئے نے!  
> کی جانن اوہ پتھر دل والے،  
> ساڈے ہنجھو اندروں سک گئے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Loki kehan eh gunga hoyea,  
> Geet ehde sabh mukk gaye ne!  
> Ki jaanan oh patthar dil vaale,  
> Saade hanjhu andron sukk gaye ne!  

**English Translation**:
> Callous observers remark that he has turned stone-mute,  
> That the fountain of his songs has dried up forever!  
> What can those marble-hearted critics ever understand  
> Of how our tears turned to salt and bone deep within?  

💡 *Commentary*: Critique of literary commentators who judged silence as creative death.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਹ ਚੁੱਪ ਸਾਡੀ ਕਬਰ ਬਣੇਗੀ,  
> ਇਹਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ ਸਮਾਵਾਂਗੇ!  
> ਜਦ ਮੌਤ ਆ ਕੇ ਬੂਹਾ ਖੜਕਾਊ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਅੰਤਮ ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਗਾਵਾਂਗੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ایہ چپ ساڈی قبر بنے گی،  
> ایہندے وچ اسیں سماواں گے!  
> جد موت آ کے بوہا کھڑکاؤ،  
> تاں انتم الوداع گاواں گے!  

**Romanized**:
> Eh chup saadi kabr banegi,  
> Ehde vich aseen samaawaange!  
> Jad maut aa ke booha kharkaa-oo,  
> Taan antam alvida gaawaange!  

**English Translation**:
> This vast silence shall become our living tomb,  
> Into whose quiet depths we shall dissolve forever!  
> Only when Death arrives to knock upon our locked door  
> Shall we break our silence to sing the final Adieu!  

💡 *Commentary*: The final song reserved solely for Death.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਚੁੱਪ (Chup)** | *Chup* | Silence / Wordlessness | A central philosophical concept in Punjabi mysticism representing the stillness beyond linguistic expression. |

## 24. Sukke Rukhan Da Shok

### **ਸੁੱਕੇ ਰੁੱਖਾਂ ਦਾ ਸੋਗ** • **سکے رخاں دا سوگ**

#### *"Mourning of the Leafless Trees"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#Animism` `#WitheredTrees` `#Autumn`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: Leafless winter trees stand like naked mourners in the cold wind, sharing the poet's silent desolation.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's deep attachment to the flora of Majha; he felt trees experienced human grief and abandonment.
- **Archival Context**: A companion piece to 'Kujh Rukh', showcasing Shiv's profound ecological and animistic empathy with trees. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸੁੱਕੇ ਰੁੱਖ ਖੜ੍ਹੇ ਨੇ ਰਾਹਾਂ ਤੇ,  
> ਨੰਗੇ ਪਿੰਡੇ ਸੋਗ ਮਨਾਉਂਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਪੱਤੇ ਝੜ ਗਏ ਸਾਰੇ ਉਹਨਾਂ ਦੇ,  
> ਚੁੱਪ-ਚਾਪ ਹੰਝੂ ਵਹਾਉਂਦੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> سکے رخ کھڑے نے راہاں تے،  
> ننگے پنڈے سوگ مناؤندے نے!  
> پتے جھڑ گئے سارے اوہناں دے،  
> چپ چاپ ہنجھو وہاؤندے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Sukke rukkh kharhe ne raahaan te,  
> Nange pinde shok manaaunde ne!  
> Patte jharh gaye saare ohnaan de,  
> Chup-chaap hanjhu vahaaunde ne!  

**English Translation**:
> Leafless withered trees stand along the deserted highway,  
> Observing funeral mourning with bare, shivering bodies!  
> Every single leaf of their spring foliage has fallen to dust,  
> Yet they weep silent, invisible tears in the winter cold!  

💡 *Commentary*: Naked branches (*nange pinde*) likened to ascetic mourners.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਪੰਛੀ ਬਹਿੰਦਾ ਇਹਨਾਂ ਤੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਰਾਹੀ ਛਾਂ ਮੰਗਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਕਿਸਮਤ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਇਹ ਰੁੱਖੜੇ,  
> ਜਿਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਕੋਲੋਂ ਜੱਗ ਲੰਘਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ کوئی پنچھی بہندا ایہناں تے،  
> نہ کوئی راہی چھاں منگدا اے!  
> ساڈی قسمت وانگوں ایہ رکھڑے،  
> جنہاں کولوں جگ لنگھدا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Na koyi panchhi behnda ehnaan te,  
> Na koyi raahi chhaan mangda ae!  
> Saadi kismat vaangoo eh rukkhre,  
> Jinhaan kolon jagg langhda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Not a solitary bird perches upon their desolate boughs,  
> Not a weary traveler pauses to ask for their shade!  
> Identical to our own forsaken, ruined destiny  
> Are these bare trees past whom the hurried world marches!  

💡 *Commentary*: Uselessness in the eyes of pragmatic society: no shade, no fruit.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਨੂੰ ਇਹ ਵੀ ਵੱਢੇ ਜਾਣਗੇ,  
> ਬਾਲਣ ਬਣ ਕੇ ਸੜਨਗੇ ਵੇ!  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਇਹ ਵੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਹੋ,  
> ਧਰਤੀ ਦੀ ਹਿੱਕ ਤੇ ਰੜਨਗੇ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کلھ نوں ایہ وی وڈھے جان گے،  
> بالن بن کے سڑن گے وے!  
> ساڈے وانگوں ایہ وی مٹی ہو،  
> دھرتی دی ہک تے رڑن گے وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kallh nu eh vi vaddhe jaange,  
> Baalan ban ke sarhange ve!  
> Saade vaangoo eh vi mitti ho,  
> Dharti di hikk te rarhange ve!  

**English Translation**:
> Tomorrow the woodcutter's ax will arrive to fell them,  
> To be burnt as firewood in indifferent ovens!  
> Like ourselves they too will dissolve into cold ash,  
> Scattered forever across the wounded breast of the earth!  

💡 *Commentary*: Final kinship between the felled tree and the dying poet.

## 25. Handheri Gali

### **ਹਨੇਰੀ ਗਲੀ** • **ہنیری گلی**

#### *"The Dark and Narrow Alley"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#Alley` `#Claustrophobia` `#City`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: Trapped in a narrow, pitch-black alleyway where the sky is reduced to a thin slit and no light can penetrate the soul.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's claustrophobia when confined to small rented rooms in old town quarters.
- **Archival Context**: Captures the suffocating old medieval quarters of Batala and Lahore, contrasting with open fields. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਸ ਹਨੇਰੀ ਗਲੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਤੁਰਦਿਆਂ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਸਾਹ ਘੁੱਟਦਾ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਰੌਸ਼ਨੀ ਦੀ ਕਿਰਨ ਨਾ ਆਵੇ,  
> ਦਰਦ ਅੰਦਰੋਂ ਖਾਂਦਾ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اس ہنیری گلی وچ توردیاں،  
> ساڈا ساہ گھٹدا جاندا اے!  
> کوئی روشنی دی کرن نہ آوے،  
> درد اندروں کھاندا جاندا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Iss haneri gali vich turdeyaan,  
> Saada saah ghuttda jaanda ae!  
> Koyi raushni di kiran na aave,  
> Dard andron khaanda jaanda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Walking down this dark and suffocating alleyway,  
> Our breath is slowly choked within our throat!  
> Not a single ray of radiant light penetrates the gloom,  
> While inward agony devours our heart piece by piece!  

💡 *Commentary*: Claustrophobic spatial metaphor for existential despair.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੰਧਾਂ ਨੇ ਘੇਰ ਲਿਆ ਸਾਨੂੰ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਰਾਹ ਨਾ ਬਾਹਰ ਜਾਣ ਦਾ ਵੇ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਕੈਦੀ ਬਣ ਕੇ ਰਹਿ ਗਏ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਦੇ ਵੀਰਾਨ ਦਾ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کندھاں نے گھیر لیا سانوں،  
> کوئی راہ نہ باہر جان دا وے!  
> اسیں قیدی بن کے رہ گئے ہاں،  
> اس دنیا دے ویران دا وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kandhaan ne gher leya saanu,  
> Koyi raah na baahar jaan da ve!  
> Aseen kaidi ban ke reh gaye haan,  
> Iss duniya de veeraan da ve!  

**English Translation**:
> Towering brick walls have encircled us on every side,  
> Leaving no avenue of escape into the open air!  
> We have become helpless, forgotten prisoners  
> Within the desolate maze of this uncaring world!  

💡 *Commentary*: Urban enclosure trapping the romantic spirit.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਸ ਗਲੀ ਦੇ ਅੰਤ ਤੇ ਮੌਤ ਖੜ੍ਹੀ ਏ,  
> ਜੋ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਗਲ ਨਾਲ ਲਾਵੇਗੀ!  
> ਇਸ ਹਨੇਰੇ ਦੀ ਕੈਦ 'ਚੋਂ ਕੱਢ ਕੇ,  
> ਚਾਨਣ ਦੇ ਦੇਸ ਲੈ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اس گلی دے انت تے موت کھڑی اے،  
> جو سانوں گل نال لاوے گی!  
> اس ہنیرے دی قید چوں کڈھ کے،  
> چانن دے دیس لے جاوے گی!  

**Romanized**:
> Iss gali de ant te maut kharhi ae,  
> Jo saanu gal naal laavegi!  
> Iss hanere di kaid chon kaddh ke,  
> Chanan de des lai jaavegi!  

**English Translation**:
> At the dead end of this dark alley stands Death,  
> Waiting to fold us into her tender, compassionate embrace!  
> Liberating our spirit from this dungeon of shadows,  
> To guide us home into the boundless realm of light!  

💡 *Commentary*: Paradoxical resolution: Death leads from darkness into light.

|:---|:---|:---|:---|

## 26. Antim Safarnama

### **ਅੰਤਮ ਸਫ਼ਰਨਾਮਾ** • **انتم سفرنامہ**

#### *"The Final Itinerary"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#TravelogueofDeath` `#Departure` `#Pilgrimage`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The traveler packs his meager baggage—a bundle of unread poems, a jar of tears, and a broken heart—for the final departure.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's recurrent sense of impending departure whenever he packed his cloth bag for a journey.
- **Archival Context**: Employs the classical travelogue genre (*safarnama*), inverting it into a journey toward the grave. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅੰਤਮ ਸਫ਼ਰ ਦੀ ਤਿਆਰੀ ਏ ਹੁਣ,  
> ਸਮਾਨ ਅਸੀਂ ਸਭ ਬੰਨ੍ਹ ਲਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਦਰਦਾਂ ਦੀ ਪੰਡ ਸੀਨੇ ਲਾ ਕੇ,  
> ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਹੁਕਮ ਵੀ ਮੰਨ ਲਿਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> انتم سفر دی تیاری اے ہن،  
> سامان اسیں سبھ بنھ لیا اے!  
> درداں دی پنڈ سینے لا کے،  
> موت دا حکم وی من لیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Antam safar di tiyaari ae hun,  
> Samaan aseen sabh bannh leya ae!  
> Dardaan di pand seene laa ke,  
> Maut da hukam vi mann leya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Preparations for the final pilgrimage are complete at last,  
> We have packed our meager belongings for the journey!  
> Pressing the heavy bundle of our sorrows against our breast,  
> We have humbly accepted the sovereign command of Death!  

💡 *Commentary*: Packing the bundle of grief (*dardaan di pand*) for the terminal journey.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੋਈ ਸੰਗੀ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਸਾਥੀ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਇਸ ਲੰਮੇ ਸੁਨਸਾਨ ਰਾਹ ਤੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਬੱਸ ਤੇਰੀ ਯਾਦ ਦਾ ਪਰਛਾਵਾਂ,  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਨਾਲ ਚੱਲੇਗਾ ਹਰ ਸਾਹ ਤੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کوئی سنگی نہ کوئی ساتھی ہووے گا،  
> اس لمبے سنسان راہ تے نی!  
> بس تیری یاد دا پرچھاواں،  
> میرے نال چلے گا ہر ساہ تے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Koyi sangi na koyi saathi hovega,  
> Iss lamme sunsaan raah te ni!  
> Bass teri yaad da parchhaawan,  
> Mere naal challega har saah te ni!  

**English Translation**:
> No traveling companion nor friend will walk beside us  
> Along that endless, desolate highway of the void!  
> Only the ghost of your unforgettable memory  
> Will accompany our footsteps with every failing breath!  

💡 *Commentary*: Absolute solitude broken only by the memory of the beloved.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਦ ਮੈਂ ਉਸ ਦੇਸ ਪਹੁੰਚ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੋਂ ਕੋਈ ਮੁੜ ਕੇ ਨਾ ਆਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਉੱਥੇ ਬੈਠ ਕੇ ਗੀਤ ਲਿਖਾਂਗਾ ਮੈਂ,  
> ਜੋ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ ਭੇਦ ਸੁਣਾਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جد میں اس دیس پہنچ جاواں گا،  
> جتھوں کوئی مڑ کے نہ آؤندا اے!  
> ایتھے بیٹھ کے گیت لکھاں گا میں،  
> جو برہا دا بھید سناؤندا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Jad main oss des pahunch jaawaanga,  
> Jithon koyi murh ke na aaunda ae!  
> Utthe baith ke geet likhaanga main,  
> Jo birha da bhed sunaaunda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> And when I arrive in that mysterious, faraway realm  
> From whose frontier no traveler has ever returned!  
> Sitting in that quiet country I shall write songs  
> Revealing the celestial mystery of separation to the dead!  

💡 *Commentary*: Writing poetry for the dead in the afterlife.

| **ਸਫ਼ਰਨਾਮਾ (Safarnama)** | *Sa-far-na-ma* | Travelogue / Chronicle of a journey | Classical literary genre documenting geographic and spiritual voyages. |

## 27. Shamshaan Da Deeva

### **ਸ਼ਮਸ਼ਾਨ ਦਾ ਦੀਵਾ** • **شمشان دا دیوا**

#### *"Lamp Burning at the Graveyard"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#GraveyardLamp` `#Mausoleum` `#Vigil`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: A solitary clay lamp burns at the graveyard threshold, casting long eerie shadows over silent tombs where all earthly pride rests.
- **Genesis**: Shiv was fascinated by the lonely lamps lit at village tombs (*smadhis*) that burned through the night.
- **Archival Context**: Traditional Punjabi practice of lighting a clay lamp (*chiragh*) at graves or cremation sites on Thursday nights. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਮਸ਼ਾਨ ਦੇ ਕੰਢੇ ਬਲਦਾ ਦੀਵਾ,  
> ਕਿਸ ਦੀ ਯਾਦ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੋਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਸੁੰਞੀ ਰਾਤ ਦੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਉੱਤੇ,  
> ਕਿਹੜਾ ਦਰਦ ਪਰੋਂਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شمشان دے کنڈھے بلدا دیوا،  
> کس دی یاد وچ روندا اے!  
> سنجی رات دے سینے اتے،  
> کہڑا درد پروندا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shamshaan de kandhe balda deeva,  
> Kis di yaad vich ronda ae!  
> Sunji raat de seene utte,  
> Kehda dard paronda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> A lonely lamp burns at the edge of the graveyard,  
> In remembrance of whose vanished face does it weep?  
> Upon the desolate, cold breast of the midnight sky,  
> What forgotten sorrow does its flickering flame thread?  

💡 *Commentary*: The cemetery lamp as a solitary weeping eye in the dark.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹਵਾਵਾਂ ਆ ਕੇ ਬੁਝਾਉਣਾ ਚਾਹੁੰਦੀਆਂ,  
> ਪਰ ਇਹ ਲੌਅ ਅੜੀ ਰਹਿੰਦੀ ਏ!  
> ਜਿਵੇਂ ਕੋਈ ਸੱਚੀ ਮੁਹੱਬਤ,  
> ਮੌਤ ਦੇ ਸਾਹਮਣੇ ਖੜੀ ਰਹਿੰਦੀ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہواواں آ کے بجھاؤنا چاہندیاں،  
> پر ایہ لوء اڑی رہندی اے!  
> جیویں کوئی سچی محبت،  
> موت دے ساہمنے کھڑی رہندی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Hawaawan aa ke bujhaaona chaahundiyaan,  
> Par eh lau arhi rehndi ae!  
> Jiven koyi sacchi mohabbat,  
> Maut de saahmne kharhi rehndi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> The nocturnal gales conspire to extinguish its light,  
> Yet its stubborn flame defies the howling wind!  
> Just like a pure, unyielding devotion  
> That stands fearless before the absolute majesty of Death!  

💡 *Commentary*: Defiance of the flame against cosmic wind.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਦ ਮੇਰੀ ਚਿਖ਼ਾ ਦੀ ਅੱਗ ਠੰਢੀ ਹੋਊ,  
> ਮੇਰੀ ਕਬਰ ਤੇ ਇਹ ਦੀਵਾ ਬਾਲਿਓ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਆਪਣੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਨਾਲ ਸੰਭਾਲਿਓ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جد میری چکھا دی اگ ٹھنڈی ہوؤ،  
> میری قبر تے ایہ دیوا بالیو!  
> میرے برہا دے گیتاں نوں،  
> اپنے سینے نال سنبھالیو!  

**Romanized**:
> Jad meri chikha di agg thandhi ho-oo,  
> Meri kabr te eh deeva baaliyo!  
> Mere birha de geetaan nu,  
> Apne seene naal sambhaaliyo!  

**English Translation**:
> When the fierce embers of my funeral pyre grow cold,  
> Kindle this humble clay lamp above my resting place!  
> And the tragic hymns of my lifelong separation  
> Preserve with tenderness within your own beating breast!  

💡 *Commentary*: The lamp on the grave as the eternal testament of love.

## 28. Vidaai Arzi

### **ਵਿਦਾਈ ਅਰਜ਼ੀ** • **وداعی عرضی**

#### *"Petition for Eternal Departure"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#Petition` `#TitleCanto` `#Farewell`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: A formal, humble petition addressed to life, asking to be excused from further attendance in the school of worldly agony.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's weariness with social obligations, literary politics, and emotional betrayal.
- **Archival Context**: Companion title-poem to 'Mainu Vida Karo', formalizing his poetic petition to be released from worldly existence. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਦੇ ਦਰਬਾਰ 'ਚ ਮੈਂ,  
> ਵਿਦਾਈ ਅਰਜ਼ੀ ਪਾਈ ਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਬਹੁਤ ਹੋ ਗਈ ਮੁਕੱਦਮੇ-ਬਾਜ਼ੀ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਜਾਣ ਦੀ ਵਾਰੀ ਆਈ ਏ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> زندگی دے دربار چ میں،  
> وداعی عرضی پائی اے نی!  
> بہت ہو گئی مقدمے بازی،  
> ہن جان دی واری آئی اے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Zindagi de darbaar ch main,  
> Vidaai arzi paayi ae ni!  
> Bahut ho gayi mukaddame-baazi,  
> Hun jaan di vaari aayi ae ni!  

**English Translation**:
> In the sovereign high tribunal of Life  
> I have submitted my formal petition for departure!  
> We have suffered enough of bitter litigation in this court,  
> And now the hour for my final exit has struck!  

💡 *Commentary*: Courtroom metaphor (*darbaar / mukaddame-baazi*) for life's endless trials.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਗਿਲਾ ਨਾ ਸ਼ਿਕਵਾ ਸਾਡਾ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਦੋਸ਼ ਜਹਾਨ ਤੇ ਵੇ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਆਪਣੀ ਮਰਜ਼ੀ ਨਾਲ ਤੁਰੇ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਇਸ ਮੌਤ ਦੇ ਸੁੰਞੇ ਮਕਾਨ ਤੇ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ کوئی گلہ نہ شکوہ ساڈا،  
> نہ کوئی دوش جہان تے وے!  
> اسیں اپنی مرضی نال ترے ہاں،  
> اس موت دے سنجے مکان تے وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Na koyi gila na shikwa saada,  
> Na koyi dosh jahaan te ve!  
> Aseen apni marzi naal ture haan,  
> Iss maut de sunje makaan te ve!  

**English Translation**:
> We harbor no bitter resentment nor grievance in our heart,  
> Nor do we cast blame upon this cruel, beautiful world!  
> We depart solely of our own deliberate volition,  
> Journeying towards the quiet, peaceful mansion of Death!  

💡 *Commentary*: Absence of rancor: accepting departure without bitterness.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਵਿਦਾ ਕਰੋ ਓ ਹਾਣੀਓ ਮੇਰਿਓ,  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਸਿਰ ਤੇ ਹੱਥ ਧਰ ਜਾਓ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਇਹਨਾਂ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਆਪਣੇ ਦਿਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਭਰ ਜਾਓ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> مینوں وداع کرو او ہانیو مہریو،  
> میرے سر تے ہتھ دھر جاؤ نی!  
> میرے برہا دے ایہناں گیتاں نوں،  
> اپنے دل وچ بھر جاؤ نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Mainu vida karo o haaniyo mereyo,  
> Mere sir te hath dhar jaao ni!  
> Mere birha de ehnaan geetaan nu,  
> Apne dil vich bhar jaao ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Bid me farewell, O cherished companions of my youth,  
> Place your hands gently upon my head in final blessing!  
> And these sorrowful hymns of my cosmic separation  
> Preserve forever within the sacred sanctuary of your hearts!  

💡 *Commentary*: Echoes the title song *Mainu Vida Karo* in its blessing of departure.
