# Dooji Maut

### **ਦੂਜੀ ਮੌਤ** • **دوجی موت**

#### *"The Second Death: Spiritual Extinction"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Main Te Main (1970)](../08_main_te_main_1970.md)
- **Original Title**: ਦੂਜੀ ਮੌਤ (دوجی موت)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1970
- **Thematic Tags**: `#SecondDeath` `#Compromise` `#SpiritualDemise`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: Exploring the concept that physical death is minor compared to the 'first death' of losing one's creative passion and integrity.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed when Shiv felt pressured to write commercially viable film lyrics.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Included in the canonical Punjabi collection 'Main te Main' (1970).

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> Fierce defense of artistic purity over commercial compromise.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Stern, philosophical cadence.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (4 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸਰੀਰ ਦਾ ਮਰਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਮੌਤ ਨਹੀਂ,  
> ਇਹ ਤਾਂ ਕੁਦਰਤ ਦਾ ਨਿਯਮ ਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਦ ਆਤਮਾ ਅੰਦਰੋਂ ਮਰ ਜਾਵੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਅਸਲੀ ਦਰਦ ਤੇ ਗ਼ਮ ਏ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جسم دا مرنا کوئی موت نہیں،  
> ایہہ تاں قدرت دا نئم اے نی!  
> جد روح اندروں مر جاوے،  
> اوہ اصلی درد تے غم اے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shareer da marna koyi maut nahin,  
> Eh taan kudrat da niyam ae ni!  
> Jad aatma andron mar jaave,  
> Oh asli dard te gham ae ni!  

#### English Translation
> The cessation of the physical body is no true death—  
> That is merely the natural law of the physical cosmos!  
> When the creative soul perishes from within,  
> That alone is the genuine, incurable agony of existence!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Distinguishing biological death from spiritual death.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜਦ ਕਵੀ ਆਪਣੇ ਗੀਤ ਵੇਚ ਦੇਵੇ,  
> ਚੰਦ ਸਿੱਕਿਆਂ ਦੀ ਖ਼ਾਤਰ ਨੀ!  
> ਤਾਂ ਸਮਝੋ ਉਹਦੀ ਅਰਥੀ ਨਿਕਲ ਗਈ,  
> ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਦੀ ਖ਼ਾਤਰ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جد شاعر اپنے گیت ویچ دیوے،  
> چند سکیاں دی خاطر نی!  
> تاں سمجھو اوہدی ارتھی نکل گئی،  
> اس دنیا دی خاطر نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Jad kavi apne geet vech deve,  
> Chand sikkeyaan di khaatar ni!  
> Taan samjho ohdi arthi nikal gayi,  
> Is duniya di khaatar ni!  

#### English Translation
> When a poet auctions the integrity of his songs  
> Merely to acquire a handful of copper coins;  
> Know with certainty that his funeral bier has set forth,  
> Murdered for the shallow applause of this world!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Commercial prostitution of art as spiritual suicide.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਭੁੱਖਾ ਮਰਨਾ ਕਬੂਲ ਕਰਾਂਗਾ,  
> ਪਰ ਲਫ਼ਜ਼ਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਦਾਗ਼ ਨਾ ਲਾਵਾਂਗਾ!  
> ਜੋ ਸੱਚ ਮੇਰੇ ਦਿਲ 'ਚ ਬਲ਼ਦਾ ਏ,  
> ਉਸਨੂੰ ਅੰਤ ਤੱਕ ਗਾਵਾਂਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں بھکھا مرنا قبول کرانگا،  
> پر لفظاں نوں داغ نہ لاوانگا!  
> جو سچ میرے دل چ بلدا اے،  
> اس نوں انت تک گاوانگا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main bhukkha marna kabool karaanga,  
> Par lafzaan nu daagh na laavaanga!  
> Jo sach mere dil ch balda ae,  
> Usnu ant tak gaavaanga!  

#### English Translation
> I shall gladly accept death by starvation,  
> Rather than permit a single stain upon the honour of my words!  
> That fiery truth which blazes inside my chest,  
> I shall sing with uncompromised throat until my final breath!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The vow of artistic poverty and unyielding integrity.*

* * *

### Stanza 4

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਆਖੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਦੂਜੀ ਮੌਤ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੋਈ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਅੱਜ ਵੀ ਜਿਊਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ ਸ਼ਬਦਾਂ 'ਚ!  
> ਜਦ ਤੱਕ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਰਹੇਗਾ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਵੱਸਾਂਗਾ ਹਰ ਧੜਕਣ 'ਚ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو آکھے میری دوجی موت نہیں ہوئی،  
> میں اج وی جیوندا ہاں شبداں چ!  
> جد تک پنجاب وچ عشق رہیگا،  
> میں وساں گا ہر دھڑکن چ!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv aakhe meri dooji maut nahin hoyi,  
> Main ajj vi jiyunda haan shabdaan ch!  
> Jad tak Punjab vich ishq rahega,  
> Main vasaanga har dharhkan ch!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv proclaims: my second death was never achieved—  
> I live on triumphant this day inside the eternal Word!  
> As long as romantic passion endures across Punjab,  
> I shall inhabit every beating human heart!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Triumph over spiritual extinction.*
