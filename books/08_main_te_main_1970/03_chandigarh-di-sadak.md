# Chandigarh Di Sadak

### **ਚੰਡੀਗੜ੍ਹ ਦੀ ਸੜਕ** • **چندی گڑھ دی سڑک**

#### *"The Asphalt Road of Chandigarh"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Main Te Main (1970)](../08_main_te_main_1970.md)
- **Original Title**: ਚੰਡੀਗੜ੍ਹ ਦੀ ਸੜਕ (چندی گڑھ دی سڑک)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1970
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Chandigarh` `#City` `#Alienation` `#Asphalt`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `modernism` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: Walking alone at night along the sterile, geometric avenues of Le Corbusier's planned city, feeling the coldness of asphalt beneath weary feet.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv lived in Sector 21 and worked at the State Bank of India in Sector 17, walking the broad avenues at night.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Included in the canonical Punjabi collection 'Main te Main' (1970).

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> One of the earliest poetic critiques of modernist urban planning in post-independence India.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Cold, solitary, percussive footsteps cadence.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (4 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਹ ਚੰਡੀਗੜ੍ਹ ਦੀ ਸੜਕ ਕਾਲ਼ੀ ਏ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਦਿਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਇਸ ਪੱਥਰ ਦਾ!  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਰੁੱਖ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਛਾਂ ਇੱਥੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਬੂਹਾ ਕਿਸੇ ਦੇ ਘਰ ਦਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ایہہ چندی گڑھ دی سڑک کالی اے،  
> کوئی دل نہیں اس پتھر دا!  
> نہ کوئی رخ نہ کوئی چھاں ایتھے،  
> نہ کوئی بوہا کسے دے گھر دا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Eh Chandigarh di sadak kaali ae,  
> Koyi dil nahin is patthar da!  
> Na koyi rukh na koyi chaan itthe,  
> Na koyi booha kise de ghar da!  

#### English Translation
> This black asphalt road of Chandigarh is unfeeling;  
> No beating heart exists within this engineered stone!  
> No organic tree nor patch of living shade dwells here,  
> Nor any open doorway inviting the traveler into a home!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The modernist planned city as an anti-human, unfeeling grid.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸੈਕਟਰਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਵੰਡੀ ਦੁਨੀਆ,  
> ਹਰ ਕੋਈ ਆਪਣੇ ਖੋਲ੍ਹ 'ਚ ਬੰਦ ਏ!  
> ਇੱਥੇ ਪਿਆਰ ਦਾ ਕੋਈ ਨਾਮ ਨਹੀਂ,  
> ਇੱਥੇ ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਰੁਪਈਆ ਪਸੰਦ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> سیکٹروں دے وچ ونڈی دنیا،  
> ہر کوئی اپنے کھولھ چ بند اے!  
> ایتھے پیار دا کوئی نام نہیں،  
> ایتھے صرف روپیہ پسند اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Sector-aan de vich vandi duniya,  
> Har koyi apne kholh ch band ae!  
> Itthe pyaar da koyi naam nahin,  
> Itthe sirf rupaiya pasand ae!  

#### English Translation
> The entire universe is partitioned into numbered sectors;  
> Every resident is locked inside their private concrete shell!  
> Here the sacred name of love is never uttered;  
> Here gold and paper currency alone command adoration!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Corbusian sectors as prisons of bourgeois isolation.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਜਦ ਤੁਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ ਇੱਥੇ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਮੇਰੇ ਕਦਮਾਂ ਦੀ ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਏ!  
> ਸਟਰੀਟ ਲਾਈਟਾਂ ਦੇ ਪੀਲੇ ਚਾਨਣ 'ਚ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਹੀ ਮੇਰਾ ਸਾਜ਼ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں رات نوں جد تردا ہاں ایتھے،  
> تاں صرف میرے قدماں دی آواز اے!  
> سٹریٹ لائٹاں دے پیلے چانن چ،  
> میرا برہا ہی میرا ساز اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main raat nu jad turda haan itthe,  
> Taan sirf mere kadmaan di aavaaz ae!  
> Street light-aan de peele chaanan ch,  
> Mera birha hi mera saaz ae!  

#### English Translation
> When I walk these empty boulevards in the dead of night,  
> Only the hollow echo of my own footsteps answers me!  
> Beneath the sickly yellow glare of electric streetlights,  
> My lifelong separation alone serves as my musical instrument!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The yellow sodium glare replacing the silver moonlight of village verse.*

* * *

### Stanza 4

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਇਸ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ 'ਚ ਗੁਆਚ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਜਿਵੇਂ ਧੁੰਦ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੋਈ ਰਾਹੀ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਗੀਤ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦੇ ਖੇਤਾਂ ਦੇ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹਨਾਂ ਦੀ ਇੱਥੇ ਕੋਈ ਥਾਂ ਹੀ ਨਹੀਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو تاں اس شہر چ گواچ گیا،  
> جویں دھند وچ کوئی راہی نی!  
> جو گیت پنجاب دے کھیتاں دے سی،  
> اوہناں دی ایتھے کوئی تھاں ہی نہیں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv taan is shehar ch guaach gaya,  
> Jiven dhund vich koyi raahi ni!  
> Jo geet Punjab de khetaan de si,  
> Ohnaan di itthe koyi thaan hi nahin!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv has been swallowed and lost inside this planned city,  
> Like a solitary wanderer vanishing inside winter fog!  
> Those raw, passionate hymns born of Punjab's soil  
> Find no home or sanctuary inside these concrete walls!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The agrarian poet suffocated by the modern administrative capital.*
