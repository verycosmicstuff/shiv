# Bheed Vich Kalla

### **ਭੀੜ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੱਲਾ** • **بھیڑ وچ کلا**

#### *"Solitary in the Metropolitan Crowd"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Main Te Main (1970)](../08_main_te_main_1970.md)
- **Original Title**: ਭੀੜ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੱਲਾ (بھیڑ وچ کلا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1970
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Crowd` `#Metropolis` `#Loneliness` `#Alienation` `#Modernity`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `modernism` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: Walking through thousands of rushing commuters in the modern city, the poet realizes that crowd density increases rather than relieves spiritual isolation.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Chandigarh after walking from Sector 17 to Sector 22 in heavy evening rush hour.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Reflects the Baudelairean flâneur theme imported into Punjabi literature, capturing the existential isolation of Sector 17, Chandigarh.

**Source**: *Punjabi Kavi Ate Kavitriyan (Marxist Critical Essays)* by Sant Singh Sekhon (1972, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, pp. 89–112). [Literary Criticism]

> "Sant Singh Sekhon termed Shiv the "Keats of Punjabi poetry" for his meter and folk metaphor, but charged him with escapism, arguing that romanticizing youth-suicide was reactionary."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਲੱਖਾਂ ਲੋਕਾਂ ਦੀ ਭੀੜ 'ਚ ਖੜ੍ਹਾ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਪਰ ਇੱਕ ਵੀ ਚਿਹਰਾ ਆਪਣਾ ਨਹੀਂ!  
> ਸਭ ਦੌੜਦੇ ਨੇ ਅੰਨ੍ਹਿਆਂ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਦੀ ਅੱਖ 'ਚ ਸੁਪਨਾ ਨਹੀਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں لکھاں لوکاں دی بھیڑ چ کھڑا ہاں،  
> پر اک وی چہرہ اپنا نہیں!  
> سبھ دوڑدے نے انھیاں وانگوں،  
> کسے دی اکھ چ سپنا نہیں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main lakkhan lokaan di bheed ch kharha haan,  
> Par ikk vi chehra aapna nahin!  
> Sabh daurhde ne anneyan vaangu,  
> Kise di akh ch supna nahin!  

#### English Translation
> I stand amidst a jostling crowd of hundreds of thousands,  
> Yet not a single countenance is familiar or mine!  
> All race forward like blind automata;  
> In not a single eye glimmers a genuine, living dream!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The atomized crowd of the capitalist metropolis.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਹ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਨਿਗਲ ਗਿਆ ਏ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ ਕਿਤੇ ਗੁਆਚ ਗਿਆ!  
> ਜੋ ਕਦੇ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦਾ ਸ਼ਾਇਰ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਮਸ਼ੀਨਾਂ ਅੰਦਰ ਨਾਚ ਗਿਆ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ایہہ شہر مینوں نگل گیا اے،  
> میرا نام کتے گواچ گیا!  
> جو کدے پنجاب دا شاعر سی،  
> اوہ مشیناں اندر ناچ گیا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Eh shehar mainu nigal gaya ae,  
> Mera naam kite guaach gaya!  
> Jo kade Punjab da shaayar si,  
> Oh masheenaan andar naach gaya!  

#### English Translation
> This mechanical city has swallowed my very flesh;  
> My identity and name have vanished into the grid!  
> He who was once the celebrated bard of Punjab  
> Has been forced to dance within the gears of machinery!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Industrial alienation destroying lyric identity.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਇਸ ਭੀੜ 'ਚੋਂ ਚੀਕ ਰਿਹਾ ਏ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਰੋਕੋ ਇਸ ਹਨੇਰ ਨੂੰ!  
> ਜੇ ਇਨਸਾਨ ਹੀ ਮਸ਼ੀਨ ਬਣ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਕੌਣ ਬਚਾਊ ਇਸ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਨੂੰ?  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو اس بھیڑ چوں چیک رہیا اے،  
> کوئی روکو اس ہنیر نوں!  
> جے انسان ہی مشین بن گیا،  
> تاں کون بچاؤ اس شہر نوں؟  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv iss bheed chon cheek reha ae,  
> Koyi roko iss haner nu!  
> Je insaan hi masheen ban gaya,  
> Taan kaun bachaau iss shehar nu?  

#### English Translation
> Shiv screams out from the depths of this rushing multitude:  
> 'Someone halt this catastrophe of darkness!  
> If the human being has transformed into a soulless machine,  
> Who remains to salvage this city from destruction?'  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The prophetic warning against total mechanization.*
