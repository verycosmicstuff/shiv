# Akhbaar Da Tukda

### **ਅਖ਼ਬਾਰ ਦਾ ਟੁਕੜਾ** • **اخبار دا ٹکڑا**

#### *"The Discarded Scrap of Newsprint"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Main Te Main (1970)](../08_main_te_main_1970.md)
- **Original Title**: ਅਖ਼ਬਾਰ ਦਾ ਟੁਕੜਾ (اخبار دا ٹکڑا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1970
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Newsprint` `#Gutter` `#Disposable` `#Modernism` `#Trash`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `modernism` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: A torn scrap of newspaper containing a poet's masterpiece blows down a filthy street gutter, discarded after wrapping fried snacks.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Chandigarh after seeing his poems printed on cheap newsprint wrapping street food.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Modernist objective-correlative: the poet compares his own commodified fame to a greasy scrap of newsprint used to wrap roadside pakoras.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਅਖ਼ਬਾਰ ਦਾ ਟੁਕੜਾ ਬਣ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਜੋ ਨਾਲ਼ੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੁਲ਼ਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਜਿਸ 'ਤੇ ਕਦੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤ ਛਪੇ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਪੈਰਾਂ ਹੇਠਾਂ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹਦਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں اخبار دا ٹکڑا بن گیا،  
> جو نالی وچ رلدا اے!  
> جس تے کدے میرے گیت چھپے سی،  
> اوہ پیراں ہیٹھاں کھلدا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main akhbaar da tukda ban gaya,  
> Jo naali vich rulda ae!  
> Jis te kade mere geet chhape si,  
> Oh pairaan hethan khulhda ae!  

#### English Translation
> I have become like a greasy scrap of discarded newsprint  
> Rolling indifferently down the open street gutter!  
> That very paper upon which my verses were printed  
> Now unrolls and tears beneath the boots of passersby!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The shocking descent of high art into urban filth.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸਵੇਰੇ ਜੋ ਖ਼ਬਰ ਬਣੀ ਸੀ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਦੀ,  
> ਸ਼ਾਮ ਨੂੰ ਕੂੜੇ 'ਚ ਸੁੱਟ ਦਿੱਤੀ!  
> ਇਸ ਮਤਲਬੀ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਨੇ ਸਾਡੀ,  
> ਸਾਰੀ ਕੀਮਤ ਹੀ ਲੁੱਟ ਲਿੱਤੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> سویرے جو خبر بنی سی دنیا دی،  
> شام نوں کوڑے چ سٹ دتی!  
> اس مطلبی دنیا نے ساڈی،  
> ساری قیمت ہی لٹ لتی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Savere jo khabar bani si duniya di,  
> Shaam nu koorhe ch sutt ditti!  
> Iss matlabi duniya ne saadi,  
> Saari keemat hi lutt litti!  

#### English Translation
> That which in the morning was hailed as the headline of the world  
> By dusk was hurled unceremoniously into the garbage heap!  
> This opportunistic, commercial world has plundered  
> Every ounce of dignity and value from our life!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The 24-hour news cycle trivializing human genius.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਪਰ ਇਸ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ 'ਤੇ,  
> ਜੋ ਸਿਆਹੀ ਲਹੂ ਦੀ ਛਪੀ ਹੋਈ ਏ!  
> ਉਹ ਪਾਣੀ ਨਾਲ਼ ਨਾ ਧੁਪੇਗੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਇਤਿਹਾਸ ਦੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਗਡੀ ਹੋਈ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو کہندا پر اس کاغذ تے،  
> جو سیاہی لہو دی چھپی ہوئی اے!  
> اوہ پانی نال نہ دھپوگی،  
> اوہ تاریخ دے سینے گڈی ہوئی اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv kehnda par iss kaghaz te,  
> Jo syaahi lahu di chhappi hoyi ae!  
> Oh paani naal na dhupegi,  
> Oh itihaas de seene gaddi hoyi ae!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv declares: 'Yet upon this rain-soaked scrap of paper,  
> That black ink which was pressed from living blood  
> Can never be washed away by sewage water—  
> It stands planted like an iron spear into the chest of history!'  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The indestructible ink of authentic suffering.*
