# Lajwanti (1961)

## ਲਾਜਵੰਤੀ • لاجونتی
### *"The Sensitive Plant (Touch-Me-Not)"*

---

## 📚 Anthology Overview

- **Author**: Shiv Kumar Batalvi (ਸਿ਼ਵ ਕੁਮਾਰ ਬਟਾਲਵੀ / شیو کمار بٹالوی)
- **Publication Year**: 1961
- **Original Publisher**: Navyug Publishers, Delhi
- **Total Master Poems in this Edition**: 28

### Literary & Historical Background
> Explores fragile female vulnerability, modesty, and emotional intimacy through the botanical metaphor of the mimosa pudica plant, which droops at the slightest human touch.

**Critical Reception**: Contains classic verses capturing village bridehood, departing palanquins, and unvoiced feminine longing.

## 1. Lajwantiye (Ni Lajwantiye)

### **ਲਾਜਵੰਤੀਏ (ਨੀ ਲਾਜਵੰਤੀਏ)** • **لاجونتیئے (نی لاجونتیئے)**

#### *"O Sensitive Mimosa Plant"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#Fragility` `#Touch` `#Modesty`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The touch-me-not plant (mimosa) becomes a metaphor for female vulnerability and bruised modesty in an ungentle world.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala, dedicated to the fragile innocence of village maidens facing patriarchal scrutiny.
- **Archival Context**: Title track of Shiv's second collection, published in 1961, which cemented his mastery of botanical symbolism. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Praised for introducing botanical imagery into romantic Punjabi verse.
- **Musical Recitation**: Tender, whisper-like tarannum cadence.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨੀ ਲਾਜਵੰਤੀਏ ਨੀ ਛੋਹ ਨਾ ਸਹਾਰੀਏ,  
> ਤੈਨੂੰ ਪੌਣ ਵੀ ਛੂਹੇ ਤਾਂ ਕੁਮਲਾਵੇਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਕਿਹੜੀ ਰੁੱਤੇ ਤੂੰ ਜੰਮੀ ਏਂ ਧਰਤ 'ਤੇ,  
> ਜੋ ਹਰ ਝੋਕੇ ਤੋਂ ਡਰਦੀ ਜਾਵੇਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نی لاجونتیئے نی چھوہ نہ سہاریئے،  
> تینوں پون وی چھوئے تاں کملاویں نی!  
> کہڑی رتے توں جمی ایں دھرتی تے،  
> جو ہر جھوکے توں ڈردی جاویں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Ni Lajwantiye ni chhoh na sahaariye,  
> Tainu paun vi chhoohe taan kumlaaven ni!  
> Kehri rutte toon jammi ain dhart te,  
> Jo har jhoke ton dardi jaaven ni!  

**English Translation**:
> O tender Mimosa, unable to bear even the lightest touch,  
> You wither if even the gentle breeze brushes your leaves!  
> In what cursed season were you born upon this earth,  
> That you tremble in fear before every passing gust?  

💡 *Commentary*: The mimosa plant (touch-me-not) represents hyper-sensitive innocence in an abrasive world.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਤੇਰੇ ਪੱਤਿਆਂ 'ਚ ਲਿਪੀ ਸ਼ਰਮ ਏਨੀ,  
> ਕਿ ਸੂਰਜ ਵੀ ਵੇਖ ਸ਼ਰਮਾਵੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਦ ਰਾਤ ਪਵੇ ਤੇਰੇ ਵਿਹੜੇ 'ਚ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਚੰਨ ਵੀ ਪੈਰ ਦਬਾਵੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> تیرے پتیاں چ لپی شرم اینی،  
> کہ سورج وی ویکھ شرماوے نی!  
> جد رات پوے تیرے ویہڑے چ،  
> تاں چن وی پیر دباوے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Tere pattiyan ch lippi sharam aini,  
> Ki sooraj vi vekh sharmaave ni!  
> Jad raat pave tere vehre ch,  
> Taan chann vi pair dabaave ni!  

**English Translation**:
> So deep is the bashfulness folded within your petals,  
> That even the burning sun turns away in shame!  
> When night descends upon your secluded courtyard,  
> Even the silver moon steps lightly upon tiptoe!  

💡 *Commentary*: Personification of celestial bodies respecting her inviolate modesty.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪਰ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਹੈ ਪੱਥਰਾਂ ਦੀ ਨਗਰੀ,  
> ਇੱਥੇ ਕੰਡਿਆਂ ਦਾ ਹੀ ਰਾਜ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਫੁੱਲ ਵਾਂਗੂ ਖਿੜਨਾ ਮਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਏ,  
> ਉੱਥੇ ਕੌਣ ਸੁਣੇ ਤੇਰੀ ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਨੀ?  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پر دنیا ہے پتھراں دی نگری،  
> ایتھے کنڈیاں دا ہی راج نی!  
> جتھے پھل وانگو کھڑنا منع اے،  
> اوتھے کون سنے تیری آواز نی؟  

**Romanized**:
> Par duniya hai pattharan di nagri,  
> Itthe kandiyan da hi raaj ni!  
> Jithe phull vaangu khirhna manaan ae,  
> Outhe kaun sune teri aavaaz ni?  

**English Translation**:
> Yet this mortal world is a city carved of stone,  
> Where thorns and briars alone hold dominion!  
> Where blooming like a tender flower is forbidden,  
> Who will pause to hear your fragile voice?  

💡 *Commentary*: The poet laments the harsh, unfeeling reality of human society towards vulnerable souls.

##### Stanza 4

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਆਖੇ ਨੀ ਮੇਰੀਏ ਕਲੀਏ,  
> ਤੂੰ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਘੁੱਟ ਪੀ ਲੈ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੇ ਜੀਣਾ ਹੈ ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆ 'ਚ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਅੱਥਰੂ ਪੀ ਕੇ ਜੀ ਲੈ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو آکھے نی میریئے کلیئے،  
> توں برہا دے گھٹ پی لے نی!  
> جے جینا ہے اس دنیا چ،  
> تاں اتھرو پی کے جی لے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv aakhe ni meriye kaliye,  
> Toon birha de ghutt pee lai ni!  
> Je jeena hai is duniya ch,  
> Taan atthru pee ke jee lai ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv says to you, O my fragile blossom:  
> Drink down the bitter sips of separation!  
> If you must survive in this unsparing world,  
> You must learn to swallow your own tears and endure!  

💡 *Commentary*: The conclusion offers the only survival strategy for the sensitive poet: inner containment of grief.

## 2. Kise Da Vi Phull Na Toro

### **ਕਿਸੇ ਦਾ ਵੀ ਫੁੱਲ ਨਾ ਤੋੜੋ** • **کسے دا وی پھل نہ توڑو**

#### *"Pluck Not the Blossom of Any Soul"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Flowers` `#Gentleness` `#Pain` `#Compassion`
- **Theme / Biome**: `modernism` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: An ecological and emotional appeal pleading for reverence toward delicate life and unspoken love.
- **Genesis**: Composed in the lush green orchards of Batala during spring.
- **Archival Context**: Reflects Shiv's profound tenderness toward fragile living things, extending Ahimsa into lyric poetry. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Exemplifies Shiv's compassionate pacifism.
- **Musical Recitation**: Gentle, pastoral rhythm.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਿਸੇ ਦਾ ਵੀ ਫੁੱਲ ਨਾ ਤੋੜੋ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਫੁੱਲਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਵੀ ਜਾਨ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਡਾਲੀ ਤੋਂ ਟੁੱਟ ਜਾਵੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਮਹਿਕ ਵੀ ਬੇ-ਪਛਾਣ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کسے دا وی پھل نہ توڑو اڑیو،  
> پھلاں وچ وی جان ہوندی اے!  
> جو ڈالی توں ٹٹ جاوے،  
> اوہ مہک وی بے-پچھان ہوندی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kise da vi phull na toro arhiyo,  
> Phullaan vich vi jaan hundi ae!  
> Jo daali ton tutt jaave,  
> Oh mehak vi be-pachhaan hundi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Pluck not the blossom of any soul, dear friends,  
> For even blossoms possess living, throbbing souls!  
> Once severed from its maternal bough,  
> Even fragrance loses its identity and dies in the dust!  

💡 *Commentary*: Severing the blossom symbolizes the trauma of forced estrangement.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੱਸਦੇ ਚਿਹਰੇ ਰੁਆ ਨਾ ਦੇਵੋ,  
> ਹਰ ਹਾਸੇ ਪਿੱਛੇ ਗ਼ਮ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਦੀ ਅੱਖ ਦਾ ਹੰਝੂ ਵੇਖੋ,  
> ਕਿੰਨਾ ਦਿਲ ਬੇ-ਰਹਿਮ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہسدے چہرے رلا نہ دیوو،  
> ہر ہاسے پچھے غم ہوندا اے!  
> کسے دی اکھ دا ہنجھو ویکھو،  
> کنا دل بے-رحم ہوندا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Hassde chehre ruaa na devo,  
> Har haase pichhe gham hunda ae!  
> Kise di akh da hanjhu vekho,  
> Kinna dil be-reham hunda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Bring not tears to laughing countenances,  
> For behind every radiant smile lies hidden grief!  
> Witness the silent tear in another's eye,  
> And ponder how callous the human heart can be!  

💡 *Commentary*: Warning against casual cruelty in social relationships.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੇ ਗੀਤ ਵੀ ਫੁੱਲਾਂ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਮਹਿਕਾਂ ਵੰਡਦੇ ਜਾਣਗੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਕਦਰ ਕਰਨਗੇ ਦਿਲ ਵਾਲ਼ੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਗਲ਼ ਨਾਲ ਲਾ ਮਾਣਗੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دے گیت وی پھلاں وانگوں،  
> مہکاں ونڈدے جانگے!  
> جو قدر کرنگے دل والے،  
> اوہ گل نال لا مانگے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv de geet vi phullaan vaangu,  
> Mehkaan vand-de jaange!  
> Jo kadar karange dil waale,  
> Oh gal naal laa maange!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv's verses too are like wild blossoms,  
> Scattering fragrance as they pass!  
> Those endowed with a compassionate heart  
> Shall press them to their bosoms and find solace!  

💡 *Commentary*: Poetry as a perennial flower that cannot be destroyed by critics.

## 3. Yarriye Ni Yarriye

### **ਯਾਰੀਏ ਨੀ ਯਾਰੀਏ** • **یاریئے نی یاریئے**

#### *"O Treacherous Friendship of Love"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Betrayal` `#Friendship` `#Disillusionment`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: Addressing love and friendship as an intoxicating trap that leaves the soul stripped of all defenses.
- **Genesis**: Written following the sudden coldness of youthful literary companions.
- **Archival Context**: Documents Shiv's disillusionment with opportunistic friends as his early fame began to crest in 1961. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Displays Shiv's early bitter existential disillusionment.
- **Musical Recitation**: Sharp, ironic folk cadence.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਯਾਰੀਏ ਨੀ ਯਾਰੀਏ ਤੂੰ ਕੀ ਕੀਤਾ,  
> ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਕਹਿ ਕੇ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਪੀਤਾ!  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਟੋਟੇ ਕਰਕੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਦਰਜ਼ੀ ਨਾ ਜਿਸਨੂੰ ਸੀਤਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> یاریئے نی یاریئے توں کی کیتا،  
> امرت کہہ کے زہر پیتا!  
> ساڈے دل دے ٹوٹے کر کے،  
> کوئی درزی نہ جس نوں سیتا!  

**Romanized**:
> Yarriye ni yarriye toon ki keeta,  
> Amrit keh ke zehar peeta!  
> Saade dil de tote karke,  
> Koyi darzi na jisnu seeta!  

**English Translation**:
> O friendship, O faithless love, what have you done?  
> Calling it nectar, we drank pure poison!  
> Tearing our living heart into shattered remnants  
> That no earthly tailor could ever stitch whole again!  

💡 *Commentary*: The irremediable nature of emotional betrayal.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਿਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਲਈ ਅਸੀਂ ਦੀਵੇ ਬਾਲ਼ੇ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਹਨੇਰਾ ਕਰ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਅਸੀਂ ਆਪਣਾ ਸਮਝਿਆ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਕੰਡਾ ਧਰ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جنہاں لئی اسیں دیوے بالے،  
> اوہی ہنیرا کر گئے نی!  
> جنہاں نوں اسیں اپنا سمجھیا،  
> اوہی کنڈا دھر گئے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Jinhaan layi assin deeve baale,  
> Uhi hanera kar gaye ni!  
> Jinhaan nu assin apna samjheya,  
> Uhi kanda dhar gaye ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Those for whom we illuminated earthen lamps  
> Were the very ones who extinguished every flame into darkness!  
> Those whom we embraced as our own flesh  
> Were the ones who planted thorns beneath our feet!  

💡 *Commentary*: The ironic reversal of loyalty into betrayal.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਦ ਮੈਂ ਰੋਇਆ ਕੱਲਿਆਂ ਬਹਿ ਕੇ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾ ਪੂੰਝੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਨੀ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਲਾਸ਼ 'ਤੇ ਆਣ ਕੇ ਹੱਸਣ,  
> ਜੋ ਕਦੇ ਕਹਿੰਦੇ ਸੀ ਤੂੰ ਸੰਝੂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جد میں رویا کلیاں بہہ کے،  
> کسے نہ پونجھے ہنجھو نی!  
> ساڈی لاش تے آن کے ہسن،  
> جو کدے کہندے سی توں سنجھو نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Jad main royea kalleyan beh ke,  
> Kise na poonjhe hanjhu ni!  
> Saadi laash te aan ke hassan,  
> Jo kade kehnde si toon sanjhu ni!  

**English Translation**:
> When I wept in solitary corners,  
> Not a single soul stepped forward to wipe away my tears!  
> They gather to laugh over my fallen corpse—  
> Those very comrades who once swore lifelong brotherhood!  

💡 *Commentary*: The ultimate isolation of the artist.

## 4. Chamba Khidheya

### **ਚੰਬਾ ਖਿੜਿਆ** • **چنبا کھڑیا**

#### *"The White Jasmine has Blossomed"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Jasmine` `#Beauty` `#Memory` `#Aroma`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The scent of white jasmine revives the painful memory of a touch that cannot return.
- **Genesis**: Jasmine bushes lined the courtyards of Batala where Shiv spent his early twenties.
- **Archival Context**: Celebrates the fragrant memory of Shiv's lost love, contrasting blooming nature with decaying inner hope. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Celebrated for olfactory imagery.
- **Musical Recitation**: Melodic, intoxicating tune.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਚੰਬਾ ਖਿੜਿਆ ਵਿਹੜੇ ਮੇਰੇ,  
> ਤੇਰੀ ਯਾਦ ਲੈ ਕੇ ਆਇਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ਬੋ ਨੂੰ ਭੁੱਲ ਗਿਆ ਸਾਂ,  
> ਉਸ ਨੇ ਫ਼ੇਰ ਜਗਾਇਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> چنبا کھڑیا ویہڑے میرے،  
> تیری یاد لے کے آیا نی!  
> جس خوشبو نوں بھل گیا ساں،  
> اس نے فیر جگایا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Chamba khirheya vehre mere,  
> Teri yaad lai ke aaya ni!  
> Jis khushboo nu bhull gaya saan,  
> Us ne fer jagaaya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The white jasmine has blossomed in my courtyard,  
> Bringing the sudden, intoxicating remembrance of you!  
> That fragrance which I strove so hard to forget  
> Has once again awakened my sleeping wounds!  

💡 *Commentary*: Involuntary memory stimulated by nature's scent.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਫੁੱਲਾਂ ਦੀ ਹਰ ਪੱਤੀ ਉੱਤੇ,  
> ਤੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਜਾਪੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਹਵਾ ਵਗੇ ਤਾਂ ਐਵੇਂ ਲੱਗਦਾ,  
> ਜਿਉਂ ਤੂੰ ਹੌਲੀ ਜਿਹੇ ਆਖੇਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پھلاں دی ہر پتی اتے،  
> تیرا نام لکھیا جاپے نی!  
> ہوا وگے تاں ایویں لگدا،  
> جیوں توں ہولی جہے آکھیں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Phullaan di har patti utte,  
> Tera naam likhiya jaape ni!  
> Hawa vage taan aiven lagda,  
> Jiyon toon hauli jihe aakhen ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Upon every delicate petal of these blossoms,  
> Your name appears visibly inscribed!  
> When the gentle evening wind stirs the leaves,  
> It feels as if you whisper softly into my ear!  

💡 *Commentary*: Nature becomes a canvas completely saturated by the beloved's presence.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪਰ ਇਹ ਚੰਬਾ ਛੇਤੀ ਮੁਰਝਾਊ,  
> ਧੁੱਪ ਪੈਣ 'ਤੇ ਝੜ ਜਾਊਗਾ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਦਿਲ ਪਰ ਉਮਰਾਂ ਤਾਈਂ,  
> ਤੇਰੇ ਗ਼ਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੜ ਜਾਊਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پر ایہہ چنبا چھیتی مرجھاؤ،  
> دھپ پین تے جھڑ جاؤگا!  
> شیو دا دل پر عمراں تائیں،  
> تیرے غم وچ سڑ جاؤگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Par eh chamba cheti murjhaau,  
> Dhupp pain te jharh jaauga!  
> Shiv da dil par umraan taayin,  
> Tere gham vich sarh jaauga!  

**English Translation**:
> Yet this white jasmine will quickly wither,  
> Shedding its petals when the fierce sun rises!  
> Yet Shiv's heart, across all lifetimes,  
> Shall continue to burn inside the kiln of your sorrow!  

💡 *Commentary*: The flower's brief lifespan contrasts with the permanence of the poet's grief.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Chamba (ਚੰਬਾ)** | *Cham-baa* | White jasmine flower | Traditional flower associated with female innocence and fragrant bridal beauty. |

## 5. Kach Da Glass

### **ਕੱਚ ਦਾ ਗਲਾਸ** • **کچ دا گلاس**

#### *"The Tumbler of Brittle Glass"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Glass` `#Fragility` `#Heart` `#Modernity`
- **Theme / Biome**: `modernism` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: Comparing the human soul to a fragile drinking glass that shatters into unrepairable shards.
- **Genesis**: Penned in a local Batala tea stall after observing a glass shatter on the stone floor.
- **Archival Context**: Marked Shiv's introduction of everyday industrial/domestic objects into modern Punjabi lyrics. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Demonstrates modern domestic objects replacing classical Sufi symbols.
- **Musical Recitation**: Brittle, staccato cadence.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਡਾ ਦਿਲ ਸੀ ਕੱਚ ਦਾ ਗਲਾਸ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਜੋ ਠੋਕਰ ਖਾ ਕੇ ਟੁੱਟ ਗਿਆ!  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਨੇ ਚੁੱਕ ਕੇ ਜੋੜਿਆ ਨਾ,  
> ਸਭ ਕੁਝ ਪਲਾਂ 'ਚ ਲੁੱਟ ਗਿਆ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ساڈا دل سی کچ دا گلاس اڑیو،  
> جو ٹھوکر کھا کے ٹٹ گیا!  
> کسے نے چک کے جوڑیا نہ،  
> سبھ کچھ پلاں چ لٹ گیا!  

**Romanized**:
> Saada dil si kach da glass arhiyo,  
> Jo thokar khaa ke tutt gaya!  
> Kise ne chukk ke jorheya na,  
> Sabh kujh palaan ch lutt gaya!  

**English Translation**:
> Our heart was like a tumbler of fragile glass, friends,  
> Which was kicked by careless feet and shattered!  
> Not a soul bent down to gather and mend the pieces,  
> And in a matter of seconds, our whole world was plundered!  

💡 *Commentary*: The modern glass object signifies the fragility of urban emotional bonds.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਿਰਚਾਂ ਖਿਲਰੀਆਂ ਰਾਹਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਲੰਘਣ ਵਾਲ਼ੇ ਲਤਾੜ ਗਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਆਏ ਸੀ ਮੱਲ੍ਹਮ ਲਾਉਣ ਬਹਾਨੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਹੋਰ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਸਾੜ ਗਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کرچاں کھلرویاں راہاں دے وچ،  
> لنگھن والے لتاڑ گئے!  
> جو آئے سی ملہم لاون بہانے،  
> اوہ ہور زخماں نوں ساڑ گئے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kirchaan khilariyaan raahaan de vich,  
> Langhan waale lataarh gaye!  
> Jo aaye si mallham laaun bahaane,  
> Oh hor zakhmaan nu saarh gaye!  

**English Translation**:
> The sharp shards were scattered across the open road,  
> Trampled underfoot by indifferent passersby!  
> Those who arrived under the pretext of bringing healing salve  
> Only poured burning acid into the fresh lacerations!  

💡 *Commentary*: False sympathy proved more destructive than direct violence.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਕੱਚ ਦੇ ਟੁਕੜੇ ਚਮਕਣ ਪਏ,  
> ਲੋਕ ਕਹਿੰਦੇ ਇਹ ਹੀਰੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਆਖੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਟੁੱਟੇ ਸੁਪਨੇ,  
> ਸਦਾ ਰਹਿੰਦੇ ਦਿਲਗੀਰੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن کچ دے ٹکڑے چمکن پئے،  
> لوک کہندے ایہہ ہیرے نے!  
> شیو آکھے میرے ٹٹے سپنے،  
> سدا رہندے دلگیرے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun kach de tukre chamkan paye,  
> Lok kehnde eh heere ne!  
> Shiv aakhe mere tutte supne,  
> Sada rehnde dilgeere ne!  

**English Translation**:
> Now the glittering shards catch the light and gleam,  
> And shallow people proclaim: 'Behold, these are diamonds!'  
> Shiv says: 'These are merely the fragments of my shattered dreams,  
> Eternally drenched in sorrow and tears!'  

💡 *Commentary*: Society mistakes the sparkling wreckage of a poet's life for decorative entertainment.

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Kirchaan (ਕਿਰਚਾਂ)** | *Kir-chaan* | Sharp glass splinters | Metaphor for painful, irreversible emotional wounds. |

## 6. Hassde Hoeyan

### **ਹੱਸਦੇ ਹੋਇਆਂ** • **ہسدے ہویاں**

#### *"With Laughter on My Lips"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Smile` `#Mask` `#Stoicism` `#Fame`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: How the poet conceals lethal suffering beneath a bright, theatrical smile so the world does not gloat.
- **Genesis**: Composed when Shiv was performing at literary mushairas while battling inner depression.
- **Archival Context**: Recited at Kavi Sammelans to huge ovations, hiding the poet's deepening physical breakdown. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Reveals the performative persona of the modern bohemian poet.
- **Musical Recitation**: Upbeat folk rhythm concealing tragic lyrics.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਸੀਂ ਹੱਸਦੇ ਹੋਇਆਂ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਪੀਤਾ,  
> ਜੱਗ ਨੂੰ ਭੇਤ ਨਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਅੱਖੀਆਂ 'ਚੋਂ ਹੰਝੂ ਡਿੱਗਣ ਨਾ ਦਿੱਤੇ,  
> ਹੋਂਠਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਸੀਅ ਲਿੱਤਾ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اسیں ہسدے ہویاں زہر پیتا،  
> جگ نوں بھیت نہ دتا نی!  
> اکھیاں چوں ہنجھو ڈگن نہ دتے،  
> ہونٹھاں نوں سی لتا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Assin hassde hoeyan zehar peeta,  
> Jagg nu bhet na ditta ni!  
> Akkhiyaan chon hanjhu diggan na ditte,  
> Hothaan nu see litta ni!  

**English Translation**:
> With laughter upon our lips we drank lethal poison,  
> Refusing to grant the callous world a glimpse of our secret!  
> We permitted not a single tear to fall from our eyes,  
> Stitching our lips firmly into silence!  

💡 *Commentary*: The heroic refusal to let detractors witness his tears.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਲੋਕ ਸਮਝਦੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਬੜੇ ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ 'ਤੇ ਤਾੜੀਆਂ ਮਾਰਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਉਹ ਕੀ ਜਾਨਣ ਅੰਦਰ ਸਾਡੇ,  
> ਰੋਜ਼ ਕਿੰਨੇ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਹਾਰਦੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> لوک سمجھدے اسیں بڑے خوش ہاں،  
> ساڈے گیتاں تے تاڑیاں ماردے نے!  
> اوہ کی جانن اندر ساڈے،  
> روز کنے سپنے ہاردے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Lok samajhde assin barhe khush haan,  
> Saade geetan te taariyan maarde ne!  
> Oh ki jaanan andar saade,  
> Roz kinne supne haarde ne!  

**English Translation**:
> The crowd imagines we dwell in perpetual joy,  
> Clapping their hands in rapturous applause at our songs!  
> How could they ever know that inside our breast,  
> Hundreds of dreams concede defeat and perish each day?  

💡 *Commentary*: The disconnect between stage fame and private torment.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਵਿਦਾ ਹੋਊਗਾ,  
> ਇਸ ਜੱਗ ਦੇ ਮੇਲੇ ਤੋਂ!  
> ਪਿੱਛੋਂ ਲੋਕੀਂ ਯਾਦ ਕਰਨਗੇ,  
> ਉਸ ਦਰਦੀਲੇ ਚੇਲੇ ਤੋਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو تاں ہس کے وداع ہوؤگا،  
> اس جگ دے میلے توں!  
> پچھوں لوکی یاد کرنگے،  
> اس دردیلے چیلے توں!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv taan hass ke vida houga,  
> Is jagg de mele ton!  
> Pichhon loki yaad karange,  
> Us dardeele chele ton!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv shall take his final departure with a smile  
> From this crowded carnival of the mortal world!  
> Only after he is gone will the people truly understand  
> The fathomless agony of this faithful disciple of love!  

💡 *Commentary*: Prophetic announcement of his graceful exit from life's fair.

|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Mela (ਮੇਲਾ)** | *May-la* | Village carnival / Fair | Classic metaphor for the fleeting, crowded illusion of human life. |

## 7. Pattal Te Phull

### **ਪੱਤਲ 'ਤੇ ਫੁੱਲ** • **پتل تے پھل**

#### *"Flowers Upon a Leaf Platter"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Pattal` `#Feast` `#Leftovers` `#Fate`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: Using the leaf-plate (pattal) from community feasts to describe the discarded leftovers of a poet's life.
- **Genesis**: Inspired by Punjabi village wedding feasts where food was served on stitched banyan leaf platters.
- **Archival Context**: Draws on village marriage feasts (dhaam) where guests dine on stitched broad leaves, discarding them afterwards. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Rare blend of ritual feasting imagery with existential poverty.
- **Musical Recitation**: Solemn, low-register cadence.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜੰਞ ਲੰਘ ਗਈ, ਮੇਲਾ ਮੁੱਕਿਆ,  
> ਪੱਤਲਾਂ ਰੁਲ਼ੀਆਂ ਧੂੜਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਪੁੱਛੇ ਉਹਨਾਂ ਫੁੱਲਾਂ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਜੋ ਸੜ ਗਏ ਦਿਲ ਦੀਆਂ ਝੂਰਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جنج لنگھ گئی، میلا مکیا،  
> پتلاں رلیاں دھوڑاں وچ!  
> کوئی نہ پچھے اوہناں پھلاں نوں،  
> جو سڑ گئے دل دیاں جھوراں وچ!  

**Romanized**:
> Janj langh gayi, mela mukkeya,  
> Pattlaan ruliyan dhooran vich!  
> Koyi na puchhe ohnaan phullaan nu,  
> Jo sarh gaye dil diyan jhooraan vich!  

**English Translation**:
> The wedding procession has passed, the festival has ended,  
> And the dried leaf-platters lie abandoned in the dust!  
> Not a soul inquires after those withered flowers  
> Which smoldered away inside the heart's quiet sorrows!  

💡 *Commentary*: The festive banquet leaves only discarded leaf plates blowing across the empty ground.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਸੀਂ ਵੀ ਪੱਤਲ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਵਰਤੇ ਗਏ,  
> ਸਭ ਨੇ ਆਪਣਾ ਸਵਾਦ ਲਿਆ!  
> ਜਦ ਭੁੱਖ ਮਿਟੀ ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਦੀ,  
> ਸਾਨੂੰ ਕੂੜੇ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਵਗਾਹ ਦਿੱਤਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اسیں وی پتل وانگوں ورتے گئے،  
> سبھ نے اپنا سواد لیا!  
> جد بھکھ مٹی اس دنیا دی،  
> سانوں کوڑے وانگوں وگاہ دتا!  

**Romanized**:
> Assin vi pattal vaangu varte gaye,  
> Sabh ne apna swaad liya!  
> Jad bhukkh mitti is duniya di,  
> Saanu koorhe vaangu wagaah ditta!  

**English Translation**:
> We too were used like disposable leaf-platters—  
> Everyone partook of our songs to satisfy their appetite!  
> And once the hunger of this world was appeased,  
> They hurled us aside like refuse into the wind!  

💡 *Commentary*: Devastating critique of how society consumes an artist's emotional output.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੇ ਗੀਤ ਪਰ ਧੂੜ 'ਚ ਰੁਲ਼ ਕੇ ਵੀ,  
> ਆਪਣੀ ਮਹਿਕ ਨਾ ਛੱਡਣਗੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਲੱਭਣਗੇ ਇਤਿਹਾਸ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦਾ,  
> ਉਹ ਸਾਡੇ ਨੈਣਾਂ 'ਚੋਂ ਕੱਢਣਗੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دے گیت پر دھوڑ چ رل کے وی،  
> اپنی مہک نہ چھڈنگے!  
> جو لبھنگے تاریخ پنجاب دا،  
> اوہ ساڈے نیناں چوں کڈھنگے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv de geet par dhoor ch rul ke vi,  
> Apni mehak na chhaddange!  
> Jo labbhnge itihaas Punjab da,  
> Oh saade nainan chon kaddhnge!  

**English Translation**:
> Yet even trampled in the dust, Shiv's verses  
> Shall never surrender their undying fragrance!  
> Those who seek the true, unvarnished history of Punjab  
> Shall have to dig it out from the tears of our eyes!  

💡 *Commentary*: His poetry stands as the emotional chronicle of Punjab's inner life.

## 8. Jogi Aaya Duaare

### **ਜੋਗੀ ਆਇਆ ਦੁਆਰੇ** • **جوگی آیا دوارے**

#### *"The Wandering Ascetic at the Threshold"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Jogi` `#Nath` `#Alms` `#SpiritualLove` `#Courtyard`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The lover appears at the beloved's courtyard as a wandering Jogi with ash-smeared skin, asking not for bread but for a single glance of recognition.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala during Shiv's deep immersion into the classical Qissa poetry of Punjab.
- **Archival Context**: Draws on the legendary Nath Jogi tradition of Gorakhnath and Waris Shah's Heer Ranjha, casting the lover as an ascetic beggar. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜੋਗੀ ਆਇਆ ਤੇਰੇ ਦੁਆਰੇ ਨੀ,  
> ਕੰਨੀਂ ਮੁੰਦਰਾਂ ਪਾਈਆਂ ਨੇ!  
> ਤਨ 'ਤੇ ਭਸਮ ਰਮਾਈ ਹੋਈ ਏ,  
> ਨੈਣਾਂ 'ਚ ਗ਼ਮ ਦੀਆਂ ਛਾਈਆਂ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جوگی آیا تیرے دوارے نی،  
> کنی مندراں پائیاں نے!  
> تن تے بھسم رمائی ہوئی اے،  
> نیناں چ غم دیاں چھائیاں نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Jogi aaya tere duaare ni,  
> Kanneen mundraan paaiyan ne!  
> Tan te bhasam ramaayi hoyi ae,  
> Nainan ch gham diyan chhaaiyan ne!  

**English Translation**:
> A wandering Jogi has arrived at your portal,  
> Heavy ringlets hanging from his pierced ears!  
> His slender body is smeared with pale white ashes,  
> And the shadows of unutterable grief haunt his eyes!  

💡 *Commentary*: The classical Nath ascetic transformation of the abandoned lover.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਉਹ ਮੰਗੇ ਸੋਨਾ-ਚਾਂਦੀ,  
> ਨਾ ਉਹ ਮੰਗੇ ਦਾਣੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਉਹ ਤਾਂ ਮੰਗੇ ਇੱਕ ਦੀਦਾਰਾ,  
> ਜਿਸਨੂੰ ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਜਾਣੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ اوہ منگے سونا چاندی،  
> نہ اوہ منگے دانے نی!  
> اوہ تاں منگے اک دیدارا،  
> جس نوں کوئی نہ جانے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Na oh mange sona-chaandi,  
> Na oh mange daane ni!  
> Oh taan mange ikk deedaara,  
> Jisnu koyi na jaane ni!  

**English Translation**:
> He asks not for offerings of gold or silver;  
> He begs not for grains of wheat to fill his bowl!  
> He seeks only a solitary glimpse of your countenance—  
> A secret prayer that no earthly soul can decipher!  

💡 *Commentary*: Refusal of material charity in favor of mystical communion.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੈਸੀ ਖ਼ੈਰ ਤੂੰ ਪਾਈ ਜੋਗੀ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਕਿ ਮੁੜ ਕੇ ਉਹ ਨਾ ਬੋਲਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਤੇਰੇ ਦਰ ਤੋਂ ਉੱਠ ਕੇ ਤੁਰ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਉਸ ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਕਾਸਾ ਖੋਲ੍ਹਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کیسی خیر توں پائی جوگی نوں،  
> کہ مڑ کے اوہ نہ بولیا نی!  
> تیرے در توں اٹھ کے تر گیا،  
> اس کدے نہ کاسا کھولیا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Kaisi khair toon paayi jogi nu,  
> Ki murh ke oh na boleya ni!  
> Tere dar ton utth ke tur gaya,  
> Us kade na kaasa kholeya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> What silent alms did you bestow upon that ascetic,  
> That he never uttered another word in his lifetime?  
> He arose from your threshold and vanished into the mist,  
> Never once unsealing his beggar's bowl before any door again!  

💡 *Commentary*: The finality of the unspoken dismissal.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਜੋਗੀ (Jogi)** | *Jo-gee* | Renunciant ascetic / Nath Yogic disciple | Sacred archetypal figure in Punjabi romance, epitomized by Ranjha who renounced kingdom and family for Heer. |
| **ਮੁੰਦਰਾਂ (Mundraan)** | *Mun-draan* | Large earrings worn by Kanphata Jogis | Signs of severed worldly identity and vows of eternal renunciation. |

## 9. Saada Chiriyaan Da Chamba Ve

### **ਸਾਡਾ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਚੰਬਾ ਵੇ** • **ساڈا چڑیاں دا چنبا وے**

#### *"Our Flock of Little Sparrows"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Daughters` `#BridalDeparture` `#Babul` `#Courtyard` `#Exile`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: Daughters compared to a transient flock of sparrows in their father's courtyard, destined to fly to distant lands without return.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala as Shiv reflected on the migration of families and the inevitable separation of daughters.
- **Archival Context**: Shiv's literary reinvention of the ancient Punjabi wedding folk song, infusing it with existential grief and Partition memory. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਡਾ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਚੰਬਾ ਵੇ ਬਾਬਲ,  
> ਅਸਾਂ ਉੱਡ ਵੇ ਜਾਣਾ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਲੰਮੀ ਉਡਾਰੀ ਵੇ ਬਾਬਲ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਦੇਸ ਨਾ ਜਾਣਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ساڈا چڑیاں دا چنبا وے بابل،  
> اساں اڈ وے جانا!  
> ساڈی لمبی اڈاری وے بابل،  
> کسے دیس نہ جانا!  

**Romanized**:
> Saada chiriyaan da chamba ve baabul,  
> Asaan udd ve jaana!  
> Saadi lammi udaari ve baabul,  
> Kise des na jaana!  

**English Translation**:
> We are merely a flock of little sparrows, O father,  
> And our wings are poised for flight!  
> Ours is a long and unreturning voyage across the horizons,  
> To a foreign country where our destiny remains unknown!  

💡 *Commentary*: The archetypal farewell of daughters in patriarchal Punjab.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਤੇਰੇ ਮਹਿਲਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਵਿੱਚ ਵੇ ਬਾਬਲ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਚਰਖਾ ਪਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਗੁੱਡੀਆਂ ਦੀ ਖੇਡ ਮੁੱਕੀ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਦਿਲ ਰੋ ਪਿਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> تیرے محلاں دے وچ وچ وے بابل،  
> ساڈا چرخا پیا اے!  
> ساڈی گڈیاں دی کھیڈ مکی،  
> ساڈا دل رو پیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Tere mehlaan de vich vich ve baabul,  
> Saada charkha peya ae!  
> Saadi guddiyan di khed mukki,  
> Saada dil ro peya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Within the safe corridors of your home, O father,  
> Our spinning wheel lies abandoned and silent!  
> The innocent childhood games of cloth dolls have ended,  
> And our hearts have broken into weeping!  

💡 *Commentary*: The charkha (spinning wheel) and dolls represent innocent girlhood.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਿਸ ਵਿਹੜੇ 'ਚ ਖੇਡੀਆਂ ਬਾਬਲ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਵਿਹੜਾ ਹੋਇਆ ਬਿਗਾਨਾ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਆਖੇ ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਕਿਸੇ ਦਾ ਨਾ ਠਿਕਾਣਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جس ویہڑے چ کھیڈیاں بابل،  
> اوہی ویہڑا ہویا بیگانہ!  
> شیو آکھے اس دنیا دے وچ،  
> کوئی کسے دا نہ ٹھکانہ!  

**Romanized**:
> Jis vehre ch khediyaan baabul,  
> Uhi vehra hoya bigaana!  
> Shiv aakhe iss duniya de vich,  
> Koyi kise da na thikaana!  

**English Translation**:
> That very courtyard where we played as children, father,  
> Has suddenly become an alien, forbidden land!  
> Shiv says: 'In this vast and rootless world,  
> No living soul possesses a permanent dwelling place!'  

💡 *Commentary*: Folk bridal exile expanded into universal existential homelessness.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਚੰਬਾ (Chamba)** | *Cham-baa* | Cluster of birds / Flock of sparrows | Tender folk metaphor for young daughters sharing a family home before marriage dispersal. |
| **ਬਾਬਲ (Baabul)** | *Baa-bul* | Father / Patriarch of the house | Sacred folk term in Punjabi marriage laments symbolizing paternal protection and emotional shelter. |

## 10. Sukka Rukkh

### **ਸੁੱਕਾ ਰੁੱਖ** • **سکا رخ**

#### *"The Withered Tree of the Barren Field"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Tree` `#Drought` `#Barren` `#Solitude` `#Defiance`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: A solitary withered tree stands in the midst of parched soil, stripped of leaves and bird nests, yet refusing to collapse.
- **Genesis**: Inspired by an ancient dead Shisham tree that stood along the path between Batala and Dera Baba Nanak.
- **Archival Context**: Direct predecessor to his famous poem 'Kujh Rukh' in Mainu Vida Karo (1963), pioneering arborial imagery in Punjabi literature. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਸੁੱਕਾ ਰੁੱਖ ਹਾਂ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਜਿਸ 'ਤੇ ਪੰਛੀ ਬਹਿੰਦੇ ਨਹੀਂ!  
> ਜਿਸਦੀ ਛਾਂ 'ਤੇ ਰਾਹੀ ਬਹਿ ਕੇ,  
> ਸੁੱਖ ਦਾ ਸਾਹ ਹੁਣ ਲੈਂਦੇ ਨਹੀਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں اک سکا رخ ہاں اڑیو،  
> جس تے پنچھی بہندے نہیں!  
> جس دی چھاں تے راہی بہہ کے،  
> سکھ دا ساہ ہن لیندے نہیں!  

**Romanized**:
> Main ikk sukka rukkh haan arhiyo,  
> Jis te panchhi behnde nahin!  
> Jisdi chhaan te raahi beh ke,  
> Sukkh da saah hun lainde nahin!  

**English Translation**:
> I am a withered and dead tree, companions,  
> Upon whose dry branches no singing birds will alight!  
> Beneath whose shade no weary traveler will pause  
> To draw a soothing breath of relief from the road!  

💡 *Commentary*: The total exhaustion of the poet's capacity to provide solace.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਮੇਰੇ 'ਤੇ ਹਰਿਆਵਲ ਆਵੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਫੁੱਲ ਖਿੜਾਵੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ!  
> ਬੱਦਲ ਆ ਕੇ ਲੰਘ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਨੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਬੂੰਦ ਪਿਲਾਵੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ میرے تے ہریاول آوے،  
> نہ کوئی پھل کھڑاوے مینوں!  
> بدل آ کے لنگھ جاندے نے،  
> کوئی نہ بوند پلاوے مینوں!  

**Romanized**:
> Na mere te haryaawal aave,  
> Na koyi phull khirhaave mainu!  
> Baddal aa ke langh jaande ne,  
> Koyi na boond pilaave mainu!  

**English Translation**:
> No emerald green renewal visits my limbs,  
> Nor does any wild blossom unfold upon my bark!  
> The rainclouds gather and drift indifferently overhead,  
> Offering not a single drop of moisture to soothe my thirst!  

💡 *Commentary*: Nature's cruelty towards that which is already drying.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪਰ ਮੈਂ ਖੜ੍ਹਾ ਹਾਂ ਸੀਨਾ ਤਾਣ ਕੇ,  
> ਝੱਖੜਾਂ ਅੱਗੇ ਝੁਕਿਆ ਨਹੀਂ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਗੀਤ ਵੀ ਏਸੇ ਰੁੱਖ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਮਰ ਕੇ ਵੀ ਕਦੇ ਮੁੱਕਿਆ ਨਹੀਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پر میں کھڑھا ہاں سینہ تان کے،  
> جھکھڑاں اگے جھکیا نہیں!  
> شیو دا گیت وی ایسے رخ وانگوں،  
> مر کے وی کدے مکیا نہیں!  

**Romanized**:
> Par main kharha haan seena taan ke,  
> Jhakhrhaan agge jhukeya nahin!  
> Shiv da geet vi aese rukkh vaangu,  
> Mar ke vi kade mukkeya nahin!  

**English Translation**:
> Yet I stand upright with defiant chest bare,  
> Refusing to bend before the howling dust-storms!  
> Shiv's song too is like this withered tree—  
> Even having perished, it shall never be extinguished!  

💡 *Commentary*: Defiance in defeat; the withered timber endures.

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਰੁੱਖ (Rukkh)** | *Rukkh* | Tree | Sacred entity in Punjabi folklore providing maternal shade (chhaan) and sanctuary to wandering birds. |

## 11. Soorma Nainan Da

### **ਸੂਰਮਾ ਨੈਣਾਂ ਦਾ** • **سورما نیناں دا**

#### *"The Kohl Lining Her Weeping Eyes"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Kohl` `#Tears` `#Eyes` `#Beauty` `#Mourning`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The black kohl applied to beautify maidenly eyes is melted and washed away by nocturnal tears of longing.
- **Genesis**: Written during the period when Shiv's muse was married into a wealthy household, leaving her in gilded sorrow.
- **Archival Context**: A quintessential example of Shiv subverting traditional Sringara (romantic adornment) into Karuna (tragic mourning). (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਤੇਰੇ ਨੈਣਾਂ ਦਾ ਸੂਰਮਾ ਅੜੀਏ,  
> ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਵਹਿ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਸੁਪਨਾ ਅਸੀਂ ਵੇਖਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਅਧੂਰਾ ਹੀ ਰਹਿ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> تیرے نیناں دا سورما اڑیئے،  
> ہنجھواں دے وچ وہہ گیا نی!  
> جو سپنا اسیں ویکھیا سی،  
> اوہ ادھورا ہی رہ گیا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Tere nainan da soorma arhiye,  
> Hanjuwan de vich veh gaya ni!  
> Jo supna aseen vekheya si,  
> Oh adhoora hi reh gaya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The dark kohl lining your tender eyes, beloved,  
> Has washed away in torrential cascades of tears!  
> That luminous dream we dared to envision together  
> Remains forever shattered and unfulfilled!  

💡 *Commentary*: Cosmetic beauty undone by genuine human agony.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਾਲ਼ੀ ਰਾਤ ਵੀ ਰੋ ਪਈ ਵੇਖ ਕੇ,  
> ਤੇਰੀ ਅੱਖ ਦੀ ਲਾਲੀ ਨੂੰ!  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਨੇ ਪੁੱਛਿਆ ਨਾ ਮਾਲਣ ਤੋਂ,  
> ਇਸ ਸੁੱਕੀ ਹੋਈ ਡਾਲੀ ਨੂੰ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کالی رات وی رو پئی ویکھ کے،  
> تیری اکھ دی لالی نوں!  
> کسے نے پچھیا نہ مالن توں،  
> اس سکی ہوئی ڈالی نوں!  

**Romanized**:
> Kaali raat vi ro payi vekh ke,  
> Teri akh di laali nu!  
> Kise ne puchheya na maalan ton,  
> Iss sukki hoyi daali nu!  

**English Translation**:
> Even the pitch-black midnight broke into tears  
> Witnessing the bloodshot inflammation of your weeping eyes!  
> Not a single patron asked of the gardener woman  
> Concerning this withered, flowerless branch!  

💡 *Commentary*: The bloodshot eye as evidence of sleepless lamentation.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਸੂਰਮਾ ਪਾ ਕੇ ਕੀ ਕਰਨਾ,  
> ਜਦ ਵੇਖਣ ਵਾਲ਼ਾ ਤੁਰ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਦਿਲ ਤਾਂ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਤੇਰਾ ਗ਼ਮ ਸੀਨੇ 'ਚ ਖੁਰ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن سورما پا کے کی کرنا،  
> جد ویکھن والا تر گیا اے!  
> شیو دا دل تاں مٹی ہویا،  
> تیرا غم سینے چ کھر گیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun soorma paa ke ki karna,  
> Jad vekhan waala tur gaya ae!  
> Shiv da dil taan mitti hoya,  
> Tera gham seene ch khur gaya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> What purpose remains in adorning the eyes with kohl  
> When he who loved to gaze into them has departed forever?  
> Shiv's heart has dissolved into anonymous earth,  
> And your sorrow has melted eternally into his breast!  

💡 *Commentary*: Adornment becomes meaningless in the beloved's absence.

## 12. Tandoor Di Agg

### **ਤੰਦੂਰ ਦੀ ਅੱਗ** • **تندور دی اگ**

#### *"The Blazing Flame of the Clay Oven"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Tandoor` `#Fire` `#Bread` `#VillageHearth` `#ConsumingPassion`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The underground earthen tandoor where village bread is baked becomes a furnace of unquenchable inner agony.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala as Shiv watched village women gather around evening clay ovens.
- **Archival Context**: Draws on the village Sanjha Chulha (communal clay oven) tradition, transforming domestic warmth into destructive longing. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਡੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਬਲ਼ੇ ਤੰਦੂਰ ਜਿਹਾ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਰੋਜ਼ ਹੀ ਰੋਟੀਆਂ ਸੜਦੀਆਂ ਨੇ!  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਸੁਆਹ ਹੋਏ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਯਾਦਾਂ ਪੈਰ ਨਾ ਧਰਦੀਆਂ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ساڈے سینے بلے تندور جہیا،  
> جتھے روز ہی روٹیاں سڑدیاں نے!  
> ساڈے دل دے سپنے سواہ ہوئے،  
> جتھے یاداں پیر نہ دھردیاں نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Saade seene balhe tandoor jiha,  
> Jitthe roz hi rotiyan sarhdiyaan ne!  
> Saade dil de supne suaah hoye,  
> Jitthe yaadaan pair na dhardiyaan ne!  

**English Translation**:
> Inside our breast burns a clay tandoor of incandescent flame,  
> Where daily the flatbreads of our sustenance char to cinders!  
> The tender dreams of our youth have turned to cold grey ash,  
> Where even hesitant memories fear to tread!  

💡 *Commentary*: Domestic nourishment transformed into metabolic combustion.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਬਾਲਣ ਪਾਇਆ ਹੱਡੀਆਂ ਦਾ,  
> ਲਹੂ ਦਾ ਤੇਲ ਛਿੜਕਾਇਆ ਏ!  
> ਇਸ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੀ ਭੱਠੀ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਸਾਨੂੰ ਰੱਬ ਨੇ ਆਪ ਤਪਾਇਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> بالن پایا ہڈیاں دا،  
> لہو دا تیل چھڑکایا اے!  
> اس برہا دی بھٹھی اندر،  
> سانوں رب نے آپ تپایا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Baalan paaya haddiyan da,  
> Lahu da tel chhirhkaaya ae!  
> Iss birha di bhatthi andar,  
> Saanu Rabb ne aap tapaaya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> We fed this furnace with fuel carved of our very bones,  
> Sprinkling it with the sacrificial oil of our own blood!  
> Within this subterranean kiln of eternal separation,  
> The Creator Himself has roasted our spirit!  

💡 *Commentary*: Metaphysical furnace fed by the poet's mortal biology.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੋਈ ਆਣ ਕੇ ਠੰਢਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਪਾਵੇ,  
> ਇਹ ਅੱਗ ਤਾਂ ਬੁੱਝਣ ਵਾਲ਼ੀ ਨਹੀਂ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਸੜ ਕੇ ਕੋਲਾ ਹੋ ਚੱਲਿਆ,  
> ਇਹ ਪੀੜ ਕਦੇ ਮੁੱਕਣ ਵਾਲ਼ੀ ਨਹੀਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کوئی آن کے ٹھنڈا پانی پاوے،  
> ایہ اگ تاں بجھن والی نہیں!  
> شیو سڑ کے کولا ہو چلیا،  
> ایہ پیڑ کدے مکن والی نہیں!  

**Romanized**:
> Koyi aan ke thanddha paani paave,  
> Eh agg taan bujjhan waali nahin!  
> Shiv sarh ke kola ho chaleya,  
> Eh peerr kade mukkan waali nahin!  

**English Translation**:
> Let anyone come and pour icy water upon it—  
> This unholy fire cannot be extinguished by earthly water!  
> Shiv has burned down into brittle black charcoal,  
> Yet this sacred pain refuses to release its hold!  

💡 *Commentary*: Charcoal as the final state of indestructible matter.

|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਤੰਦੂਰ (Tandoor)** | *Tan-door* | Cylindrical clay oven | The heart of communal village cooking in Punjab, transformed here into a crucible of spiritual torment. |

## 13. Doli Turi Kehraan Di

### **ਡੋਲੀ ਤੁਰੀ ਕਹਿਰਾਂ ਦੀ** • **ڈولی تری قہراں دی**

#### *"The Tragic Palanquin Moves Away"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Doli` `#Palanquin` `#Betrothal` `#TragicDeparture` `#Majha`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: Watching the bridal palanquin carry away the beloved while the lover stands by the village crossroads like a living corpse.
- **Genesis**: Composed after the wedding of the Gurdaspur magistrate's daughter, whom Shiv loved deeply.
- **Archival Context**: Documents the definitive trauma of Shiv's life: the marriage of his first love to a district official, sealing his lifelong vow of mourning. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਡੋਲੀ ਤੁਰੀ ਕਹਿਰਾਂ ਦੀ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਜਨਾਜ਼ਾ ਨਿਕਲ ਗਿਆ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਮੁੱਖ ਨੂੰ ਪੂਜਿਆ ਕਰਦੇ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਗ਼ੈਰਾਂ ਦੇ ਘਰ ਚੱਲ ਗਿਆ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ڈولی تری قہراں دی اڑیو،  
> ساڈا جنازہ نکل گیا!  
> جس مکھ نوں پوجیا کردے سی،  
> اوہ غیراں دے گھر چل گیا!  

**Romanized**:
> Doli turi kehraan di arhiyo,  
> Saada janaaza nikal gaya!  
> Jis mukkh nu poojeya karde si,  
> Oh gairaan de ghar chal gaya!  

**English Translation**:
> The bridal palanquin has departed in devastating wrath, friends;  
> It is our funeral bier that has taken to the road!  
> That sacred face which we worshiped like a deity  
> Has crossed over into the threshold of strangers!  

💡 *Commentary*: The bridal doli equated to a funeral bier (janaaza).

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਹਾਰਾਂ ਨੇ ਚੁੱਕਿਆ ਕਾਠ ਦਾ ਡੋਲਾ,  
> ਪਰ ਸਾਡੇ ਸਾਹ ਘੁੱਟ ਦਿੱਤੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਫੁੱਲ ਸੀ ਸਾਡੇ ਪਿਆਰ ਦੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਰਾਹਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੁੱਟ ਦਿੱਤੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کہاراں نے چکیا کاٹھ دا ڈولا،  
> پر ساڈے ساہ گھٹ دتے!  
> جو پھل سی ساڈے پیار دے،  
> اوہ راہاں دے وچ سٹ دتے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kahaaraan ne chukkeya kaath da dola,  
> Par saade saah ghutt ditte!  
> Jo phull si saade pyaar de,  
> Oh raahaan de vich sutt ditte!  

**English Translation**:
> The four bearers hoisted the wooden litter upon their shoulders,  
> Yet in doing so, they strangled the breath from our lungs!  
> Those fragile blossoms of our devotion  
> Were cast down and trampled in the roadside dust!  

💡 *Commentary*: Palanquin bearers acting as unintentional executioners.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੀ ਜੂਹ 'ਚ ਖੜ੍ਹ ਕੇ ਸ਼ਿਵ,  
> ਕਿਸਨੂੰ ਆਵਾਜ਼ਾਂ ਮਾਰੇਗਾ?  
> ਜੋ ਤੁਰ ਗਿਆ ਉਹ ਮੁੜਨਾ ਨਹੀਂ,  
> ਇਹ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਗੁਜ਼ਾਰੇਗਾ?  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن پنڈ دی جوہ چ کھڑ کے شیو،  
> کس نوں آوازاں مارےگا؟  
> جو تر گیا اوہ مڑنا نہیں،  
> ایہہ زندگی کویں گزارےگا؟  

**Romanized**:
> Hun pind di jooh ch kharh ke Shiv,  
> Kisnu aawaazaan maarega?  
> Jo tur gaya oh murhna nahin,  
> Eh zindagi kiven guzaarega?  

**English Translation**:
> Standing alone upon the outer boundary of the village, Shiv,  
> Whom will you ever summon with your desperate cries?  
> She who has departed shall never return;  
> How will you ever survive the remainder of this hollow life?  

💡 *Commentary*: The village border (jooh) as the limit of existence.

|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਡੋਲੀ (Doli)** | *Do-lee* | Bridal palanquin | Covered wooden litter in which a bride was carried to her in-laws' home, symbol of irreversible separation from paternal home. |
| **ਜੂਹ (Jooh)** | *Jooh* | Village pasture boundary | The geographical limit of the village beyond which lies the unknown outside world. |

## 14. Meenh Varsiya Sawan Da

### **ਮੀਂਹ ਵਰ੍ਹਿਆ ਸਾਵਣ ਦਾ** • **مینہ ورھیا ساون دا**

#### *"The Torrential Downpour of Sawan"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Monsoon` `#Sawan` `#Rain` `#Lightning` `#Separation`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The monsoon downpour brings joy to lovers and farmers, but delivers drowning agony to the solitary heart.
- **Genesis**: Penned during a stormy monsoon night in Batala when roof leaks forced Shiv to write by candlelight.
- **Archival Context**: Inverts the joyous tradition of Barsaat/Teej poetry into an acute sensory experience of solitary torment. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੀਂਹ ਵਰ੍ਹਿਆ ਸਾਵਣ ਦਾ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਛੱਤਾਂ ਚੋਣ ਲੱਗੀਆਂ ਨੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੀਆਂ ਕੰਧਾਂ ਕੱਚੀਆਂ,  
> ਹੰਝੂ ਧੋਣ ਲੱਗੀਆਂ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> مینہ ورھیا ساون دا اڑیو،  
> چھتاں چون لگیاں نے!  
> میرے دل دیاں کندھاں کچیاں،  
> ہنجھو دھون لگیاں نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Meenh varsiya sawan da arhiyo,  
> Chhattaan chon lagiyan ne!  
> Mere dil diyan kandhaan kachiyan,  
> Hanjhu dhon lagiyan ne!  

**English Translation**:
> The torrential monsoon rain of Sawan pours down, friends,  
> And the mud roofs have begun to leak!  
> The unbaked earthen walls of my heart  
> Are being washed away by dissolving tears!  

💡 *Commentary*: The leaking roof corresponds to the crumbling emotional structure.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਬੱਦਲ ਗੱਜੇ, ਬਿਜਲੀ ਚਮਕੇ,  
> ਡਰਦਾ ਕਾਲਜਾ ਕੰਬੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਸਭ ਸੁਹਾਗਣਾਂ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਬੈਠੀਆਂ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਯਾਰ ਬੇਗਾਨੇ ਅੰਬੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> بدل گجے، بجلی چمکے،  
> ڈردا کالجا کنبے نی!  
> سبھ سہاگناں گھر وچ بیٹھیاں،  
> ساڈا یار بیگانے امبے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Baddal gajje, bijli chamke,  
> Darda kaalja kambe ni!  
> Sabh suhaaganaan ghar vich baitthiyan,  
> Saada yaar begaane ambe ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Thunder rumbles fiercely and lightning flashes across the skies;  
> My terrified heart shudders in darkness!  
> All wedded maidens sit safely sheltered within their homes,  
> While my beloved rests beneath another's mango bough!  

💡 *Commentary*: Contrast between domestic security and outsider alienation.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਹ ਸਾਵਣ ਸਾਡੇ ਵਾਸਤੇ ਤਾਂ,  
> ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਸੁਨੇਹਾ ਲਿਆਇਆ ਏ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਪਾਣੀ 'ਚ ਧਰਤੀ ਸਿੰਜੀ,  
> ਉਸ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਰੋਲ ਵਗਾਇਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ایہہ ساون ساڈے واسطے تاں،  
> موت دا سُنیہا لیایا اے!  
> جس پانی چ دھرتی سنجی،  
> اس سانوں رول وگایا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Eh sawan saade vaaste taan,  
> Maut da suneha leyaaya ae!  
> Jis paani ch dharti sinji,  
> Us saanu rol vagaaya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> For our broken soul, this rainy season of Sawan  
> Has arrived bearing the decree of death!  
> That very water that irrigates and revives the thirsty soil  
> Has swept us away in its violent, muddy flood!  

💡 *Commentary*: Life-giving water converted into fatal deluge.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਸਾਵਣ (Sawan)** | *Saa-wan* | The peak monsoon month (July-August) | Traditional romantic season of swings (peengh), dancing, and union, here inverted into a season of drowning grief. |

## 15. Chhanna Bhar Zeher

### **ਛੰਨਾ ਭਰ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ** • **چھنا بھر زہر**

#### *"A Bronze Bowl Filled with Poison"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Poison` `#BronzeBowl` `#Defiance` `#Martyrdom` `#Socrates`
- **Theme / Biome**: `rebellion` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: A bronze bowl brimming with deadly venom is presented to the poet, who drains it with philosophical ecstasy.
- **Genesis**: Composed when conservative Punjabi critics accused Shiv of glorifying despair and decadence.
- **Archival Context**: Evokes the historical martyrdom of Waris Shah's Heer and Meera Bai, embracing the poison of societal rejection. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਲੋਕਾਂ ਦਿੱਤਾ ਛੰਨਾ ਭਰ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਦਾ,  
> ਅਸਾਂ ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਮੂੰਹ ਨੂੰ ਲਾਇਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਸਮਝਦੇ ਸੀ ਅਸੀਂ ਮਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ,  
> ਉਹਨਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਸੱਚ ਵਿਖਾਇਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> لوکاں دتا چھنا بھر زہر دا،  
> اساں ہس کے منہ نوں لایا نی!  
> جو سمجھدے سی اسیں مر جاواںگے،  
> اوہناں نوں سچ وکھایا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Lokaan ditta chhanna bhar zehar da,  
> Asaan hass ke munh nu laaya ni!  
> Jo samajhde si aseen mar jaavaange,  
> Ohnaan nu sach vikhaaya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The righteous crowd offered a bronze bowl brimming with poison,  
> And with a smile upon our lips, we drained it to the dregs!  
> Those who believed we would perish in ignominy  
> Were made to witness the transcendent triumph of truth!  

💡 *Commentary*: Embracing poison as the ultimate vindication of the lover.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਵੀ ਸਾਡੇ ਲਹੂ 'ਚ ਰਲ ਕੇ,  
> ਗੀਤ ਬਣ ਕੇ ਫੁੱਟ ਪਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਦਰਦ ਅਮਰ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਹਰ ਜ਼ੁਲਮ ਦਾ ਪਾਂਡਾ ਟੁੱਟ ਪਿਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> زہر وی ساڈے لہو چ رل کے،  
> گیت بن کے پھٹ پیا اے!  
> ساڈے دل دا درد امر ہویا،  
> ہر ظلم دا پانڈا ٹٹ پیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Zehar vi saade lahu ch ral ke,  
> Geet ban ke phutt peya ae!  
> Saade dil da dard amar hoya,  
> Har zulm da paanda tutt peya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Even that lethal poison, mingling with our bloodstream,  
> Burst forth in a fountain of immortal verse!  
> The sacred agony of our heart attained everlasting life,  
> While the clay vessel of their cruelty shattered into dust!  

💡 *Commentary*: Transmutation of toxicity into high lyric art.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਝੁਕਿਆ ਜ਼ਾਲਮ ਅੱਗੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਹੀ ਕਦੇ ਉਹ ਝੁਕੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਤੱਕ ਜੱਗ 'ਤੇ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਰਹੇਗਾ,  
> ਇਹ ਗੀਤ ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਰੁਕੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو کدے نہ جھکیا ظالم اگے،  
> نہ ہی کدے اوہ جھکےگا!  
> جد تک جگ تے برہا رہےگا،  
> ایہہ گیت کدے نہ رکےگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv kade na jhukeya zaalam agge,  
> Na hi kade oh jhukega!  
> Jad takk jagg te birha rahega,  
> Eh geet kade na rukega!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv never bowed his neck before the tyrant,  
> Nor shall he ever bend his knee in submission!  
> As long as holy longing walks this mortal earth,  
> This defiant song shall never be silenced!  

💡 *Commentary*: The eternal survival of Birha against censorship.

##### Cultural Glossary

## 16. Kachhi Kandh

### **ਕੱਚੀ ਕੰਧ** • **کچی کندھ**

#### *"The Unbaked Mud Wall"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#MudWall` `#Erosion` `#Fragility` `#Monsoon` `#Promises`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: Human promises are compared to an unbaked village mud wall that melts and crumbles during the first heavy shower.
- **Genesis**: Inspired by the collapsed mud houses of displaced refugees in post-Partition Batala.
- **Archival Context**: A potent architectural metaphor from rural Majha homes, symbolizing the fragility of unwritten human vows. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਡਾ ਪਿਆਰ ਸੀ ਕੱਚੀ ਕੰਧ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਜੋ ਪਹਿਲੇ ਮੀਂਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਢਹਿ ਗਈ ਏ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਇੱਟ ਨਾ ਬਚੀ ਸਲਾਮਤ ਹੁਣ,  
> ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਹੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਰਹਿ ਗਈ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ساڈا پیار سی کچی کندھ وانگوں،  
> جو پہلے مینہہ وچ ڈھہہ گئی اے!  
> کوئی اٹ نہ بچی سلامت ہن،  
> صرف مٹی ہی مٹی رہ گئی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Saada pyaar si kachhi kandh vaangu,  
> Jo pehle meenh vich dheh gayi ae!  
> Koyi itt na bachi salaamat hun,  
> Sirf mitti hi mitti reh gayi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Our love was like an unbaked mud wall,  
> Collapsing in ruins beneath the very first summer shower!  
> Not a single brick remains intact or unbroken;  
> Nothing but desolate mud and wet clay is left behind!  

💡 *Commentary*: The erosion of vulnerable rural relationships under environmental stress.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਿਸ ਕੰਧ ਦੇ ਉਹਲੇ ਬਹਿੰਦੇ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਕੰਧ ਸਾਡੇ 'ਤੇ ਡਿੱਗ ਪਈ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਛਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਆਪਣਾ ਸਮਝਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਛਾਂ ਸਾਡਾ ਸਾਹ ਖੋਹ ਗਈ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جس کندھ دے اوہلے بہندے سی،  
> اوہی کندھ ساڈے تے ڈگ پئی نی!  
> جس چھاں نوں اپنا سمجھیا سی،  
> اوہی چھاں ساڈا ساہ کھوہ گئی نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Jis kandh de ohle behnde si,  
> Uhi kandh saade te digg payi ni!  
> Jis chhaan nu apna samjheya si,  
> Uhi chhaan saada saah khoh gayi ni!  

**English Translation**:
> That very wall beneath whose shelter we once sat  
> Has fallen directly upon our own shoulders!  
> That protective shade which we claimed as our own sanctuary  
> Has crushed the living breath from our body!  

💡 *Commentary*: The refuge transforms into the instrument of destruction.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਨੂੰ ਕੀ ਰੋਣਾ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਜੋ ਧਰਤ 'ਚ ਜਾ ਕੇ ਰਲ਼ ਗਈ ਏ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੀ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਵੀ ਇਸ ਕੰਧ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੀ ਢਲ਼ ਗਈ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن مٹی نوں کی رونا اڑیو،  
> جو دھرتی چ جا کے رل گئی اے!  
> شیو دی زندگی وی اس کندھ وانگوں،  
> ہنجھواں دے وچ ہی ڈھل گئی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun mitti nu ki rona arhiyo,  
> Jo dhart ch jaa ke ral gayi ae!  
> Shiv di zindagi vi iss kandh vaangu,  
> Hanjuwan de vich hi dhal gayi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Why weep over the fallen clay now, dear friends,  
> When it has already dissolved back into the mother earth?  
> Shiv's life, too, like this frail mud wall,

💡 *Commentary*: Organic dissolution into the native soil.

## 17. Dillan De Saudagar

### **ਦਿਲਾਂ ਦੇ ਸੌਦਾਗਰ** • **دلاں دے سوداگر**

- **Theme / Biome**: `modernism` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: In the cynical bazaar of modern society, love and bleeding hearts are weighed and traded like ordinary merchandise.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala as publishers and agents began profiting off Shiv's early verses while leaving him penniless.
- **Archival Context**: Reflects Shiv's first encounters with the commercial publishing world of Lahore and Jalandhar, where poetry was traded as merchandise. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਸ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ 'ਚ ਵੱਸਦੇ ਨੇ ਸੌਦਾਗਰ,

> ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਦੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਤੋਲ ਕੇ ਉਹ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਗ਼ਮ ਦੀਆਂ ਕੀਮਤਾਂ ਧਰਦੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اس شہر چ وسدے نے سوداگر،  
> جو دلاں دا سودا کردے نے!

> ساڈے غم دیاں قیمتاں دھردے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Iss shehar ch vassde ne saudagar,  
> Jo dillan da sauda karde ne!  
> Akhiyan de hanjhu tol ke oh,  
> Saade gham diyan keemataan dharde ne!  

**English Translation**:
> In this modern city dwell cold merchants  
> Who trade in living, beating human hearts!  
> Weighing the tears of our eyes on commercial scales,  
> They affix cynical price tags upon our sacred grief!  

💡 *Commentary*: The commodification of artistic suffering in the capitalist city.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਸੀਂ ਆਪਣਾ ਆਪ ਲੁਟਾ ਬੈਠੇ,

> ਇਸ ਮਤਲਬੀ ਸੰਸਾਰ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اسیں اپنا آپ لٹا بیٹھے،  
> اس جھوٹھے کاروبار دے وچ!  
> سانوں ککھوں ہولے کر دتا،  
> اس مطلبی سنسار دے وچ!  

**Romanized**:
> Assin aapna aap lutaa baithe,  
> Iss jhoothe kaarobaar de vich!  
> Saanu kakhon haule kar ditta,  
> Iss matlabi sansaar de vich!  

**English Translation**:
> We squandered our entire being and identity  
> Within the gears of this fraudulent marketplace!  
> They rendered us lighter than dry straw  
> Inside this ruthless world of selfish exploitation!  

💡 *Commentary*: Total spiritual bankruptcy resulting from uneven commercial exchange.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਪਰ ਇਹ ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਮੁੱਲ,

> تاں ساری دنیا گاؤگی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv kehnda par eh dil da mull,

> Taan saari duniya gaaugi!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv declares: 'No mountain of worldly wealth  
> Can ever purchase the true worth of this heart!  
> When the pen of the poet begins its fiery dance,  
> The whole world shall rise to sing these verses!'  

💡 *Commentary*: Art's transcendent victory over market commercialism.

## 18. Mitti Da Bawa

### **ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦਾ ਬਾਵਾ** • **مٹی دا باوا**

#### *"The Clay Idol That Will Not Speak"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#FolkMyth` `#ClayDoll` `#MittidaBawa`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The beloved is likened to an unhearing, mute clay doll (mitti da bawa) who remains impassive despite tearful prayers and devotion.
- **Genesis**: Shiv took popular folk refrains sung by village women at spinning bees and gave them dark psychological resonance.
- **Archival Context**: Direct adaptation of the ancient Punjabi folk song 'Mitti da bawa nayin bolda', transformed into a modernist meditation on unresponsive love. (*Citation: `AMRITA-1974-NAGMANI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦਾ ਬਾਵਾ ਨਈਂ ਬੋਲਦਾ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਲੱਖ ਵਾਰੀ ਸਦਕੇ ਜਾਵਾਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਸੀਨੇ ਨਾਲ ਲਾ ਕੇ ਰੋਂਦੀ ਰਵਾਂ,  
> ਕਿਵੇਂ ਆਪਣਾ ਹਾਲ ਸੁਣਾਵਾਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> مٹی دا باوا نہیں بولدا،  
> میں لکھ واری صدقے جاواں نی!  
> سینے نال لا کے روندی رواں،  
> کویں اپنا حال سناواں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Mitti da bawa nayin bolda,  
> Main lakkh vaari sadke jaawan ni!  
> Seene naal laa ke rondi rawaan,  
> Kiven apna haal sunaawan ni!  

**English Translation**:
> This idol of clay will not utter a single word,  
> Though a hundred thousand times I sacrifice my soul!  
> Pressing it tightly against my weeping breast,  
> How can I speak the anguish that consumes my life?  

💡 *Commentary*: Reframes the folk lyric of maternal loss into a metaphor for emotionally deadened romantic devotion.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਇਹ ਹੱਸੇ ਨਾ ਇਹ ਰੋਵੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਗੱਲ ਦਿਲ ਦੀ ਫੋਲੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਪੱਥਰਾਂ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਚੁੱਪ ਸਾਧੀ ਏ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਭੇਦ ਅੰਦਰ ਦਾ ਨਾ ਖੋਲ੍ਹੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ ایہ ہسے نہ ایہ رووے،  
> نہ کوئی گل دل دی پھولے نی!  
> پتھراں وانگوں چپ سادھی اے،  
> کوئی بھید اندر دا نہ کھولھے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Na eh hasse na eh rove,  
> Na koyi gall dil di fole ni!  
> Patthraan vaangoo chup saadhi ae,  
> Koyi bhed andar da na khole ni!  

**English Translation**:
> It neither smiles in affection nor weeps in pity,  
> Nor shares the hidden secrets of its silent heart!  
> Maintaining a grave silence like cold river granite,  
> It refuses to unlock the mystery of its soul!  

💡 *Commentary*: The unresponsive lover rendered as an inanimate pagan icon.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜੇ ਮੀਂਹ ਵਰ੍ਹਿਆ ਤਾਂ ਖੁਰ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਇਹ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦਾ ਪਰਾਹੁਣਾ ਵੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਨੈਣਾਂ ਦੇ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਇਹਨੇ ਇੱਕ ਦਿਨ ਰੁੜ੍ਹ ਜਾਣਾ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جے مینہہ ورھیا تاں کھر جاوے گا،  
> ایہ مٹی دا پرہونا وے!  
> میرੇ نیناں دے ہنجھواں وچ،  
> ایہنے اک دن رڑھ جانا وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Je meenh varheya taan khur jaavega,  
> Eh mitti da parahuna ve!  
> Mere nainaan de hanjhuwan vich,  
> Ehne ikk din rurh jaana ve!  

**English Translation**:
> When the heavy monsoon rain falls upon the courtyard,  
> This guest of unbaked clay will dissolve into mud!  
> In the torrential flood of my own streaming tears,  
> It is destined one day to wash away without trace!  

💡 *Commentary*: Fragility of the clay figure: human emotion dissolves the very object it adores.

## 19. Geet Meria Ve

### **ਗੀਤ ਮੇਰਿਆ ਵੇ** • **گیت میریا وے**

> ਸੱਜਣਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿਹੜੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹਿ ਜਾਹ ਵੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਮੇਰੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਲਦਾ,  
> ਉਹਦੇ ਕੰਨੀਂ ਹੌਲੀ ਦੇਣੇ ਕਹਿ ਜਾਹ ਵੇ!

> اوہدے کنیں ہولی دینے کہہ جاہ وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Geet meria ve udd ke jaah tu,  
> Sajjnaan de vehre vich beh jaah ve!  
> Jo dard mere seene vich balda,  
> Ohde kanneen hauli dene keh jaah ve!  

**English Translation**:
> O my song, spread your wings and take flight,  
> Descend softly into the courtyard of my beloved!  
> That burning agony which consumes my breast,  
> Whisper it tenderly into her listening ear!  

💡 *Commentary*: The poem as an animistic, flying messenger bridging geographic estrangement.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜੇ ਉਹ ਪੁੱਛੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਹਾਲ ਕਦੇ,  
> ਤੂੰ ਆਖੀਂ ਉਹ ਤਾਂ ਜਿਊਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਬੱਸ ਤੇਰੇ ਦਿੱਤੇ ਗ਼ਮਾਂ ਦੀ ਮਾਲਾ,  
> ਹਰ ਪਲ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਨਾਲ ਪਰੋਂਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جے اوہ پچھے میرا حال کدے،  
> توں آکھیں اوہ تاں جیوندا اے!  
> بس تیرے دتے غماں دی مالا،  
> ہر پل ہنجھواں نال پروندا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Je oh puchhe mera haal kade,  
> Tu aakheen oh taan jiyunda ae!  
> Bass tere ditte ghamaan di maala,  
> Har pal hanjhuwan naal paronda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> If ever she should inquire of my condition,  
> Answer her gently that he still draws breath!  
> Only that with the dark rosary of sorrows she gifted,  
> He threads unceasing tears with every passing heartbeat!  

💡 *Commentary*: Rosary of sorrows (ghamaan di maala) blends Sufi remembrance with romantic devotion.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜੇ ਉਹ ਸੁਣ ਕੇ ਰੋ ਪਵੇ ਤਾਂ,  
> ਉਹਦੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਚੁੰਮ ਕੇ ਮੁੜ ਆਵੀਂ!  
> ਮੇਰੀ ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਸਨੇਹਾ,  
> ਉਹਦੀ ਝੋਲੀ ਪਾ ਕੇ ਮੁੜ ਆਵੀਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جے اوہ سن کے رو پوے تاں،  
> اوہدے ہنجھو چم کے مڑ آویں!  
> میری موت دا آخری سنیہا،  
> اوہدی جھولی پا کے مڑ آویں!  

**Romanized**:
> Je oh sun ke ro pave taan,  
> Ohde hanjhu chumm ke murh aaveen!  
> Meri maut da aakhiri suneha,  
> Ohdi jholi paa ke murh aaveen!  

**English Translation**:
> And if she should weep upon hearing your melody,  
> Kiss her falling tears and return swiftly to me!  
> Placing the final testament of my impending death  
> Reverently into her lap before you take your leave!  

💡 *Commentary*: Placing grief in the lap (*jholi*) evokes ancient Punjabi blessings and sacrifices.

## 20. Dukh Paradesan De

### **ਦੁੱਖ ਪ੍ਰਦੇਸਾਂ ਦੇ** • **دکھ پردیساں دے**

#### *"The Sorrows of Homeless Exile"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#Pardes` `#Exile` `#Wanderer`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The traveler laments the cruel indifference of foreign lands (*pardes*), where nobody recognizes the sorrow in a stranger's eyes.
- **Genesis**: Shiv lived briefly in Nabha, Baijnath, and Chandigarh, continually aching for the familiar village soil of Majha.
- **Archival Context**: Documents Shiv's emotional displacement when working in clerkships away from his home district of Gurdaspur. (*Citation: `SHARMA-1979-SOLITARY`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਦੁੱਖ ਪ੍ਰਦੇਸਾਂ ਦੇ ਬੜੇ ਔਖੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਆਪਣਾ ਨਾ ਬਿਗਾਨਾ ਵੇ!  
> ਸਾਰਾ ਦਿਨ ਮੈਂ ਧੁੱਪੇ ਸੜਦਾ,  
> ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਸੁੰਞਾ ਏ ਠਿਕਾਣਾ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> دکھ پردیساں دے بڑے اوکھے،  
> نہ کوئی اپنا نہ بیگانہ وے!  
> سارا دن میں دھپے سڑدا،  
> رات نوں سنجا اے ٹھکانہ وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Dukh paradesan de barhe aukhe,  
> Na koyi apna na bigaana ve!  
> Saara din main dhuppe sarhda,  
> Raat nu sunja ae thikaana ve!  

**English Translation**:
> Unbearable are the afflictions of foreign exile,  
> Where none is kin nor even a familiar stranger!  
> All day long I scorch beneath an unfeeling sun,  
> While night brings only an empty, desolate resting place!  

💡 *Commentary*: Classic Punjabi folklore trope of Pardes (foreign land) as emotional death.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇੱਥੋਂ ਦੇ ਲੋਕੀਂ ਪੱਥਰ ਦਿਲ ਨੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਦਾ ਮੁੱਲ ਨਾ ਜਾਣੇ!  
> ਆਪਣੇ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਮਿੱਠੀ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਰੋਂਦੇ ਸੀ ਹਾਣ-ਪ੍ਰਵਾਣੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ایتھوں دے لوکی پتھر دل نے،  
> کوئی ہنجھواں دا مل نہ جانے!  
> اپنے پنڈ دی مٹی مٹھی،  
> جتھے روندے سی ہان پروانے!  

**Romanized**:
> Itthon de loki patthar dil ne,  
> Koyi hanjhuwan da mull na jaane!  
> Apne pind di mitti mitthi,  
> Jithe ronde si haan-parwaane!  

**English Translation**:
> The inhabitants of this distant city possess hearts of stone,  
> None among them can appraise the worth of a tear!  
> Sweet beyond measure was the soil of my native village,  
> Where childhood companions wept together in shared grief!  

💡 *Commentary*: Contrasts urban emotional sterility with the communal empathy of the ancestral village.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਾਸ਼ ਮੈਂ ਪੰਛੀ ਬਣ ਕੇ ਉੱਡਾਂ,  
> ਮੁੜ ਜਾਵਾਂ ਉਸ ਦੇਸ ਨਿਆਰੇ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਮਾਂ ਦੀ ਗੋਦੀ,  
> ਤੇ ਸੱਜਣਾਂ ਦੇ ਨੈਣ ਕਜਾਰੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کاش میں پنچھی بن کے اڈاں،  
> مڑ جاواں اس دیس نیارے!  
> جتھے میری ماں دی گودی،  
> تے سجناں دے نین کجارے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kaash main panchhi ban ke uddaan,  
> Murh jaawaan oss des nyaare!  
> Jithe meri maan di godi,  
> Te sajjnaan de nain kajaare!  

**English Translation**:
> Would that I could transform into a bird and take wing,  
> Returning forever to that wondrous, sacred land!  
> Where waits the warm, forgiving lap of my mother,  
> And the kohl-lined eyes of my lost beloved!  

💡 *Commentary*: Evokes the maternal lap and kohl eyes as the twin sanctuaries of Punjab.

## 21. Chambe Di Booti

### **ਚੰਬੇ ਦੀ ਬੂਟੀ** • **چنبے دی بوٹی**

#### *"Jasmine Plant in My Breast"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#SufiSymbolism` `#SultanBahu` `#Chamba`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The plant of jasmine (white unrequited love) rooted deep within the poet's breast grows fragrant on the nourishment of tears.
- **Genesis**: Shiv merges medieval Sufi allegory with twentieth-century romantic lyricism.
- **Archival Context**: Direct homage to Sufi master Sultan Bahu's famous Abyat: 'Alif Allah chambe di booti murshid mann vich laai hoo'. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੇਰੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਚੰਬੇ ਦੀ ਬੂਟੀ,  
> ਇਸ਼ਕ ਤੇਰੇ ਨੇ ਲਾਈ ਵੇ!  
> ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਦਾ ਮੈਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਪਾਇਆ,  
> ਜਦੋਂ ਖ਼ਿਜ਼ਾਂ ਦੀ ਰੁੱਤ ਆਈ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میرے سینے وچ چنبے دی بوٹی،  
> عشق تیرے نے لائی وے!  
> ہنجھواں دا میں پانی پایا،  
> جدوں خزاں دی رت آئی وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Mere seene vich chambe di booti,  
> Ishq tere ne laayi ve!  
> Hanjhuwan da main paani paaya,  
> Jadon khizaan di rutt aayi ve!  

**English Translation**:
> A fragile plant of white jasmine  
> Your unsparing love planted within my breast!  
> With the salt water of my weeping eyes I nourished its root  
> When the chilling season of autumn stripped the boughs!  

💡 *Commentary*: Direct dialogue with Sultan Bahu's Sufi imagery of the spiritual plant.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਸ ਬੂਟੀ ਨੂੰ ਫੁੱਲ ਜੋ ਲੱਗੇ,  
> ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਰੰਗ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੰਗੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਜਾਣੇ ਇਹ ਮਹਿਕ ਉਦਾਸੀ,  
> ਜੋ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮਾਂ ਤੋਂ ਮੰਗੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اس بوٹی نوں پھل جو لگے،  
> برہا دے رنگ وچ رنگے نے!  
> کوئی نہ جانے ایہ مہک اداسی،  
> جو دل دے زخماں توں منگے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Iss booti nu phull jo lagge,  
> Birha de rang vich range ne!  
> Koyi na jaane eh mehek udaasi,  
> Jo dil de zakhmaan ton mange ne!  

**English Translation**:
> The delicate blossoms that sprouted upon this stem  
> Are dyed in the deep, somber hues of separation!  
> None in this indifferent world discerns that sad perfume  
> Extracted from the open wounds of my broken heart!  

💡 *Commentary*: Fragrance of jasmine equated with distilled grief.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਦ ਮੇਰੀ ਜਿੰਦ ਮੁੱਕ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਇਹ ਬੂਟੀ ਕਬਰ ਤੇ ਖਿੜੇਗੀ ਵੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਮਰਨ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਵੀ ਸੱਜਣਾ,  
> ਤੇਰੇ ਪੈਰਾਂ ਹੇਠਾਂ ਵਿਛੇਗੀ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جد میری جند مک جاوے گی،  
> ایہ بوٹی قبر تے کھڑے گی وے!  
> میرے مرن توں بعد وی سجنا،  
> تیرے پیراں ہیٹھاں وچھے گی وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Jad meri jind mukk jaavegi,  
> Eh booti kabr te khirhegi ve!  
> Mere maran ton baad vi sajjna,  
> Tere pairaan hethaan vichhegi ve!  

**English Translation**:
> When this weary thread of life snaps at last,  
> This jasmine plant shall blossom above my quiet tomb!  
> Even long after I am dust, O unyielding beloved,  
> Its fallen petals will carpet the path beneath your feet!  

💡 *Commentary*: Post-mortem devotion: unrequited love extending past cremation into vegetative rebirth.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਚੰਬਾ (Chamba)** | *Cham-ba* | White Jasmine (Jasminum grandiflorum) | Sacred flower in Punjabi Sufi poetry symbolizing pure, unblemished spiritual devotion. |

## 22. Kothe Te Kankan

### **ਕੋਠੇ ਤੇ ਕਣਕਾਂ** • **کوٹھے تے کنکاں**

#### *"Wheat Drying on the Mud Rooftop"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#AgrarianDomestic` `#Rooftop` `#Harvest`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: A young bride spreads wheat grains upon the mud rooftop under the midday sun, weeping as pigeons peck away her youth.
- **Genesis**: Shiv observed village women singing mournful verses on rooftops in the afternoon quiet.
- **Archival Context**: Depicts the domestic chore of village women spreading harvested wheat to dry before milling. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੋਠੇ ਤੇ ਕਣਕਾਂ ਸੁੱਕਣੇ ਪਾਈਆਂ,  
> ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਝੜੀਆਂ ਕਣੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਕਿਸ ਨੂੰ ਦੱਸਾਂ ਆਪਣੇ ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਦੁੱਖੜਾ,  
> ਜੋ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਿਰ ਤੇ ਬਣੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کوٹھے تے کنکاں سکنے پائیاں،  
> اکھیاں وچوں جھڑیاں کنیاں نی!  
> کس نوں دساں اپنے دل دا دکھڑا،  
> جو میرے سر تے بنیاں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Kothe te kankan sukkne paaiyaan,  
> Akhiyaan vichon jharhiyaan kaniyaan ni!  
> Kis nu dassaan apne dil da dukhrha,  
> Jo mere sir te baniyaan ni!  

**English Translation**:
> I spread the golden wheat upon the flat mud roof to dry,  
> While from my brimming eyes fell drops like bitter rain!  
> To whom can I confess the affliction of my wounded heart,  
> And the heavy trials that have gathered upon my head?  

💡 *Commentary*: Wheat drying in the sun contrasted with tears raining down.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਚਿੜੀਆਂ ਆਈਆਂ ਚੋਗ ਚੁਗਣ ਲਈ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਜੋਬਨ ਖਾ ਗਈਆਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਹਾਸਿਆਂ ਨਾਲ ਸੀ ਵਿਹੜਾ ਗੂੰਜਦਾ,  
> ਉਹ ਸਭ ਰੋਣੇ ਪਾ ਗਈਆਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> چڑیاں آئیاں چوگ چگن لئی،  
> میرا جوبن کھا گئیاں نی!  
> جنہاں ہاسیاں نال سی ویہڑا گونجدا،  
> اوہ سبھ رونے پا گئیاں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Chiriyaan aaiyaan chog chugan layi,  
> Mera joban khaa gaiyaan ni!  
> Jinhaan haasiyaan naal si vehra goonjda,  
> Oh sabh rone paa gaiyaan ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Flocks of hungry sparrows arrived to peck the grain,  
> And pecked away the prime of my blooming youth!  
> Those sweet laughter that once resonated across the courtyard  
> Have dissolved into unceasing funeral wails!  

💡 *Commentary*: Grain pecking becomes an animistic metaphor for youth being devoured by time.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਾਮ ਢਲੀ ਤੇ ਕਣਕ ਸਮੇਟੀ,  
> ਦਰਦ ਨਾ ਪਰ ਸਮੇਟਿਆ ਜਾਵੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਇਸ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੀ ਅੱਗ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੜਦੀ ਜਿੰਦ,  
> ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਬੁਝਣੇ ਪਾਵੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شام ڈھلی تے کنک سمیٹی،  
> درد نہ پر سمیٹیا جاوے نی!  
> اس برہا دی اگ وچ سڑدی جند،  
> کدے نہ بجھنے پاوے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shaam dhali te kanak sameti,  
> Dard na par sametiya jaave ni!  
> Iss birha di agg vich sarhdi jind,  
> Kade na bujhne paave ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Dusk descended and I gathered the dried wheat in my skirt,  
> Yet the boundless agony of my heart could not be gathered!  
> This fragile life burning in the furnace of separation  
> Finds no water to quench its consuming flame!  

💡 *Commentary*: Domestic gathering (sametna) contrasted with uncontainable grief.

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਕੋਠਾ (Kotha)** | *Ko-tha* | Flat mud rooftop / Terrace | Traditional flat clay roof of Punjabi mud houses used for drying grains and private evening contemplation. |

## 23. Shokh Husan

### **ਸ਼ੋਖ਼ ਹੁਸਨ** • **شوخ حسن**

#### *"Restless Beauty Fleeting Away"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#Beauty` `#Ephemeral` `#Youth`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: Youthful beauty is celebrated as a restless, elusive flame that dazzles the world for an instant before vanishing into ash.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's reflection on the vanity of physical allure and the inevitability of decay.
- **Archival Context**: Examines the transitory nature of physical beauty, a classic romantic trope Shiv inherited from Urdu ghazals. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ੋਖ਼ ਹੁਸਨ ਤੇਰਾ ਬਿਜਲੀ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਇੱਕ ਪਲ ਚਮਕਿਆ ਅੰਬਰ ਤੇ!  
> ਫਿਰ ਕਾਲੀਆਂ ਘਟਾਵਾਂ ਘਿਰ ਆਈਆਂ,  
> ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਦੇ ਮੰਦਰ ਤੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شوخ حسن تیرا بجلی وانگوں،  
> اک پل چمکیا انبر تے!  
> پھر کالیاں گھٹاواں گھر آئیاں،  
> اس دنیا دے مندر تے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shokh husan tera bijli vaangoo,  
> Ikk pal chamkeya ambar te!  
> Phir kaaliyaan ghataawaan ghir aaiyaan,  
> Iss duniya de mandar te!  

**English Translation**:
> Your restless, incandescent beauty like lightning  
> Flashed for a blinding instant across the firmament!  
> Then pitch-black thunderclouds gathered anew  
> Above the solemn sanctuary of this worldly realm!  

💡 *Commentary*: Lightning (bijli) as the ultimate trope of dazzling yet momentary splendor.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੌਣ ਸੰਭਾਲੇ ਇਸ ਚੰਚਲਤਾ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਕੌਣ ਇਹਦਾ ਇਤਬਾਰ ਕਰੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਪਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਆਵੇ ਤੇ ਪਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਜਾਵੇ,  
> ਉਸ ਉੱਤੇ ਕੌਣ ਨਿਸਾਰ ਕਰੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کون سنبھالے اس چنچلتا نوں،  
> کون ایہدا اعتبار کرے!  
> جو پل وچ آوے تے پل وچ جاوے،  
> اس اتے کون نثار کرے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kaun sambhaale iss chanchalta nu,  
> Kaun ehda aitbaar kare!  
> Jo pal vich aave te pal vich jaave,  
> Uss utte kaun nisaar kare!  

**English Translation**:
> Who can capture this elusive, fluttering charm?  
> Who can place their trembling faith in its pledge?  
> That which arrives in a breath and departs in the next,  
> Who would sacrifice their immortal soul upon its altar?  

💡 *Commentary*: The danger of anchoring existential hopes in fleeting youth.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਸਨ ਵੀ ਇੱਕ ਦਿਨ ਢਲ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਜਿਵੇਂ ਫੁੱਲ ਧਰਤ ਤੇ ਡਿੱਗਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਬੱਸ ਗੀਤ ਹੀ ਬਚਣਗੇ ਆਸ਼ਿਕ ਦੇ,  
> ਜੋ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੁਲਗਦੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> حسن وی اک دن ڈھل جاوے گا،  
> جیویں پھل دھرتی تے ڈگدے نے!  
> بس گیت ہی بچن گے عاشق دے،  
> جو برہا دے وچ سلگدے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Husan vi ikk din dhal jaavega,  
> Jiven phull dhart te diggde ne!  
> Bass geet hi bachange aashiq de,  
> Jo birha de vich sulagde ne!  

**English Translation**:
> Even radiant beauty must decline one fateful day,  
> Just as withered blossoms collapse upon the dust!  
> Only the burning songs of the grieving lover will endure,  
> Smoldering forever in the eternal embers of separation!  

💡 *Commentary*: Art outlasts physical form: only poetry survives the grave.

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਸ਼ੋਖ਼ (Shokh)** | *Shokh* | Restless / Playful / Vivid | Loan word from Persian-Urdu poetry describing quick, vibrant, and teasing youthful beauty. |

## 24. Nain Tarasde

### **ਨੈਣ ਤਰਸਦੇ** • **نین ترسدے**

#### *"Eyes Starving for a Single Glimpse"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#Gaze` `#Tears` `#Yearning`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The eyes of the waiting lover have turned dry and hollow with decades of staring down the desolate highway.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala during the long winter nights of 1960.
- **Archival Context**: Features Shiv's mastery of ocular metaphors, a trademark of his poetry noted by Amrita Pritam. (*Citation: `AMRITA-1974-NAGMANI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨੈਣ ਤਰਸਦੇ ਦੀਦਾਰ ਨੂੰ ਤੇਰੇ,  
> ਸੁੱਕ ਗਈਆਂ ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਦੀਆਂ ਧਾਰਾਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਕਦੋਂ ਮੁੜੇਂਗਾ ਸਾਡੇ ਵਿਹੜੇ,  
> ਲੰਘ ਗਈਆਂ ਕਈ ਬਹਾਰਾਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نین ترسدے دیدار نوں تیرے،  
> سک گئیاں اکھیاں دیاں دھاراں نی!  
> کدوں مڑیں گا ساڈے ویہڑے،  
> لنگھ گئیاں کئی بہاراں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Nain tarasde deedaar nu tere,  
> Sukk gaiyaan akhiyaan diyan dhaaraan ni!  
> Kadon murenge saade vehre,  
> Langh gaiyaan kayi bahaaraan ni!  

**English Translation**:
> My starving eyes crave a single glimpse of your face,  
> The flowing torrents of my tears have run dry to bone!  
> When will you return to our desolate courtyard?  
> Countless springs have blossomed and withered away!  

💡 *Commentary*: The drying up of tears represents the transition from fresh grief to chronic desolation.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਰਾਹਾਂ ਉੱਤੇ ਧੂੜਾਂ ਉੱਡਦੀਆਂ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਪੈਰ ਨਾ ਪਿੰਡ ਨੂੰ ਆਵੇ ਵੇ!  
> ਹਰ ਆਹਟ ਤੇ ਦਿਲ ਧੜਕਦਾ ਏ,  
> ਪਰ ਹਵਾ ਹੀ ਬੂਹਾ ਖੜਕਾਵੇ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> راہاں اتے دھوڑاں اڈدیاں،  
> کوئی پیر نہ پنڈ نوں آوے وے!  
> ہر آہٹ تے دل دھڑکدا اے،  
> پر ہوا ہی بوہا کھڑکاوے وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Raahaan utte dhooraan udd-diyaan,  
> Koyi pair na pind nu aave ve!  
> Har aahat te dil dharakda ae,  
> Par hawa hi booha kharkave ve!  

**English Translation**:
> Clouds of dry dust whirl along the abandoned highway,  
> Not a single traveler approaches the quiet village!  
> At every whisper of a rustling leaf my heart leaps in terror,  
> Only to find the cruel wind rattling the empty door!  

💡 *Commentary*: Auditory illusions created by intense longing.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਤਾਂ ਇਹ ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਮੀਚਣੀਆਂ ਨੇ,  
> ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਸੁਰਮਾ ਪਾ ਕੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੁੱਕ ਜਾਣਾ ਇਹ ਤਾਂਘ ਦਾ ਸਫ਼ਰ,  
> ਤੇਰੀ ਯਾਦ ਸੀਨੇ ਨਾਲ ਲਾ ਕੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن تاں ایہ اکھیاں میچنیاں نے،  
> موت دا سورمہ پا کے نی!  
> مک جانا ایہ تانگھ دا سفر،  
> تیری یاد سینے نال لا کے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun taan eh akhiyaan meechniyaan ne,  
> Maut da soorma paa ke ni!  
> Mukk jaana eh taangh da safar,  
> Teri yaad seene naal laa ke ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Now these starving eyes must close forever,  
> Lined with the dark, terminal kohl of Death!  
> The long, agonizing pilgrimage of longing shall end,  
> Pressing your sacred memory against my still heart!  

💡 *Commentary*: Kohl of death (*maut da soorma*) symbolizes the peaceful blindness of the grave.

## 25. Pinjra Te Panchhi

### **ਪਿੰਜਰਾ ਤੇ ਪੰਛੀ** • **پنجرہ تے پنچھی**

#### *"The Caged Sparrow"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#Captivity` `#FemaleEnclosure` `#Pardah`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The caged bird's wings have withered from long confinement; even when the cage door is opened, it lacks the will to take flight.
- **Genesis**: Early thematic precursor to 'Loona', examining female emotional captivity in domestic structures.
- **Archival Context**: Reflects the psychological conditioning of Punjabi women under feudal purdah, unable to fly even when granted freedom. (*Citation: `SAHITYA-1967-LOONA`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸੋਨੇ ਦੇ ਪਿੰਜਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਬੈਠਾ ਏ ਪੰਛੀ,  
> ਗੀਤ ਗਾਵੇ ਪਰ ਰੋ ਰੋ ਕੇ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਸਮਝੇ ਇਹਦੀ ਬੋਲੀ,  
> ਕਿਵੇਂ ਸੁਣਾਵੇ ਦਰਦ ਢੋ ਢੋ ਕੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> سونے دے پنجرے وچ بیٹھا اے پنچھی،  
> گیت گاوے پر رو رو کے!  
> کوئی نہ سمجھے ایہدی بولی،  
> کویں سناوے درد ڈھو ڈھو کے!  

**Romanized**:
> Sone de pinjre vich baitha ae panchhi,  
> Geet gaave par ro ro ke!  
> Koyi na samjhe ehdi boli,  
> Kiven sunaave dard dho dho ke!  

**English Translation**:
> Confined within a gilded golden cage perches the bird,  
> Singing melodious songs through streaming tears!  
> Not a soul in the courtyard comprehends its foreign tongue,  
> Nor feels the crushing weight of the sorrow it carries!  

💡 *Commentary*: The golden cage (sone da pinjra) represents wealthy, oppressive domestic matrimony.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨੀਲੇ ਅੰਬਰ ਦੀ ਤਾਂਘ ਸੀ ਇਹਨੂੰ,  
> ਖੰਭ ਸੜ ਗਏ ਕੈਦਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਨੀ!  
> ਹੁਣ ਜੇ ਬੂਹਾ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹ ਵੀ ਜਾਵੇ,  
> ਉੱਡਣ ਦੀ ਤਾਕਤ ਨਾ ਰਹੀ ਪਿੰਡ ਵਿੱਚ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نیلے انبر دی تانگھ سی ایہنوں،  
> کھنبھ سڑ گئے قیداں وچ نی!  
> ہن جے بوہا کھلھ وی جاوے،  
> اڈن دی طاقت نہ رہی پنڈ وچ نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Neele ambar di taangh si ehnu,  
> Khambh sarh gaye kaidaan vich ni!  
> Hun je booha khullh vi jaave,  
> Uddan di taakat na rahi pind vich ni!  

**English Translation**:
> It once yearned for the boundless expanse of the blue sky,  
> Yet its wings have withered away in prolonged confinement!  
> Now even if the cage door should be flung wide open,  
> No strength remains in its body to take flight across the fields!  

💡 *Commentary*: Psychological adaptation to captivity: internalizing one's own cage.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੌਤ ਹੀ ਆ ਕੇ ਇਸ ਪੰਛੀ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਆਜ਼ਾਦ ਕਰੇਗੀ ਪਿੰਜਰੇ 'ਚੋਂ!  
> ਫਿਰ ਇਹਦੀ ਰੂਹ ਉੱਡ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਇਸ ਝੂਠੇ ਸੰਸਾਰ ਦੇ ਘੇਰੇ 'ਚੋਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> موت ہی آ کے اس پنچھی نوں،  
> آزاد کرے گی پنجرے چوں!  
> پھر ایہدی روح اڈ جاوے گی،  
> اس جھوٹھے سنسار دے گھیرے چوں!  

**Romanized**:
> Maut hi aa ke iss panchhi nu,  
> Aazaad karegi pinjre chon!  
> Phir ehdi rooh udd jaavegi,  
> Iss jhoothe sansaar de ghere chon!  

**English Translation**:
> Only Death will arrive with gentle hands  
> To emancipate this weary captive from its gilded prison!  
> And then its liberated spirit shall soar forever  
> Beyond the suffocating perimeter of this false universe!  

💡 *Commentary*: Death as the only true release from social captivity.

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਪਿੰਜਰਾ (Pinjra)** | *Pinj-ra* | Bird cage | Metaphor for domestic enclosure, arranged marriage, and female disempowerment in rural Punjab. |

## 26. Kachhi Kandh Te Deeva

### **ਕੱਚੀ ਕੰਧ ਤੇ ਦੀਵਾ** • **کچی کندھ تے دیوا**

#### *"Lamp upon the Earthen Wall"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#ClayLamp` `#Wind` `#Fragility`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: A small earthen lamp flickers bravely on a fragile mud wall, trembling at every gust of wind like a human soul awaiting collapse.
- **Genesis**: Shiv identified deeply with the precarious existence of poor village mud homes during monsoon floods.
- **Archival Context**: Employs the quintessential Punjabi image of the earthen lamp placed in a wall niche (*aala*). (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੱਚੀ ਕੰਧ ਤੇ ਦੀਵਾ ਬਲਦਾ,  
> ਹਵਾ ਦੇ ਬੁੱਲ੍ਹੇ ਡਰਾਉਂਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੀ ਧੜਕਣ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਕੰਬਦੇ ਸਾਹ ਘਬਰਾਉਂਦੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کچی کندھ تے دیوا بلدا،  
> ہوا دے بلھے ڈراؤندے نے!  
> میرے دل دی دھڑکن وانگوں،  
> کنبدے ساہ گھبراؤندے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kachhi kandh te deeva balda,  
> Hawa de bullhe daraaunde ne!  
> Mere dil di dharhkan vaangoo,  
> Kambde saah ghabraaunde ne!  

**English Translation**:
> A fragile lamp burns upon the unbaked earthen wall,  
> Terrified by every gust of approaching night wind!  
> Just like the frantic palpitation of my heart,  
> Its trembling breath flutters in dread of annihilation!  

💡 *Commentary*: The flickering flame mirrors the fragile human pulse.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਤੇਲ ਵੀ ਮੁੱਕਣ ਵਾਲਾ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਬੱਤੀ ਵੀ ਸੜ ਗਈ ਕਾਲੀ ਨੀ!  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾ ਆ ਕੇ ਓਟ ਬਣਾਈ,  
> ਨਾ ਕਿਸੇ ਨੇ ਸਾਰ ਸੰਭਾਲੀ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> تیل وی مکن والا ہویا،  
> بتی وی سڑ گئی کالی نی!  
> کسے نہ آ کے اوٹ بنائی،  
> نہ کسے نے سار سنبھالی نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Tel vi mukkan vaala hoyea,  
> Batti vi sarh gayi kaali ni!  
> Kise na aa ke oat banaayi,  
> Na kise ne saar sambhaali ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The small saucer of oil is nearly exhausted,  
> The cotton wick has charred black and brittle!  
> No passerby stepped forward to shield the flame with their palm,  
> Nor did anyone care to replenish its dwindling light!  

💡 *Commentary*: Lack of shelter (oat) as societal neglect of the sensitive artist.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਦੋਂ ਇਹ ਕੰਧ ਮੀਂਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਢਹੇਗੀ,  
> ਦੀਵਾ ਵੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਹੋ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਇਹਦਾ ਨਾਂ-ਨਿਸ਼ਾਨ ਵੀ,  
> ਹਨੇਰੀ ਰਾਤ 'ਚ ਖੋ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جدوں ایہ کندھ مینہہ وچ ڈھے گی،  
> دیوا وی مٹی ہو جاوے گا!  
> میرے وانگوں ایہدا ناں نشان وی،  
> ہنیری رات چ کھو جاوے گا!  

**Romanized**:
> Jadon eh kandh meenh vich dhehegi,  
> Deeva vi mitti ho jaavega!  
> Mere vaangoo ehda naan-nishaan vi,  
> Haneri raat ch kho jaavega!  

**English Translation**:
> When this fragile mud wall collapses in the monsoon deluge,  
> The earthen lamp too will dissolve into nameless clay!  
> Just like my own forgotten existence in this world,  
> Its memory will be swallowed up by the boundless dark!  

💡 *Commentary*: Double dissolution: both the supporting wall and the lamp dissolve into identical mud.

|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਆਲ੍ਹਾ (Aala)** | *Aa-la* | Niche carved into a mud wall | Small recessed alcove in village homes designed to hold clay oil lamps away from drafts. |

## 27. Sohne Rang Da Geet

### **ਸੋਹਣੇ ਰੰਗ ਦਾ ਗੀਤ** • **سوہنے رنگ دا گیت**

#### *"Song of Beautiful Hues"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#LyricMelody` `#FolkSinger` `#TragicSong`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The poet weaves a song of breathtaking musical color, only to discover that the truest dye is the dark crimson of blood.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's meditation on his own craft: that great lyrical beauty requires the sacrifice of the singer's vitality.
- **Archival Context**: Shiv composed this after listening to wandering Mirasi folk bards singing Heer at a Batala shrine. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸੋਹਣੇ ਰੰਗ ਦਾ ਗੀਤ ਬਣਾਇਆ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣੇ ਲਹੂ ਨੂੰ ਘੋਲ ਕੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਸਮਝੇ ਇਹ ਤਾਂ ਮੌਜ ਦੀ ਗੱਲ ਏ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਗਾਇਆ ਦਿਲ ਨੂੰ ਫੋਲ ਕੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> سوہنے رنگ دا گیت بنایا،  
> میں اپنے لہو نوں گھول کے نی!  
> دنیا سمجھے ایہ تاں موج دی گل اے،  
> میں گایا دل نوں پھول کے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Sohne rang da geet banaaya,  
> Main apne lahoo nu ghol ke ni!  
> Duniya samjhe eh taan mauj di gall ae,  
> Main gaaya dil nu fole ke ni!  

**English Translation**:
> I crafted a song of breathtaking, radiant color  
> By dissolving my own warm blood into the verse!  
> The callous world assumed it was a merry pastime,  
> While I sang having flayed open the depths of my soul!  

💡 *Commentary*: Metaphor of dissolving blood (lahoo ghol ke) to create art.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸੁਰਾਂ ਤਾਂ ਸਭ ਨੇ ਸੁਣ ਲਈਆਂ ਨੇ,  
> ਪਰ ਦਰਦ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾ ਵੇਖਿਆ ਵੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਅੱਗ ਮੇਰੇ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਲਦੀ,  
> ਉਸ ਨੂੰ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾ ਸੇਕਿਆ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> سراں تاں سبھ نے سن لئیاں نے،  
> پر درد کسے نہ ویکھیا وے!  
> جو اگ میرے ہنجھواں وچ بلدی،  
> اس نوں کسے نہ سیکیا وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Suraan taan sabh ne sun laiyaan ne,  
> Par dard kise na vekheya ve!  
> Jo agg mere hanjhuwan vich baldi,  
> Uss nu kise na sekeya ve!  

**English Translation**:
> All ears readily drunk the intoxicating melody,  
> Yet not a single soul perceived the underlying agony!  
> The wild fire that raged within my weeping tears  
> Remained unacknowledged by the cheering listeners!  

💡 *Commentary*: The tragedy of the popular singer: audiences applaud the beauty of the wound.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਹ ਗੀਤ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਰਨ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਵੀ,  
> ਹਵਾਵਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੂੰਜਣਗੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਅੱਜ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਕਮਲਾ ਆਖਦੇ ਨੇ,  
> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੇਰੀ ਕਬਰ ਨੂੰ ਚੁੰਮਣਗੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ایہ گیت میرے مرن توں بعد وی،  
> ہواواں وچ گونجن گے نی!  
> جو اج مینوں کملا آکھدے نے،  
> کلھ میری قبر نوں چمن گے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Eh geet mere maran ton baad vi,  
> Hawaawan vich goonjange ni!  
> Jo ajj mainu kamla aakhde ne,  
> Kallh meri kabr nu chummange ni!  

**English Translation**:
> These songs long after my physical departure  
> Shall continue to reverberate through the winds of Punjab!  
> Those who mock me today as a madman lost in despair  
> Will tomorrow kneel to kiss the soil of my silent grave!  

💡 *Commentary*: Prophetic declaration of post-mortem vindication.

## 28. Kujh Kadam Hor

### **ਕੁਝ ਕਦਮ ਹੋਰ** • **کجھ قدم ہور**

#### *"A Few Steps More"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#Journey` `#Weariness` `#TerminalPath`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The exhausted wanderer urges his failing feet to walk just a few steps more until the sanctuary of the grave is reached.
- **Genesis**: Composed during Shiv's trek through the Shivalik foothills near Baijnath in 1960.
- **Archival Context**: Reflects the weary wanderlust of young Shiv during his solitary travels through Kangra and Dalhousie. (*Citation: `SHARMA-1979-SOLITARY`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੁਝ ਕਦਮ ਹੋਰ ਚੱਲ ਓ ਜਿੰਦੇ,  
> ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ ਆਉਣ ਵਾਲੀ ਏ!  
> ਇਸ ਲੰਮੇ ਦਰਦ ਦੇ ਸਫ਼ਰ ਦੀ,  
> ਸ਼ਾਮ ਢਲਣ ਵਾਲੀ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کجھ قدم ہور چل او جندے،  
> منزل آؤن والی اے!  
> اس لمبے درد دے سفر دی،  
> شام ڈھلن والی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kujh kadam hor chal o jinde,  
> Manzil aaon vaali ae!  
> Iss lamme dard de safar di,  
> Shaam dhalan vaali ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Walk just a few paces more, O weary soul,  
> The final destination is nearly in sight!  
> Upon this unending, agonizing pilgrimage  
> The ultimate dusk is preparing to fall!  

💡 *Commentary*: Address to the weary self (jind) urging endurance until the release of death.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪੈਰਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਛਾਲੇ ਪੈ ਗਏ ਨੇ,  
> ਕੰਡਿਆਂ ਨੇ ਲਹੂ-ਲੁਹਾਣ ਕੀਤਾ ਏ!  
> ਪਰ ਇਸ ਬੇਵਫ਼ਾ ਜਹਾਨ ਨੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਰਹਿਮ ਨਾ ਸਾਡੇ ਤੇ ਕੀਤਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پیراں وچ چھالے پے گئے نے،  
> کنڈیاں نے لہو لہان کیتا اے!  
> پر اس بےوفا جہان نے،  
> کوئی رحم نہ ساڈے تے کیتا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Pairaan vich chhaale pai gaye ne,  
> Kandiyaan ne lahoo-luhaan keeta ae!  
> Par iss bewafa jahaan ne,  
> Koyi reham na saade te keeta ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Painful blisters have erupted beneath our soles,  
> Sharp briars have stained our footsteps in blood!  
> Yet this treacherous and uncaring world  
> Refused to bestow a single grain of mercy!  

💡 *Commentary*: Physical exhaustion mirroring moral desolation.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਉਹ ਸਾਹਮਣੇ ਕਬਰਿਸਤਾਨ ਦਿਸਦਾ ਏ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਚੁੱਪ ਦੀ ਚਾਦਰ ਤਣੀ ਹੋਈ ਏ!  
> ਉੱਥੇ ਜਾ ਕੇ ਸੌਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ ਅਸੀਂ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਸਾਡੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਬਣੀ ਹੋਈ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اوہ ساہمنے قبرستان دسدا اے،  
> جتھے چپ دی چادر تنی ہوئی اے!  
> ایتھے جا کے سون جاواں گے اسیں،  
> جتھے ساڈی مٹی بنی ہوئی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Oh saahmne kabristaan disda ae,  
> Jithe chup di chaadar tani hoyi ae!  
> Utthe jaa ke saun jaawaange aseen,  
> Jithe saadi mitti bani hoyi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Look yonder: the quiet graveyard comes into view,  
> Where the white shroud of silence is peacefully draped!  
> There we shall lay our head to sleep at last,  
> Rejoining the cool earth from which our flesh was made!  

💡 *Commentary*: The graveyard viewed not as terrifying horror, but as the peaceful, welcoming home.
