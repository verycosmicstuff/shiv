# Mainu Tera Shabab Lai Baitha

### **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਤੇਰਾ ਸ਼ਬਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ** • **مینوں تیرا شباب لے بیٹھا**

#### *"The Splendor of Your Youth Ruined Me"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Dardmandan Diyan Aahan (1964)](../06_dardmandan_diyan_aahan_1964.md)
- **Original Title**: ਮੈਨੂੰ ਤੇਰਾ ਸ਼ਬਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ (مینوں تیرا شباب لے بیٹھا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1964
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Ghazal` `#Shabab` `#Wine` `#Intoxication` `#Masterpiece`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: Classic ghazal charting the poet's complete self-surrender to the beloved's intoxicating beauty and his alcoholic descent in the taverns of Chandigarh.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed during Shiv's bohemian years in Sector 17, Chandigarh.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Immortalized in legendary musical renditions across the subcontinent, capturing Shiv's tragic bohemian life in Chandigarh.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> Universally celebrated as one of the finest romantic ghazals in modern Indian literature.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Lyrical Ghazal rhythm with playful, tragic undertones.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (4 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਤੇਰਾ ਸ਼ਬਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ,  
> ਰੰਗ ਗੋਰਾ ਗੁਲਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> مینوں تیرا شباب لے بیٹھا،  
> رنگ گورا گلاب لے بیٹھا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Mainu tera shabaab lai baitha,  
> Rang gora gulaab lai baitha!  

#### English Translation
> The blinding splendor of your youth ruined me,  
> Your fair countenance, flushed like a wild rose, consumed me!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The opening matla attributes self-ruin to the intoxicating beauty of the beloved.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਡਰ ਸੀ ਕਿਤੇ ਨਾ ਲੈ ਬੈਠੇ,  
> ਲੈ ਹੀ ਬੈਠਾ ਜਨਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> دل دا ڈر سی کتے نہ لے بیٹھے،  
> لے ہی بیٹھا جناب لے بیٹھا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Dil da dar si kite na lai baithe,  
> Lai hi baitha janaab lai baitha!  

#### English Translation
> The heart feared from the start that it would destroy me;  
> And in the end, honored sir, it destroyed me utterly!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Playful irony: the inevitable catastrophe foretold by intuition.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕਿੰਨੀ ਬੀਤੀ ਤੇ ਕਿੰਨੀ ਬਾਕੀ ਏ,  
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਏਹੋ ਹਿਸਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کنی بیتی تے کنی باقی اے،  
> مینوں ایہو حساب لے بیٹھا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kinni beeti te kinni baaki ae,  
> Mainu eho hisaab lai baitha!  

#### English Translation
> How much of life has been squandered, and how much yet remains?  
> This relentless calculation alone has devoured my spirit!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Existential dread of the ticking clock.*

* * *

### Stanza 4

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਨੂੰ ਇੱਕ ਗ਼ਮ 'ਤੇ ਹੀ ਭਰੋਸਾ ਸੀ,  
> ਗ਼ਮ ਤੋਂ ਕੋਰਾ ਜਵਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو نوں اک غم تے ہی بھروسہ سی،  
> غم توں کورا جواب لے بیٹھا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv nu ikk gham te hi bharosa si,  
> Gham ton kora jawaab lai baitha!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv placed his faith only in the loyalty of his sorrow;  
> Yet from that very sorrow he received a cold, absolute refusal!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Ultimate metaphysical abandonment: even sorrow betrays the poet.*
