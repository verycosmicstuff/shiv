# Shama Bujh Gayi

### **ਸ਼ਮਾ ਬੁੱਝ ਗਈ** • **شمع بجھ گئی**

#### *"The Candle Flickered Out in the Wind"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Dardmandan Diyan Aahan (1964)](../06_dardmandan_diyan_aahan_1964.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸ਼ਮਾ ਬੁੱਝ ਗਈ (شمع بجھ گئی)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1964
- **Thematic Tags**: `#DardmandaanDianAahaan` `#Ghazal` `#CandleandMoth` `#Shama`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: The candle that burned through the night flickers and dies as morning arrives; the moth has burnt to ash, and the gathering disperses.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's feeling of exhaustion after all-night mushairas when the crowds dispersed and left him alone in dark halls.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Employs the classical Urdu-Persian *Shama-Parwana* (candle and moth) trope, rooted in Punjabi metric cadence.

**Source**: *Punjabi Kavi Ate Kavitriyan (Marxist Critical Essays)* by Sant Singh Sekhon (1972, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, pp. 89–112). [Literary Criticism]

> "Sant Singh Sekhon termed Shiv the "Keats of Punjabi poetry" for his meter and folk metaphor, but charged him with "palayanwad" (reactionary escapism), arguing that romanticizing youth-suicide was reactionary in post-Partition agrarian Punjab."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਮਾ ਬੁੱਝ ਗਈ ਮਹਿਫ਼ਿਲ ਮੁੱਕੀ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਸਭ ਆਪੋ-ਆਪਣੇ ਘਰ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਬੱਸ ਪਰਵਾਨਾ ਸੜ ਕੇ ਰਾਖ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਜੋ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਦਾਅਵੇ ਕਰ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شمع بجھ گئی محفل مکی،  
> ہن سبھ آپو اپنے گھر گئے نی!  
> بس پروانہ سڑ کے راکھ ہویا،  
> جو دل دے دعوے کر گئے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shama bujh gayi mehfil mukki,  
> Hun sabh aapo-apne ghar gaye ni!  
> Bass parwaana sarh ke raakh hoyea,  
> Jo dil de daawe kar gaye ni!  

#### English Translation
> The candle has flickered out and the gathering has dispersed,  
> All guests have returned to the shelter of their homes!  
> Only the devoted moth was incinerated to cold grey ash,  
> Having staked its immortal life upon the covenant of love!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The classical *parwaana* (moth) sacrificed while spectators return home.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹਨੇਰਾ ਛਾ ਗਿਆ ਚਾਰੇ ਪਾਸੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਰੌਸ਼ਨੀ ਦੀ ਕਿਰਨ ਨਾ ਰਹੀ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਜਿੰਦ ਵੀ ਉਸੇ ਸ਼ਮਾ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਜਿਸ ਵਿੱਚ ਜਿਊਣ ਦੀ ਤਾਂਘ ਨਾ ਰਹੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہنیرا چھا گیا چارے پاسے،  
> کوئی روشنی دی کرن نہ رہی!  
> ساڈی جند وی اسے شمع وانگوں،  
> جس وچ جیون دی تانگھ نہ رہی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hanera chhaa gaya chaare paase,  
> Koyi raushni di kiran na rahi!  
> Saadi jind vi usse shama vaangoo,  
> Jis vich jiyun di taangh na rahi!  

#### English Translation
> Suffocating darkness has descended upon the empty chamber,  
> Not a single ray of radiant light has survived!  
> Our fragile life was identical to that dying candle,  
> Within whose burnt wick no yearning for survival remained!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Extinguished candle (*bujhi shama*) as image of suicidal exhaustion.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜਦ ਸੂਰਜ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਫਿਰ ਚੜ੍ਹੇਗਾ,  
> ਨਵੀਂ ਮਹਿਫ਼ਿਲ ਨਵੇਂ ਲੋਕ ਹੋਣਗੇ!  
> ਪਰ ਸਾਡੇ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਸੜਨ ਵਾਲੇ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਸੁੰਞੀ ਕਬਰ ਦੇ ਸੋਗ ਹੋਣਗੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جد سورج کلھ پھر چڑھے گا،  
> نوی محفل نویں لوک ہون گے!  
> پر ساڈے وانگوں سڑن والے،  
> کسے سنجی قبر دے سوگ ہون گے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Jad suraj kallh phir charhega,  
> Naveen mehfil navein lok honge!  
> Par saade vaangoo sarhan vaale,  
> Kise sunji kabr de shok honge!  

#### English Translation
> When tomorrow's sun rises upon this bustling city,  
> A fresh gathering with new laughing faces will assemble!  
> Yet those who burned to ash like ourselves  
> Will sleep as forgotten mourners within a deserted grave!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The indifferent cycle of society replacing the dead artist.*
