# Sharab Te Shikwa

### **ਸ਼ਰਾਬ ਤੇ ਸ਼ਿਕਵਾ** • **شراب تے شکوہ**

#### *"Liquor and Lamentation"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Dardmandan Diyan Aahan (1964)](../06_dardmandan_diyan_aahan_1964.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸ਼ਰਾਬ ਤੇ ਸ਼ਿਕਵਾ (شراب تے شکوہ)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1964
- **Thematic Tags**: `#DardmandaanDianAahaan` `#Ghazal` `#Liquor` `#Intoxication`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: The tavern wine fails to numb the agonizing memory of the beloved; the poet curses the bottle that cannot grant oblivion.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv famously told Balwant Gargi: 'I drink not for intoxication, but to make the skin of my soul thick enough to endure the day.'

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Documents Shiv's complex relationship with alcohol as an anesthetic for his hypersensitive nervous system.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਰਾਬ ਨੇ ਵੀ ਕੋਈ ਅਸਰ ਨਾ ਕੀਤਾ,  
> ਦਰਦ ਅਜੇ ਵੀ ਜਾਗ ਰਿਹਾ ਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਯਾਦ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਭੁਲਾਉਣਾ ਚਾਹਿਆ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਦਿਲ ਨੂੰ ਡੰਗ ਰਿਹਾ ਏ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شراب نے وی کوئی اثر نہ کیتا،  
> درد اجے وی جاگ رہا اے نی!  
> جس یاد نوں میں بھلاؤنا چاہیا،  
> اوہی دل نوں ڈنگ رہا اے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Sharab ne vi koyi asar na keeta,  
> Dard aje vi jaag reha ae ni!  
> Jis yaad nu main bhulaaona chaaheya,  
> Uhi dil nu dang reha ae ni!  

#### English Translation
> Even the strongest tavern liquor failed to dull the wound,  
> The sharp agony remains awake and pulsing in my breast!  
> That very memory which I sought to drown in oblivion  
> Stings like a horned viper at the core of my heart!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Alcohol failing to numb the acute memory of love.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਬੋਤਲਾਂ ਖ਼ਾਲੀ ਹੋ ਗਈਆਂ ਸਾਰੀਆਂ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਨਸ਼ਾ ਪਰ ਦਰਦ ਦਾ ਏ ਵੇ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਦਵਾ ਨਾ ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਜੋ ਮੇਰੇ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ ਨੂੰ ਭਰਦਾ ਏ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> بوتلاں خالی ہو گئیاں ساریاں،  
> ساڈا نشہ پر درد دا اے وے!  
> کوئی دوا نہ اس دنیا وچ،  
> جو میرے زخم نوں بھردا اے وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Botlaan khaali ho gaiyaan saariyaan,  
> Saada nasha par dard da ae ve!  
> Koyi dawa na iss duniya vich,  
> Jo mere zakham nu bharda ae ve!  

#### English Translation
> All the dark bottles on the tavern table lie emptied,  
> Yet our true intoxication is the drunkenness of pure grief!  
> There exists no medicinal balm in this mortal universe  
> Capable of sealing the open, bleeding chasm of our wound!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Grief itself as the supreme, unyielding intoxicant (*nasha dard da*).*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਤਾਂ ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਜਾਮ ਹੀ ਪੀਣਾ ਏ,  
> ਜੋ ਸਭ ਨਸ਼ਿਆਂ ਤੋਂ ਵੱਡਾ ਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਨੂੰ ਪੀ ਕੇ ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਜਾਗੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਨਸ਼ਾ ਹੀ ਸਾਡਾ ਅੱਡਾ ਏ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن تاں موت دا جام ہی پینا اے،  
> جو سبھ نشیاں توں وڈا اے نی!  
> جس نوں پی کے کوئی نہ جاگے،  
> اوہ نشہ ہی ساڈا اڈا اے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun taan maut da jaam hi peena ae,  
> Jo sabh nashiyaan ton vadda ae ni!  
> Jis nu pee ke koyi na jaage,  
> Oh nasha hi saada adda ae ni!  

#### English Translation
> Now only the final goblet of Death remains to be drained,  
> An intoxication vaster than all worldly wines combined!  
> Drinking which no slumbering traveler ever awakens,  
> That eternal oblivion is our true and final home!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Death as the ultimate and only permanent anesthetic.*
