# Ishq Di Baazi

### **ਇਸ਼ਕ ਦੀ ਬਾਜ਼ੀ** • **عشق دی بازی**

#### *"The High Wager of Passion"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Dardmandan Diyan Aahan (1964)](../06_dardmandan_diyan_aahan_1964.md)
- **Original Title**: ਇਸ਼ਕ ਦੀ ਬਾਜ਼ੀ (عشق دی بازی)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1964
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Ghazal` `#Ishq` `#Wager` `#Sacrifice` `#Martyrdom`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `rebellion` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: The lover enters the arena of passion knowing that the wager requires the forfeit of life, yet plays with ecstatic abandon.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Chandigarh during a late-night poetic symposium.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Employs the classical Urdu/Persian ghazal tradition of Ishq as a fatal tournament where the lover willingly bets his head.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਸ਼ਕ ਦੀ ਬਾਜ਼ੀ ਜਿੱਤ ਨਾ ਸਕੇ,  
> ਅਸਾਂ ਸਿਰ ਦਾ ਸੌਦਾ ਲਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ!  
> ਜੋ ਸਾਹ ਸੀ ਸਾਡੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਉਹ ਤੇਰੇ ਨਾਮ ਵਹਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> عشق دی بازی جت نہ سکے،  
> اساں سر دا سودا لا دتا!  
> جو ساہ سی ساڈے سینے وچ،  
> اوہ تیرے نام وہا دتا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Ishq di baazi jitt na sake,  
> Asaan sir da sauda laa ditta!  
> Jo saah si saade seene vich,  
> Oh tere naam vahaa ditta!  

#### English Translation
> We could not emerge victorious in the high wager of love,  
> Yet we staked our very severed head on the board!  
> Every living breath that pulsed inside our chest  
> We poured out as an unreturning offering in your name!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Staking one's head (sir da sauda) as the Sufi price of divine love.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਦੁਨੀਆ ਨੇ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਕਾਫ਼ਰ ਕਿਹਾ,  
> ਜਦ ਮੰਦਰ-ਮਸਜਿਦ ਛੱਡ ਦਿੱਤੇ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਤੇਰੇ ਦਰ 'ਤੇ ਸਿਰ ਧਰ ਕੇ,  
> ਸਭ ਵਹਿਮ ਦਿਲੋਂ ਹੀ ਕੱਢ ਦਿੱਤੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> دنیا نے سانوں کافر کہیا،  
> جد مندر مسجد چھڈ دتے!  
> اسیں تیرے در تے سر دھر کے،  
> سبھ وہم دلوں ہی کڈھ دتے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Duniya ne saanu kaafir keha,  
> Jad mandar-masjid chhadd ditte!  
> Aseen tere dar te sir dhar ke,  
> Sabh vahim dilon hi kaddh ditte!  

#### English Translation
> The orthodox world branded us heretics and infidels  
> When we abandoned both the temple and the mosque!  
> Laying our forehead upon your sacred threshold,  
> We purged every ritual superstition from our spirit!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Transcendence of formal religious orthodoxy through personal devotion.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਹਾਰ ਕੇ ਵੀ ਬਾਦਸ਼ਾਹ ਬਣਿਆ,  
> ਇਸ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਦੇ ਖ਼ਾਲੀ ਤਖ਼ਤ ਉੱਤੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਜਿੱਤ ਗਏ ਉਹ ਰੋਣਗੇ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਲਿਖੇ ਹੋਏ ਇਸ ਵਕਤ ਉੱਤੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو ہار کے وی بادشاہ بنیا،  
> اس عشق دے خالی تخت اتے!  
> جو جت گئے اوہ رونگے،  
> ساڈے لکھے ہوئے اس وقت اتے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv haar ke vi baadshah baneya,  
> Iss ishq de khaali takht utte!  
> Jo jitt gaye oh roange,  
> Saade likhe hoye iss vaqt utte!  

#### English Translation
> Even in defeat, Shiv was crowned emperor  
> Upon this sovereign, empty throne of sacred love!  
> Those who supposedly won worldly triumphs shall weep in envy  
> Reading the immortal verses inscribed by his hand across time!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The defeated lover crowned as sovereign of the poetic realm.*
