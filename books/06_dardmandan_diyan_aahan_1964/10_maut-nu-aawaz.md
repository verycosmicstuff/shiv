# Maut Nu Aawaz

### **ਮੌਤ ਨੂੰ ਆਵਾਜ਼** • **موت نوں آواز**

#### *"Summoning Death into the Cold Night"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Dardmandan Diyan Aahan (1964)](../06_dardmandan_diyan_aahan_1964.md)
- **Original Title**: ਮੌਤ ਨੂੰ ਆਵਾਜ਼ (موت نوں آواز)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1964
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Death` `#Summons` `#Cold` `#Winter` `#Deliverance`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: Standing beneath the icy winter stars, the poet calls out to Death to end his unbearable physical and emotional weariness.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Chandigarh during a freezing December night in 1964.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Closes Dardmandaan Dian Aahaan (1964), directly calling for physical death as an end to psychic disintegration.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਠੰਢੀ ਰਾਤ 'ਚ ਖੜ੍ਹ ਕੇ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਮੌਤ ਨੂੰ ਆਵਾਜ਼ਾਂ ਮਾਰੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਸਾਹਾਂ ਦੀ ਡੋਰੀ ਮੁੱਕ ਚੱਲੀ,  
> ਮੇਰੀਆਂ ਸਭ ਆਸਾਂ ਹਾਰੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں ٹھنڈی رات چ کھڑ کے اڑیو،  
> موت نوں آوازاں ماریاں نی!  
> میرے ساہاں دی ڈوری مک چلی،  
> میریاں سبھ آساں ہاریاں نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main thanddhi raat ch kharh ke arhiyo,  
> Maut nu aawaazaan maariyaan ni!  
> Mere saahaan di dori mukk challey,  
> Meriyaan sabh aasaan haariyaan ni!  

#### English Translation
> Standing beneath the freezing winter midnight, friends,  
> I called out with open arms to Death itself!  
> The slender thread of my fluttering breaths has exhausted its span;  
> Every lingering hope of my youth has surrendered to defeat!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The active invocation of death in physical winter.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਦਾਰੂ ਕੰਮ ਆਈ ਮੇਰੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਦੁਆ ਨੇ ਤਾਰਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਦਰਦ ਨੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਜਿਊਣਾ ਖੋਹਿਆ,  
> ਉਸਨੇ ਹੀ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਮਾਰਿਆ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> نہ کوئی دارو کم آئی میرے،  
> نہ کوئی دعا نے تاریا نی!  
> جس درد نے میرا جیونا کھوہیا،  
> اس نے ہی مینوں ماریا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Na koyi daaru kamm aayi mere,  
> Na koyi dua ne taareya ni!  
> Jis dard ne mera jiyuna khoheya,  
> Usne hi mainu maareya ni!  

#### English Translation
> Not a single pharmaceutical medicine brought relief;  
> Not a single pious prayer could salvage my sinking boat!  
> That very agonizing passion which robbed me of the will to live  
> Has delivered the final, merciful stroke of death!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Neither medicine nor prayer can cure metaphysical longing.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਆ ਜਾ ਮੌਤੇ ਗਲ਼ ਲਾ ਲੈ ਮੈਨੂੰ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਦੇਰ ਨਾ ਲਾਈਂ ਤੂੰ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਸਾਂਭ ਲੈ,  
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਅਮਰ ਬਣਾਈਂ ਤੂੰ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> آ جا موتے گل لا لے مینوں،  
> ہن دیر نہ لائیں توں!  
> شیو دے گیتاں نوں سانبھ لے،  
> مینوں امر بنائیں توں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Aa jaa maute gal laa lai mainu,  
> Hun der na laayin toon!  
> Shiv de geetan nu saambh lai,  
> Mainu amar banaayin toon!  

#### English Translation
> Come forth, O gentle Death, and fold me into your embrace;  
> Tarry no longer in the shadows!  
> Take under your eternal guardianship the orphaned verses of Shiv,  
> And grant him everlasting immortality beyond the grave!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Death as the custodian who grants immortality to verse.*
