# Ghamman Da Parona

### **ਗ਼ਮਾਂ ਦਾ ਪ੍ਰਾਹੁਣਾ** • **غماں دا پروہنا**

#### *"The Uninvited Guest of Sorrow"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Dardmandan Diyan Aahan (1964)](../06_dardmandan_diyan_aahan_1964.md)
- **Original Title**: ਗ਼ਮਾਂ ਦਾ ਪ੍ਰਾਹੁਣਾ (غماں دا پروہنا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1964
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Guest` `#Hospitality` `#Sorrow` `#Nocturnal` `#Courtyard`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: Sorrow arrives at the door like a revered, uninvited guest at twilight, and the poet serves it with the fine wine of his tears.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala during solitary winter evenings.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Transforms the sacred Punjabi tradition of unconditional hospitality (Pahunchaar) into an intimate banquet with Grief.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਗ਼ਮਾਂ ਦਾ ਪ੍ਰਾਹੁਣਾ ਆਇਆ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਬੂਹਾ ਖੋਲ੍ਹ ਕੇ ਬਹਿ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਨਾ ਉਹ ਉੱਠੇ, ਨਾ ਉਹ ਜਾਵੇ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਦਿਲ ਹੀ ਲੈ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> غماں دا پروہنا آیا اڑیو،  
> بوہا کھول کے بہہ گیا نی!  
> نہ اوہ اٹھے، نہ اوہ جاوے،  
> ساڈا دل ہی لے گیا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Ghamman da parona aaya arhiyo,  
> Booha kholh ke beh gaya ni!  
> Na oh utthe, na oh jaave,  
> Saada dil hi lai gaya ni!  

#### English Translation
> The guest of Sorrow has arrived at our threshold, friends,  
> Unbolting the door and settling firmly inside!  
> He neither arises nor takes his departure;  
> He has taken complete possession of our heart!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Grief as the permanent guest occupying the house of life.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਸਾਂ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਦੀ ਚੂਰੀ ਕੁੱਟੀ,  
> ਦਰਦ ਦਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਪਿਲਾਇਆ ਏ!  
> ਇਸ ਅਣ-ਸੱਦੇ ਮਹਿਮਾਨ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਅਸਾਂ ਸੀਨੇ ਨਾਲ਼ ਲਗਾਇਆ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اساں ہنجھواں دی چوری کٹی،  
> درد دا پانی پلایا اے!  
> اس ان-سدے مہمان نوں،  
> اساں سینے نال لگایا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Asaan hanjuwan di choori kutti,  
> Dard da paani pilaaya ae!  
> Iss an-sadde mehmaan nu,  
> Asaan seene naal lagaaya ae!  

#### English Translation
> We crumbled a sweet offering fashioned of our tears,  
> Serving him the bitter water of our own agony!  
> To this uninvited, nocturnal guest of destiny,  
> We extended our warmest, most intimate embrace!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Treating sorrow with exquisite traditional hospitality.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਇਹ ਪ੍ਰਾਹੁਣਾ ਸਾਡਾ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਉਮਰਾਂ ਤਾਈਂ ਰਹੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਤੱਕ ਦੀਵਾ ਬਲੇਗਾ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਇਹ ਸਾਡੇ ਕੋਲ਼ ਹੀ ਬਹੇਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو کہندا ایہہ پروہنا ساڈا،  
> ہن عمراں تائیں رہےگا!  
> جد تک دیوا بلےگا گھر وچ،  
> ایہہ ساڈے کول ہی بہےگا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv kehnda eh parona saada,  
> Hun umraan taayin rahega!  
> Jad takk deeva balega ghar vich,  
> Eh saade kol hi bahega!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv declares: 'This solemn guest of ours  
> Shall dwell with us through all our remaining lifetimes!  
> As long as the frail clay lamp flickers in this room,  
> He shall sit faithfully right beside our shoulder!'  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Sorrow as lifelong faithful companion.*
