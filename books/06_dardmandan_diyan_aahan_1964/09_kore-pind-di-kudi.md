# Kore Pind Di Kudi

### **ਕੋਰੇ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੀ ਕੁੜੀ** • **کورے پنڈ دی کڑی**

#### *"The Maiden of the Pristine Village"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Dardmandan Diyan Aahan (1964)](../06_dardmandan_diyan_aahan_1964.md)
- **Original Title**: ਕੋਰੇ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੀ ਕੁੜੀ (کورے پنڈ دی کڑی)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1964
- **Thematic Tags**: `#VillageMaiden` `#Innocence` `#Purity` `#Nostalgia` `#Pastoral`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: A nostalgic tribute to the pure, uncorrupted village maiden whose unaffected smile remains the only holy thing in a cynical world.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala after visiting relatives in rural Gurdaspur.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Dedicated to the innocent village girls of Majha who never knew the corrosive vanity of modern urban life.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਉਹ ਕੋਰੇ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੀ ਸਾਦੀ ਕੁੜੀ,  
> ਜਿਸਦੇ ਹਾਸੇ 'ਚ ਫੁੱਲ ਖਿੜਦੇ ਸੀ!  
> ਜਦੋਂ ਲੰਘਦੀ ਸੀ ਖੇਤਾਂ 'ਚੋਂ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਕਣਕਾਂ ਦੇ ਸਿੱਟੇ ਗਿੜਦੇ ਸੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اوہ کورے پنڈ دی سادی کڑی،  
> جس دے ہاسے چ پھل کھڑدے سی!  
> جدوں لنگھدی سی کھیتاں چوں،  
> تاں کنکاں دے سٹے گڑدے سی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Oh kore pind di saadi kudi,  
> Jisde haase ch phull khirhde si!  
> Jadon langhdi si khetaan chon,  
> Taan kankan de sitte girhde si!  

#### English Translation
> She was an innocent maiden of a pristine, untouched village,  
> In whose unforced laughter wild blossoms seemed to bloom!  
> Whenever she walked past the green agrarian fields,  
> The heavy golden ears of wheat bowed down in greeting!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Nature recognizing and responding to innocent grace.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਉਹਨੂੰ ਸ਼ਹਿਰੀ ਰੰਗ ਨਾ ਚੜ੍ਹਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਨਾ ਝੂਠੇ ਬੋਲ ਬੋਲਦੀ ਸੀ!  
> ਆਪਣੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਪਾਕ ਸੁਪਨਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਉਹ ਮਿੱਠੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ 'ਚ ਘੋਲਦੀ ਸੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اوہنوں شہری رنگ نہ چڑھیا سی،  
> نہ جھوٹھے بول بولدی سی!  
> اپنے دل دے پاک سپنیاں نوں،  
> اوہ مٹھے گیتاں چ گھولدی سی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Ohnu shehri rang na charheya si,  
> Na jhoothe bol boldi si!  
> Aapne dil de paak supniyaan nu,  
> Oh mitthe geetan ch gholdi si!  

#### English Translation
> No artificial urban varnish had ever tarnished her spirit;  
> No calculating falsehood ever passed her lips!  
> The sacred, unblemished dreams of her tender heart  
> She dissolved into sweet, spontaneous folk songs!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Purity of agrarian maidenhood contrasted with urban duplicity.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਉਹ ਵੀ ਵਿਆਹੀ ਗਈ ਦੂਰ ਕਿਤੇ,  
> ਪਿੰਡ ਸੁੰਞਾ ਕਰ ਗਈ ਏ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਦਿਲ ਪਰ ਉਸ ਕਲੀ ਲਈ,  
> ਸਦਾ ਬਿਰਹਾ 'ਚ ਭਰ ਗਈ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن اوہ وی ویاہی گئی دور کتے،  
> پنڈ سنجا کر گئی اے!  
> شیو دا دل پر اس کلی لئی،  
> سدا برہا چ بھر گئی اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun oh vi vyaahi gayi door kite,  
> Pind sunjna kar gayi ae!  
> Shiv da dil par us kali layi,  
> Sada birha ch bhar gayi ae!  

#### English Translation
> Now she too has been wedded and carried far away,  
> Leaving the ancestral village hollow and deserted!  
> Yet Shiv's heart, for that unplucked wild blossom,  
> Shall remain filled forever with the holy perfume of longing!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The inevitable loss of pastoral innocence to marital exile.*
