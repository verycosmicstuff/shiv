# Loona: Chautha Ank (Ichhran da Virlaap)

### **ਲੂਣਾ : ਚੌਥਾ ਅੰਕ (ਰਾਣੀ ਇੱਛਰਾਂ ਦਾ ਵਿਰਲਾਪ)** • **لونا : چوتھا انک (رانی اچھراں دا ورلاپ)**

#### *"Act IV: Queen Ichhran's Grief & The New Queen"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Loona (1965)](../07_loona_1965.md)
- **Original Title**: ਲੂਣਾ : ਚੌਥਾ ਅੰਕ (ਰਾਣੀ ਇੱਛਰਾਂ ਦਾ ਵਿਰਲਾਪ) (لونا : چوتھا انک (رانی اچھراں دا ورلاپ))
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1965
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Loona` `#VersePlay` `#SahityaAkademi` `#Feminism` `#Epic`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `feminism` | **Landscape Biome**: `barren_mountain_loona`

**Synopsis**: Queen Ichhran mourns her husband Salwan's disgraceful lust in wedding a girl younger than his own son, weeping for her isolated son Puran.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Puran had been sequestered in an underground vault for 18 years due to astrological warnings; Ichhran's heartbreak presages the catastrophe.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Awarded the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1967, establishing Shiv Kumar Batalvi at age 31 as the youngest laureate in Indian literary history.

**Source**: *Sahitya Akademi Awards Official Citation: Loona (1965)* by Sahitya Akademi Secretariat (1967, Sahitya Akademi, Rabindra Bhavan, New Delhi, Citation Registry 1967). [Institutional Record]

> "Awarded to Shiv Kumar Batalvi for his epic blank-verse play Loona. At age 31, he became the youngest recipient in the history of the National Academy of Letters, sparking envy and moral controversy."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> Won the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1967. Radically re-evaluated female bodily agency in Indian literature.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Dramatic, impassioned verse-play declamation in classical Punjabi blank verse.
