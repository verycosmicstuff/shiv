# Loona: Chhevaan Ank (Bag vich Sair)

### **ਲੂਣਾ : ਛੇਵਾਂ ਅੰਕ (ਬਾਗ਼ 'ਚ ਸੈਰ ਤੇ ਦੋਸ਼)** • **لونا : چھواں انک (باغ وچ سیر تے الزام)**

#### *"Act VI: The Evening Walk & The Deadly Accusation"*

**Synopsis**: Humiliated by Puran's rejection and terrified of discovery, Loona meets Salwan in the palace gardens and turns her wounded fury into an accusation.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv portrays Loona's accusation not as demonic malice, but as the desperate, self-defensive reflex of an entrapped woman in a ruthless feudal court.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
