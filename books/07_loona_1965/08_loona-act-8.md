**Synopsis**: The concluding act: the truth erupts in the royal assembly. Loona screams her final curse against patriarchal society, shattering 1,000 years of silence.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Won the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1967. Loona is vindicated as an immortal symbol of female bodily agency and anti-patriarchal rebellion.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Awarded the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1967, establishing Shiv Kumar Batalvi at age 31 as the youngest laureate in Indian literary history.

**Source**: *Sahitya Akademi Awards Official Citation: Loona (1965)* by Sahitya Akademi Secretariat (1967, Sahitya Akademi, Rabindra Bhavan, New Delhi, Citation Registry 1967). [Institutional Record]

> "Awarded to Shiv Kumar Batalvi for his epic blank-verse play Loona. At age 31, he became the youngest recipient in the history of the National Academy of Letters, sparking envy and moral controversy."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> Won the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1967. Radically re-evaluated female bodily agency in Indian literature.
