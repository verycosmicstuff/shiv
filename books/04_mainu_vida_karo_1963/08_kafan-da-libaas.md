# Kafan Da Libaas

### **ਕਫ਼ਨ ਦਾ ਲਿਬਾਸ** • **کفن دا لباس**

#### *"The White Shroud as My Only Garment"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਕਫ਼ਨ ਦਾ ਲਿਬਾਸ (کفن دا لباس)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Shroud` `#Burial` `#White` `#Purity` `#Mortality`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The white unstitched shroud is described as the only honest robe that fits the shivering, naked soul of the poet without hypocrisy.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala after attending the cremation of a young companion.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Shiv's stark meditation on unstitched white cloth (Kafan), stripping away all poetic ornament in the face of death.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਕਫ਼ਨ ਦਾ ਲਿਬਾਸ ਪੁਆ ਦੇਵੋ,  
> ਇਹ ਰੇਸ਼ਮ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਾੜਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਇਸ ਚਿੱਟੀ ਚਾਦਰ ਦੇ ਹੇਠਾਂ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਦਿਲ ਰੱਬ ਨੂੰ ਪੁਕਾਰਦਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> مینوں کفن دا لباس پوا دیوو،  
> ایہہ ریشم مینوں ساڑدا اے!  
> اس چٹی چادر دے ہیٹھاں،  
> میرا دل رب نوں پکاردا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Mainu kafan da libaas puaa devo,  
> Eh resham mainu saarhda ae!  
> Iss chitti chaadar de hethan,  
> Mera dil Rabb nu pukaarda ae!  

#### English Translation
> Drape upon my body the unstitched robe of the white shroud;  
> This worldly silk scorches and suffocates my skin!  
> Beneath this pristine white sheet of mortality,  
> My naked heart calls out directly to the Divine!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The white shroud as spiritual relief from hypocritical luxury.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕੋਈ ਜੇਬ ਨਹੀਂ ਇਸ ਕਫ਼ਨ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਲਾਲਚ ਨਾਲ਼ ਨਾ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਜੋ ਕਮਾਇਆ ਸੀ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ ਧਨ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਰੂਹ ਨੂੰ ਰੁਸ਼ਨਾਵੇਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کوئی جیب نہیں اس کفن دے وچ،  
> کوئی لالچ نال نہ جاوےگا!  
> جو کمایا سی برہا دا دھن،  
> اوہی روح نوں روشناوےگا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Koyi jeb nahin iss kafan de vich,  
> Koyi laalach naal na jaavega!  
> Jo kamaaya si birha da dhan,  
> Uhi rooh nu rushnaavega!  

#### English Translation
> There are no pockets sewn into this white shroud;  
> No worldly greed or gold can accompany me on this voyage!  
> Only that wealth of holy grief which I gathered in life  
> Shall illuminate the journey of my departing spirit!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The classic folk adage of the pocketless shroud.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਤੁਰ ਚੱਲਿਆ ਸਫ਼ੈਦ ਹੋ ਕੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਦਾਗ਼ ਨਾ ਲਾਇਆ ਦਾਮਨ 'ਤੇ!  
> ਜਦ ਧਰਤੀ ਰੋਊਗੀ ਮੇਰੇ ਜਾਣ ਪਿੱਛੋਂ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਫੁੱਲ ਖਿੜਨਗੇ ਚਮਨ 'ਤੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو تاں تر چلیا سفید ہو کے،  
> کوئی داغ نہ لایا دامن تے!  
> جد دھرتی روؤگی میرے جان پچھوں،  
> تاں پھل کھڑنگے چمن تے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv taan tur challeya safaid ho ke,  
> Koyi daagh na laaya daaman te!  
> Jad dharti rougi mere jaan pichhon,  
> Taan phull khirhnge chaman te!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv departs cloaked in untainted, spotless white,  
> Leaving not a single stain of deceit upon his robe!  
> When this sacred soil weeps after my departure,  
> Fragrant wild blossoms shall burst forth across the gardens!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The immaculate poet leaving the garden fertilized by his tears.*
