# Shamshaan Di Raakh

### **ਸ਼ਮਸ਼ਾਨ ਦੀ ਰਾਖ਼** • **شمشاں دی راکھ**

#### *"The Holy Ashes of the Cremation Ground"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸ਼ਮਸ਼ਾਨ ਦੀ ਰਾਖ਼ (شمشاں دی راکھ)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Cremation` `#Ashes` `#Pyre` `#Mortality` `#Transcendence`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The cremation ground (Shamshaan) is portrayed as the only place of true equality, where all pride and power turn to cooling grey ash.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala during solitary walks past the cremation ground on the town outskirts.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Draws on the funeral ghats along the Ravi and Beas where Shiv spent nocturnal hours contemplating mortality.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਸ਼ਮਸ਼ਾਨ ਦੀ ਰਾਖ਼ 'ਚ ਵੇਖਿਆ,  
> ਸਭ ਹੰਕਾਰ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਹੋ ਗਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਕਹਿੰਦੇ ਸੀ ਅਸੀਂ ਬਾਦਸ਼ਾਹ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਉਹ ਵੀ ਇੱਕੋ ਚਿਤਾ 'ਚ ਸੋ ਗਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں شمشاں دی راکھ چ ویکھیا،  
> سبھ ہنکار مٹی ہو گئے!  
> جو کہندے سی اسیں بادشاہ ہاں،  
> اوہ وی اکو چتا چ سو گئے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main shamshaan di raakh ch vekheya,  
> Sabh hankaar mitti ho gaye!  
> Jo kehnde si aseen baadshah haan,  
> Oh vi ikko chita ch so gaye!  

#### English Translation
> Looking into the cooling ashes of the cremation ground,  
> I saw all human arrogance and vanity dissolved into dust!  
> Those who proclaimed themselves emperors and monarchs  
> Slept quietly within the very same heap of gray cinders!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The great leveller: the pyre erases all worldly hierarchy.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਸ ਰਾਖ਼ 'ਚ ਕੋਈ ਜਾਤ ਨਾ ਦਿਸਦੀ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਅਮੀਰ ਨਾ ਗ਼ਰੀਬ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਅੱਗ ਨੇ ਸਾੜ ਕੇ ਪਾਕ ਕੀਤਾ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਰੂਹ ਦਾ ਨਸੀਬ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اس راکھ چ کوئی ذات نہ دسدی،  
> نہ کوئی امیر نہ غریب نی!  
> جو اگ نے ساڑ کے پاک کیتا،  
> اوہی روح دا نصیب نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Iss raakh ch koyi jaat na dissdi,  
> Na koyi ameer na gareeb ni!  
> Jo agg ne saarh ke paak keeta,  
> Uhi rooh da naseeb ni!  

#### English Translation
> Within these ashes, no trace of caste or lineage remains;  
> Neither the billionaire nor the destitute pauper can be told apart!  
> That which the sacred fire purified and reduced to ash  
> Alone represents the true, unburdened destiny of the spirit!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Fire as the ultimate purifier of social falsehoods.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਵੀ ਇਸੇ ਰਾਖ਼ 'ਚ ਰਲ਼ ਜਾਊ,  
> ਇਹ ਸੋਚ ਕੇ ਦਿਲ ਮੁਸਕਾਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੀ ਅੱਗ 'ਚ ਸੜਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਅਮਰ ਸੁੱਖ ਹੁਣ ਪਾਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو وی اسے راکھ چ رل جاؤ،  
> ایہہ سوچ کے دل مسکاوندا اے!  
> جو برہا دی اگ چ سڑیا سی،  
> اوہ امر سکھ ہن پاوندا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv vi isse raakh ch ral jaau,  
> Eh soch ke dil muskaaunda ae!  
> Jo birha di agg ch sarheya si,  
> Oh amar sukkh hun paaunda ae!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv too shall dissolve into these very gray ashes;  
> Pondering this final grace, his weary heart smiles in peace!  
> He who burned through an entire lifetime in the fire of separation  
> Now attains at last the everlasting sanctuary of silence!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Peace found in total dissolution.*
