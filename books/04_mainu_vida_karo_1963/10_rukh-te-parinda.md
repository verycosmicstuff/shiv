# Rukh Te Parinda

### **ਰੁੱਖ ਤੇ ਪਰਿੰਦਾ** • **رخ تے پرندہ**

#### *"The Tree and the Dying Bird"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਰੁੱਖ ਤੇ ਪਰਿੰਦਾ (رخ تے پرندہ)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Tree` `#Bird` `#Dialogue` `#Mortality` `#Dusk`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: A philosophical dialogue between an ancient tree that cannot move and a wounded bird that cannot fly anymore.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala as Shiv watched a wounded sparrow take shelter in an old Shisham tree.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> A poignant allegorical dialogue composed when Shiv stayed at his father's village house during a convalescence period.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਰੁੱਖ ਨੇ ਪੁੱਛਿਆ ਪੰਛੀ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਤੂੰ ਕਿਉਂ ਅੱਜ ਗੀਤ ਨਾ ਗਾਵੇਂ ਨੀ?  
> ਤੇਰੇ ਖੰਭਾਂ 'ਚੋਂ ਲਹੂ ਕਿਉਂ ਸਿੰਮਦਾ,  
> ਤੂੰ ਉੱਡ ਕੇ ਕਿਉਂ ਨਾ ਜਾਵੇਂ ਨੀ?  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> رخ نے پچھیا پنچھی نوں،  
> توں کیوں اج گیت نہ گاویں نی؟  
> تیرے کھنبھاں چوں لہو کیوں سمدا،  
> توں اڈ کے کیوں نہ جاویں نی؟  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Rukkh ne puchheya panchhi nu,  
> Toon kyon ajj geet na gaaven ni?  
> Tere khambhaan chon lahu kyon simmda,  
> Toon udd ke kyon na jaaven ni?  

#### English Translation
> The ancient tree asked of the little bird:  
> 'Why do you utter no melody today, gentle singer?  
> Why does crimson blood seep from between your feathers?  
> Why do you not take wing and soar across the blue horizon?'  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The tree's inquiry into the bird's sudden silence.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਪੰਛੀ ਰੋ ਕੇ ਆਖਣ ਲੱਗਾ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਸ਼ਿਕਾਰੀ ਨੇ ਖੰਭ ਤੋੜਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਆਇਆ ਸਾਂ ਤੇਰੀ ਛਾਂ ਦੇ ਉਹਲੇ,  
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਮੌਤ ਨੇ ਅੱਜ ਮੋੜਿਆ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> پنچھی رو کے آکھن لگا،  
> میرا شکاری نے کھنبھ توڑیا اے!  
> میں آیا ساں تیری چھاں دے اوہلے،  
> مینوں موت نے اج موڑیا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Panchhi ro ke aakhan lagga,  
> Mera shikaari ne khambh toreya ae!  
> Main aaya saan teri chhaan de ohle,  
> Mainu maut ne ajj morheya ae!  

#### English Translation
> Through weeping breaths, the bird replied:  
> 'A cruel hunter has shattered my flying wing!  
> I have come to take refuge beneath your maternal shade,  
> For death has summoned me back from life's journey today!'  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The wounded bird seeking final sanctuary in nature.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਰੁੱਖ ਨੇ ਗਲ਼ ਨਾਲ ਲਾਇਆ ਪੰਛੀ,  
> ਤੇ ਪੱਤਿਆਂ ਦੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਕੇਰੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਦੋਵੇਂ ਰੋ ਪਏ,  
> ਇਸ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਦੇ ਘੋਰ ਹਨੇਰੇ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> رخ نے گل نال لایا پنچھی،  
> تے پتیاں دے ہنجھو کیرے نی!  
> شیو کہندا دوویں رو پئے،  
> اس شام دے گھور ہنیرے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Rukkh ne gal naal laaya panchhi,  
> Te pattiyan de hanjhu kere ni!  
> Shiv kehnda dovein ro paye,  
> Iss shaam de ghor hanere ni!  

#### English Translation
> The ancient tree embraced the dying bird within its boughs,  
> Shedding dew-like tears of leaves in communion!  
> Shiv says: 'Both broke into unutterable weeping together  
> Within the gathering, terrifying gloom of this twilight!'  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Universal solidarity in shared suffering.*
