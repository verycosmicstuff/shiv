# Siveyaan Di Raakh

### **ਸਿਵਿਆਂ ਦੀ ਰਾਖ** • **سویاں دی راکھ**

#### *"Ashes of the Smoldering Pyre"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸਿਵਿਆਂ ਦੀ ਰਾਖ (سویاں دی راکھ)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#Ashes` `#Cremation` `#Nihilism`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: Cold ashes remaining after the pyre has died down: all beauty, fame, and tragic agony reduced to a handful of grey dust.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's ongoing confrontation with the absolute physical finality of cremation.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Written after Shiv attended the cremation of a close friend in Batala, profoundly shaken by the swiftness of incineration.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸਿਵਿਆਂ ਦੀ ਰਾਖ ਫਰੋਲ ਕੇ ਵੇਖੋ,  
> ਕੀ ਬਚਿਆ ਇਸ ਜੀਵਨ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਕਦੇ ਸੋਹਣਾ ਮੁੱਖੜਾ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਮਿਲ ਗਿਆ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> سویاں دی راکھ پھول کے ویکھو،  
> کی بچیا اس جیون وچوں نی!  
> جو کدے سوہنا مکھڑا سی،  
> اوہ مل گیا مٹی وچوں نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Siveyaan di raakh farol ke vekho,  
> Ki bacheya iss jeevan vichon ni!  
> Jo kade sohna mukhrha si,  
> Oh mil gaya mitti vichon ni!  

#### English Translation
> Stir the cold ashes of the cremation ground and behold  
> What residue survives of this proud, turbulent life!  
> That face which was once radiant beyond earthly comparison  
> Has merged indistinguishably with the common soil!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The stirring of cold ashes (*raakh farol ke*) as the ultimate memento mori.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹਵਾ ਉਡਾਵੇ ਇਸ ਰਾਖ ਨੂੰ ਹੁਣ,  
> ਖੇਤਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਖਿਲਾਰਦੀ ਏ!  
> ਜਿਵੇਂ ਕੋਈ ਰੋਂਦੀ ਮਾਂ ਆਪਣੇ,  
> ਪੁੱਤ ਨੂੰ ਦੂਰੋਂ ਤਾੜਦੀ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہوا اڈاوے اس راکھ نوں ہن،  
> کھیتاں وچ کھلارد ی اے!  
> جیویں کوئی روندی ماں اپنے،  
> پت نوں دوروں تاڑدی اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hawa udaave iss raakh nu hun,  
> Khetaan vich khilaardi ae!  
> Jiven koyi rondi maan apne,  
> Putt nu dooron taardi ae!  

#### English Translation
> The restless wind lifts these ashes upon its breath,  
> Scattering them gently across the harvested wheat fields!  
> Just as a grieving mother watches from the courtyard gate  
> As her departing son disappears into the distance!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Ashes scattering over farmland: returning nutrients to the earth.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਸੀਂ ਵੀ ਇੱਕ ਦਿਨ ਰਾਖ ਬਣਾਂਗੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਯਾਦ ਕਰੇਗਾ ਵੇ!  
> ਬੱਸ ਇਹ ਗੀਤ ਹਵਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੂੰਜਣਗੇ,  
> ਜਦ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ ਕੋਈ ਦੀਵਾ ਬਲੇਗਾ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اسیں وی اک دن راکھ بناں گے،  
> کوئی نہ سانوں یاد کرے گا وے!  
> بس ایہ گیت ہوا وچ گونجن گے،  
> جد برہا دا کوئی دیوا بلے گا وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Aseen vi ikk din raakh banaange,  
> Koyi na saanu yaad karega ve!  
> Bass eh geet hawa vich goonjange,  
> Jad birha da koyi deeva balega ve!  

#### English Translation
> We too shall one day be reduced to identical grey ash,  
> And none in this uncaring world will preserve our name!  
> Only these tragic songs shall echo upon the nocturnal winds  
> Whenever the sacred lamp of longing is kindled in the dark!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Immortality conceded only to the song, not the singer.*
