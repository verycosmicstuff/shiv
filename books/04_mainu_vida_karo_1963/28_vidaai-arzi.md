# Vidaai Arzi

### **ਵਿਦਾਈ ਅਰਜ਼ੀ** • **وداعی عرضی**

#### *"Petition for Eternal Departure"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਵਿਦਾਈ ਅਰਜ਼ੀ (وداعی عرضی)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#Petition` `#TitleCanto` `#Farewell`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: A formal, humble petition addressed to life, asking to be excused from further attendance in the school of worldly agony.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's weariness with social obligations, literary politics, and emotional betrayal.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Companion title-poem to 'Mainu Vida Karo', formalizing his poetic petition to be released from worldly existence.

**Source**: *Punjabi Kavi Ate Kavitriyan (Marxist Critical Essays)* by Sant Singh Sekhon (1972, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, pp. 89–112). [Literary Criticism]

> "Sant Singh Sekhon termed Shiv the "Keats of Punjabi poetry" for his meter and folk metaphor, but charged him with "palayanwad" (reactionary escapism), arguing that romanticizing youth-suicide was reactionary in post-Partition agrarian Punjab."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਦੇ ਦਰਬਾਰ 'ਚ ਮੈਂ,  
> ਵਿਦਾਈ ਅਰਜ਼ੀ ਪਾਈ ਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਬਹੁਤ ਹੋ ਗਈ ਮੁਕੱਦਮੇ-ਬਾਜ਼ੀ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਜਾਣ ਦੀ ਵਾਰੀ ਆਈ ਏ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> زندگی دے دربار چ میں،  
> وداعی عرضی پائی اے نی!  
> بہت ہو گئی مقدمے بازی،  
> ہن جان دی واری آئی اے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Zindagi de darbaar ch main,  
> Vidaai arzi paayi ae ni!  
> Bahut ho gayi mukaddame-baazi,  
> Hun jaan di vaari aayi ae ni!  

#### English Translation
> In the sovereign high tribunal of Life  
> I have submitted my formal petition for departure!  
> We have suffered enough of bitter litigation in this court,  
> And now the hour for my final exit has struck!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Courtroom metaphor (*darbaar / mukaddame-baazi*) for life's endless trials.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਗਿਲਾ ਨਾ ਸ਼ਿਕਵਾ ਸਾਡਾ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਦੋਸ਼ ਜਹਾਨ ਤੇ ਵੇ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਆਪਣੀ ਮਰਜ਼ੀ ਨਾਲ ਤੁਰੇ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਇਸ ਮੌਤ ਦੇ ਸੁੰਞੇ ਮਕਾਨ ਤੇ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> نہ کوئی گلہ نہ شکوہ ساڈا،  
> نہ کوئی دوش جہان تے وے!  
> اسیں اپنی مرضی نال ترے ہاں،  
> اس موت دے سنجے مکان تے وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Na koyi gila na shikwa saada,  
> Na koyi dosh jahaan te ve!  
> Aseen apni marzi naal ture haan,  
> Iss maut de sunje makaan te ve!  

#### English Translation
> We harbor no bitter resentment nor grievance in our heart,  
> Nor do we cast blame upon this cruel, beautiful world!  
> We depart solely of our own deliberate volition,  
> Journeying towards the quiet, peaceful mansion of Death!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Absence of rancor: accepting departure without bitterness.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਵਿਦਾ ਕਰੋ ਓ ਹਾਣੀਓ ਮੇਰਿਓ,  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਸਿਰ ਤੇ ਹੱਥ ਧਰ ਜਾਓ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਇਹਨਾਂ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਆਪਣੇ ਦਿਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਭਰ ਜਾਓ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> مینوں وداع کرو او ہانیو مہریو،  
> میرے سر تے ہتھ دھر جاؤ نی!  
> میرے برہا دے ایہناں گیتاں نوں،  
> اپنے دل وچ بھر جاؤ نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Mainu vida karo o haaniyo mereyo,  
> Mere sir te hath dhar jaao ni!  
> Mere birha de ehnaan geetaan nu,  
> Apne dil vich bhar jaao ni!  

#### English Translation
> Bid me farewell, O cherished companions of my youth,  
> Place your hands gently upon my head in final blessing!  
> And these sorrowful hymns of my cosmic separation  
> Preserve forever within the sacred sanctuary of your hearts!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Echoes the title song *Mainu Vida Karo* in its blessing of departure.*
