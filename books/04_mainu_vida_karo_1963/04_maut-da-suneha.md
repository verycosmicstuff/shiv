# Maut Da Suneha

### **ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਸੁਨੇਹਾ** • **موت دا سُنیہا**

#### *"The Tidings of Approaching Death"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਸੁਨੇਹਾ (موت دا سُنیہا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Death` `#Premonition` `#Messenger` `#Shadows`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: Death arrives not as a grim reaper, but as an intimate bridegroom delivering an overdue invitation to peace.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala during a high fever.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Reflects Shiv's lifelong premonition that he would die young, echoing Keats and Shelley.

**Source**: *Shiv Batalvi: A Solitary and Passionate Singer* by Dr. O.P. Sharma (1979, Sterling Publishers, New Delhi, pp. 12–45, 180–205). [Primary Memoir]

> "Authoritative academic biography documenting his childhood in Bara Pind Lohtian (now Pakistan), the trauma of the 1947 Partition flight, his clerkship at the State Bank of India Chandigarh, and his final collapse in Kir Mangyal."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਸੁਨੇਹਾ ਆਇਆ ਏ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਬੂਹਾ ਖੜਕਾ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਬਲ਼ਦੇ ਹੋਏ ਮੱਥੇ 'ਤੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਠੰਢਾ ਹੱਥ ਰੱਖ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> موت دا سُنیہا آیا اے،  
> کوئی بوہا کھڑکایا گیا نی!  
> میرے بلدے ہوئے متھے تے،  
> کوئی ٹھنڈا ہتھ رکھ گیا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Maut da suneha aaya ae,  
> Koyi booha kharhkaa gaya ni!  
> Mere balhde hoye matthe te,  
> Koyi thanddha hath rakh gaya ni!  

#### English Translation
> The solemn tidings of approaching death have arrived;  
> Someone has knocked softly upon my darkened door!  
> Upon my burning, fever-struck forehead  
> A soothing, cold palm has rested with maternal tenderness!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Death as a gentle, relieving touch cooling existential fever.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਡਰ ਏ, ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਝੋਰਾ ਏ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਤਾਂ ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ੀ ਨਾਲ਼ ਤੁਰਨਾ ਏ!  
> ਇਸ ਜੀਵਨ ਦੇ ਝੂਠੇ ਝੰਮੇਲਿਆਂ 'ਚੋਂ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਹੁਣ ਰੱਬ ਵੱਲ ਮੁੜਨਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> نہ کوئی ڈر اے، نہ کوئی جھورا اے،  
> میں تاں خوشی نال ترنا اے!  
> اس جیون دے جھوٹھے جھمیلیاں چوں،  
> میں ہن رب ول مڑنا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Na koyi darr ae, na koyi jhora ae,  
> Main taan khushi naal turna ae!  
> Iss jeevan de jhoothe jhameliyaan chon,  
> Main hun Rabb vall murhna ae!  

#### English Translation
> There is no terror lingering in my heart, nor any sorrow;  
> I prepare to set forth upon this journey with profound relief!  
> From the exhausting entanglements of this deceitful world,  
> I now turn my face back toward the eternal Divine!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Freedom from worldly entanglement.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਨੂੰ ਮੌਤ ਨਾ ਡਰਾ ਸਕੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਤਾਂ ਮੌਤ ਨੂੰ ਗਲ਼ ਲਾਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਸੂਰਜ ਡੁੱਬੂਗਾ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਦਾ,  
> ਉਹ ਇੱਕ ਨਵਾਂ ਗੀਤ ਗਾਵੇਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو نوں موت نہ ڈرا سکی،  
> اوہ تاں موت نوں گل لاوےگا!  
> جد سورج ڈبوگا شام دا،  
> اوہ اک نواں گیت گاوےگا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv nu maut na daraa saki,  
> Oh taan maut nu gal laavega!  
> Jad suraj dubbooga shaam da,  
> Oh ikk nawaan geet gaavega!  

#### English Translation
> Death could never strike terror into the heart of Shiv;  
> He shall clasp death warmly in an embrace like a bride!  
> When the evening sun sinks into the western horizon,  
> His lips shall burst into a triumphant, immortal hymn!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Eros and Thanatos unite as death is embraced as a bride.*
