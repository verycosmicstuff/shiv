# Chup De Vass

### **ਚੁੱਪ ਦੇ ਵੱਸ** • **چپ دے وس**

#### *"Under the Dominion of Silence"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਚੁੱਪ ਦੇ ਵੱਸ (چپ دے وس)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#MainuVidaKaro` `#Silence` `#Submission` `#Despair`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The poet surrenders unconditionally to silence, recognizing that words have lost their potency against the immensity of grief.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's acknowledgment that the deepest layer of human agony exists beyond the reach of lyric vocabulary.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Reflects Shiv's periods of depressive withdrawal where he refused to speak or recite for weeks at a time.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਸੀਂ ਚੁੱਪ ਦੇ ਵੱਸ ਪੈ ਗਏ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਬੋਲ ਨਾ ਸਾਥੋਂ ਹੋਵੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਅਸੀਂ ਸੀਨੇ ਸੰਭਾਲਿਆ,  
> ਉਹ ਅੰਦਰੋਂ ਅੰਦਰੀਂ ਰੋਵੇ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اسیں چپ دے وس پے گئے ہاں،  
> ہن بول نہ ساتھوں ہووے نی!  
> جو درد اسیں سینے سنبھالیا،  
> اوہ اندروں اندریں رووے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Aseen chup de vass pai gaye haan,  
> Hun bol na saathon hove ni!  
> Jo dard aseen seene sambhaaleya,  
> Oh andron andreen rove ni!  

#### English Translation
> We have fallen under the absolute dominion of Silence,  
> No words can cross the threshold of our lips anymore!  
> That colossal agony we treasured within our breast  
> Weeps in solitary darkness deep inside our soul!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Silence (*chup*) personified as an authoritarian master.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਲੋਕੀਂ ਕਹਿਣ ਇਹ ਗੁੰਗਾ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਗੀਤ ਇਹਦੇ ਸਭ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਏ ਨੇ!  
> ਕੀ ਜਾਣਨ ਉਹ ਪੱਥਰ ਦਿਲ ਵਾਲੇ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਅੰਦਰੋਂ ਸੁੱਕ ਗਏ ਨੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> لوکی کہن ایہ گنگا ہویا،  
> گیت ایہدے سبھ مک گئے نے!  
> کی جانن اوہ پتھر دل والے،  
> ساڈے ہنجھو اندروں سک گئے نے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Loki kehan eh gunga hoyea,  
> Geet ehde sabh mukk gaye ne!  
> Ki jaanan oh patthar dil vaale,  
> Saade hanjhu andron sukk gaye ne!  

#### English Translation
> Callous observers remark that he has turned stone-mute,  
> That the fountain of his songs has dried up forever!  
> What can those marble-hearted critics ever understand  
> Of how our tears turned to salt and bone deep within?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Critique of literary commentators who judged silence as creative death.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਹ ਚੁੱਪ ਸਾਡੀ ਕਬਰ ਬਣੇਗੀ,  
> ਇਹਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ ਸਮਾਵਾਂਗੇ!  
> ਜਦ ਮੌਤ ਆ ਕੇ ਬੂਹਾ ਖੜਕਾਊ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਅੰਤਮ ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਗਾਵਾਂਗੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ایہ چپ ساڈی قبر بنے گی،  
> ایہندے وچ اسیں سماواں گے!  
> جد موت آ کے بوہا کھڑکاؤ،  
> تاں انتم الوداع گاواں گے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Eh chup saadi kabr banegi,  
> Ehde vich aseen samaawaange!  
> Jad maut aa ke booha kharkaa-oo,  
> Taan antam alvida gaawaange!  

#### English Translation
> This vast silence shall become our living tomb,  
> Into whose quiet depths we shall dissolve forever!  
> Only when Death arrives to knock upon our locked door  
> Shall we break our silence to sing the final Adieu!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The final song reserved solely for Death.*
