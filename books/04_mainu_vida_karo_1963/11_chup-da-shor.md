# Chup Da Shor

### **ਚੁੱਪ ਦਾ ਸ਼ੋਰ** • **چپ دا شور**

#### *"The Deafening Clamor of Absolute Silence"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਚੁੱਪ ਦਾ ਸ਼ੋਰ (چپ دا شور)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Silence` `#Noise` `#Solitude` `#Echo` `#Void`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `modernism` | **Landscape Biome**: `snowy_cabin_night`

**Synopsis**: In the deserted house after loved ones have departed, the unbroken silence rings louder in the ears than an artillery barrage.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Chandigarh after moving into an empty bachelor flat.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Pioneering Punjabi modernism by treating silence not as the absence of sound, but as an unbearable psychological roar.

**Source**: *Punjabi Kavi Ate Kavitriyan (Marxist Critical Essays)* by Sant Singh Sekhon (1972, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, pp. 89–112). [Literary Criticism]

> "Sant Singh Sekhon termed Shiv the "Keats of Punjabi poetry" for his meter and folk metaphor, but charged him with escapism, arguing that romanticizing youth-suicide was reactionary."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਸ ਸੁੰਞੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਦੇ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਚੁੱਪ ਦਾ ਸ਼ੋਰ ਸੁਣਾਈ ਦਿੰਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਚਿਹਰਾ ਕਦੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਸ਼ੀਸ਼ੇ 'ਚੋਂ ਨਾ ਦਿਸਦਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اس سنجے کمرے دے اندر،  
> چپ دا شور سنائی دیندا اے!  
> جو چہرہ کدے میرا سی،  
> اوہ شیشے چوں نہ دسدا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Iss sunjne kamre de andar,  
> Chup da shor sunayi dinda ae!  
> Jo chehra kade mera si,  
> Oh sheeshe chon na dissda ae!  

#### English Translation
> Within the hollow walls of this deserted room,  
> The deafening clamor of absolute silence rings out!  
> That vibrant countenance which once belonged to me  
> Is no longer visible in the cold reflection of the mirror!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The paradox of deafening silence and vanishing identity.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕੰਧਾਂ ਵੀ ਗੱਲਾਂ ਕਰਦੀਆਂ ਨੇ,  
> ਪਰ ਕੋਈ ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਨਾ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਏ!  
> ਮੇਰੀ ਆਪਣੀ ਹੀ ਧੜਕਣ ਹੁਣ,  
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਮੌਤ ਵੱਲ ਬੁਲਾਉਂਦੀ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کندھاں وی گلاں کردیاں نے،  
> پر کوئی آواز نہ آوندی اے!  
> میری اپنی ہی دھڑکن ہن،  
> مینوں موت ول بلاوندی اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kandhaan vi gallan kardiyaan ne,  
> Par koyi aawaaz na aaundi ae!  
> Meri aapni hi dhadkan hun,  
> Mainu maut vall bulaaundi ae!  

#### English Translation
> Even the bare walls whisper among themselves,  
> Yet no articulate human sound reaches my ears!  
> The desperate throbbing of my own heartbeat now  
> Summons me inexorably toward the gates of death!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Heartbeat counting down mortal time.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਇਸ ਚੁੱਪ 'ਚੋਂ ਗੀਤ ਬਣਾਊ,  
> ਜੋ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਨੂੰ ਹਿਲਾ ਦੇਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਸਾਰੀ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਸੌਂ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਇਹ ਸ਼ੋਰ ਜਗ੍ਹਾ ਦੇਵੇਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو اس چپ چوں گیت بناؤ،  
> جو دنیا نوں ہلا دیوےگا!  
> جد ساری دنیا سوں جاوےگی،  
> ایہہ شور جگا دیوےگا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv iss chup chon geet banaau,  
> Jo duniya nu hila devega!  
> Jad saari duniya saun jaavegi,  
> Eh shor jaghaa devega!  

#### English Translation
> From the marrow of this silence, Shiv shall forge a song  
> That will shake the foundations of the sleeping world!  
> When all of humanity has sunk into complacent slumber,  
> This roaring silence shall awaken them to truth!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Silence transmuted into prophetic poetry.*
