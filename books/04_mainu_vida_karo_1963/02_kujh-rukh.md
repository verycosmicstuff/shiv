# Kujh Rukh (Rukh)

### **ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ (ਰੁੱਖਾਂ ਦੀ ਕਵਿਤਾ)** • **کجھ رخ**

#### *"Some Trees: The Tree Elegies"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ (ਰੁੱਖਾਂ ਦੀ ਕਵਿਤਾ) (کجھ رخ)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Trees` `#Kinship` `#Ecology` `#Mothers` `#Reincarnation`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: Shiv personifies trees as family members—some as sons, some as mothers, some as grandfathers—longing to be reincarnated as a tree.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala while resting beneath old neem and banyan trees.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> One of the most anthologized and revered ecological poems in South Asian literature, establishing tree kinship as spiritual ethics.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> One of the most celebrated ecological poems in the Punjabi language.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Lyrical, flowing rhythm capturing the sway of boughs in wind.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਪੁੱਤ ਲੱਗਦੇ ਨੇ,  
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਲੱਗਦੇ ਮਾਵਾਂ!  
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਨੂੰਹਾਂ ਧੀਆਂ ਲੱਗਦੇ,  
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਵਾਂਗ ਭਰਾਵਾਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کجھ رخ مینوں پت لگدے نے،  
> کجھ رخ لگدے ماواں!  
> کجھ رخ نونہاں دھیاں لگدے،  
> کجھ رخ وانگ بھراواں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kujh rukh mainu putt lagde ne,  
> Kujh rukh lagde maavan!  
> Kujh rukh noohaan dhiyaan lagde,  
> Kujh rukh vaang bharaavan!  

#### English Translation
> Some trees appear to me as beloved sons,  
> Some trees appear as nurturing mothers!  
> Some trees seem like daughters and brides,  
> And some stand sturdy like protective brothers!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Kinship with nature: trees form an entire family network replacing human estrangement.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਮੇਰੇ ਬਾਬੇ ਵਾਕਣ,  
> ਪੱਤਰ ਟਾਵਾਂ ਟਾਵਾਂ!  
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਮੇਰੀ ਦਾਦੀ ਵਰਗੇ,  
> ਚੂਰੀ ਪਾਵਣ ਕਾਵਾਂ!  
> ਇੱਕ ਮੇਰੀ ਮਹਿਬੂਬਾ ਵਾਕਣ,  
> ਮਿੱਠਾ ਅੰਬ ਦਾ ਬੂਟਾ!  
> ਕੁਝ ਰੁੱਖ ਮੇਰਾ ਦਿਲ ਕਰਦਾ ਏ,  
> ਮੋਢੇ ਚੁੱਕ ਖਿਡਾਵਾਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کجھ رخ میرے بابے واکن،  
> پتر تاواں تاواں!  
> کجھ رخ میری دادی ورگے،  
> چوری پاون کاواں!  
> اک میری محبوبہ واکن،  
> مٹھا انب دا بوٹا!  
> کجھ رخ میرا دل کردا اے،  
> موڈھے چک کھڈاواں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kujh rukh mere baabe vaakan,  
> Pattar taavaan taavaan!  
> Kujh rukh meri daadi varge,  
> Choori paavan kaavan!  
> Ikk meri mehbooba vaakan,  
> Mittha amb da boota!  
> Kujh rukh mera dil karda ae,  
> Modhe chukk khidaavaan!  

#### English Translation
> Some trees are like my venerable grandfather,  
> Bearing only sparse, weathered leaves upon their crown!  
> Some trees are gentle like my grandmother,  
> Crumbling sweet bread to feed the morning crows!  
> One tree is tender like my beloved—  
> A sweet, fragrant sapling of mango!  
> And some young saplings my heart yearns  
> To hoist upon my shoulders and bounce in play!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Intergenerational lineage mirrored in trees from withered elders to playful saplings.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੇਰਾ ਵੀ ਇਹ ਦਿਲ ਕਰਦਾ ਏ,  
> ਰੁੱਖ ਦੀ ਜੂਨੇ ਆਵਾਂ!  
> ਜੋ ਤੁਸਾਂ ਮੇਰਾ ਗੀਤ ਹੈ ਸੁਣਨਾ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਰੁੱਖਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਗਾਵਾਂ!  
> ਰੁੱਖ ਤਾਂ ਮੇਰੀ ਮਾਂ ਵਰਗੇ ਨੇ,  
> ਜੀਵਣ ਰੁੱਖਾਂ ਦੀਆਂ ਛਾਵਾਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میرا وی ایہہ دل کردا اے،  
> رخ دی جونے آواں!  
> جو تساں میرا گیت ہے سننا،  
> میں رخاں وچ گاواں!  
> رخ تاں میری ماں ورگے نے،  
> جیون رخاں دیاں چھاواں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Mera vi eh dil karda ae,  
> Rukh di joone aavan!  
> Jo tusaan mera geet hai sunna,  
> Main rukkhaan vich gaavan!  
> Rukh taan meri maan varge ne,  
> Jeevan rukkhaan diyan chaavan!  

#### English Translation
> My heart longs with deepest desire  
> To be reborn into the incarnation of a tree!  
> If you ever wish to hear my song again,  
> I shall sing to you from within the rustling leaves!  
> For trees are gentle like my own mother—  
> Long live the compassionate shelter of trees!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The ultimate longing to dissolve human suffering into photosynthetic life.*
