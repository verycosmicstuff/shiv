# Vidaai Geet

### **ਵਿਦਾਈ ਗੀਤ** • **وداعئی گیت**

#### *"The Valedictory Song of the Wayfarer"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਵਿਦਾਈ ਗੀਤ (وداعئی گیت)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Farewell` `#Valediction` `#Journey` `#Epitaph` `#Punjab`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: A grand valedictory song addressing the earth, the sky, the rivers, and future generations of Punjabi lovers.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala as the final concluding poem for the 1963 manuscript.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Closes the architectural arc of Mainu Vida Karo (1963), solidifying Shiv's status as the poet of eternal farewell.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੇਰਾ ਗੀਤ ਸੁਣੋ ਮੇਰੇ ਹਾਣੀਓ,  
> ਇਹ ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਤਾਰ ਹਿਲਾਈ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਿਲ 'ਚ ਸੀ ਉਹ ਕਹਿ ਦਿੱਤਾ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਜਾਣ ਦੀ ਰੁੱਤ ਆਈ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میرا گیت سنو میرے ہانیو،  
> ایہہ آخری تار ہلائی اے!  
> جو دل چ سی اوہ کہہ دتا،  
> ہن جان دی رت آئی اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Mera geet suno mere haaniyo,  
> Eh aakhri taar hilaayi ae!  
> Jo dil ch si oh keh ditta,  
> Hun jaan di rutt aayi ae!  

#### English Translation
> Listen to my song, O companions of my youth;  
> I have plucked the final resonating string of my instrument!  
> Whatever was concealed within my heart I have poured out;  
> Now the unsparing season of departure has arrived!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The final chord played upon the instrument of life.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕਿਸੇ ਯਾਰ ਨੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਕਦਰ ਨਾ ਜਾਣੀ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਦਿਲ ਨੇ ਭੇਤ ਨਾ ਪਾਇਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਦਰਦਾਂ ਦਾ ਇੱਕ ਪਹਾੜ ਸਾਂ,  
> ਜੋ ਧਰਤੀ 'ਤੇ ਢਹਿ ਆਇਆ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کسے یار نے میری قدر نہ جانی،  
> کسے دل نے بھیت نہ پایا نی!  
> میں درداں دا اک پہاڑ ساں،  
> جو دھرتی تے ڈھہہ آیا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kise yaar ne meri kadar na jaani,  
> Kise dil ne bhet na paaya ni!  
> Main dardaan da ikk pahaar saan,  
> Jo dharti te dheh aaya ni!  

#### English Translation
> Not a single companion recognized the sacred worth of my love;  
> Not a single living soul penetrated the mystery of my breast!  
> I was a colossal mountain of holy sorrows  
> That collapsed in devastating landslide upon the plains!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The mountain of sorrow collapsing onto the plain.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਮੇਰੀ ਇਸ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਮੇਰੇ ਸੰਸਾਰ ਨੂੰ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਤੁਰ ਚੱਲਿਆ,  
> ਸੌਂਪ ਕੇ ਆਪਣੇ ਪਿਆਰ ਨੂੰ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> الوداع میری اس زندگی نوں،  
> الوداع میرے سنسار نوں!  
> شیو تاں ہس کے تر چلیا،  
> سونپ کے اپنے پیار نوں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Alvida meri iss zindagi nu,  
> Alvida mere sansaar nu!  
> Shiv taan hass ke tur challeya,  
> Saump ke aapne pyaar nu!  

#### English Translation
> Farewell to this brief, agonized incarnation!  
> Farewell to this bustling, uncaring universe!  
> Shiv sets forth upon his journey with a serene smile,  
> Entrusting his immortal love to the keeping of time!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The peaceful, triumphant conclusion of Mainu Vida Karo.*
