# Mainu Vida Karo

### **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਵਿਦਾ ਕਰੋ (ਸਿਰਲੇਖ ਕਵਿਤਾ)** • **مینوں وداع کرو**

#### *"Bid Me Farewell: Title Poem"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਮੈਨੂੰ ਵਿਦਾ ਕਰੋ (ਸਿਰਲੇਖ ਕਵਿਤਾ) (مینوں وداع کرو)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Departure` `#Farewell` `#Mortality` `#Punjab` `#Altar`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The poet asks his companions and his native land to release him from life's obligations and bid him a solemn farewell.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Written during a period of acute sickness and hospitalization in Chandigarh.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> The title poem of his 1963 collection, composed in Chandigarh during a period of acute physical exhaustion and liver complications.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> Regarded as one of his most haunting valedictory anthems.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Sung in deep, sorrowful tarannum cadence.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (4 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਵਿਦਾ ਕਰੋ ਮੇਰੇ ਦੋਸਤੋ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਜਾਣ ਦਾ ਵੇਲਾ ਆ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਗੀਤ ਮੈਂ ਗਾਉਣੇ ਆਇਆ ਸਾਂ,  
> ਉਹ ਗ਼ਮ ਦੀ ਭੇਟ ਚੜ੍ਹਾ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> مینوں وداع کرو میرے دوستو،  
> میرا جان دا ویلا آ گیا اے!  
> جو گیت میں گاؤنے آیا ساں،  
> اوہ غم دی بھینٹ چڑھا گیا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Mainu vida karo mere dosto,  
> Mera jaan da vela aa gaya ae!  
> Jo geet main gaaune aaya saan,  
> Oh gham di bhent charha gaya ae!  

#### English Translation
> Bid me farewell, my beloved comrades,  
> The hour of my final departure has arrived!  
> Those celestial melodies I came to sing upon this earth  
> I have laid as an unblemished sacrifice upon the altar of grief!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The opening couplet presents the poet's life as a concluded mission of emotional offering.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਨਾ ਪਾਓ ਮੇਰੇ ਗਲ਼ ਹਾਰ ਕੋਈ,  
> ਨਾ ਰੋਵੋ ਮੇਰੇ ਜਾਣ ਪਿੱਛੋਂ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਤਾਂ ਇੱਕ ਬੱਦਲ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਸਾਂ,  
> ਜੋ ਵਰ੍ਹ ਕੇ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਿਆ ਸਾਵਣ 'ਚੋਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> نہ پاؤ میرے گل ہار کوئی،  
> نہ رووو میرے جان پچھوں!  
> میں تاں اک بادل وانگوں ساں،  
> جو ورھ کے مک گیا ساون چوں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Na paao mere gal haar koyi,  
> Na rovo mere jaan pichhon!  
> Main taan ikk baddal vaangu saan,  
> Jo varh ke mukk gaya Saavan chon!  

#### English Translation
> Place no celebratory garlands around my neck,  
> Nor shed bitter tears after I have departed!  
> For I was merely like a wandering raincloud  
> That spent itself in torrential rain and vanished from the monsoon sky!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The cloud metaphor: the poet exhausts his entire substance in tears and disappears.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦੀ ਧਰਤੀ ਤੋਂ,  
> ਜੋ ਪੀੜਾਂ ਦਾ ਵਰਦਾਨ ਲਿਆ!  
> ਉਹ ਸ਼ਬਦਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਢਾਲ਼ ਕੇ ਮੈਂ,  
> ਹਰ ਦਰਦੀਲੇ ਨੂੰ ਦਾਨ ਦਿੱਤਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں پنجاب دی دھرتی توں،  
> جو پیڑاں دا وردان لیا!  
> اوہ شبداں دے وچ ڈھال کے میں،  
> ہر دردیلے نوں دان دتا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main Punjab di dharti ton,  
> Jo peerran da vardaan liya!  
> Oh shabdaan de vich dhaal ke main,  
> Har dardeele nu daan ditta!  

#### English Translation
> From the sacred soil of Punjab,  
> Whatever blessing of agony I was granted,  
> I forged into immortal living words  
> And distributed freely as alms to every suffering soul!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Suffering as a spiritual blessing (vardaan) shared generously with humanity.*

* * *

### Stanza 4

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜੇ ਕੋਈ ਭੁੱਲ-ਚੁੱਕ ਹੋਈ ਹੋਵੇ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਬਖ਼ਸ਼ ਦੇਣਾ ਲੋਕੋ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਤਾਂ ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਕੀਤਾ,  
> ਹੋਰ ਨਾ ਕੁਝ ਜਾਣਿਆ ਲੋਕੋ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جے کوئی بھل-چک ہوئی ہووے،  
> تاں مینوں بخش دینا لوگو!  
> میں تاں صرف عشق کیتا،  
> ہور نہ کچھ جانیا لوگو!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Je koyi bhull-chukk hoyi hove,  
> Taan mainu bakhsh dena loko!  
> Main taan sirf ishq keeta,  
> Hor na kujh jaaneya loko!  

#### English Translation
> If any transgression was committed by my hand,  
> Then grant me forgiveness, O people of Punjab!  
> For I knew only how to love without measure,  
> And understood nothing else in this calculating world!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Humility before his audience, asking forgiveness for living solely through passion.*
