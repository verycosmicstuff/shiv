# Dard Da Khel

### **ਦਰਦ ਦਾ ਖੇਲ** • **درد دا کھیل**

#### *"The Staged Game of Suffering"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਦਰਦ ਦਾ ਖੇਲ (درد دا کھیل)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Game` `#Dice` `#Gambling` `#TragicGame` `#Illusion`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `modernism` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: Life is a rigged gambling game where the player wagers his heart and the house of fate always wins.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Chandigarh during nocturnal card games with artists.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Evokes the ancient Mahabharata motif of Chaupar (dice game), depicting human life as a rigged gambling match.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਸਾਂ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਨਾਲ਼ ਜੁਆ ਖੇਡਿਆ,  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਹਰ ਇੱਕ ਬਾਜ਼ੀ ਹਾਰ ਗਈ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਦਿਲ 'ਤੇ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਮਾਣ ਬੜਾ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਹੋਣੀ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਮਾਰ ਗਈ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اساں زندگی نال جوا کھیڈیا،  
> ساڈی ہر اک بازی ہار گئی!  
> جس دل تے سانوں مان بڑا سی،  
> اوہی ہونی سانوں مار گئی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Asaan zindagi naal juaa khediya,  
> Saadi har ikk baazi haar gayi!  
> Jis dil te saanu maan barha si,  
> Uhi honi saanu maar gayi!  

#### English Translation
> We played a reckless game of dice with life itself,  
> And every single wager ended in total defeat!  
> That passionate heart of which we were so immensely proud  
> Was the very stake upon which fate delivered death!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Life as a high-stakes gambling match lost to fate.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸਾਡੇ ਪਾਸੇ ਪੁੱਠੇ ਪੈ ਗਏ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਦੁਸ਼ਮਣ ਜਿੱਤ ਕੇ ਹੱਸ ਪਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਉਮਰ ਦਾ ਸਾਰਾ ਸਰਮਾਇਆ,  
> ਪਲਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੀ ਖੱਸ ਪਿਆ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ساڈے پاسے پٹھے پے گئے اڑیو،  
> دشمن جت کے ہس پیا اے!  
> ساڈی عمر دا سارا سرمایہ،  
> پلاں دے وچ ہی کھس پیا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Saade paase putthe pai gaye arhiyo,  
> Dushman jitt ke hass peya ae!  
> Saadi umar da saara sarmaaya,  
> Palaan de vich hi khass peya ae!  

#### English Translation
> The rolling dice fell contrary to our prayers, friends;  
> The callous adversary won and laughed in triumphant mockery!  
> The entire accumulated capital of our youthful years  
> Was plundered and snatched away in a heartbeat!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Total loss of artistic and emotional capital.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਹਾਰ ਕੇ ਵੀ ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਰੋਇਆ,  
> ਉਸ ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਬਾਜ਼ੀ ਛੱਡ ਦਿੱਤੀ!  
> ਇਸ ਜੱਗ ਦੇ ਝੂਠੇ ਖੇਲ 'ਚੋਂ,  
> ਉਸ ਆਪਣੀ ਹਸਤੀ ਕੱਢ ਲਿੱਤੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو ہار کے وی کدے نہ رویا،  
> اس ہس کے بازی چھڈ دتی!  
> اس جگ دے جھوٹھے کھیل چوں،  
> اس اپنی ہستی کڈھ لتی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv haar ke vi kade na royea,  
> Us hass ke baazi chhadd ditti!  
> Iss jagg de jhoothe khel chon,  
> Us aapni hasti kaddh litti!  

#### English Translation
> Even in defeat, Shiv shed not a single humiliated tear;  
> With a serene smile, he vacated the gambling table forever!  
> From this fraudulent, rigged game of the mortal world,  
> He withdrew his sovereign existence and walked free!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Victory achieved by walking away from the rigged game.*
