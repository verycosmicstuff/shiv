# Zehar Da Pyaala

### **ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਦਾ ਪਿਆਲਾ** • **زہر دا پیالہ**

#### *"The Goblet of Poison"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਦਾ ਪਿਆਲਾ (زہر دا پیالہ)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Poison` `#Goblet` `#Tragedy` `#Socrates`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `rebellion` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: The poet drinks from the poisoned goblet of life, finding that pain is the only authentic companion that never betrays.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala during late-night gatherings with fellow poets.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Often cited by biographer Mohan Bhandari as an allegory for Shiv's escalating dependence on country liquor to numb grief.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਦਾ ਪਿਆਲਾ ਪੀ ਲਿਆ ਏ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਦੀ ਹੁਣ ਲੋੜ ਨਹੀਂ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਦਰਦ ਨੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਸਾਥ ਦਿੱਤਾ,  
> ਉਸ ਦਰਦ ਦੀ ਕੋਈ ਤੋੜ ਨਹੀਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں زہر دا پیالہ پی لیا اے،  
> کوئی امرت دی ہن لوڑ نہیں!  
> جس درد نے میرا ساتھ دتا،  
> اس درد دی کوئی توڑ نہیں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main zehar da pyaala pee liya ae,  
> Koyi amrit di hun lorh nahin!  
> Jis dard ne mera saath ditta,  
> Us dard di koyi torh nahin!  

#### English Translation
> I have drained the poisoned goblet to its bitter dregs;  
> There is no longer any yearning for heavenly nectar!  
> That loyal agony which stood steadfast by my side  
> Possesses no antidote in all the realms of existence!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Acceptance of poison over false promises of healing.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਲੋਕੀਂ ਕਹਿੰਦੇ ਨੇ ਇਹ ਸ਼ਰਾਬੀ ਏ,  
> ਜੋ ਰਾਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਕੁਰਲਾਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਉਹ ਕੀ ਜਾਨਣ ਇਸ ਪਿਆਲੇ 'ਚੋਂ,  
> ਉਹ ਆਪਣਾ ਲਹੂ ਲੰਘਾਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> لوکی کہندے نے ایہہ شرابی اے،  
> جو راتاں نوں کرلاؤندا اے!  
> اوہ کی جانن اس پیالے چوں،  
> اوہ اپنا لہو لنگھاؤندا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Loki kehnde ne eh sharaabi ae,  
> Jo raataan nu kurlaaunda ae!  
> Oh ki jaanan iss pyaale chon,  
> Oh aapna lahu langhaaunda ae!  

#### English Translation
> The righteous declare: 'He is merely an unhinged drunkard  
> Who wails into the shadows of the lonely midnight!'  
> How could they ever perceive that from this chalice  
> He is swallowing the hot, sacrificial blood of his own heart?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Subverting the bourgeois moral condemnation of the bohemian.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਪਚਾ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਇਹ ਜੱਗ ਵੇਖ ਕੇ ਡਰਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਮਰ ਕੇ ਵੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਮਰ ਸਕਿਆ,  
> ਉਹ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਖੜ੍ਹਦਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو تاں ہس کے زہر پچا گیا،  
> ایہہ جگ ویکھ کے ڈردا اے!  
> جو مر کے وی نہیں مر سکیا،  
> اوہ گیتاں دے وچ کھڑدا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv taan hass ke zehar pachaa gaya,  
> Eh jagg vekh ke darda ae!  
> Jo mar ke vi nahin mar sakeya,  
> Oh geetan de vich kharhda ae!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv digested the deadly poison with an untroubled smile;  
> This cowardly world shudders merely to look upon him!  
> He who could not be slain even by death itself  
> Stands resurrected forever in the fortress of his songs!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The invincible immortality of the poetic soul.*
