# Aakhri Salaam

### **ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਸਲਾਮ** • **آخری سلام**

#### *"The Final Salutation to the World"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Mainu Vida Karo (1963)](../04_mainu_vida_karo_1963.md)
- **Original Title**: ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਸਲਾਮ (آخری سلام)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1963
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Salutation` `#Farewell` `#Peace` `#Sunset`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: A dignified, gracious bow to friends and foes alike, concluding the poet's earthly dialogue.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala as Shiv prepared for his move to Chandigarh in late 1963.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Composed as a formal farewell poem to the literary societies of Jalandhar and Ludhiana before his move to Chandigarh.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੇਰਾ ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਸਲਾਮ ਕਬੂਲ ਕਰੋ,  
> ਇਸ ਜੱਗ ਦੇ ਵਸਣ ਵਾਲ਼ਿਓ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਵੇਖ ਕੇ,  
> ਆਪਣਾ ਹਾਸਾ ਹੱਸਣ ਵਾਲ਼ਿਓ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میرا آخری سلام قبول کرو،  
> اس جگ دے وسن والیو!  
> میرے دل دے زخماں نوں ویکھ کے،  
> اپنا ہاسا ہسن والیو!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Mera aakhri salaam kabool karo,  
> Iss jagg de vasan waaliyo!  
> Mere dil de zakhmaan nu vekh ke,  
> Aapna haasa hassan waaliyo!  

#### English Translation
> Accept my final salutation and bow,  
> O residents of this crowded, bustling earth!  
> Ye who gazed upon the raw wounds of my heart  
> And burst into mocking laughter and derision!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Gracious farewell addressing detractors without bitterness.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਤੁਹਾਥੋਂ ਕੁਝ ਨਾ ਮੰਗਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਥੋੜ੍ਹਾ ਪਿਆਰ ਹੀ ਮੰਗਿਆ ਸੀ!  
> ਪਰ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਤਾਂ ਮੇਰੀ ਰੂਹ ਨੂੰ ਵੀ,  
> ਕੰਡਿਆਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਟੰਗਿਆ ਸੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں تہاڈھوں کچھ نہ منگیا سی،  
> صرف تھوڑا پیار ہی منگیا سی!  
> پر تسیں تاں میری روح نوں وی،  
> کنڈیاں دے وچ ٹنگیا سی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main tuhaathon kujh na mangeya si,  
> Sirf thohrha pyaar hi mangeya si!  
> Par tuseen taan meri rooh nu vi,  
> Kandiyan de vich tangeya si!  

#### English Translation
> I had sought no treasure or land from your hands;  
> I had asked only for a humble measure of love!  
> Yet you took my tender, defenseless spirit  
> And suspended it upon a cross of cruel thorns!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The modest request of the artist met with social crucifixion.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਚੱਲਿਆ ਏ ਦੂਰ ਕਿਤੇ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਕੋਈ ਕੰਡਾ ਨਾ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਉਸ ਰੱਬ ਦੇ ਚਰਨਾਂ 'ਚ ਬਹਿ ਕੇ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਗੀਤ ਸੁਖੀ ਹੋ ਸੋਵੇਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن شیو چلیا اے دور کتے،  
> جتھے کوئی کنڈا نہ ہووےگا!  
> اس رب دے چرناں چ بہہ کے،  
> میرا گیت سکھی ہو سووےگا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun Shiv challeya ae door kite,  
> Jitthe koyi kanda na hovega!  
> Us Rabb de charnaan ch beh ke,  
> Mera geet sukhi ho sovega!  

#### English Translation
> Now Shiv departs for a distant, unmapped realm  
> Where no thorn of cruelty shall ever pierce the flesh!  
> Resting beneath the tranquil feet of the Divine,  
> My weary song shall slumber in perpetual peace!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Slumber at the divine feet as ultimate sanctuary.*
