# Aate Diyan Chiriyaan (1962)

## ਆਟੇ ਦੀਆਂ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ • آٹے دیاں چڑیاں
### *"Sparrows of Dough"*

---

## 📚 Anthology Overview

- **Author**: Shiv Kumar Batalvi (ਸਿ਼ਵ ਕੁਮਾਰ ਬਟਾਲਵੀ / شیو کمار بٹالوی)
- **Publication Year**: 1962
- **Original Publisher**: Navyug Publishers, Delhi
- **Total Master Poems in this Edition**: 30

### Literary & Historical Background
> Takes its title from an ancient Punjabi ritual wherein women facing childlessness kneaded sparrows out of wheat dough and floated them down rivers as offerings. Shiv uses the dough sparrows as emblems of delicate creations doomed to dissolve in turbulent waters.

**Critical Reception**: Praised for transforming folk ritual into high psychological literature.

## 1. Aate Diyan Chiriyaan

### **ਆਟੇ ਦੀਆਂ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ (ਸਿਰਲੇਖ ਕਵਿਤਾ)** • **آٹے دیاں چڑیاں**

#### *"The Dough-Sparrows: Title Poem"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#DoughBirds` `#Folklore` `#Mothers` `#Innocence` `#Hunger`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: Village mothers mold dough sparrows to placate hungry children during hardship; Shiv uses this to depict the frail, counterfeit joys of human existence.
- **Genesis**: Inspired by watching women shape dough into bird figures before baking in the village tandoor.
- **Archival Context**: The title poem of Shiv's third book (1962), hailed by Punjabi critics as an extraordinary metaphor for surrogate maternal consolation. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Regarded as one of the most brilliant symbols of Punjabi maternal folk life in modern literature.
- **Musical Recitation**: Tender, rhythmic folk cadence.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮਾਏ ਨੀ ਮਾਏ ਤੇਰੀਆਂ ਗੁੰਨ੍ਹੀਆਂ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ,  
> ਉੱਡ ਨਾ ਸਕੀਆਂ ਅੰਬਰਾਂ ਵੱਲ ਨੀ!  
> ਭੁੱਖੇ ਢਿੱਡਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਬਹਿਲਾਵਣ ਖ਼ਾਤਰ,  
> ਤੂੰ ਵੀ ਘੜ ਲਈ ਨਵੀਂ ਛਲ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> مائے نی مائے تیریاں گنہیاں چڑیاں،  
> اڈ نہ سکیاں عنبراں ول نی!  
> بھکھے ڈھڈاں نوں بہلاون خاطر،  
> توں وی گھڑ لئی نویں چھل نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Maye ni maye teriyan gunnhiyaan chiriyaan,  
> Udd na sakiyaan ambaraan vall ni!  
> Bhukkhe dhiddaan nu behlaavan khaatar,  
> Toon vi gharh layi naveen chhal ni!  

**English Translation**:
> O mother, my mother, those sparrows you molded of wheat flour  
> Could never take wing and soar toward the blue heavens!  
> Merely to console the hollow pangs of hungry child-bellies,  
> You too devised this gentle, innocent deception!  

💡 *Commentary*: The mother sculpts dough birds to pacify hungry children, symbolizing how culture invents consolations for mortal lack.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਦ ਤੰਦੂਰ ਦੇ ਸੇਕ 'ਚ ਪਈਆਂ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਖੰਭ ਉਹਨਾਂ ਦੇ ਸੜ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਅਸਾਂ ਵੇਖੇ ਸੀ ਉੱਡਣੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੜ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جد تندور دے سیک چ پئیاں،  
> تاں کھنبھ اوہناں دے سڑ گئے نی!  
> جو سپنے اساں ویکھے سی اڈنے،  
> اوہ مٹی دے وچ گڑ گئے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Jad tandoor de sek ch paiyaan,  
> Taan khambh ohnaan de sarh gaye ni!  
> Jo supne assan vekhe si uddne,  
> Oh mitti de vich garh gaye ni!  

**English Translation**:
> When thrust into the fierce heat of the clay tandoor,  
> Their flour-molded wings scorched and burned away!  
> Those dreams we watched in childhood, yearning to fly,  
> Were swallowed back into the unyielding dust of the earth!  

💡 *Commentary*: Baking hardens the playful bird into rigid nourishment, destroying flight.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਡੀ ਉਮਰ ਵੀ ਆਟੇ ਵਰਗੀ,  
> ਜੋ ਹੱਥਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਮਿੱਧੀ ਗਈ!  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਬੇ-ਦਰਦ ਸਮੇਂ ਦੇ ਹੱਥੋਂ,  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਹੋਣੀ ਇੰਜ ਹੀ ਲਿਖੀ ਗਈ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ساڈی عمر وی آٹے ورگی،  
> جو ہتھاں وچ مدھی گئی!  
> کسے بے-درد سمے دے ہتھوں،  
> ساڈی ہونی انج ہی لکھی گئی!  

**Romanized**:
> Saadi umar vi aate vargi,  
> Jo hathaan vich middhi gayi!  
> Kise be-dard samey de hatthon,  
> Saadi honi injj hi likhi gayi!  

**English Translation**:
> Our entire lifespan too was like moist kneaded flour,  
> Kneaded and bruised beneath unfeeling hands!  
> By the callous hand of an indifferent fate,  
> Our destiny was inscribed in this very manner!  

💡 *Commentary*: The human soul kneaded and bruised by external circumstance.

##### Stanza 4

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੇ ਗੀਤ ਵੀ ਆਟੇ ਦੀਆਂ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ,  
> ਜੋ ਲੋਕਾਂ ਨੇ ਚੱਬ ਛੱਡੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਪਰ ਇਹਨਾਂ ਦੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੀਆਂ ਹੂਕਾਂ,  
> ਜੱਗ ਦੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਖੁੱਭ ਛੱਡੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دے گیت وی آٹے دیاں چڑیاں،  
> جو لوکاں نے چب چھڈے نے!  
> پر ایہناں دے دل دیاں ہوکاں،  
> جگ دے سینے کھبھ چھڈے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv de geet vi aate diyan chiriyaan,  
> Jo lokaan ne chabb chhadde ne!  
> Par ehnaan de dil diyan hookan,  
> Jagg de seene khubh chhadde ne!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv's verses too are dough-sparrows,  
> Which the public consumed to satisfy their hunger!  
> Yet the desperate wails echoing from their heart  
> Remain lodged forever inside the collective conscience of the world!  

💡 *Commentary*: Poetry consumed as nourishment while its tragic origin pierces the reader.

## 2. Mere Dila Mere Aazaad Panchhi

### **ਮੇਰੇ ਦਿਲਾ ਮੇਰੇ ਆਜ਼ਾਦ ਪੰਛੀ** • **میرے دلا میرے آزاد پنچھی**

#### *"O My Heart, My Wild Free Bird"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Freedom` `#Cage` `#SoulBird` `#Sovereignty`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The poet commands his restless soul-bird to resist the golden cages of social convention.
- **Genesis**: Written during Shiv's refusal to pursue a conventional government clerical career.
- **Archival Context**: Written during Shiv's vocal refusal to compromise his lyric voice for government bureaucratic posts in Chandigarh. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Anthem of artistic sovereignty over bureaucratic conformity.
- **Musical Recitation**: Soaring, lyrical rhythm.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੇਰੇ ਦਿਲਾ ਮੇਰੇ ਆਜ਼ਾਦ ਪੰਛੀ,  
> ਤੂੰ ਕਿਸ ਪਿੰਜਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਫਸ ਗਿਆ ਏਂ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਸੋਨੇ ਦੀਆਂ ਸੀਖਾਂ ਪਿੱਛੇ,  
> ਤੂੰ ਰੋ ਰੋ ਕੇ ਹੀ ਹੱਸ ਪਿਆ ਏਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میرے دلا میرے آزاد پنچھی،  
> توں کس پنجرے وچ پھس گیا ایں!  
> جتھے سونے دیاں سیخاں پچھے،  
> توں رو رو کے ہی ہس پیا ایں!  

**Romanized**:
> Mere dila mere aazaad panchhi,  
> Toon kis pinjre vich phas gaya ain!  
> Jithe sone diyan seekhaan pichhe,  
> Toon ro ro ke hi hass peya ain!  

**English Translation**:
> O my heart, my wild, unfettered bird,  
> In what gilded cage have you become ensnared?  
> Where behind bars of polished gold,  
> You have learned to weep through forced, hollow smiles!  

💡 *Commentary*: The golden cage represents material comfort at the expense of spiritual liberty.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਛੱਡ ਦੇ ਇਹ ਸ਼ਹਿਰੀ ਬੰਦਿਸ਼ਾਂ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਤੇ ਮੁੜ ਚੱਲ ਆਪਣੇ ਬੇਲਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਰਾਵੀ ਦਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਵਗਦਾ,  
> ਉਹਨਾਂ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੇ ਖੁੱਲ੍ਹੇ ਮੇਲਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> چھڈ دے ایہہ شہری بندشاں نوں،  
> تے مڑ چل اپنے بیلیاں نوں!  
> جتھے راوی دا پانی وگدا،  
> اوہناں پنڈ دے کھلے میلیاں نوں!  

**Romanized**:
> Chhadd de eh shehri bandishaan nu,  
> Te murh chal apne beleyaan nu!  
> Jithe Raavi da paani vagda,  
> Ohnaan pind de khullhe meleyaan nu!  

**English Translation**:
> Break free from these stifling urban constraints,  
> And return to your wild, untamed riverbanks!  
> Where the holy waters of the Ravi flow freely,  
> To the boundless, joyous gatherings of your rural homeland!  

💡 *Commentary*: Nostalgia for the pre-Partition agrarian riverbanks along the Ravi.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜੇ ਖੰਭ ਤੇਰੇ ਕੱਟ ਦਿੱਤੇ ਹਾਕਮਾਂ ਨੇ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਤੂੰ ਗੀਤਾਂ 'ਚ ਉਡਾਰੀ ਭਰ ਲੈ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੇ ਪੈਰਾਂ 'ਚ ਜ਼ੰਜੀਰਾਂ ਪਈਆਂ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਅੰਬਰਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਸਜਦਾ ਕਰ ਲੈ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جے کھنبھ تیرے کٹ دتے حاکماں نے،  
> تاں توں گیتاں چ اڈاری بھر لے نی!  
> جے پیراں چ زنجیراں پئیاں،  
> تاں عنبراں نوں سجدہ کر لے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Je khambh tere katt ditte haakmaan ne,  
> Taan toon geetan ch udaari bhar lai ni!  
> Je pairaan ch zanjeeraan paiyaan,  
> Taan ambaraan nu sajda kar lai ni!  

**English Translation**:
> If the rulers have clipped the feathers of your wings,  
> Then take flight upon the soaring currents of your songs!  
> If heavy iron shackles bind your earthly feet,  
> Then bow your forehead in worship to the boundless skies!  

💡 *Commentary*: Artistic creation as the ultimate transcendence of political oppression.

##### Stanza 4

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਉੱਡੂਗਾ ਹੱਦਾਂ ਪਾਰ ਕਰਕੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਸਰਹੱਦ ਨਾ ਰੋਕ ਸਕੇਗੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਅੱਗ ਬਲੇਗੀ ਇਸ ਸੀਨੇ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਹਵਾ ਨਾ ਝੋਕ ਸਕੇਗੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو تاں اڈوگا حداں پار کر کے،  
> کوئی سرحد نہ روک سکے گی!  
> جو اگ بلے گی اس سینے اندر،  
> کوئی ہوا نہ جھوک سکے گی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv taan udduga haddaan paar karke,  
> Koyi sarhad na rok sakegi!  
> Jo agg balegi is seene andar,  
> Koyi hawa na jhok sakegi!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv shall fly far across every man-made border,  
> No partition wire can ever hold back his spirit!  
> The flame that blazes inside this defiant chest  
> No worldly tempest shall ever be able to quench!  

💡 *Commentary*: Shiv's explicit rejection of the Radcliffe border dividing East and West Punjab.

| **Raavi (ਰਾਵੀ)** | *Raa-vee* | The River Ravi | The river of Lahore and Shakargarh that was partitioned in 1947. |

## 3. Panchhi Ho Jaavan

### **ਪੰਛੀ ਹੋ ਜਾਵਾਂ** • **پنچھی ہو جاواں**

#### *"Would That I Were a Bird"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Flight` `#Escape` `#Skies` `#Longing`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The yearning to transform into a migratory bird and fly across borders to see the beloved one last time.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala as Shiv gazed up at Siberian crane flocks passing over the Punjab plains.
- **Archival Context**: Exemplifies the Sufi motif of the Murgh-e-Rooh (Soul-Bird) longing to escape the cage of bodily mortality. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਾਸ਼ ਮੈਂ ਪੰਛੀ ਹੋ ਜਾਵਾਂ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਉੱਡ ਕੇ ਤੇਰੇ ਵਿਹੜੇ ਜਾਵਾਂ!  
> ਤੇਰੇ ਘਰ ਦੀ ਕੰਧ 'ਤੇ ਬਹਿ ਕੇ,  
> ਦਰਦ ਵਿਛੋੜੇ ਦੇ ਮੈਂ ਗਾਵਾਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کاش میں پنچھی ہو جاواں اڑیو،  
> اڈ کے تیرے ویہڑے جاواں!  
> تیرے گھر دی کندھ تے بہہ کے،  
> درد وچھوڑے دے میں گاواں!  

**Romanized**:
> Kaash main panchhi ho jaavan arhiyo,  
> Udd ke tere vehre jaavan!  
> Tere ghar di kandh te beh ke,  
> Dard vichhorhe de main gaavan!  

**English Translation**:
> Would that I could transform into a wild bird, friends,  
> And fly straight into your secluded courtyard!  
> Perching upon the parapet of your dwelling,  
> I would sing the heartbreaking songs of separation!  

💡 *Commentary*: Metamorphosis as the only vehicle to breach social barricades.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਪੁੱਛੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਜਾਤ ਨਾ ਧਰਮ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਰੋਕੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਰਾਹ ਨੀ!  
> ਤੇਰੇ ਨੈਣਾਂ 'ਚੋਂ ਹੰਝੂ ਪੀ ਕੇ,  
> ਮੁੱਕ ਜਾਵੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਸਾਹ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ کوئی پچھے میری ذات نہ دھرم،  
> نہ کوئی روکے میری راہ نی!  
> تیرے نیناں چوں ہنجھو پی کے،  
> مک جاوے میرا ساہ نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Na koyi puchhe meri jaat na dharam,  
> Na koyi roke meri raah ni!  
> Tere nainan chon hanjhu pee ke,  
> Mukk jaave mera saah ni!  

**English Translation**:
> No rigid elder would interrogate my caste or creed;  
> No sentry would block my path with iron bars!  
> Drinking down the falling tears from your weeping eyes,  
> Let my final breath quietly expire in peace!  

💡 *Commentary*: The bird transcends social hierarchies and caste prohibitions.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਪੰਛੀ ਬਣ ਕੇ ਮਰਿਆ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾ ਪਾਇਆ ਚੋਗ ਨੀ!  
> ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆ 'ਚ ਰਹਿ ਗਿਆ ਬਾਕੀ,  
> ਉਸਦਾ ਅਮਰ ਵਿਯੋਗ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو تاں پنچھی بن کے مریا،  
> کسے نہ پایا چوگ نی!  
> اس دنیا چ رہ گیا باقی،  
> اس دا امر ویوگ نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv taan panchhi ban ke mareya,  
> Kise na paaya chog ni!  
> Iss duniya ch reh gaya baaki,  
> Usda amar viyog ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv perished having transformed into that wild bird,  
> With not a single soul scattering grains for his nourishment!  
> All that remains behind in this fleeting world  
> Is the immortal lament of his holy separation!  

💡 *Commentary*: The starving soul-bird immortalized through song.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਚੋਗ (Chog)** | *Chog* | Birdseed / Grain scattered for birds | Spiritual nourishment provided by divine grace or the beloved's affection. |

## 4. Thohar De Phull

### **ਥੋਹਰ ਦੇ ਫੁੱਲ** • **تھوہر دے پھل**

#### *"Blossoms of the Thorny Cactus"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Cactus` `#Thorns` `#BarrenLand` `#Pain`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `barren_mountain_loona`
- **Synopsis**: Even the thorny desert cactus produces fragile blossoms that bleed when touched.
- **Genesis**: Inspired by the wild cactus hedges dividing agrarian field boundaries across Gurdaspur.
- **Archival Context**: Extends the cactus metaphor from Piran da Paraga into a meditation on beauty flourishing amid hostility. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਸੀਂ ਥੋਹਰਾਂ ਦੇ ਫੁੱਲ ਹਾਂ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਕੰਡਿਆਂ ਵਿਚਕਾਰ ਖਿੜੇ ਹਾਂ!  
> ਜੋ ਵੀ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਛੂਹਣ ਆਇਆ,  
> ਉਸਦੇ ਹੱਥ ਲਹੂ-ਲੁਹਾਣ ਕਰ ਛੱਡੇ ਹਾਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اسیں تھوہراں دے پھل ہاں اڑیو،  
> کنڈیاں وچکار کھڑے ہاں!  
> جو وی سانوں چھوہن آیا،  
> اس دے ہتھ لہو لہان کر چھڈے ہاں!  

**Romanized**:
> Aseen thohraan de phull haan arhiyo,  
> Kandiyan vichkaar khirhe haan!  
> Jo vi saanu chhoohan aaya,  
> Usde hath lahu-luhaan kar chhadde haan!  

**English Translation**:
> We are the blossoms of the wild desert cactus, friends,  
> Blooming in the very heart of vicious thorns!  
> Whosoever reached out to touch our petals in love  
> Departed with palms lacerated and dripping blood!  

💡 *Commentary*: The paradox of involuntary cruelty in the wounded artist.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਡੀ ਕਿਸਮਤ 'ਚ ਮਹਿਕ ਨਾ ਲਿਖੀ,  
> ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਚੋਭਾਂ ਹੀ ਚੋਭਾਂ ਨੇ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਧੁੱਪਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੜ ਗਏ,  
> ਸਾਡੀਆਂ ਸੜੀਆਂ ਸਭ ਰੀਝਾਂ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ساڈی قسمت چ مہک نہ لکھی،  
> صرف چوبھاں ہی چوبھاں نے!  
> اسیں دھپاں دے وچ سڑ گئے،  
> ساڈیاں سڑیاں سبھ ریجھاں نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Saadi kismat ch mehak na likhi,  
> Sirf chobhaan hi chobhaan ne!  
> Aseen dhuppan de vich sarh gaye,  
> Saadiyan sariyan sabh reejhaan ne!  

**English Translation**:
> Sweet fragrance was not inscribed in our destiny;  
> Nothing but barbed prickles and wounds belong to our fate!  
> We were scorched dry beneath the blistering summer sun,  
> And all our youthful desires turned to brittle ash!  

💡 *Commentary*: Drought and prickles replacing garden roses.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੇ ਗੀਤ ਵੀ ਥੋਹਰ ਵਰਗੇ,  
> ਜੋ ਪੱਥਰਾਂ 'ਚੋਂ ਵੀ ਉੱਗਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਸੁਣਦੇ ਨੇ ਦਿਲ ਵਾਲ਼ੇ,  
> ਉਹਨਾਂ ਦੇ ਨੈਣਾਂ 'ਚੋਂ ਹੰਝੂ ਵਗਦੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دے گیت وی تھوہر ورگے،  
> جو پتھراں چوں وی اگدے نے!  
> جو سندے نے دل والے،  
> اوہناں دے نیناں چوں ہنجھو وگدے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv de geet vi thohar varge,  
> Jo patthran chon vi uggde ne!  
> Jo sunde ne dil waale,  
> Ohnaan de nainan chon hanjhu vagde ne!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv's verses too are like wild desert cacti,  
> Sprouting obstinately from barren boulders of granite!  
> Those endowed with a compassionate spirit who listen  
> Find rivers of tears flowing involuntarily from their eyes!  

💡 *Commentary*: Poetry that thrives in conditions impossible for conventional art.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਥੋਹਰ (Thohar)** | *Tho-har* | Euphorbia / Prickly cactus | Hardy thorny plant growing in desolate wastelands, symbol of stubborn survival and bitter grief. |

## 5. Mittraan Di Yaad

### **ਮਿੱਤਰਾਂ ਦੀ ਯਾਦ** • **متراں دی یاد**

#### *"The Remembrance of Lost Comrades"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Comrades` `#Nostalgia` `#Separation` `#Youth`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: Memories of childhood comrades who scattered across continents, leaving the village empty and haunted.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala during the mass migration of Punjabi youth to England.
- **Archival Context**: Written after several of Shiv's closest schoolmates moved to the UK during the early 1960s Punjabi diaspora wave. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮਿੱਤਰਾਂ ਦੀ ਯਾਦ ਆਈ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਵਿਹੜਾ ਸੁੰਞਾ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਹੱਸਦੇ-ਖੇਡਦੇ ਹੁੰਦੇ ਸਾਂ,  
> ਉਹ ਥਾਂ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ 'ਚ ਖੋ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> متراں دی یاد آئی اڑیو،  
> ویہڑا سنجا ہو گیا نی!  
> جتھے ہسدے کھڈدے ہندے ساں،  
> اوہ تھاں ہنجھواں چ کھو گیا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Mittraan di yaad aayi arhiyo,  
> Vehra sunjna ho gaya ni!  
> Jitthe hassde-khed-de hunde saan,  
> Oh thaan hanjuwan ch kho gaya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The sudden remembrance of lost comrades arrived, friends,  
> And the ancestral courtyard turned desolate and hollow!  
> That spot where once we laughed and played in abandon  
> Has dissolved and vanished into an ocean of tears!  

💡 *Commentary*: The traumatic emptiness left by diaspora departures.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੋਈ ਪਰਦੇਸ ਸਿਧਾਰ ਗਿਆ ਏ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦੀ ਗੋਦ 'ਚ ਸੌਂ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਉਮਰ ਦਾ ਹਾਸਾ-ਠੱਠਾ,  
> ਇੱਕ ਪਲਕ ਝਪਕਦੇ ਖੋਹ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کوئی پردیس سدھار گیا اے،  
> کوئی مٹی دی گود چ سوں گیا اے!  
> ساڈی عمر دا ہاسا ٹھٹھا،  
> اک پلک جھپکدے کھوہ گیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Koyi pardes sidhaar gaya ae,  
> Koyi mitti di godd ch saun gaya ae!  
> Saadi umar da haasa-thattha,  
> Ikk palak jhapakde khoh gaya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> One comrade has sailed away into far foreign lands;  
> Another has fallen asleep forever inside the lap of the earth!  
> All the radiant laughter of our youthful years  
> Was snatched away in the twinkling of an eye!  

💡 *Commentary*: Twin fates of migration and premature mortality.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕੱਲਾ ਬਹਿ ਕੇ ਰੋਂਦਾ ਏ,  
> ਇਸ ਉੱਜੜੀ ਹੋਈ ਜੂਹ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਆਣ ਕੇ ਹਾਲ ਨਾ ਪੁੱਛੇ,  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਤੜਪਦੀ ਹੋਈ ਰੂਹ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو کلا بہہ کے روندا اے،  
> اس اجڑی ہوئی جوہ دے وچ!  
> کوئی آن کے حال نہ پچھے،  
> ساڈی تڑپدی ہوئی روح دے وچ!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv kalla beh ke ronda ae,  
> Iss ujjri hoyi jooh de vich!  
> Koyi aan ke haal na puchhe,  
> Saadi tarapdi hoyi rooh de vich!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv sits completely alone, weeping,  
> Within this devastated and abandoned village boundary!  
> Not a single soul comes to inquire after our plight  
> Or soothe the writhing torment of our spirit!  

💡 *Commentary*: The poet left behind as the solitary chronicler of ruin.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਪਰਦੇਸ (Pardes)** | *Par-des* | Foreign lands / Diaspora | The painful reality of Punjabi economic migration separating families and lifelong comrades. |

<a id="kore-kaghaz-te-likh-bhejea"></a>

## 6. Kore Kaghaz Te Likh Bhejea

### **ਕੋਰੇ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ 'ਤੇ ਲਿਖ ਭੇਜਿਆ** • **کورے کاغذ تے لکھ بھیجیا**

#### *"I Inscribed Upon a Blank Sheet of Paper"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#BlankPage` `#UnsentLetter` `#Ink` `#Silence`
- **Theme / Biome**: `modernism` / `snowy_cabin_night`
- **Synopsis**: Sending a completely blank sheet of paper to the beloved, because words cannot contain the magnitude of the wound.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala when Shiv found himself unable to write to his estranged muse.
- **Archival Context**: Famous meta-poetic exploration of the limits of language, where silence says more than written words. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਕੋਰੇ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ 'ਤੇ ਲਿਖ ਭੇਜਿਆ,  
> ਤੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ ਨਾ ਆਇਆ ਮੁੱਖ 'ਤੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਮੈਂ ਸੀਨੇ 'ਚ ਸਾਂਭਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਡਿੱਗ ਪਿਆ ਸੁੱਕੇ ਰੁੱਖ 'ਤੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں کورے کاغذ تے لکھ بھیجیا،  
> تیرا نام نہ آیا مکھ تے نی!  
> جو درد میں سینے چ سانبھیا سی،  
> اوہ ڈگ پیا سکے رخ تے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Main kore kaghaz te likh bhejea,  
> Tera naam na aaya mukkh te ni!  
> Jo dard main seene ch saambheya si,  
> Oh digg peya sukke rukkh te ni!  

**English Translation**:
> I dispatched a message upon a blank white page,  
> Refusing to let your name pass upon my lips!  
> That immense agony I sheltered deep inside my chest  
> Has collapsed upon the branches of a withered tree!  

💡 *Commentary*: The blank page as the highest form of articulate speech.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਸ ਕੋਰੇਪਨ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੀ ਸਭ ਕੁਝ ਸੀ,  
> ਜੋ ਲਫ਼ਜ਼ਾਂ 'ਚ ਨਾ ਆ ਸਕਿਆ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਜੋ ਤੂਫ਼ਾਨ ਸੀ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਸ਼ਾਇਰ ਨਾ ਗਾ ਸਕਿਆ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اس کورےپن دے وچ ہی سبھ کچھ سی،  
> جو لفظاں چ نہ آ سکیا!  
> میرے دل دا جو طوفان سی،  
> کوئی شاعر نہ گا سکیا!  

**Romanized**:
> Iss korepan de vich hi sabh kujh si,  
> Jo lafzaan ch na aa sakeya!  
> Mere dil da jo toofaan si,  
> Koyi shaayar na gaa sakeya!  

**English Translation**:
> Within this stark blankness resided everything  
> Which ordinary words could never dare utter!  
> That apocalyptic tempest raging in my breast  
> No living poet could ever encompass in song!  

💡 *Commentary*: The inadequacy of conventional poetic vocabulary.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਇਹ ਕੋਰਾ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ ਹੀ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਖ਼ਤ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਪੜ੍ਹੇਗੀ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਰੋਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਇਹ ਸੱਚ ਦਾ ਪਰਬਤ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دا ایہہ کورا کاغذ ہی،  
> میرا آخری خط ہووےگا!  
> جد پڑھوگی دنیا رووےگی،  
> ایہہ سچ دا پربت ہووےگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv da eh kora kaghaz hi,  
> Mera aakhri khat hovega!  
> Jad parhegi duniya rovegi,  
> Eh sach da parbat hovega!  

**English Translation**:
> This pristine blank parchment of Shiv's  
> Shall stand as my final testament and letter!  
> When future generations read its silence, they shall weep;  
> It shall stand enduring like an unyielding mountain of truth!  

💡 *Commentary*: The silent letter outlasting vocal poetry.

## 7. Sassi Punnu Da Dard

### **ਸੱਸੀ-ਪੁੰਨੂੰ ਦਾ ਦਰਦ** • **سسی پنوں دا درد**

#### *"The Torment of Sassi in the Blazing Sands"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Sassi` `#Desert` `#BurningSands` `#Thal` `#LoveMartyrdom`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `barren_mountain_loona`
- **Synopsis**: Invoking the legendary romance of Sassi who perished in the burning sands of the Thal desert searching for Punnu.
- **Genesis**: Composed after reading Hashim Shah's masterpiece at his father's library in Batala.
- **Archival Context**: Pays homage to Hashim Shah's classical Punjabi Qissa of Sassi Punnu, transmuting the desert of Thal into Shiv's own psychological terrain. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਸੱਸੀ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਥਲ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਤਪਦੀ ਰੇਤ 'ਤੇ ਤੁਰਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਪੈਰਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਛਾਲੇ ਪਏ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਤਨ ਮਿੱਟੀ 'ਚ ਖੁਰਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں سسی وانگوں تھل دے وچ،  
> تپدی ریت تے تریا نی!  
> میرے پیراں دے وچ چھالے پئے،  
> ساڈا تن مٹی چ کھریا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Main Sassi vaangu thal de vich,  
> Tapdi ret te tureya ni!  
> Mere pairaan de vich chhaale paye,  
> Saada tan mitti ch khureya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Like Sassi wandering through the howling Thar desert,  
> I walked barefoot upon the blistering sands!  
> Agonizing blisters erupted beneath my soles,  
> And my mortal flesh dissolved into the hot clay!  

💡 *Commentary*: Identification with the tragic female protagonist of Punjabi folklore.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪੁੰਨੂੰ ਦੇ ਊਠਾਂ ਦਾ ਪੈੜ ਨਾ ਲੱਭਾ,  
> ਲੰਘ ਗਿਆ ਕਾਫ਼ਲਾ ਦੂਰ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੀ ਪਿਆਸ ਬੁਝਾਵਣ ਵਾਲ਼ਾ ਕੋਈ ਨਾ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਲਹੂ ਵਹਿ ਗਿਆ ਜ਼ਰੂਰ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پنوں دے اوٹھاں دا پیڑ نہ لبھا،  
> لنگھ گیا قافلہ دور نی!  
> میری پیاس بجھاون والا کوئی نہ،  
> میرا لہو وہہ گیا ضرور نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Punnu de oothaan da pairh na labbha,  
> Langh gaya kaafila door ni!  
> Meri pyaas bujhaavan waala koyi na,  
> Mera lahu veh gaya zaroor ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Not a trace remained of the footprints of Punnu's camels;  
> The distant caravan has vanished far beyond the horizons!  
> There was no merciful soul to quench my dying thirst,  
> While my life's blood poured out upon the unpitying dunes!  

💡 *Commentary*: The vanished caravan leaving only wind-swept sand.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਵੀ ਸੱਸੀ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਮਰੇਗਾ,  
> ਇਸ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਮਾਰੂਥਲ ਅੰਦਰ!  
> ਪਰ ਉਸਦੇ ਗੀਤ ਸਦਾ ਗੂੰਜਣਗੇ,  
> ਹਰ ਦਰਦੀਲੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਅੰਦਰ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو وی سسی وانگوں مرےگا،  
> اس برہا دے ماروتھل اندر!  
> پر اس دے گیت سدا گونجنگے،  
> ہر دردیلے دل دے اندر!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv vi Sassi vaangu marega,  
> Iss birha de maaruthal andar!  
> Par usde geet sada goonjange,  
> Har dardeele dil de andar!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv too shall meet his death like Sassi  
> Inside this howling desert wasteland of separation!  
> Yet his ringing verses shall echo perpetually  
> Deep within the hollow sanctuary of every wounded heart!  

💡 *Commentary*: Transcendence achieved through tragic martyrdom in the desert.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਸੱਸੀ (Sassi)** | *Sas-see* | Legendary heroine of Sindh and Punjab | Epitome of relentless, faithful passion who crossed the scorching Thar desert in pursuit of her abducted lover Punnu. |
| **ਥਲ (Thal)** | *Thal* | The great sandy desert of Punjab/Sindh | Metaphor for the soul's barren wasteland during agonizing separation. |

## 8. Kachhe Dudh Di Dhaar

### **ਕੱਚੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਦੀ ਧਾਰ** • **کچے ددھ دی دھار**

#### *"The Stream of Fresh Unboiled Milk"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Milk` `#Purity` `#VillageMorning` `#Fragility` `#Pastoral`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The pure white stream of unboiled milk freshly drawn at dawn represents the fleeting, unblemished innocence of early love.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala during early morning visits to the family cattle-byres.
- **Archival Context**: Celebrates the pristine morning rituals of Majha villages, using unboiled milk as an image of uncorrupted, vulnerable youth. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਤੇਰਾ ਪਿਆਰ ਸੀ ਕੱਚੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਦੀ ਧਾਰ,  
> ਜੋ ਧਰਤੀ 'ਤੇ ਡੁੱਲ੍ਹ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਨਾ ਉਹ ਭਾਂਡੇ 'ਚ ਸੰਭਾਲਿਆ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਸਾਰਾ ਨੂਰ ਹੀ ਭੁੱਲ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> تیرا پیار سی کچے ددھ دی دھار،  
> جو دھرتی تے ڈلھ گیا نی!  
> نہ اوہ بھانڈے چ سنبھالیا گیا،  
> ساڈا سارا نور ہی بھل گیا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Tera pyaar si kachhe dudh di dhaar,  
> Jo dharti te dullh gaya ni!  
> Na oh bhaande ch sambhaaleya gaya,  
> Saada saara noor hi bhull gaya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Your love was like a fresh stream of unboiled milk  
> That spilled irrevocably upon the thirsty earth!  
> It could not be gathered back into the earthen vessel,  
> And all its radiant purity was lost in the mud!  

💡 *Commentary*: Spilled milk as the classic Punjabi metaphor for irreversible emotional waste.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਚਿੱਟਾ ਦੁੱਧ ਵੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਨੇ ਕਦਰ ਨਾ ਜਾਣੀ ਨੀ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਉਮਰਾਂ ਭਰ ਰੋਂਦੇ ਰਹੇ,  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਈ ਜਿੰਦ ਨਿਮਾਣੀ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> چٹا ددھ وی مٹی ہویا،  
> کسے نے قدر نہ جانی نی!  
> اسیں عمراں بھر روندے رہے،  
> ساڈی مک گئی جند نمانی نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Chitta dudh vi mitti hoya,  
> Kise ne kadar na jaani ni!  
> Aseen umraan bhar ronde rahe,  
> Saadi mukk gayi jind nimaani ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The glistening white milk dissolved into the gray dust;  
> Not a single soul recognized its divine sacredness!  
> We wept through the entirety of our numbered years,  
> Until our humble, fragile life reached its end!  

💡 *Commentary*: Sacred pastoral nourishment wasted on callous ground.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੇ ਗੀਤ ਵੀ ਇਸ ਦੁੱਧ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਸੱਚੇ ਤੇ ਸੁੱਚੇ ਸੀ ਅੜੀਓ!  
> ਪਰ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਨੇ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਮਾਰੇ ਸੜਿਓ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دے گیت وی اس ددھ وانگوں،  
> سچے تے سچے سی اڑیو!  
> پر دنیا نے زہر ملایا،  
> ساڈے سپنے مارے سڑیو!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv de geet vi iss dudh vaangu,  
> Sachhe te suchhe si arhiyo!  
> Par duniya ne zehar milaaya,  
> Saade supne maare sarhiyo!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv's verses too were like this unboiled milk—  
> Utterly pure, holy, and untainted by guile, friends!  
> Yet the envious world poured lethal venom into the cup,  
> Murdering our blossoming dreams in cold malice!  

💡 *Commentary*: The poisoning of lyric innocence by cynical critics.

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|

## 9. Teeje Pehar Di Dhupp

### **ਤੀਜੇ ਪਹਿਰ ਦੀ ਧੁੱਪ** • **تیجے پہر دی دھپ**

#### *"The Slanted Sunlight of Late Afternoon"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Afternoon` `#DecliningSun` `#Aging` `#Shadows` `#Twilight`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The fading, golden sunlight of late afternoon reminds the poet that youth is fleeting and evening's darkness is already descending.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala as Shiv sat on the rooftop watching the sun sink over the railway line.
- **Archival Context**: Famous for its psychological mapping of time, where the third watch of the day (Teeja Pehar) represents the swift passing of youth. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਤੀਜੇ ਪਹਿਰ ਦੀ ਧੁੱਪ ਢਲ਼ ਗਈ,  
> ਛਾਵਾਂ ਲੰਮੀਆਂ ਹੋਈਆਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੀਆਂ ਸੱਧਰਾਂ ਅੱਜ,  
> ਕੰਧਾਂ ਉਹਲੇ ਰੋਈਆਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> تیجے پہر دی دھپ ڈھل گئی،  
> چھاواں لمیاں ہوئیاں نی!  
> میرے دل دیاں سدھراں اج،  
> کندھاں اوہلے روئیاں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Teeje pehar di dhupp dhal gayi,  
> Chhaawaan lammiyan hoiyan ni!  
> Mere dil diyan saddhraan ajj,  
> Kandhaan ohle roiyan ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The sunlight of late afternoon has slanted and declined,  
> And the shadows of the trees have stretched impossibly long!  
> Today, the deep, secret yearnings of my heart  
> Have wept in solitude behind the crumbling mud walls!  

💡 *Commentary*: Elongated shadows symbolizing the encroaching chill of mortality.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜੋ ਦਿਨ ਚੜ੍ਹਿਆ ਸੀ ਹਾਸੇ ਲੈ ਕੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਦੇ ਗ਼ਮ 'ਚ ਡੁੱਬ ਚੱਲਿਆ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਸਾਹਾਂ ਦਾ ਪੰਛੀ ਵੀ ਹੁਣ,  
> ਆਪਣਾ ਆਲ੍ਹਣਾ ਲੱਭ ਚੱਲਿਆ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جو دن چڑھیا سی ہاسے لے کے،  
> اوہ شام دے غم چ ڈب چلیا!  
> میرے ساہاں دا پنچھی وی ہن،  
> اپنا آلہنا لبھ چلیا!  

**Romanized**:
> Jo din chadheya si haase lai ke,  
> Oh shaam de gham ch dubb chaleya!  
> Mere saahaan da panchhi vi hun,  
> Aapna aalhana labbh chaleya!  

**English Translation**:
> That luminous day which dawned bearing laughter and song  
> Is now drowning into the mournful gloom of dusk!  
> The weary bird of my fluttering breaths too  
> Has set out in search of its final, quiet nest!  

💡 *Commentary*: The bird seeking nest as the soul preparing for eternal rest.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੀ ਉਮਰ ਵੀ ਤੀਜੇ ਪਹਿਰ ਜਿਹੀ,  
> ਜੋ ਛੇਤੀ ਹੀ ਮੁੱਕ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ!  
> ਰਾਤ ਪਵੇਗੀ ਕਾਲ਼ੀ ਬੋਲੀ,  
> ਸਭ ਦੀ ਆਸ ਥੱਕ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دی عمر وی تیجے پہر جہئی،  
> جو چھیتی ہی مک جاوےگی!  
> رات پوےگی کالی بولی،  
> سبھ دی آس تھک جاوےگی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv di umar vi teeje pehar jihi,  
> Jo cheti hi mukk jaavegi!  
> Raat pavegi kaali boli,  
> Sabh di aas thakk jaavegi!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv's lifespan too resembles this late afternoon sun,  
> Which will swiftly extinguish into darkness!  
> The pitch-black, silent night will fall upon the fields,  
> And every lingering human hope will surrender to weariness!  

💡 *Commentary*: Precocious acceptance of his imminent early demise.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਤੀਜਾ ਪਹਿਰ (Teeja Pehar)** | *Tee-jaa Pe-har* | The third quadrant of the day (roughly 3 PM to 6 PM) | The traditional Punjabi marker of declining daylight, metaphor for the onset of aging or approaching end of youth. |

## 10. Khabran Da Daur

### **ਖ਼ਬਰਾਂ ਦਾ ਦੌਰ** • **خبراں دا دور**

#### *"The Era of Printed Rumors and Gossips"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Gossip` `#Rumors` `#Press` `#UrbanAlienation` `#Media`
- **Theme / Biome**: `modernism` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: In the modern era of commercial newspapers and town gossip, intimate human agony is printed as sensational news to sell editions.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Jalandhar after tabloid articles mocked his bohemian lifestyle.
- **Archival Context**: Documents Shiv's fury at sensationalist Urdu and Punjabi newspaper columns that spread false gossip about his alcoholism and private life. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਡੇ ਦਰਦਾਂ ਦੀ ਖ਼ਬਰ ਛਪੀ ਏ,  
> ਸਾਰੇ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਦੇ ਅਖ਼ਬਾਰਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ!  
> ਲੋਕਾਂ ਸਾਡਾ ਮਜ਼ਾਕ ਉਡਾਇਆ,  
> ਬਹਿ ਕੇ ਚੌਕਾਂ-ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ساڈے درداں دی خبر چھپی اے،  
> سارے شہر دے اخباراں وچ!  
> لوکاں ساڈا مذاق اڈایا،  
> بہہ کے چوکاں بازاراں وچ!  

**Romanized**:
> Saade dardaan di khabar chhapi ae,  
> Saare shehar de akhbaaraan vich!  
> Lokaan saada mazaak udaaya,  
> Beh ke chaukaan-bazaaraan vich!  

**English Translation**:
> The sensational news of our private heartbreak was printed  
> Across all the tabloid newspapers of the city!  
> The righteous citizens mocked and ridiculed our name,  
> Sitting idle across the bazaars and public squares!  

💡 *Commentary*: The degradation of private tragedy into sensational newsprint.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਿਸੇ ਨੇ ਸਾਡਾ ਦਿਲ ਨਾ ਵੇਖਿਆ,  
> ਸਭ ਨੇ ਸਿਆਹੀ ਪੜ੍ਹੀ ਅੜੀਓ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਕਲਮ ਨੇ ਸਾਡਾ ਲਹੂ ਪੀਤਾ,  
> ਉਹ ਝੂਠ ਦੇ ਥੰਮ੍ਹ 'ਤੇ ਖੜ੍ਹੀ ਅੜੀਓ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کسے نے ساڈا دل نہ ویکھیا،  
> سبھ نے سیاہی پڑھی اڑیو!  
> جس قلم نے ساڈا لہو پیتا،  
> اوہ جھوٹ دے تھمبھ تے کھڑھی اڑیو!  

**Romanized**:
> Kise ne saada dil na vekheya,  
> Sabh ne syaahi parhi arhiyo!  
> Jis kalam ne saada lahu peeta,  
> Oh jhooth de thammh te kharhi arhiyo!  

**English Translation**:
> Not a single soul bothered to look upon our living heart;  
> Everyone merely devoured the sensational black ink, friends!  
> That journalistic pen which drank our very blood  
> Stands erected upon a pillar of despicable falsehood!  

💡 *Commentary*: Commercial journalism profiting off the artist's private wounds.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਇਹ ਛਪੀਆਂ ਖ਼ਬਰਾਂ,  
> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਨੂੰ ਰੱਦੀ ਹੋ ਜਾਣਗੀਆਂ!  
> ਪਰ ਸਾਡੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਦੀਆਂ ਸਤਰਾਂ,  
> ਯੁੱਗਾਂ ਤਾਈਂ ਰੋ ਜਾਣਗੀਆਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو کہندا ایہہ چھپیاں خبراں،  
> کلھ نوں ردی ہو جانگیاں!  
> پر ساڈے گیتاں دیاں سطراں،  
> یگاں تائیں رو جانگیاں!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv kehnda eh chhappiyan khabran,  
> Kallh nu raddi ho jaangiyan!  
> Par saade geetan diyan satraan,  
> Yugaan taayin ro jaangiyan!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv declares: 'These sensational printed headlines  
> Shall turn into worthless scrap paper by tomorrow!  
> Yet the bleeding verses of our lyric songs  
> Shall continue to weep across centuries of human time!'  

💡 *Commentary*: Ephemeral newsprint versus eternal poetry.

## 11. Chanani Raat Da Haal

### **ਚਾਨਣੀ ਰਾਤ ਦਾ ਹਾਲ** • **چاننی رات دا حال**

#### *"The Plight of the Silver Moonlit Night"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Moonlight` `#Insomnia` `#NocturnalGrief` `#Silver` `#Courtyard`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: The bright silver moon turns the familiar courtyard into a chilling expanse of white bones for the abandoned lover.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala during sleepless summer nights spent on the open rooftop.
- **Archival Context**: Inverts classical Indian romantic imagery of moonlit nights (Chandni Raat) into a haunting graveyard of sleepless despair. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਚਾਨਣੀ ਰਾਤ ਨੇ ਕਹਿਰ ਕਮਾਇਆ,  
> ਵਿਹੜਾ ਚਿੱਟਾ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਸੌਂਦੇ ਸੀ ਗਲ਼ ਲਾ ਕੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਥਾਂ ਸੁੰਞਾ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> چاننی رات نے قہر کمایا،  
> ویہڑا چٹا ہو گیا نی!  
> جتھے سوندے سی گل لا کے،  
> اوہ تھاں سنجا ہو گیا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Chanani raat ne kehar kamaaya,  
> Vehra chitta ho gaya ni!  
> Jitthe saunde si gal laa ke,  
> Oh thaan sunjna ho gaya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The bright moonlight has wrought unsparing wrath tonight;  
> The entire courtyard has turned blindingly pale and white!  
> That very spot where once we slept clasped in each other's embrace  
> Has become an eerie, deserted wilderness!  

💡 *Commentary*: Moonlight stripped of romantic warmth, appearing as blinding white shroud.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਚੰਨ ਵੀ ਹੱਸਦਾ ਸਾਡੇ ਉੱਤੇ,  
> ਤਾਰੇ ਵੇਖ ਕੇ ਰੋਂਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਜਾਗਦੇ ਰਾਤਾਂ ਕੱਟੀਆਂ,  
> ਲੋਕੀਂ ਚੈਨ ਨਾਲ਼ ਸੌਂਦੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> چن وی ہسدا ساڈے اتے،  
> تارے ویکھ کے روندے نے!  
> اسیں جاگدے راتاں کٹیاں،  
> لوکی چین نال سوندے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Chann vi hassda saade utte,  
> Taare vekh ke ronde ne!  
> Aseen jaagde raataan kattiyan,  
> Loki chain naal saunde ne!  

**English Translation**:
> The silver moon smiles down upon our desolation with cold mockery,  
> While the sympathetic stars weep witnessing our plight!  
> We passed the tortuous hours of the night in sleepless vigilance,  
> While the comfortable world slumbered in effortless peace!  

💡 *Commentary*: Cosmic mockery contrasting with earthly indifference.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੇ ਨੈਣਾਂ 'ਚੋਂ ਨੀਂਦ ਉੱਡ ਗਈ,  
> ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਅੱਥਰੂ ਰਹਿ ਗਏ ਨੇ!  
> ਇਸ ਚਾਨਣੀ ਦੇ ਕਫ਼ਨ ਦੇ ਹੇਠਾਂ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਦਿਲ ਹੀ ਢਹਿ ਗਏ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دے نیناں چوں نیند اڈ گئی،  
> صرف اتھرو رہ گئے نے!  
> اس چاننی دے کفن دے ہیٹھاں،  
> ساڈے دل ہی ڈھہہ گئے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv de nainan chon neend udd gayi,  
> Sirf atthru reh gaye ne!  
> Iss chanani de kafan de hethan,  
> Saade dil hi dheh gaye ne!  

**English Translation**:
> All sleep has vanished forever from Shiv's eyes;  
> Nothing but scalding tears remain to keep company!  
> Beneath the cold, silver shroud of this moonlight,  
> Our very heart has collapsed in terminal ruin!  

💡 *Commentary*: Moonlight as a gleaming burial shroud (kafan).

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਚਾਨਣੀ ਰਾਤ (Chanani Raat)** | *Chaa-na-nee Raat* | Moonlit night | Classical trope of romantic lovers' rendezvous, here transformed into a chilling torment of solitary abandonment. |

## 12. Supne Te Parchhaven

### **ਸੁਪਨੇ ਤੇ ਪਰਛਾਵੇਂ** • **سپنے تے پرچھاویں**

#### *"Dreams and Fleeting Shadows"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Dreams` `#Shadows` `#Illusion` `#Maya` `#Unreality`
- **Theme / Biome**: `modernism` / `snowy_cabin_night`
- **Synopsis**: The poet realizes that the dreams of youth were merely unsubstantial shadows cast by the setting sun of hope.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala during late autumn as the days shortened rapidly.
- **Archival Context**: Explores the philosophical concept of Maya (cosmic illusion), contrasting the solid world of matter with phantom memories. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਸਾਂ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਵੇਖੇ ਸੀ ਜਿਊਣ ਦੇ,  
> ਪਰ ਨਿਕਲੇ ਪਰਛਾਵੇਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਉਂ-ਜਿਉਂ ਅਸੀਂ ਨੇੜੇ ਗਏ,  
> ਉਹ ਹੋਰ ਦੂਰ ਹੁੰਦੇ ਜਾਵੇਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اساں سپنے ویکھے سی جیون دے،  
> پر نکلے پرچھاویں نی!  
> جیوں جیوں اسیں نیڑے گئے،  
> اوہ ہور دور ہندے جاویں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Asaan supne vekhe si jiyun de,  
> Par nikle parchhaaven ni!  
> Jiyon-jiyon aseen nerhe gaye,  
> Oh hor door hunde jaaven ni!  

**English Translation**:
> We nurtured luminous dreams of truly living,  
> Yet they revealed themselves to be mere phantoms and shadows!  
> The closer we strove to embrace them with open arms,  
> The farther they retreated into the unreachable distance!  

💡 *Commentary*: The mirage of romantic happiness retreating on approach.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਯਾ ਵੀ ਸਾਡਾ ਸਾਥ ਛੱਡ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਜਦ ਘੁੱਪ ਹਨੇਰਾ ਆਇਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਰੌਸ਼ਨੀ 'ਤੇ ਮਾਣ ਸੀ ਸਾਨੂੰ,  
> ਉਸਨੇ ਹੀ ਭਰਮਾਇਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> سایہ وی ساڈا ساتھ چھڈ گیا،  
> جد گھپ ہنیرا آیا نی!  
> جس روشنی تے مان سی سانوں،  
> اس نے ہی بھرمایا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Saaya vi saada saath chhadd gaya,  
> Jad ghupp hanera aaya ni!  
> Jis raushni te maan si saanu,  
> Usne hi bharmaaya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Even our own faithful shadow abandoned our companionship  
> The moment pitch-black darkness descended upon us!  
> That deceptive light of which we were so proud  
> Was the very illumination that led us astray into illusion!  

💡 *Commentary*: Desertion of the shadow in darkness; light as deceiver.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਹੁਣ ਪਰਛਾਵਿਆਂ ਪਿੱਛੇ ਨਹੀਂ ਦੌੜਦਾ,  
> ਉਸ ਸੱਚ ਪਛਾਣ ਲਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਇਸ ਜੱਗ ਦੇ ਝੂਠੇ ਸੁਪਨਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਮਿੱਟੀ 'ਚ ਛਾਣ ਲਿਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو ہن پرچھاویاں پچھے نہیں دوڑدا،  
> اس سچ پچھان لیا اے!  
> اس جگ دے جھوٹھے سپنیاں نوں،  
> مٹی چ چھان لیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv hun parchhaaviyan pichhe nahin daurhda,  
> Us sach pachhaan leya ae!  
> Iss jagg de jhoothe supniyaan nu,  
> Mitti ch chhaan leya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv no longer runs frantically after fleeting shadows;  
> He has recognized the stark, unbending truth of existence!  
> The fraudulent, gilded dreams of this deceitful world  
> He has sifted and discarded into the indifferent dust!  

💡 *Commentary*: Liberation through complete disillusionment.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਪਰਛਾਵੇਂ (Parchhaven)** | *Par-chhaa-ven* | Shadows / Silhouettes | Classic metaphor for ephemeral worldly attachments and the deceptive nature of sensory experience. |

## 13. Lokan De Bol Kaure

### **ਲੋਕਾਂ ਦੇ ਬੋਲ ਕੌੜੇ** • **لوکاں دے بول کوڑے**

#### *"The Bitter Taunts of the World"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Taunts` `#Slander` `#VillageGossip` `#Wounds` `#Society`
- **Theme / Biome**: `rebellion` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The barbed, venomous words of village elders and town gossips cut deeper into the poet's spirit than physical daggers.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala when elders attempted to force Shiv into conventional marriage and employment.
- **Archival Context**: A fierce counter-attack against conservative elders of Batala who castigated Shiv's bohemian nocturnal poetry readings. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਲੋਕਾਂ ਦੇ ਬੋਲ ਕੌੜੇ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਤੀਰਾਂ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਖੁੱਭਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਮਿਲਦੇ ਸੀ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਤੱਕ,  
> ਅੱਜ ਵੇਖ ਕੇ ਨੈਣ ਝੁਕਦੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> لوکاں دے بول کوڑے اڑیو،  
> تیراں وانگوں کھبھدے نے!  
> جو ہس کے ملدے سی کلھ تک،  
> اج ویکھ کے نین جھکدے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Lokaan de bol kaure arhiyo,  
> Teeraan vaangu khubhde ne!  
> Jo hass ke milde si kallh takk,  
> Ajj vekh ke nain jhukde ne!  

**English Translation**:
> The bitter, barbed taunts of the world, friends,  
> Penetrate the flesh like poisoned iron arrows!  
> Those who greeted us with warm smiles until yesterday  
> Now avert their eyes in calculated coldness as we pass!  

💡 *Commentary*: The social ostracization of the nonconformist poet.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਡੀ ਗ਼ਰੀਬੀ ਦਾ ਮਜ਼ਾਕ ਉਡਾਇਆ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਫਟੇ ਹੋਏ ਚੋਲੇ 'ਤੇ!  
> ਉਹ ਕੀ ਜਾਨਣ ਕਿੰਨਾ ਦਰਦ ਏ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਇਸ ਮਨ ਦੇ ਭੋਲੇ 'ਤੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ساڈی غریبی دا مذاق اڈایا،  
> ساڈے پھٹے ہوئے چولے تے!  
> اوہ کی جانن کنا درد اے،  
> ساڈے اس من دے بھولے تے!  

**Romanized**:
> Saadi gareebi da mazaak udaaya,  
> Saade phatte hoye chole te!  
> Oh ki jaanan kinna dard ae,  
> Saade iss man de bhole te!  

**English Translation**:
> They mocked our destitute poverty and bohemian lack,  
> Pointing fingers at our tattered, dust-stained tunic!  
> How could their small, calculating minds ever fathom  
> The immense holy agony sheltered in this innocent breast?  

💡 *Commentary*: Material poverty contrasted with spiritual wealth.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਪਰ ਇਹ ਬੋਲ ਤੁਹਾਡੇ,  
> ਇੱਕ ਦਿਨ ਫੁੱਲ ਬਣ ਜਾਣਗੇ!  
> ਜਦ ਮੇਰੀ ਕਲਮ ਦੀ ਅੱਗ ਬਲੇਗੀ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਸਾਰੇ ਸਿਰ ਝੁਕਾਣਗੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو کہندا پر ایہہ بول تہاڈے،  
> اک دن پھل بن جانگے!  
> جد میری قلم دی اگ بلےگی،  
> تاں سارے سر جھکانگے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv kehnda par eh bol tuhaade,  
> Ikk din phull ban jaange!  
> Jad meri kalam di agg balegi,  
> Taan saare sir jhukaange!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv replies: 'These cruel, poisoned words of yours  
> Shall one day turn into wreaths of reverence!  
> When the fierce flame of my poetic pen blazes forth,  
> Every proud head among you shall bow in repentance!'  

💡 *Commentary*: Prophetic vindication of artistic genius over petty gossip.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਚੋਲਾ (Chola)** | *Cho-laa* | Long loose tunic worn by Sufi dervishes and mendicants | Symbol of renunciation, simplicity, and indifference to bourgeois fashion. |

## 14. Chirhi Bichhari Koonjaan Di

### **ਚਿੜੀ ਵਿਛੜੀ ਕੂੰਜਾਂ ਦੀ** • **چڑی وچھڑی کونجاں دی**

- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala as autumn flocks migrated from the Himalayas down to the Punjab plains.
- **Archival Context**: Employs the ancient Punjabi folklore trope of the 'Koonj Vichhadi' (separated crane) immortalized by Guru Nanak in the Guru Granth Sahib. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਕੂੰਜਾਂ ਦੀ ਡਾਰ 'ਚੋਂ ਵਿਛੜੀ,  
> ਕੱਲੀ ਰਹਿ ਗਈ ਅੰਬਰਾਂ 'ਤੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਸਾਥੀ ਲੰਘ ਗਏ ਦੂਰ ਕਿਤੇ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਰੋਣਾ ਸੁਣੇ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਧਰਤਾਂ 'ਤੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں کونجاں دی ڈار چوں وچھڑی،  
> کلی رہ گئی عنبراں تے!  
> میرے ساتھی لنگھ گئے دور کتے،  
> میرا رونا سنے نہ کوئی دھرتاں تے!  

**Romanized**:
> Main koonjaan di daar chon vichhrhi,  
> Kalli reh gayi ambaraan te!  
> Mere saathi langh gaye door kite,  
> Mera rona sune na koyi dhartaan te!  

**English Translation**:
> I was severed from the flying chevron of migratory cranes,  
> Left entirely stranded and solitary in the vast skies!  
> My lifelong companions have soared far beyond the distant mountains,  
> While not a living soul upon the earth pauses to hear my wailing!  

💡 *Commentary*: The archetypal tragedy of exile from the collective flock.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਥੱਲੇ ਦਿਸਣ ਸ਼ਿਕਾਰੀ ਜਾਲ਼ ਵਿਛਾਈ,  
> ਉੱਤੇ ਬੱਦਲ ਗੱਜਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਖੰਭਾਂ 'ਚ ਹੁਣ ਜ਼ੋਰ ਰਿਹਾ ਨਾ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਸਾਹ ਵੀ ਰੱਜਦੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> تھلے دسن شکاری جال وچھائی،  
> اتے بدل گجدے نے!  
> میرے کھنبھاں چ ہن زور رہا نہ،  
> ساڈے ساہ وی رجدے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Thalle dissan shikaari jaal vichhaayi,  
> Utte baddal gajjde ne!  
> Mere khambhaan ch hun zor reha na,  
> Saade saah vi rajjde ne!  

**English Translation**:
> Below me, ruthless hunters wait with wide nets spread across the reeds,  
> While above my head, terrifying stormclouds rumble!  
> All physical strength has deserted my exhausted wings,  
> And my very breaths prepare to surrender life!  

💡 *Commentary*: Hunters below and thunder above; the trapped soul.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਹਾਲ ਵੀ ਇਸ ਕੂੰਜ ਜਿਹਾ,  
> ਜੋ ਮਿੱਟੀ 'ਤੇ ਡਿੱਗ ਪਊਗੀ!  
> ਪਰ ਉਸਦੀ ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਚੀਕ ਸੁਣ ਕੇ,  
> ਇਹ ਸਾਰੀ ਧਰਤ ਕੰਬ ਪਊਗੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دا حال وی اس کونج جہیا،  
> جو مٹی تے ڈگ پؤگی!  
> پر اس دی آخری چیک سن کے،  
> ایہہ ساری دھرتی کنب پؤگی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv da haal vi iss koonj jiha,  
> Jo mitti te digg paugi!  
> Par usdi aakhri cheek sun ke,  
> Eh saari dhart kamb paugi!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv's tragic fate mirrors that of this abandoned crane,  
> Destined to plummet lifeless onto the indifferent soil!  
> Yet hearing the piercing shriek of its dying breath,  
> The entire width of this earth shall shudder to its core!  

💡 *Commentary*: The final cry that shakes the moral foundation of the world.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਕੂੰਜ (Koonj)** | *Koonj* | Demoiselle Crane (migratory bird from Siberia) | Sacred emblem in Punjabi Sufi and Gurbani poetry of the wandering soul separated from its heavenly homeland. |

## 15. Nadi Kinare

### **ਨਦੀ ਕਿਨਾਰੇ** • **ندی کنارے**

#### *"Beside the River's Restless Current"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#River` `#Water` `#Currents` `#Beas` `#Impermanence`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: Sitting beside the rushing river currents, watching dry leaves and floating petals drift away toward an unknown ocean.
- **Genesis**: Composed by the banks of the Beas river during the monsoon swelling.
- **Archival Context**: Rooted in Shiv's frequent solitary wanderings along the banks of the Beas and Ravi rivers near Batala. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਦੀ ਕਿਨਾਰੇ ਬਹਿ ਕੇ ਵੇਖਿਆ,  
> ਪਾਣੀ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਵਗਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਦਾ ਹਰ ਪਲ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਇਸ ਵਹਿਣ ਜਿਹਾ ਲੱਗਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ندی کنارے بہہ کے ویکھیا،  
> پانی کویں وگدا اے!  
> ساڈی زندگی دا ہر پل اڑیو،  
> اس وہین جہیا لگدا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Nadi kinare beh ke vekheya,  
> Paani kiven vagda ae!  
> Saadi zindagi da har pal arhiyo,  
> Iss vahan jiha lagda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Sitting beside the solitary riverbank, I watched  
> How ruthlessly the wild currents surge forward!  
> Every fleeting heartbeat of our mortal existence, friends,  
> Resembles this unreturning, relentless torrent!  

💡 *Commentary*: The Heraclitean river of time where no moment can be revisited.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜੋ ਪੱਤਾ ਡਿੱਗਿਆ ਸ਼ਾਖ਼ ਤੋਂ ਟੁੱਟ ਕੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਮੁੜ ਕੇ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਉਣਾ!  
> ਇਸ ਵਹਿਣ ਨੇ ਸਭ ਨੂੰ ਰੋੜ੍ਹ ਲਿਆ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਨੇ ਨਹੀਂ ਬਚਾਉਣਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جو پتا ڈگیا شاخ توں ٹٹ کے،  
> اوہ مڑ کے نہیں آونا!  
> اس وہین نے سبھ نوں روڑھ لیا،  
> کسے نے نہیں بچاونا!  

**Romanized**:
> Jo patta diggeya shaakh ton tutt ke,  
> Oh murh ke nahin aauna!  
> Iss vahan ne sabh nu rorh leya,  
> Kise ne nahin bachauna!  

**English Translation**:
> That dry leaf which snapped from its branch and fell  
> Can never return to its maternal bough again!  
> This unsparing flood has swept away all living things;  
> Not a single earthly savior exists to rescue them!  

💡 *Commentary*: Total submission to cosmic time.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਗੀਤ ਵੀ ਨਦੀ ਵਰਗਾ,  
> ਜੋ ਸਾਗਰ ਵੱਲ ਜਾ ਰਿਹਾ ਏ!  
> ਆਪਣੇ ਦਰਦ ਦੇ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਨਾਲ਼,  
> ਇਹ ਧਰਤ ਮਹਿਕਾ ਰਿਹਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دا گیت وی ندی ورگا،  
> جو ساگر ول جا رہیا اے!  
> اپنے درد دے ہنجھواں نال،  
> ایہہ دھرتی مہکا رہیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv da geet vi nadi varga,  
> Jo saagar vall jaa reha ae!  
> Aapne dard de hanjuwan naal,  
> Eh dhart mehkaa reha ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv's verses too resemble this sacred rushing river,  
> Journeying steadily toward the vast, primordial ocean!  
> With the glistening tears of its fathomless sorrow,  
> It irrigates and perfumes the parched soil of Punjab!  

💡 *Commentary*: The river merges into the cosmic ocean of eternity.

|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਵਹਿਣ (Vahan)** | *Va-han* | River current / Flowing torrent | Classic spiritual metaphor for the inescapable flow of Samsara (worldly flux). |

## 16. Babul Dian Dheeyan

### **ਬਾਬਲ ਦੀਆਂ ਧੀਆਂ** • **بابل دیاں دھیاں**

#### *"The Daughters of the Paternal Hearth"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Daughters` `#Babul` `#Exile` `#PaternalHome` `#Tears`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The daughter laments having to abandon the hearth where she grew up, confronting the patriarchal law that declares daughters guests in their own homes.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala after witnessing a poignant village bidai (bridal departure).
- **Archival Context**: Directly anticipated Shiv's feminist defense of women's inner dignity in Loona (1965), championing the daughter's voice. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਬਾਬਲ ਦੀਆਂ ਧੀਆਂ ਰੋ ਰੋ ਆਖਣ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਕੀ ਕਸੂਰ ਵੇ ਬਾਬਲ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਵਿਹੜੇ 'ਚ ਜੰਮੀਆਂ-ਪਲੀਆਂ,  
> ਉਸ ਤੋਂ ਕਰ ਦਿੱਤਾ ਦੂਰ ਵੇ ਬਾਬਲ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> بابل دیاں دھیاں رو رو آکھن،  
> ساڈا کی قصور وے بابل!  
> جس ویہڑے چ جمیاں پلیاں،  
> اس توں کر دتا دور وے بابل!  

**Romanized**:
> Baabul diyan dheeyan ro ro aakhan,  
> Saada ki kasoor ve baabul!  
> Jis vehre ch jammiyaan-paliyaan,  
> Us ton kar ditta door ve baabul!  

**English Translation**:
> Through streaming tears, the daughters of the hearth cry out:  
> 'What transgression was ours, O revered father?  
> That very courtyard where we were born and sheltered  
> You have severed and exiled us far away from it!'  

💡 *Commentary*: The radical questioning of the patriarchal custom of female displacement.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪੁੱਤਰਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਦਿੱਤੀਆਂ ਜਾਇਦਾਦਾਂ,  
> ਸਾਨੂੰ ਦਿੱਤੀ ਵਿਦਾਈ ਵੇ ਬਾਬਲ!  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਹਿੱਸੇ ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਹੰਝੂ ਆਏ,  
> ਕੈਸੀ ਰੀਤ ਚਲਾਈ ਵੇ ਬਾਬਲ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پتراں نوں دتیاں جائداداں،  
> سانوں دتی وداعئی وے بابل!  
> ساڈے حصے صرف ہنجھو آئے،  
> کیسی ریت چلائی وے بابل!  

**Romanized**:
> Puttraan nu dittiyaan jaaedaadaan,  
> Saanu ditti vidaayi ve baabul!  
> Saade hisse sirf hanjhu aaye,  
> Kaisi reet chalaayi ve baabul!  

**English Translation**:
> To the sons you bequeathed lands, houses, and ancestral estates;  
> To us daughters you bestowed only banishment and tears!  
> In our portion of the inheritance fell only bitter grief;  
> What cruel, unfeeling custom have your elders instituted?'  

💡 *Commentary*: Critique of gendered inheritance laws in agrarian Punjab.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਪਰ ਧੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਦਰਦ,  
> ਇਹ ਧਰਤੀ ਸਦਾ ਗਾਵੇਗੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਰੋਈਆਂ ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਉਹਨਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਰੱਬ ਗਲ਼ ਲਾਵੇਗੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو کہندا پر دھیاں دا درد،  
> ایہہ دھرتی سدا گاوےگی!  
> جو روئیاں اس دنیا دے وچ،  
> اوہناں نوں رب گل لاوےگی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv kehnda par dheeyan da dard,  
> Eh dharti sada gaavegi!  
> Jo roiyan iss duniya de vich,  
> Ohnaan nu Rabb gal laavegi!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv declares: 'The holy sorrow of these banished daughters  
> This sacred earth shall sing through all the ages!  
> Those tender souls who wept in this patriarchal world  
> Shall be gathered warmly into the divine embrace of God!'  

💡 *Commentary*: Spiritual sanctification of the victimized daughters.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਜਾਇਦਾਦ (Jaaedaad)** | *Jaa-e-daad* | Ancestral property / Land inheritance | Agrarian land traditionally reserved exclusively for male heirs, leaving women legally and economically vulnerable. |

## 17. Parbat De Ohle

### **ਪਰਬਤ ਦੇ ਉਹਲੇ** • **پربت دے اوہلے**

#### *"Behind the Solitary Mountain"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#Mountains` `#Shivalik` `#Horizon`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The beloved has departed behind the insurmountable mountain ridges where no letter or song can penetrate.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's geographic separation from his beloved who was married to a government officer in a mountain station.
- **Archival Context**: Written during Shiv's travels near Dalhousie in the Shivalik foothills, looking towards the plains. (*Citation: `SHARMA-1979-SOLITARY`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪਰਬਤ ਦੇ ਉਹਲੇ ਤੁਰ ਗਿਆ ਸੱਜਣ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਕੋਲ ਬੱਸ ਹੰਝੂ ਰਹਿ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਉੱਚੀਆਂ ਧਾਰਾਂ ਰੋਕ ਲਏ ਰਾਹ,  
> ਦਰਦ ਸਾਡੇ ਦਿਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹਿ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پربت دے اوہلے تر گیا سجن،  
> ساڈے کول بس ہنجھو رہ گئے نی!  
> اچیاں دھاراں روک لئے راہ،  
> درد ساڈے دل وچ بہہ گئے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Parbat de ohle tur gaya sajjan,  
> Saade kol bass hanjhu reh gaye ni!  
> Ucchiyaan dhaaraan rok laye raah,  
> Dard saade dil vich beh gaye ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Behind the distant mountain ridge my beloved has departed,  
> Leaving only scalding tears to keep company with my heart!  
> Towering stony peaks have blocked the mountain paths,  
> While boundless sorrow has encamped forever in my soul!  

💡 *Commentary*: The mountain (parbat) as a physical and emotional barricade.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਬੱਦਲ ਆਉਂਦੇ ਪਰਬਤ ਵੱਲੋਂ,  
> ਸਨੇਹਾ ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਲਿਆਉਂਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਠੰਢੀਆਂ ਪੌਣਾਂ ਛੋਹ ਕੇ ਲੰਘਣ,  
> ਹੋਰ ਵੀ ਅੱਗ ਲਗਾਉਂਦੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> بدل آؤندے پربت ولوں،  
> سنیہا کوئی نہ لیاؤندے نے!  
> ٹھنڈیاں پوناں چھوہ کے لنگھن،  
> ہور وی اگ لگاؤندے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Baddal aaunde parbat vallon,  
> Saneha koyi na lyaaunde ne!  
> Thandhiyaan paunan chhoh ke langhan,  
> Hor vi agg lagaaunde ne!  

**English Translation**:
> Drifting clouds glide down from those lofty crags,  
> Yet they bring no written word of love from his hand!  
> Chilly mountain breezes brush past my shivering flesh,  
> Only to stoke the flames of longing fiercer than before!  

💡 *Commentary*: Inversion of the classical Kalidasa Meghaduta (cloud-messenger) motif: the clouds bring silence.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਾਸ਼ ਮੈਂ ਬਣ ਜਾਂ ਬੱਦਲ ਕਾਲਾ,  
> ਪਰਬਤ ਪਾਰ ਉੱਡ ਜਾਵਾਂ ਵੇ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਮਾਹੀ ਵਸਦਾ,  
> ਉਹਦੇ ਕਦਮੀਂ ਵਰ੍ਹ ਜਾਵਾਂ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کاش میں بن جاں بدل کالا،  
> پربت پار اڈ جاواں وے!  
> جتھے میرا ماہی وسدا،  
> اوہدے قدمیں ورھ جاواں وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kaash main ban jaan baddal kaala,  
> Parbat paar udd jaawaan ve!  
> Jithe mera maahi vasda,  
> Ohde kadmeen varh jaawaan ve!  

**English Translation**:
> Would that I could transform into a dark monsoon cloud,  
> Soaring unimpeded across those insurmountable ridges!  
> To that distant valley where my wanderer dwells,  
> And dissolve into torrential rain beneath his feet!  

💡 *Commentary*: Self-dissolution as the ultimate mode of reaching the beloved.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਧਾਰਾਂ (Dhaaraan)** | *Dhaa-raan* | Mountain ridges / Ranges | The Shivalik hills visible from northern Punjab districts like Gurdaspur and Hoshiarpur. |

## 18. Churiyaan Da Jorha

### **ਚੂੜੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਜੋੜਾ** • **چوڑیاں دا جوڑا**

#### *"Pair of Glass Bangles"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#Bangles` `#FolkTragic` `#Suhag`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The green glass bangles bought with youthful tenderness shatter against stone, cutting the wrists that wore them.
- **Genesis**: Shiv captures the vulnerability of young brides whose romantic illusions shatter upon entering patriarchal households.
- **Archival Context**: Glass bangles in Punjabi folklore represent suhag (marital fortune); their breaking is the ultimate omen of widowhood or betrayal. (*Citation: `AMRITA-1974-NAGMANI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸੱਜਣਾਂ ਨੇ ਚੂੜੀਆਂ ਲੈ ਦਿੱਤੀਆਂ ਸੀ,  
> ਕੱਚ ਦੀਆਂ ਹਰੀਆਂ-ਭਰੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਪਰ ਉਹ ਹੱਥੋਂ ਛੁੱਟ ਕੇ ਟੁੱਟੀਆਂ,  
> ਪੱਥਰ ਤੇ ਜਦ ਪਈਆਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> سجناں نے چوڑیاں لے دتیاں سی،  
> کچ دیاں ہریاں بھریاں نی!  
> پر اوہ ہتھوں چھٹ کے ٹٹیاں،  
> پتھر تے جد پئیاں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Sajjnaan ne churiyaan lai dittiyaan si,  
> Kach diyan hariyaan-bhariyaan ni!  
> Par oh hatthon chhutt ke tuttiyaan,  
> Patthar te jad paiyaan ni!  

**English Translation**:
> My beloved gifted me a pair of glass bangles,  
> Vibrant green as fresh monsoon clover!  
> Yet they slipped from trembling wrists and shattered  
> The moment they struck the unyielding courtyard stone!  

💡 *Commentary*: Green bangles symbolize youthful prosperity and conjugal hope.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਟੋਟੇ-ਟੋਟੇ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ ਕੱਚ,  
> ਲਹੂ-ਲੁਹਾਣ ਕਲਾਈਆਂ ਹੋਈਆਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਸੀ ਸਜਾਏ ਅਸੀਂ,  
> ਉਹ ਸਭ ਰੁਦਨ-ਦੁਹਾਈਆਂ ਹੋਈਆਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ٹوٹے ٹوٹے ہو گیا کچ،  
> لہو لہان کلائیاں ہوئیاں نی!  
> جو سپنے سی سجائے اسیں،  
> اوہ سبھ ردن دہائیاں ہوئیاں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Tote-tote ho gaya kach,  
> Lahoo-luhaan kalaaiyaan hoiyaan ni!  
> Jo supne si sajaaye aseen,  
> Oh sabh rudan-duhaaiyaan hoiyaan ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The fragile glass fractured into a thousand splintered shards,  
> Staining my soft wrists with trickling crimson blood!  
> Those radiant matrimonial dreams we had nurtured together  
> Have dissolved into piercing wails of inconsolable mourning!  

💡 *Commentary*: Physical wounding from the broken romantic token.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਸੁੰਞੀਆਂ ਬਾਹਾਂ ਵੇਖ ਕੇ ਰੋਵਾਂ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਕੱਚ ਨੂੰ ਜੋੜੇ ਵੇ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਦਿਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਦਾ ਕਹਿਰ ਵਸੇ,  
> ਉਸ ਨੂੰ ਕੌਣ ਮੋੜੇ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن سنجیاں باہاں ویکھ کے روواں،  
> کوئی نہ کچ نوں جوڑے وے!  
> جس دل وچ عشق دا قہر وسے،  
> اس نوں کون موڑے وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun sunjiyaan baahaan vekh ke rowaan,  
> Koyi na kach nu joreh ve!  
> Jis dil vich ishq da kehar vasey,  
> Uss nu kaun moreh ve!  

**English Translation**:
> Now I gaze upon my bare, unadorned arms and weep,  
> For no craftsman can reassemble shattered glass!  
> And when the ferocious tempest of tragic passion invades a heart,  
> Who possesses the power to turn back its devastating flood?  

💡 *Commentary*: Bare arms (*sunjiyaan baahaan*) signify permanent bereavement.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਚੂੜੀਆਂ (Churiyaan)** | *Choo-ree-yaan* | Glass bangles | Sacred ornament of Punjabi brides; breaking bangles traditionally signals tragic loss or widowhood. |

## 19. Babul Da Ghar

### **ਬਾਬਲ ਦਾ ਘਰ** • **بابل دا گھر**

#### *"The Father’s Lost Courtyard"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#Babul` `#Doli` `#DepartingDaughter`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The departing bride looks back at her father's mud home, knowing that after stepping into the palanquin, she is an exile forever.
- **Genesis**: Shiv frequently wrote from the female persona, capturing the trauma of patriarchal transfer.
- **Archival Context**: Draws on the heartrending Punjabi folk genre of Alahnian (bridal departure elegies), mourning the loss of the father's protective realm. (*Citation: `AMRITA-1974-NAGMANI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਛੁੱਟ ਗਿਆ ਬਾਬਲ ਦਾ ਘਰ ਸਾਥੋਂ,  
> ਪਰਦੇਸੀ ਡੋਲੀ ਬਹਿ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਗਲੀਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਚਪਨ ਬੀਤਿਆ,  
> ਉਹ ਰਾਹ ਪਰਾਏ ਰਹਿ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> چھٹ گیا بابل دا گھر ساتھوں،  
> پردیسی ڈولی بہہ گئے نی!  
> جنہاں گلیاں وچ بچپن بیتیا،  
> اوہ راہ پرائے رہ گئے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Chhutt gaya babul da ghar saathon,  
> Paradesi doli beh gaye ni!  
> Jinhaan galiyaan vich bachpan beetiya,  
> Oh raah paraaye reh gaye ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Forever left behind is my father's sheltering home,  
> As I step into the palanquin of strangers!  
> Those sunlit alleys where my happy childhood played  
> Have turned overnight into alien, forbidden pathways!  

💡 *Commentary*: Bridal transfer viewed as psychological exile from native identity.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮਾਂ ਰੋਵੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਗਲ ਲੱਗ ਕੇ,  
> ਬਾਬਲ ਦੀਆਂ ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਨਮ ਹੋਈਆਂ!  
> ਵੀਰਾਂ ਨੇ ਮੋਢਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ ਡੋਲੀ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਸਾਡੀਆਂ ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ੀਆਂ ਸਭ ਗ਼ਮ ਹੋਈਆਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ماں رووے میرے گل لگ کے،  
> بابل دیاں اکھیاں نم ہوئیاں!  
> ویراں نے موڈھا دتا ڈولی نوں،  
> ساڈیاں خوشیاں سبھ غم ہوئیاں!  

**Romanized**:
> Maan rove mere gal lagg ke,  
> Babul diyaan akhiyaan nam hoiyaan!  
> Veeraan ne modha ditta doli nu,  
> Saadiyaan khushiyaan sabh gham hoiyaan!  

**English Translation**:
> My weeping mother clung to my neck in desperation,  
> While my father's stern eyes brimmed with silent sorrow!  
> My brothers lifted the poles of the palanquin onto their shoulders,  
> And all the radiant joys of our house collapsed into mourning!  

💡 *Commentary*: The traditional departure scene where brothers carry the palanquin.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਮੁੜ ਕੇ ਨਾ ਆਉਣਾ ਉਸ ਵਿਹੜੇ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ ਚੋਗ ਚੁਗਦੀਆਂ ਸੀ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਕਿਸਮਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਤਾਂ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਲਿਖਿਆ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਅੱਗਾਂ ਦਿਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਲਦੀਆਂ ਸੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن مڑ کے نہ آؤنا اس ویہڑے،  
> جتھے چڑیاں چوگ چگدیاں سی!  
> ساڈی قسمت وچ تاں برہا لکھیا،  
> جتھے اگاں دل وچ بلدیاں سی!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun murh ke na aaona oss vehre,  
> Jithe chiriyaan chog chugdiyaan si!  
> Saadi kismat vich taan birha likhiya,  
> Jithe aggaan dil vich baldiyaan si!  

**English Translation**:
> Never again shall I return as a daughter to that courtyard  
> Where the morning sparrows pecked the scattered grain!  
> In our tragic destiny only eternal separation was ordained,  
> Where embers of grief must burn silently within our breast!  

💡 *Commentary*: The irreversible loss of childhood innocence and home.

## 20. Meenh Te Mrigshala

### **ਮੀਂਹ ਤੇ ਮ੍ਰਿਗਸ਼ਾਲਾ** • **مینہہ تے مرگ شالا**

#### *"Rain upon the Forest Grove"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#Monsoon` `#DeerSkin` `#AsceticRain`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: Monsoon rain lashes the forest, drenching the hermit's deerskin rug; sensuous worldly love collides with ascetic renunciation.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's ongoing internal war between hedonistic sensual love and ascetic death-longing.
- **Archival Context**: Contrasts the erotic, fertile rain of the monsoon with the cold, celibate meditation skin (*mrigshala*) of an ascetic. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੁਸਲਾਧਾਰ ਮੀਂਹ ਵਰ੍ਹਿਆ ਜੰਗਲ 'ਚ,  
> ਭਿੱਜ ਗਈ ਮ੍ਰਿਗਸ਼ਾਲਾ ਜੋਗੀ ਦੀ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਸਮਝੇ ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਦਰਦ ਕਹਾਣੀ ਇਸ ਰੋਗੀ ਦੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> مسلادھار مینہہ ورھیا جنگل چ،  
> بھج گئی مرگ شالا جوگی دی!  
> کوئی نہ سمجھے اس دنیا وچ،  
> درد کہانی اس روگی دی!  

**Romanized**:
> Muslaadhaar meenh varheya jangal ch,  
> Bhijj gayi mrigshala jogi di!  
> Koyi na samjhe iss duniya vich,  
> Dard kahaani iss rogi di!  

**English Translation**:
> Torrential monsoon rain lashed the deep wilderness,  
> Drenching the ascetic deerskin rug of the solitary jogi!  
> In this shallow world no passing pilgrim can fathom  
> The consuming sickness of a heart poisoned by love!  

💡 *Commentary*: Ascetic deerskin (*mrigshala*) exposed to the sensual assault of the monsoon.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਬੱਦਲਾਂ ਨੇ ਗਰਜ ਕੇ ਸ਼ੋਰ ਮਚਾਇਆ,  
> ਬਿਜਲੀ ਨੇ ਚੀਰਿਆ ਸੀਨਾ ਅੰਬਰ ਦਾ!  
> ਜਿਵੇਂ ਕੋਈ ਯਾਦ ਪੁਰਾਣੀ ਆ ਕੇ,  
> ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ਾ ਖੜਕਾਵੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਮੰਦਰ ਦਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> بدلاں نے گرج کے شور مچایا،  
> بجلی نے چیریا سینہ انبر دا!  
> جیویں کوئی یاد پرانی آ کے،  
> دروازہ کھڑکاوے میرے مندر دا!  

**Romanized**:
> Baddlaan ne garaj ke shor machaaya,  
> Bijli ne cheereya seena ambar da!  
> Jiven koyi yaad puraani aa ke,  
> Darwaaza kharkaave mere mandar da!  

**English Translation**:
> The dark clouds roared with terrifying thunder,  
> While lightning tore open the vulnerable breast of the sky!  
> Just as an ancient, unhealed memory arrives unbidden  
> To rattle the locked doors of my quiet inner temple!  

💡 *Commentary*: Thunderstorm externalizing psychic memory bursts.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਸ ਮੀਂਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੈਂ ਰੁੜ੍ਹ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ,  
> ਪੱਥਰਾਂ ਨਾਲ ਟਕਰਾ ਕੇ ਵੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ ਅੰਤ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਮੌਤ ਨੂੰ ਗਲ ਨਾਲ ਲਾ ਕੇ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اس مینہہ وچ میں رڑھ جاواں گا،  
> پتھراں نال ٹکرا کے وے!  
> میرے برہا دا انت ہووے گا،  
> موت نوں گل نال لا کے وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Iss meenh vich main rurh jaawaanga,  
> Patthraan naal takraa ke ve!  
> Mere birha da ant hovega,  
> Maut nu gal naal laa ke ve!  

**English Translation**:
> In this torrential flood I shall be swept away,  
> Battering my broken limbs against the river granite!  
> And thus the agonizing odyssey of my separation shall end,  
> Embracing Death as my beloved and final bride!  

💡 *Commentary*: Eroticization of death through water and river submersion.

##### Cultural Glossary

|:---|:---|:---|:---|

## 21. Dharat Di Hikk

### **ਧਰਤ ਦੀ ਹਿੱਕ** • **دھرت دی ہک**

#### *"The Soil’s Wounded Breast"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#EarthasMother` `#AgrarianPain` `#Furrows`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The iron plough tears open the breast of Mother Earth; the poet identifies his heart with the ploughed furrows waiting for seed.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's empathy for the agrarian cycles of Gurdaspur and the violent intimacy of farming.
- **Archival Context**: Personifies the soil of Punjab as a maternal body wounded by the iron plough, blending peasant reality with existential grief. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹਲ ਦੀ ਫਾਲੀ ਨੇ ਚੀਰ ਦਿੱਤੀ ਏ,  
> ਧਰਤ ਮਾਂ ਦੀ ਕੋਮਲ ਹਿੱਕ ਵੇ!  
> ਅੰਦਰੋਂ ਨਿਕਲਿਆ ਦਰਦ ਪੁਰਾਣਾ,  
> ਲਹੂ ਦਾ ਰੰਗ ਏ ਬੜਾ ਬਰੀਕ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہل دی پھالی نے چیر دتی اے،  
> دھرتی ماں دی کومل ہک وے!  
> اندروں نکلیا درد پرانا،  
> لہو دا رنگ اے بڑا باریک وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Hal di phaali ne cheer ditti ae,  
> Dh زمین maan di komal hikk ve!  
> Andron nikaleya dard puraana,  
> Lahoo da rang ae barha bareek ve!  

**English Translation**:
> The sharpened iron blade of the plough has slashed open  
> The tender, sacred breast of Mother Earth!  
> From within her torn womb wells an ancient agony,  
> Whose secret blood is finer than human sight can behold!  

💡 *Commentary*: The iron ploughshare (*phaali*) as an instrument of sacrificial violence.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਿਸਾਨ ਨੇ ਬੀਜ ਖਿਲਾਰ ਦਿੱਤੇ ਨੇ,  
> ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਕਣਕ ਦੇ ਦਾਣੇ!  
> ਧਰਤੀ ਨੇ ਸਭ ਸੀਨੇ ਲਾ ਲਏ,  
> ਕੀ ਜਾਣੇ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਦੇ ਸਿਆਣੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کسان نے بیج کھلار دتے نے،  
> ہنجھواں وانگوں کنک دے دانے!  
> دھرتی نے سبھ سینے لا لئے،  
> کی جانے دنیا دے سیانے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kisaan ne beej khilaar ditte ne,  
> Hanjhuwan vaangoo kanak de daane!  
> Dharti ne sabh seene laa laye,  
> Ki jaane duniya de siyaane!  

**English Translation**:
> The peasant scattered the seed-grain across the furrow,  
> Glistening like tears upon the freshly turned loam!  
> The patient earth gathered every seed into her dark embrace,  
> Though the haughty wise men of the city comprehend nothing!  

💡 *Commentary*: Grain as tears buried in the soil to sprout into tragic song.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੇਰੇ ਦਿਲ ਨੂੰ ਵੀ ਚੀਰਿਆ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਨੇ,  
> ਦਰਦ ਦਾ ਬੀਜ ਖਿਲਾਰਨ ਲਈ!  
> ਇੱਕ ਦਿਨ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਦਾ ਫ਼ਸਲ ਉੱਗੇਗਾ,  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਨੂੰ ਸ਼ਿੰਗਾਰਨ ਲਈ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میرے دل نوں وی چیریا عشق نے،  
> درد دا بیج کھلارن لئی!  
> اک دن گیتاں دا فصل اگے گا،  
> میرے برہا نوں شنگارن لئی!  

**Romanized**:
> Mere dil nu vi cheereya ishq ne,  
> Dard da beej khilaaran layi!  
> Ikk din geetaan da fasal uggega,  
> Mere birha nu shingaaran layi!  

**English Translation**:
> So too was my heart ploughed open by tragic passion,  
> Solely that the dark seeds of grief might be planted deep!  
> One day a golden harvest of songs shall sprout from my dust,  
> To adorn the immortal brow of cosmic separation!  

💡 *Commentary*: Somatic transformation: the poet's heart is the fertile field where poetry grows.

|:---|:---|:---|:---|

## 22. Pani Te Parchhawe

### **ਪਾਣੀ ਤੇ ਪਰਛਾਵੇਂ** • **پانی تے پرچھاUnion**

#### *"Shadows Gliding upon Canal Water"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#WaterReflections` `#Canals` `#Illusions`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: Shadows dance upon running canal water, dissolving the moment a hand reaches down to embrace them.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's meditation on the impossibility of possessing love; it remains a shimmering reflection on moving water.
- **Archival Context**: Composed along the Upper Bari Doab Canal banks outside Batala, a favorite nocturnal walking retreat for Shiv. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਹਿਰ ਦੇ ਪਾਣੀ ਤੇ ਪਰਛਾਵਾਂ ਡਿੱਗਿਆ,  
> ਤੇਰੇ ਸੋਹਣੇ ਮੁੱਖੜੇ ਦਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਹੱਥ ਵਧਾਇਆ ਫੜਨ ਦੇ ਲਈ,  
> ਪਰ ਰੂਪ ਮਿਟ ਗਿਆ ਸੁੱਖੜੇ ਦਾ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہر دے پانی تے پرچھاواں ڈگیا،  
> تیرے سوہنے مکھڑے دا نی!  
> میں ہتھ ودھایا پھڑن دے لئی،  
> پر روپ مٹ گیا سکھڑے دا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Nehar de paani te parchhaawan diggeya,  
> Tere sohne mukhrhe da ni!  
> Main hath vadhaaya pharan de layi,  
> Par roop mitt gaya sukhre da ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Upon the flowing canal water fell the reflection  
> Of your radiant, breathtaking countenance!  
> I reached out my trembling hands to embrace the vision,  
> Yet the shimmering image dissolved into rippling foam!  

💡 *Commentary*: Canal water as the mirror of transient erotic ecstasy.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪਾਣੀ ਵਗਦਾ ਜਾਵੇ ਤੇਜ਼ੀ ਨਾਲ,  
> ਮੁੜ ਕੇ ਕੰਢੇ ਨਾ ਆਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਜਿਵੇਂ ਬੀਤਿਆ ਵੇਲਾ ਜੋਬਨ ਦਾ,  
> ਬੱਸ ਯਾਦਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੁਲਾਉਂਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پانی وگدا جاوے تیزی نال،  
> مڑ کے کنڈھے نہ آؤندا اے!  
> جیویں بیتیا ویلا جوبن دا،  
> بس یاداں وچ رلاندا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Paani wagda jaave tezi naal,  
> Murh ke kandhe na aaunda ae!  
> Jiven beetiya vela joban da,  
> Bass yaadaan vich rulaaunda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> The rushing current sweeps relentlessly onward,  
> Never to return to the bank it just caressed!  
> Just like the squandered season of our youthful prime,  
> Which returns only as a ghost to torment our tears!  

💡 *Commentary*: Heraclitean motif of the unreturning river applied to human youth.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਵੀ ਇਸ ਪਾਣੀ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਵਗ ਰਿਹਾ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਸੁੰਞੇ ਸਮੁੰਦਰ ਵੱਲ ਜਾ ਰਿਹਾ ਹਾਂ!  
> ਆਪਣੇ ਹੀ ਪਰਛਾਵੇਂ ਤੋਂ ਡਰ ਕੇ,  
> ਮੌਤ ਦੇ ਗੀਤ ਗਾ ਰਿਹਾ ਹਾਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں وی اس پانی وانگوں وگ رہا ہاں،  
> سنجے سمندر ول جا رہا ہاں!  
> اپنے ہی پرچھاویں توں ڈر کے،  
> موت دے گیت گا رہا ہاں!  

**Romanized**:
> Main vi iss paani vaangoo wag reha haan,  
> Sunje samundar vall jaa reha haan!  
> Apne hi parchhaawen ton dar ke,  
> Maut de geet gaa reha haan!  

**English Translation**:
> I too am flowing like this turbulent current,  
> Rushing headlong towards the desolate, empty ocean!  
> Terrified of my own solitary, elongated shadow,  
> I sing desperate hymns to approaching Death!  

💡 *Commentary*: The ocean as the formless void of death (*sunja samundar*).

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਨਹਿਰ (Nehar)** | *Ne-har* | Irrigation canal | The vast British-built canal network crisscrossing Punjab (Upper Bari Doab Canal in Batala). |

## 23. Ghuggiyan Da Jora

### **ਘੁੱਗੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਜੋੜਾ** • **گھگیاں دا جوڑا**

#### *"Pair of Mourning Doves"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#Doves` `#Bereavement` `#VillageTrees`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: A pair of doves coos on a dried acacia bough; when the hunter strikes one down, the surviving dove dies of inconsolable grief.
- **Genesis**: Shiv took the folklore legend of the faithful dove (*ghuggi*) that refuses food after the loss of its mate.
- **Archival Context**: The mourning dove (*ghuggi*) is the classical bird of bereavement in rural Punjabi folklore. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਿੱਕਰ ਦੀ ਟਾਹਣੀ ਤੇ ਬੈਠਾ ਸੀ ਜੋੜਾ,  
> ਘੁੱਗੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਪਿਆਰ ਨਿਆਰਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਇੱਕ ਸ਼ਿਕਾਰੀ ਨੇ ਤੀਰ ਚਲਾਇਆ,  
> ਟੁੱਟ ਗਿਆ ਦੋਹਾਂ ਦਾ ਸਹਾਰਾ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ککر دی ٹاہنی تے بیٹھا سی جوڑا،  
> گھگیاں دا پیار نیارا نی!  
> اک شکاری نے تیر چلایا،  
> ٹٹ گیا دوہاں دا سہارا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Kikkar di taahni te baitha si jora,  
> Ghuggiyan da pyaar nyaara ni!  
> Ikk shikaari ne teer chalaaya,  
> Tutt gaya dohaan da sahaara ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Perched upon the thorny acacia branch rested a pair,  
> Two doves bound in tender, miraculous devotion!  
> A cruel hunter unloosed his fatal arrow from the bush,  
> Shattering forever their mutual sanctuary!  

💡 *Commentary*: The hunter represents sudden, senseless mortality cutting down love.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਰ ਘੁੱਗੀ ਡਿੱਗ ਪਿਆ ਲਹੂ 'ਚ ਭਿੱਜ ਕੇ,  
> ਮਾਦਾ ਰੋਵੇ ਕੁਰਲਾ ਕੇ ਵੇ!  
> ਨਾ ਉਹ ਉੱਡੇ ਨਾ ਚੋਗ ਚੁਗੇ ਹੁਣ,  
> ਬੈਠੀ ਮਰੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਲਾ ਕੇ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نر گھگی ڈگ پیا لہو چ بھج کے،  
> مادہ رووے کرلا کے وے!  
> نہ اوہ اڈے نہ چوگ چگے ہن،  
> بیٹھی مرے سینے لا کے وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Nar ghuggi digg peya lahoo ch bhij ke,  
> Maada rove kurlaa ke ve!  
> Na oh udde na chog chuge hun,  
> Baithi mare seene laa ke ve!  

**English Translation**:
> The male dove collapsed drenched in warm crimson blood,  
> While his faithful mate wept with piercing wails!  
> Neither would she take wing nor peck at grain again,  
> Choosing to perish upon the spot, mourning his fallen form!  

💡 *Commentary*: Folk belief that doves never take another mate and starve upon bereavement.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਡਾ ਹਾਲ ਵੀ ਉਸ ਘੁੱਗੀ ਵਰਗਾ,  
> ਹਾਣੀ ਸਾਡਾ ਤੁਰ ਗਿਆ ਪਾਰ ਨੀ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਵੀ ਚੁੱਪ-ਚਾਪ ਮਰ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਜਾਣੇ ਇਹ ਪਿਆਰ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ساڈا حال وی اس گھگی ورگا،  
> ہانی ساڈا تر گیا پار نی!  
> اسیں وی چپ چاپ مر جاواں گے،  
> کوئی نہ جانے ایہ پیار نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Saada haal vi oss ghuggi varga,  
> Haani saada tur gaya paar ni!  
> Aseen vi chup-chaap mar jaawaange,  
> Koyi na jaane eh pyaar ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Our condition is identical to that inconsolable dove,  
> For the companion of our youth has crossed into the beyond!  
> We too shall quietly close our eyes and expire in silence,  
> While this callous world remains blind to our devotion!  

💡 *Commentary*: The poet claims the dove's fatal fidelity as his own creed.

| **ਘੁੱਗੀ (Ghuggi)** | *Ghug-gee* | Collared dove | Emblem of innocence and tragic bereavement in rural Punjab; its mournful coo is considered a lament. |

## 24. Kachhe Kothe Da Booha

### **ਕੱਚੇ ਕੋਠੇ ਦਾ ਬੂਹਾ** • **کچے کوٹھے دا بوہا**

#### *"Door of the Clay Hearth"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#ClayHome` `#Threshold` `#Poverty`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The decaying timber door of a humble mud room sags on its hinges, creaking in the wind like an old man coughing in the dark.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's family lived in modest mud-plastered quarters in Batala after losing their prosperous ancestral lands in 1947.
- **Archival Context**: Documents the impoverished architectural reality of post-Partition refugee settlements in rural Punjab. (*Citation: `SHARMA-1979-SOLITARY`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੱਚੇ ਕੋਠੇ ਦਾ ਬੂਹਾ ਸਾਡਾ,  
> ਚੀਕ ਰਿਹਾ ਏ ਰਾਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਵੇਂ ਕੋਈ ਬੁੱਢਾ ਹੌਕੇ ਭਰਦਾ,  
> ਯਾਦ ਕਰੇ ਮੁਲਾਕਾਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کچے کوٹھے دا بوہا ساڈا،  
> چیک رہا اے راتاں نوں نی!  
> جیویں کوئی بڈھا ہوکے بھردا،  
> یاد کرے ملاقاتاں نوں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Kachhe kothe da booha saada,  
> Cheek reha ae raataan nu ni!  
> Jiven koyi buddha hoke bharda,  
> Yaad kare mulaakaataan nu ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The sagging door of our humble mud dwelling  
> Groans and creaks through the lonely winter nights!  
> Just like a decrepit old man heaving agonizing sighs  
> As he remembers the warm meetings of departed youth!  

💡 *Commentary*: Animistic personification: the timber door takes on elderly human infirmity.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੱਚੀਆਂ ਕੰਧਾਂ ਚੋ ਰਹੀਆਂ ਨੇ,  
> ਸਾਉਣ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਦੀਆਂ ਝੜੀਆਂ 'ਚ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਤਾਂ ਦਰਦ ਸੰਭਾਲ ਕੇ ਬੈਠੇ,  
> ਸੁੰਞੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੀਆਂ ਘੜੀਆਂ 'ਚ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کچیاں کندھاں چو رہیاں نے،  
> ساؤن مہینے دیاں جھڑیاں چ!  
> اسیں تاں درد سنبھال کے بیٹھے،  
> سنجے دل دیاں گھڑیاں چ!  

**Romanized**:
> Kachhiyaan kandhaan cho rahiyaan ne,  
> Saun maheene diyaan jhariyaan ch!  
> Aseen taan dard sambhaal ke baithe,  
> Sunje dil diyaan ghariyaan ch!  

**English Translation**:
> The unbaked mud walls leak muddy rainwater  
> Under the relentless downpours of the month of Saawan!  
> We sit huddled inside, nursing our immortal grief  
> Through the empty, ticking hours of a shattered heart!  

💡 *Commentary*: Monsoon downpours (*jhariyaan*) dissolving mud walls mirror internal emotional dissolution.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇੱਕ ਦਿਨ ਇਹ ਕੋਠਾ ਢਹਿ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਮਿੱਟੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਮਿਲ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਜਿੰਦ ਵੀ ਮੁੱਕ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਤੇ ਦਰਦ ਦੀ ਅੱਗ ਬੁੱਝ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اک دن ایہ کوٹھا ڈھہہ جاوے گا،  
> مٹی مٹی وچ مل جاوے گی!  
> ساڈی جند وی مک جاوے گی،  
> تے درد دی اگ بجھ جاوے گی!  

**Romanized**:
> Ikk din eh kotha dheh jaavega,  
> Mitti mitti vich mil jaavegi!  
> Saadi jind vi mukk jaavegi,  
> Te dard di agg bujh jaavegi!  

**English Translation**:
> One fateful day this fragile room will collapse to the ground,  
> Clay returning to clay in quiet, final reunion!  
> Our weary breath shall cease its painful labor,  
> And the searing fire of this grief will finally be extinguished!  

💡 *Commentary*: Peace found only in total return to dust.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਕੱਚਾ ਕੋਠਾ (Kachha Kotha)** | *Kach-cha Ko-tha* | Unbaked mud room | Humble dwelling made of sundried clay bricks and timber beams, vulnerable to heavy monsoon rains. |

## 25. Suneha Hawan Da

### **ਸੁਨੇਹਾ ਹਵਾਵਾਂ ਦਾ** • **سنیہا ہواواں دا**

#### *"Whisper of the Northern Wind"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#WindMessenger` `#ColdBreeze` `#Exile`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The icy northern gale brings a phantom message from across the mountains, whispering that the beloved has forgotten the wanderer.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's acute sensitivity to changes in weather and seasonal winds, which he associated with shifting emotional moods.
- **Archival Context**: Features the north wind (*parbhati poun*) that brings Himalayan chill into the plains of Punjab in December. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਉੱਤਰ ਵੱਲੋਂ ਹਵਾ ਜੋ ਆਈ,  
> ਕੰਬਣੀ ਛੇੜ ਗਈ ਸੀਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਸਨੇਹਾ ਲਿਆਈ ਲੱਗਦਾ,  
> ਇਸ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اتر ولوں ہوا جو آئی،  
> کنبنی چھیڑ گئی سینے وچ!  
> کوئی سنیہا لیائی لگدا،  
> اس برہا دے مہینے وچ!  

**Romanized**:
> Uttar vallon hawa jo aayi,  
> Kambani chherh gayi seene vich!  
> Koyi saneha lyaayi lagda,  
> Iss birha de maheene vich!  

**English Translation**:
> The icy wind that blew down from the northern peaks  
> Sent a violent shiver through the chambers of my breast!  
> It seems to have brought a spectral message on its wings  
> In this desolate, frozen month of separation!  

💡 *Commentary*: The northern wind (uttar di hawa) brings physical chill and emotional dread.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਆਖੇ ਤੇਰਾ ਸੱਜਣ ਭੁੱਲ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਉਹ ਤੈਨੂੰ ਯਾਦ ਨਾ ਕਰਦਾ!  
> ਪਰਦੇਸਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਜਾ ਕੇ ਉਹ ਤਾਂ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਹੋਰ ਤੇ ਮਰਦਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> آکھے تیرا سجن بھل گیا،  
> ہن اوہ تینوں یاد نہ کردا!  
> پردیساں وچ جا کے اوہ تاں،  
> کسے ہور تے مردا!  

**Romanized**:
> Aakhe tera sajjan bhull gaya,  
> Hun oh tainu yaad na karda!  
> Paradesan vich jaa ke oh taan,  
> Kise hor te marda!  

**English Translation**:
> It whispers cruelly that your beloved has forgotten your face,  
> That he no longer holds your name in reverent memory!  
> Dwelling far away in those opulent foreign cities,  
> His heart has surrendered to another companion!  

💡 *Commentary*: The wind as the bearer of unbearable truths or paranoiac doubts.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੇ ਹਵਾ ਤੂੰ ਝੂਠ ਨਾ ਬੋਲ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਦਿਲ ਨਾ ਐਵੇਂ ਤੋੜ ਵੇ!  
> ਜੇ ਉਹ ਭੁੱਲ ਵੀ ਗਿਆ ਏ ਸਾਨੂੰ,  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਨਹੀਂ ਛੱਡਣੀ ਇਹ ਲੋੜ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اے ہوا توں جھوٹھ نہ بول،  
> میرا دل نہ ایویں توڑ وے!  
> جے اوہ بھل وی گیا اے سانوں،  
> اسیں نہیں چھڈنی ایہ لوڑ وے!  

**Romanized**:
> He hawa tu jhooth na bol,  
> Mera dil na aiven torh ve!  
> Je oh bhull vi gaya ae saanu,  
> Aseen nahin chhadni eh lorh ve!  

**English Translation**:
> O merciless wind, do not speak these treacherous lies,  
> Do not shatter my defenseless heart without cause!  
> Even if he has truly consigned our name to the dust,  
> We shall never relinquish the sacred need of his love!  

💡 *Commentary*: Defiance: unrequited love persists even when betrayal is confirmed.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਸੁਨੇਹਾ (Suneha)** | *Su-ne-ha* | Verbal message / Tidings | Crucial element in folk romances carried by birds, winds, or wandering bards. |

## 26. Ret Da Mahal

### **ਰੇਤ ਦਾ ਮਹਿਲ** • **ریت دا محل**

#### *"Castle Built of River Sand"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#Sandcastle` `#FutileHope` `#Decay`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The poet builds an opulent palace out of river sand, watching with tearful fascination as the first gentle wave dissolves its spires.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's awareness that his romantic ideals were too delicate to survive the harsh reality of feudal Punjab.
- **Archival Context**: A quintessential Shiv metaphor: creating magnificent architectural monuments out of perishable materials. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਰੇਤ ਦਾ ਮਹਿਲ ਬਣਾਇਆ ਅਸੀਂ,  
> ਦਰਿਆ ਦੇ ਕੰਢੇ ਤੇ ਬਹਿ ਕੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਸੋਚਿਆ ਸੀ ਇੱਥੇ ਵਸਾਂਗੇ ਦੋਵੇਂ,  
> ਸੁੱਖ ਦੇ ਪਲ ਕੁਝ ਕਹਿ ਕੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ریت دا محل بنایا اسیں،  
> دریا دے کنڈھے تے بہہ کے نی!  
> سوچیا سی ایتھے وساں گے دوویں،  
> سکھ دے پل کجھ کہہ کے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Ret da mahal banaaya aseen,  
> Dariya de kandhe te beh ke ni!  
> Sochiya si itthe vasaange dovein,  
> Sukh de pal kujh keh ke ni!  

**English Translation**:
> We built a magnificent palace of glistening river sand,  
> Sitting together upon the lonely embankment of the stream!  
> We dreamed we would dwell within its sunlit chambers,  
> Sharing quiet words of tenderness through peaceful hours!  

💡 *Commentary*: The sandcastle as the grand illusion of youthful love.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇੱਕ ਛੱਲ ਆਈ ਦਰਿਆ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ,  
> ਮਹਿਲ ਉਹ ਪਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੋੜ੍ਹ ਗਈ ਨੀ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਉਮਰਾਂ ਦੀ ਜੋ ਕਮਾਈ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹਨੂੰ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਮਰੋੜ ਗਈ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اک چھل آئی دریا وچوں،  
> محل اوہ پل وچ روڑھ گئی نی!  
> ساڈی عمراں دی جو کمائی سی،  
> اوہنوں مٹی وچ مروڑ گئی نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Ikk chhall aayi dariya vichon,  
> Mahal oh pal vich rorh gayi ni!  
> Saadi umraan di jo kamaayi si,  
> Ohnu mitti vich marorh gayi ni!  

**English Translation**:
> A single cresting wave surged from the river's heart,  
> And swept our palace away in the fraction of an instant!  
> The entire accumulated treasure of our mortal lifetimes  
> Was crushed and mangled back into common, featureless mud!  

💡 *Commentary*: The river wave (*chhall*) represents indifferent, unstoppable time.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਖੜ੍ਹੇ ਹਾਂ ਕੰਢੇ ਤੇ ਇਕੱਲੇ,  
> ਖ਼ਾਲੀ ਹੱਥ ਤੇ ਰੋਂਦੀਆਂ ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਰੇਤ ਦੇ ਘਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਬਣਦੇ ਹੁੰਦੇ,  
> ਇਹ ਗੱਲਾਂ ਜੱਗ ਨੇ ਸੱਚੀਆਂ ਆਖੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن کھڑے ہاں کنڈھے تے اکلی،  
> خالی ہتھ تے روندیاں اکھیاں نی!  
> ریت دے گھر نہیں بندے ہوندے،  
> ایہ گلاں جگ نے سچیاں آکھیاں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun kharhe haan kandhe te ikalle,  
> Khaali hath te rondiyaan akhiyaan ni!  
> Ret de ghar nahin bande hunde,  
> Eh gallan jagg ne sacchiyaan aakhiyaan ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Now we stand deserted upon the barren shoreline,  
> With empty hands and burning, weeping eyes!  
> Houses sculpted of sand can never withstand the storm—  
> Alas, how terribly true were the warnings of this callous world!  

💡 *Commentary*: Belated vindication of cynical worldly wisdom over romantic passion.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਛੱਲ (Chhall)** | *Chhall* | River wave / Surge | The sudden rising wave in a monsoon-fed river in Punjab that floods sandy banks. |

## 27. Chanan Di Toli

### **ਚਾਨਣ ਦੀ ਟੋਲੀ** • **چانن دی ٹولی**

#### *"Company of Morning Rays"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#Morning` `#Sunbeams` `#GriefasLight`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The company of morning rays enters the dark chamber like an uninvited festive crowd, disturbing the sacred gloom of the grieving poet.
- **Genesis**: Shiv lived largely by night; daylight felt like a harsh, uninvited interrogation of his private melancholy.
- **Archival Context**: Explores Shiv's unique sensory synesthesia where light is experienced as physical weight and intrusion. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਚਾਨਣ ਦੀ ਟੋਲੀ ਆ ਵੜੀ ਵਿਹੜੇ,  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਨੀਂਦ ਉਚਾਟਣ ਆ ਗਈ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਅਸੀਂ ਲੁਕਾ ਕੇ ਰੱਖਿਆ,  
> ਉਸ ਨੂੰ ਜੱਗ ਨੂੰ ਵਿਖਾਉਣ ਆ ਗਈ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> چانن دی ٹولی آ وڑی ویہڑے،  
> ساڈی نیند اچاٹن آ گئی نی!  
> جو درد اسیں لکا کے رکھیا،  
> اس نوں جگ نوں وکھاؤن آ گئی نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Chanan di toli aa varhi vehre,  
> Saadi neend uchaatan aa gayi ni!  
> Jo dard aseen lukaa ke rakkheya,  
> Uss nu jagg nu vikhaaon aa gayi ni!  

**English Translation**:
> A festive company of morning rays burst into the courtyard,  
> Arriving solely to shatter the fragile sanctuary of our sleep!  
> That private wound which we had concealed so carefully  
> It comes to expose to the mocking gaze of the world!  

💡 *Commentary*: Light seen as an intrusive invader violating the privacy of grief.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਿਰਨਾਂ ਨੱਚਣ ਕੰਧਾਂ ਉੱਤੇ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਦਿਲ ਤੇ ਛੁਰੀਆਂ ਚੱਲਣ ਵੇ!  
> ਇਸ ਚਾਨਣ ਦੇ ਮੇਲੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਸਾਹ ਅੰਗਿਆਰਾਂ 'ਚ ਬਲਣ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کرناں نچن کندھاں اتے،  
> ساڈے دل تے چھریاں چلن وے!  
> اس چانن دے میلے وچ،  
> ساڈے ساہ انگیاڑاں چ بلن وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kirnaan nachan kandhaan utte,  
> Saade dil te chhuriyaan challan ve!  
> Iss chanan de mele vich,  
> Saade saah angiyaaraan ch balan ve!  

**English Translation**:
> Sunbeams dance playfully upon the mud-plastered walls,  
> Yet their dance cuts like jagged daggers across our heart!  
> Amidst this joyous carnival of morning radiance,  
> Our breath smolders like embers in a deserted hearth!  

💡 *Commentary*: Daggers of light (chhuriyaan) cutting across the mourning soul.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਨੂੰ ਕਾਲੀ ਰਾਤ ਹੀ ਭਾਉਂਦੀ ਏ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਵਹਾ ਸਕਦੇ ਹਾਂ!  
> ਇਸ ਚਾਨਣ ਦੇ ਸ਼ੋਰ ਤੋਂ ਦੂਰ,  
> ਆਪਣਾ ਦਰਦ ਸੁਣਾ ਸਕਦੇ ਹਾਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> سانوں کالی رات ہی بھاؤندی اے،  
> جتھے ہنجھو وہا سکدے ہاں!  
> اس چانن دے شور توں دور،  
> اپنا درد سنا سکدے ہاں!  

**Romanized**:
> Saanu kaali raat hi bhaaundi ae,  
> Jithe hanjhu vahaa sakde haan!  
> Iss chanan de shor ton door,  
> Apna dard sunaa sakde haan!  

**English Translation**:
> Only the velvet sanctuary of pitch-black night pleases us,  
> Where unobserved we can pour forth our flowing tears!  
> Far removed from the blinding clamor of daylight,  
> Where in silence we may confess our mortal agony!  

💡 *Commentary*: Nocturnal preference: night as the only compassionate mother.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਨੀਂਦ ਉਚਾਟਣਾ (Neend Uchaatna)** | *Neend U-chaat-na* | To startle or banish sleep | Punjabi idiom describing the abrupt, painful awakening from comforting slumber into harsh reality. |

## 28. Chup Di Kauli

### **ਚੁੱਪ ਦੀ ਕੌਲੀ** • **چپ دی کولی**

#### *"Bowl of Quiet Tears"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#BowlofTears` `#BeggarBowl` `#Kashkol`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: The poet sits like a wandering mendicant holding out an earthen bowl, begging not for bread, but for a single tear of understanding.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's self-identification as a fakir or beggar of emotional communion in a transactional world.
- **Archival Context**: Invokes the Sufi beggar's bowl (*kashkol*), transformed here into an earthenware bowl collecting unshed tears. (*Citation: `AMRITA-1974-NAGMANI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਚੁੱਪ ਦੀ ਕੌਲੀ ਹੱਥ ਵਿੱਚ ਫੜ ਕੇ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਦੇ ਦਰ ਤੇ ਖੜੋਤਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਖ਼ੈਰ ਮੁਹੱਬਤ ਦੀ ਪਾਵੇ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਮੋਤੀ ਖੋਹਤਾ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> چپ دی کولی ہتھ وچ پھڑ کے،  
> میں دنیا دے در تے کھڑوتا نی!  
> نہ کوئی خیر محبت دی پاوے،  
> میں دل دا موتی کھوہتا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Chup di kauli hath vich pharh ke,  
> Main duniya de dar te kharhota ni!  
> Na koyi khair mohabbat di paave,  
> Main dil da moti khohta ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Holding the earthen bowl of silence in my trembling hands,  
> I stood as a beggar at the threshold of this world!  
> Yet not a single soul dropped the alms of love into my dish,  
> And I lost the priceless pearl of my innocent heart!  

💡 *Commentary*: The beggar of love (ishq da fakir) receiving empty silence from society.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਲੋਕੀਂ ਦਿੰਦੇ ਨੇ ਪੱਥਰ ਮੈਨੂੰ,  
> ਜਦ ਮੈਂ ਪਿਆਰ ਦੀ ਭਿੱਖਿਆ ਮੰਗਾਂ ਵੇ!  
> ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਦੇ ਜ਼ਾਲਮ ਹਾਸਿਆਂ ਤੋਂ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣਾ ਆਪ ਛੁਪਾ ਕੇ ਲੰਘਾਂ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> لوکی دیندے نے پتھر مینوں،  
> جد میں پیار دی بھکھیا منگاں وے!  
> اس دنیا دے ظالم ہاسیاں توں،  
> میں اپنا آپ چھپا کے لنگھاں وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Loki dinde ne patthar mainu,  
> Jad main pyaar di bhikkhiya mangaan ve!  
> Iss duniya de zaalim haasiyaan ton,  
> Main apna aap chhupaa ke langhaan ve!  

**English Translation**:
> Callous men toss sharp stones into my begging bowl  
> Whenever I plead for the gentle alms of affection!  
> Shrinking from the cruel, mocking laughter of the crowd,  
> I slip away into the shadows to conceal my weeping!  

💡 *Commentary*: Stones instead of bread: biblical and folk resonance of rejection.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਹ ਕੌਲੀ ਮੇਰੀ ਕਬਰ ਤੱਕ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਇਹਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਮੇਰੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਹੋਣਗੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਮਰਨ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਇਹ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਵਾਲੇ,  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਰੋਣਗੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ایہ کولی میری قبر تک جاوے گی،  
> ایہندے وچ میرے ہنجھو ہون گے نی!  
> میرے مرن توں بعد ایہ دنیا والے،  
> میرے گیتاں نوں رون گے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Eh kauli meri kabr takk jaavegi,  
> Ehde vich mere hanjhu honge ni!  
> Mere maran ton baad eh duniya vaale,  
> Mere geetaan nu ronge ni!  

**English Translation**:
> This humble earthen bowl shall accompany me to the grave,  
> Filled to the brim with my accumulated, unspilt tears!  
> And long after I have turned to cold, silent dust,  
> The inhabitants of this very world will weep over my songs!  

💡 *Commentary*: The begging bowl buried with the poet as his only worldly possession.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਕੌਲੀ (Kauli)** | *Kau-lee* | Small earthen or metal bowl | Traditional rustic vessel used for meals or by wandering holy men as an alms bowl. |

## 29. Virlaap Geet

### **ਵਿਰਲਾਪ ਗੀਤ** • **ورلاپ گیت**

#### *"Lament for the Unborn Spring"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#Virlaap` `#Elegy` `#Wail`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: A piercing lamentation for a spring that withered in the seed, for a youth that died before it could taste the nectar of fulfillment.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's identification with the unfulfilled lives of young Punjabi women who died in childbirth or forced marriages.
- **Archival Context**: Employs the formal Punjabi women's lament genre (*siapa* and *keerne*), elevated into existential literature. (*Citation: `AMRITA-1974-NAGMANI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਿੱਥੇ ਗਈਆਂ ਉਹ ਮਿੱਠੀਆਂ ਰੁੱਤਾਂ,  
> ਕਿੱਥੇ ਗਏ ਉਹ ਹਾਸੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਵਿਹੜੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਪੈ ਗਏ ਰੋਣੇ,  
> ਸੁੰਞੇ ਪਏ ਨੇ ਕਾਸੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کتھے گئیاں اوہ مٹھیاں رتاں،  
> کتھے گئے اوہ ہاسے نی!  
> ساڈے ویہڑے وچ پے گئے رونے،  
> سنجے پئے نے کاسے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Kitthe gaiyaan oh mitthiyaan ruttaan,  
> Kitthe gaye oh haase ni!  
> Saade vehre vich pai gaye rone,  
> Sunje paye ne kaase ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Whither have vanished those sweet, fragrant seasons?  
> Whither have fled those bright, unburdened smiles?  
> In our ancestral courtyard only funeral wails resound,  
> While our earthenware dishes lie empty and untasted!  

💡 *Commentary*: Opens with the classic rhetorical interrogative (*Kitthe gaiyaan*) of Punjabi elegiac poetry.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੋਇਲ ਵੀ ਅੱਜ ਰੋ ਰੋ ਕੂਕੇ,  
> ਬੋਹੜਾਂ ਤੇ ਮਾਤਮ ਛਾਇਆ ਏ!  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਜੋਬਨ ਦੀ ਖਿੜਦੀ ਕਲੀ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਕਿਸ ਪਾਪੀ ਨੇ ਮੁਰਝਾਇਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کوئل وی اج رو رو کوکے،  
> بوہڑاں تے ماتم چھایا اے!  
> ساڈے جوبن دی کھڑدی کلی نوں،  
> کس پاپی نے مرجھایا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Koyal vi ajj ro ro kooke,  
> Bohraan te maatam chhaaya ae!  
> Saade joban di khirhdi kali nu,  
> Kis paapi ne murjhaaya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Even the dark songbird of the grove wails in mourning today,  
> While the ancient banyan trees stand cloaked in grief!  
> That blossoming rosebud of our tender youthful prime,  
> What heartless villain has crushed it into the dust?  

💡 *Commentary*: The cuckoo's song transformed from herald of love to mourning bird.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਵਿਰਲਾਪ ਕਰੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਰੂਹ ਅੱਜ ਸਾਰੀ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਹੰਝੂ ਪੂੰਝਣ ਆਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਮਰਨ ਤੇ ਇਹ ਜ਼ਾਲਮ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ,  
> ਝੂਠੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਵਹਾਵੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ورلاپ کرے میری روح اج ساری،  
> کوئی نہ ہنجھو پونجھن آوے گا!  
> میرے مرن تے ایہ ظالم دنیا،  
> جھوٹھے ہنجھو وہاوے گا!  

**Romanized**:
> Virlaap kare meri rooh ajj saari,  
> Koyi na hanjhu poonjhan aavega!  
> Mere maran te eh zaalim duniya,  
> Jhoothe hanjhu vahaavega!  

**English Translation**:
> My entire soul raises an unceasing, desolate lament,  
> Knowing full well that none will step forward to wipe these tears!  
> Yet upon my death, this very callous, hypocritical world  
> Will stage grand mourning with counterfeit saltwater tears!  

💡 *Commentary*: Scorn for society's hypocritical post-mortem sanctimony.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਵਿਰਲਾਪ (Virlaap)** | *Vir-laap* | Wailing / Ritual lamentation | Formal rhythmic lamentation recited by women in Punjab following a tragic death. |

## 30. Aate Da Diya

### **ਆਟੇ ਦਾ ਦੀਵਾ** • **آٹے دا دیوا**

#### *"The Lamp Sculpted of Dough"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#DoughLamp` `#FolkRitual` `#RiverSacrifice`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: A lamp sculpted of kneaded dough is lit and cast upon the dark canal waters; it burns heroically even as its flour body softens and dissolves.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's ultimate metaphor for his own poetry: burning with holy fire while the physical body melts into dissolution.
- **Archival Context**: Companion to the dough sparrows (*aate diyan chiriyaan*); village women fashioned lamps of kneaded flour to float down the canal on Kartik Purnima. (*Citation: `AMRITA-1974-NAGMANI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਆਟੇ ਦਾ ਦੀਵਾ ਘੜ ਕੇ ਮੈਂ,  
> ਦਰਿਆ ਦੇ ਪਾਣੀ ਤਾਰਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਘਿਓ ਦੀ ਬੱਤੀ ਬਾਲ ਕੇ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਆਪਣਾ ਆਪ ਨਿਵਾਰਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> آٹے دا دیوا گھڑ کے میں،  
> دریا دے پانی تاریا نی!  
> گھیو دی بتی بال کے اندر،  
> اپنا آپ نواریا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Aate da deeva gharh ke main,  
> Dariya de paani taareya ni!  
> Ghio di batti baal ke andar,  
> Apna aap nivaareya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Sculpting a fragile lamp of kneaded wheat dough with my own hands,  
> I floated it gently upon the dark, rushing canal waters!  
> Igniting the cotton wick steeped in fragrant ghee within its bowl,  
> I made a sacrificial offering of my entire mortal existence!  

💡 *Commentary*: The dough lamp (*aate da deeva*) as an emblem of self-sacrificing art.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪਾਣੀ ਦੀਆਂ ਛੱਲਾਂ ਛੋਹਣ ਇਸ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਆਟਾ ਖੁਰਦਾ ਜਾਵੇ ਵੇ!  
> ਪਰ ਲੌਅ ਇਹਦੀ ਅੰਬਰ ਵੱਲ ਤੱਕੇ,  
> ਜਿਵੇਂ ਮੌਤ ਨੂੰ ਹੱਸ ਬੁਲਾਵੇ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پانی دیاں چھلاں چھوہن اس نوں،  
> آٹا کھردا جاوے وے!  
> پر لوء ایہدی انبر ول تکے،  
> جیویں موت نوں ہس بلاوے وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Paani diyaan chhalaan chhohan iss nu,  
> Aata khurda jaave ve!  
> Par lau ehdi ambar vall takke,  
> Jiven maut nu hass bulaave ve!  

**English Translation**:
> The cold river waves lap relentlessly against its flour rim,  
> And the soft dough begins to soften and dissolve in the deep!  
> Yet its golden flame points defiantly upwards towards the stars,  
> As though summoning Death with an ecstatic, fearless smile!  

💡 *Commentary*: The body dissolves while the spiritual flame remains pointed towards eternity.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅੰਤ ਸਮੇਂ ਜਦ ਦੀਵਾ ਡੁੱਬਿਆ,  
> ਪਾਣੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੌਸ਼ਨੀ ਫੈਲ ਗਈ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਦੀ ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ਬੂ ਸੱਜਣਾ,  
> ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਦੇ ਮਨ ਬਹਿ ਗਈ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> انت سمیں جد دیوا ڈبیا،  
> پانی وچ روشنی پھیل گئی نی!  
> میرے گیتاں دی خوشبو سجنا،  
> اس دنیا دے من بہہ گئی نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Ant samayn jad deeva dubbeya,  
> Paani vich raushni phail gayi ni!  
> Mere geetaan di khushboo sajjna,  
> Iss duniya de mann beh gayi ni!  

**English Translation**:
> In the final moment when the waterlogged lamp sank beneath the surface,  
> A flash of celestial radiance diffused across the dark flood!  
> And the immortal perfume of my tragic songs, O beloved,  
> Settled forever deep within the heart of this weeping world!  

💡 *Commentary*: Triumph in destruction: the sinking lamp illuminates the water.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਆਟੇ ਦਾ ਦੀਵਾ (Aate da Deeva)** | *Aa-te da Dee-va* | Lamp molded from flour dough | Sacred ritual lamp floated on rivers during Hindu and folk festivals to pray for ancestral peace or childbearing. |
