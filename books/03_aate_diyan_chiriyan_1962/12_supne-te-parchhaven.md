# Supne Te Parchhaven

### **ਸੁਪਨੇ ਤੇ ਪਰਛਾਵੇਂ** • **سپنے تے پرچھاویں**

#### *"Dreams and Fleeting Shadows"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aate Diyan Chiriyaan (1962)](../03_aate_diyan_chiriyan_1962.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸੁਪਨੇ ਤੇ ਪਰਛਾਵੇਂ (سپنے تے پرچھاویں)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1962
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Dreams` `#Shadows` `#Illusion` `#Maya` `#Unreality`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `modernism` | **Landscape Biome**: `snowy_cabin_night`

**Synopsis**: The poet realizes that the dreams of youth were merely unsubstantial shadows cast by the setting sun of hope.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala during late autumn as the days shortened rapidly.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Explores the philosophical concept of Maya (cosmic illusion), contrasting the solid world of matter with phantom memories.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਸਾਂ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਵੇਖੇ ਸੀ ਜਿਊਣ ਦੇ,  
> ਪਰ ਨਿਕਲੇ ਪਰਛਾਵੇਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਉਂ-ਜਿਉਂ ਅਸੀਂ ਨੇੜੇ ਗਏ,  
> ਉਹ ਹੋਰ ਦੂਰ ਹੁੰਦੇ ਜਾਵੇਂ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اساں سپنے ویکھے سی جیون دے،  
> پر نکلے پرچھاویں نی!  
> جیوں جیوں اسیں نیڑے گئے،  
> اوہ ہور دور ہندے جاویں نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Asaan supne vekhe si jiyun de,  
> Par nikle parchhaaven ni!  
> Jiyon-jiyon aseen nerhe gaye,  
> Oh hor door hunde jaaven ni!  

#### English Translation
> We nurtured luminous dreams of truly living,  
> Yet they revealed themselves to be mere phantoms and shadows!  
> The closer we strove to embrace them with open arms,  
> The farther they retreated into the unreachable distance!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The mirage of romantic happiness retreating on approach.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸਾਯਾ ਵੀ ਸਾਡਾ ਸਾਥ ਛੱਡ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਜਦ ਘੁੱਪ ਹਨੇਰਾ ਆਇਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਰੌਸ਼ਨੀ 'ਤੇ ਮਾਣ ਸੀ ਸਾਨੂੰ,  
> ਉਸਨੇ ਹੀ ਭਰਮਾਇਆ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> سایہ وی ساڈا ساتھ چھڈ گیا،  
> جد گھپ ہنیرا آیا نی!  
> جس روشنی تے مان سی سانوں،  
> اس نے ہی بھرمایا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Saaya vi saada saath chhadd gaya,  
> Jad ghupp hanera aaya ni!  
> Jis raushni te maan si saanu,  
> Usne hi bharmaaya ni!  

#### English Translation
> Even our own faithful shadow abandoned our companionship  
> The moment pitch-black darkness descended upon us!  
> That deceptive light of which we were so proud  
> Was the very illumination that led us astray into illusion!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Desertion of the shadow in darkness; light as deceiver.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਹੁਣ ਪਰਛਾਵਿਆਂ ਪਿੱਛੇ ਨਹੀਂ ਦੌੜਦਾ,  
> ਉਸ ਸੱਚ ਪਛਾਣ ਲਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਇਸ ਜੱਗ ਦੇ ਝੂਠੇ ਸੁਪਨਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਮਿੱਟੀ 'ਚ ਛਾਣ ਲਿਆ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو ہن پرچھاویاں پچھے نہیں دوڑدا،  
> اس سچ پچھان لیا اے!  
> اس جگ دے جھوٹھے سپنیاں نوں،  
> مٹی چ چھان لیا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv hun parchhaaviyan pichhe nahin daurhda,  
> Us sach pachhaan leya ae!  
> Iss jagg de jhoothe supniyaan nu,  
> Mitti ch chhaan leya ae!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv no longer runs frantically after fleeting shadows;  
> He has recognized the stark, unbending truth of existence!  
> The fraudulent, gilded dreams of this deceitful world  
> He has sifted and discarded into the indifferent dust!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Liberation through complete disillusionment.*
