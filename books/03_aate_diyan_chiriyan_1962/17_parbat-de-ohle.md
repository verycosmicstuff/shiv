# Parbat De Ohle

### **ਪਰਬਤ ਦੇ ਉਹਲੇ** • **پربت دے اوہلے**

#### *"Behind the Solitary Mountain"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aate Diyan Chiriyaan (1962)](../03_aate_diyan_chiriyan_1962.md)
- **Original Title**: ਪਰਬਤ ਦੇ ਉਹਲੇ (پربت دے اوہلے)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1962
- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#Mountains` `#Shivalik` `#Horizon`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `village_monsoon`

**Synopsis**: The beloved has departed behind the insurmountable mountain ridges where no letter or song can penetrate.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's geographic separation from his beloved who was married to a government officer in a mountain station.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Written during Shiv's travels near Dalhousie in the Shivalik foothills, looking towards the plains.

**Source**: *Shiv Batalvi: A Solitary and Passionate Singer* by Dr. O.P. Sharma (1979, Sterling Publishers, New Delhi, pp. 12–45, 180–205). [Primary Memoir]

> "Authoritative academic biography documenting his childhood in Bara Pind Lohtian (now Pakistan), the trauma of the 1947 Partition flight, his clerkship at the State Bank of India Chandigarh, and his final collapse in Kir Mangyal."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਪਰਬਤ ਦੇ ਉਹਲੇ ਤੁਰ ਗਿਆ ਸੱਜਣ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਕੋਲ ਬੱਸ ਹੰਝੂ ਰਹਿ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਉੱਚੀਆਂ ਧਾਰਾਂ ਰੋਕ ਲਏ ਰਾਹ,  
> ਦਰਦ ਸਾਡੇ ਦਿਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹਿ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> پربت دے اوہلے تر گیا سجن،  
> ساڈے کول بس ہنجھو رہ گئے نی!  
> اچیاں دھاراں روک لئے راہ،  
> درد ساڈے دل وچ بہہ گئے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Parbat de ohle tur gaya sajjan,  
> Saade kol bass hanjhu reh gaye ni!  
> Ucchiyaan dhaaraan rok laye raah,  
> Dard saade dil vich beh gaye ni!  

#### English Translation
> Behind the distant mountain ridge my beloved has departed,  
> Leaving only scalding tears to keep company with my heart!  
> Towering stony peaks have blocked the mountain paths,  
> While boundless sorrow has encamped forever in my soul!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The mountain (parbat) as a physical and emotional barricade.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਬੱਦਲ ਆਉਂਦੇ ਪਰਬਤ ਵੱਲੋਂ,  
> ਸਨੇਹਾ ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਲਿਆਉਂਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਠੰਢੀਆਂ ਪੌਣਾਂ ਛੋਹ ਕੇ ਲੰਘਣ,  
> ਹੋਰ ਵੀ ਅੱਗ ਲਗਾਉਂਦੇ ਨੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> بدل آؤندے پربت ولوں،  
> سنیہا کوئی نہ لیاؤندے نے!  
> ٹھنڈیاں پوناں چھوہ کے لنگھن،  
> ہور وی اگ لگاؤندے نے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Baddal aaunde parbat vallon,  
> Saneha koyi na lyaaunde ne!  
> Thandhiyaan paunan chhoh ke langhan,  
> Hor vi agg lagaaunde ne!  

#### English Translation
> Drifting clouds glide down from those lofty crags,  
> Yet they bring no written word of love from his hand!  
> Chilly mountain breezes brush past my shivering flesh,  
> Only to stoke the flames of longing fiercer than before!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Inversion of the classical Kalidasa Meghaduta (cloud-messenger) motif: the clouds bring silence.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕਾਸ਼ ਮੈਂ ਬਣ ਜਾਂ ਬੱਦਲ ਕਾਲਾ,  
> ਪਰਬਤ ਪਾਰ ਉੱਡ ਜਾਵਾਂ ਵੇ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਮਾਹੀ ਵਸਦਾ,  
> ਉਹਦੇ ਕਦਮੀਂ ਵਰ੍ਹ ਜਾਵਾਂ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کاش میں بن جاں بدل کالا،  
> پربت پار اڈ جاواں وے!  
> جتھے میرا ماہی وسدا،  
> اوہدے قدمیں ورھ جاواں وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kaash main ban jaan baddal kaala,  
> Parbat paar udd jaawaan ve!  
> Jithe mera maahi vasda,  
> Ohde kadmeen varh jaawaan ve!  

#### English Translation
> Would that I could transform into a dark monsoon cloud,  
> Soaring unimpeded across those insurmountable ridges!  
> To that distant valley where my wanderer dwells,  
> And dissolve into torrential rain beneath his feet!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Self-dissolution as the ultimate mode of reaching the beloved.*
