# Aate Diyan Chiriyaan

### **ਆਟੇ ਦੀਆਂ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ (ਸਿਰਲੇਖ ਕਵਿਤਾ)** • **آٹے دیاں چڑیاں**

#### *"The Dough-Sparrows: Title Poem"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aate Diyan Chiriyaan (1962)](../03_aate_diyan_chiriyan_1962.md)
- **Original Title**: ਆਟੇ ਦੀਆਂ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ (ਸਿਰਲੇਖ ਕਵਿਤਾ) (آٹے دیاں چڑیاں)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1962
- **Thematic Tags**: `#DoughBirds` `#Folklore` `#Mothers` `#Innocence` `#Hunger`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `village_monsoon`

**Synopsis**: Village mothers mold dough sparrows to placate hungry children during hardship; Shiv uses this to depict the frail, counterfeit joys of human existence.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Inspired by watching women shape dough into bird figures before baking in the village tandoor.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> The title poem of Shiv's third book (1962), hailed by Punjabi critics as an extraordinary metaphor for surrogate maternal consolation.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> Regarded as one of the most brilliant symbols of Punjabi maternal folk life in modern literature.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Tender, rhythmic folk cadence.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (4 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮਾਏ ਨੀ ਮਾਏ ਤੇਰੀਆਂ ਗੁੰਨ੍ਹੀਆਂ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ,  
> ਉੱਡ ਨਾ ਸਕੀਆਂ ਅੰਬਰਾਂ ਵੱਲ ਨੀ!  
> ਭੁੱਖੇ ਢਿੱਡਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਬਹਿਲਾਵਣ ਖ਼ਾਤਰ,  
> ਤੂੰ ਵੀ ਘੜ ਲਈ ਨਵੀਂ ਛਲ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> مائے نی مائے تیریاں گنہیاں چڑیاں،  
> اڈ نہ سکیاں عنبراں ول نی!  
> بھکھے ڈھڈاں نوں بہلاون خاطر،  
> توں وی گھڑ لئی نویں چھل نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Maye ni maye teriyan gunnhiyaan chiriyaan,  
> Udd na sakiyaan ambaraan vall ni!  
> Bhukkhe dhiddaan nu behlaavan khaatar,  
> Toon vi gharh layi naveen chhal ni!  

#### English Translation
> O mother, my mother, those sparrows you molded of wheat flour  
> Could never take wing and soar toward the blue heavens!  
> Merely to console the hollow pangs of hungry child-bellies,  
> You too devised this gentle, innocent deception!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The mother sculpts dough birds to pacify hungry children, symbolizing how culture invents consolations for mortal lack.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜਦ ਤੰਦੂਰ ਦੇ ਸੇਕ 'ਚ ਪਈਆਂ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਖੰਭ ਉਹਨਾਂ ਦੇ ਸੜ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਅਸਾਂ ਵੇਖੇ ਸੀ ਉੱਡਣੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੜ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جد تندور دے سیک چ پئیاں،  
> تاں کھنبھ اوہناں دے سڑ گئے نی!  
> جو سپنے اساں ویکھے سی اڈنے،  
> اوہ مٹی دے وچ گڑ گئے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Jad tandoor de sek ch paiyaan,  
> Taan khambh ohnaan de sarh gaye ni!  
> Jo supne assan vekhe si uddne,  
> Oh mitti de vich garh gaye ni!  

#### English Translation
> When thrust into the fierce heat of the clay tandoor,  
> Their flour-molded wings scorched and burned away!  
> Those dreams we watched in childhood, yearning to fly,  
> Were swallowed back into the unyielding dust of the earth!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Baking hardens the playful bird into rigid nourishment, destroying flight.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸਾਡੀ ਉਮਰ ਵੀ ਆਟੇ ਵਰਗੀ,  
> ਜੋ ਹੱਥਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਮਿੱਧੀ ਗਈ!  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਬੇ-ਦਰਦ ਸਮੇਂ ਦੇ ਹੱਥੋਂ,  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਹੋਣੀ ਇੰਜ ਹੀ ਲਿਖੀ ਗਈ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ساڈی عمر وی آٹے ورگی،  
> جو ہتھاں وچ مدھی گئی!  
> کسے بے-درد سمے دے ہتھوں،  
> ساڈی ہونی انج ہی لکھی گئی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Saadi umar vi aate vargi,  
> Jo hathaan vich middhi gayi!  
> Kise be-dard samey de hatthon,  
> Saadi honi injj hi likhi gayi!  

#### English Translation
> Our entire lifespan too was like moist kneaded flour,  
> Kneaded and bruised beneath unfeeling hands!  
> By the callous hand of an indifferent fate,  
> Our destiny was inscribed in this very manner!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The human soul kneaded and bruised by external circumstance.*

* * *

### Stanza 4

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੇ ਗੀਤ ਵੀ ਆਟੇ ਦੀਆਂ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ,  
> ਜੋ ਲੋਕਾਂ ਨੇ ਚੱਬ ਛੱਡੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਪਰ ਇਹਨਾਂ ਦੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੀਆਂ ਹੂਕਾਂ,  
> ਜੱਗ ਦੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਖੁੱਭ ਛੱਡੇ ਨੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو دے گیت وی آٹے دیاں چڑیاں،  
> جو لوکاں نے چب چھڈے نے!  
> پر ایہناں دے دل دیاں ہوکاں،  
> جگ دے سینے کھبھ چھڈے نے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv de geet vi aate diyan chiriyaan,  
> Jo lokaan ne chabb chhadde ne!  
> Par ehnaan de dil diyan hookan,  
> Jagg de seene khubh chhadde ne!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv's verses too are dough-sparrows,  
> Which the public consumed to satisfy their hunger!  
> Yet the desperate wails echoing from their heart  
> Remain lodged forever inside the collective conscience of the world!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Poetry consumed as nourishment while its tragic origin pierces the reader.*
