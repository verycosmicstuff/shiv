# Kachhe Dudh Di Dhaar

### **ਕੱਚੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਦੀ ਧਾਰ** • **کچے ددھ دی دھار**

#### *"The Stream of Fresh Unboiled Milk"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aate Diyan Chiriyaan (1962)](../03_aate_diyan_chiriyan_1962.md)
- **Original Title**: ਕੱਚੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਦੀ ਧਾਰ (کچے ددھ دی دھار)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1962
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Milk` `#Purity` `#VillageMorning` `#Fragility` `#Pastoral`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: The pure white stream of unboiled milk freshly drawn at dawn represents the fleeting, unblemished innocence of early love.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala during early morning visits to the family cattle-byres.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Celebrates the pristine morning rituals of Majha villages, using unboiled milk as an image of uncorrupted, vulnerable youth.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਤੇਰਾ ਪਿਆਰ ਸੀ ਕੱਚੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਦੀ ਧਾਰ,  
> ਜੋ ਧਰਤੀ 'ਤੇ ਡੁੱਲ੍ਹ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਨਾ ਉਹ ਭਾਂਡੇ 'ਚ ਸੰਭਾਲਿਆ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਸਾਰਾ ਨੂਰ ਹੀ ਭੁੱਲ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> تیرا پیار سی کچے ددھ دی دھار،  
> جو دھرتی تے ڈلھ گیا نی!  
> نہ اوہ بھانڈے چ سنبھالیا گیا،  
> ساڈا سارا نور ہی بھل گیا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Tera pyaar si kachhe dudh di dhaar,  
> Jo dharti te dullh gaya ni!  
> Na oh bhaande ch sambhaaleya gaya,  
> Saada saara noor hi bhull gaya ni!  

#### English Translation
> Your love was like a fresh stream of unboiled milk  
> That spilled irrevocably upon the thirsty earth!  
> It could not be gathered back into the earthen vessel,  
> And all its radiant purity was lost in the mud!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Spilled milk as the classic Punjabi metaphor for irreversible emotional waste.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਚਿੱਟਾ ਦੁੱਧ ਵੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਨੇ ਕਦਰ ਨਾ ਜਾਣੀ ਨੀ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਉਮਰਾਂ ਭਰ ਰੋਂਦੇ ਰਹੇ,  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਈ ਜਿੰਦ ਨਿਮਾਣੀ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> چٹا ددھ وی مٹی ہویا،  
> کسے نے قدر نہ جانی نی!  
> اسیں عمراں بھر روندے رہے،  
> ساڈی مک گئی جند نمانی نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Chitta dudh vi mitti hoya,  
> Kise ne kadar na jaani ni!  
> Aseen umraan bhar ronde rahe,  
> Saadi mukk gayi jind nimaani ni!  

#### English Translation
> The glistening white milk dissolved into the gray dust;  
> Not a single soul recognized its divine sacredness!  
> We wept through the entirety of our numbered years,  
> Until our humble, fragile life reached its end!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Sacred pastoral nourishment wasted on callous ground.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੇ ਗੀਤ ਵੀ ਇਸ ਦੁੱਧ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਸੱਚੇ ਤੇ ਸੁੱਚੇ ਸੀ ਅੜੀਓ!  
> ਪਰ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਨੇ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਮਿਲਾਇਆ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਮਾਰੇ ਸੜਿਓ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو دے گیت وی اس ددھ وانگوں،  
> سچے تے سچے سی اڑیو!  
> پر دنیا نے زہر ملایا،  
> ساڈے سپنے مارے سڑیو!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv de geet vi iss dudh vaangu,  
> Sachhe te suchhe si arhiyo!  
> Par duniya ne zehar milaaya,  
> Saade supne maare sarhiyo!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv's verses too were like this unboiled milk—  
> Utterly pure, holy, and untainted by guile, friends!  
> Yet the envious world poured lethal venom into the cup,  
> Murdering our blossoming dreams in cold malice!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The poisoning of lyric innocence by cynical critics.*
