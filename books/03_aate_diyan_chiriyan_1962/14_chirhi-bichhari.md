# Chirhi Bichhari Koonjaan Di

### **ਚਿੜੀ ਵਿਛੜੀ ਕੂੰਜਾਂ ਦੀ** • **چڑی وچھڑی کونجاں دی**

#### *"The Solitary Bird Separated from the Crane Flock"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aate Diyan Chiriyaan (1962)](../03_aate_diyan_chiriyan_1962.md)
- **Original Title**: ਚਿੜੀ ਵਿਛੜੀ ਕੂੰਜਾਂ ਦੀ (چڑی وچھڑی کونجاں دی)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1962
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Cranes` `#Koonj` `#Flock` `#Exile` `#Flight`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: A solitary bird that lost its migratory flock circles helplessly over the darkening marshes as night falls.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala as autumn flocks migrated from the Himalayas down to the Punjab plains.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Employs the ancient Punjabi folklore trope of the 'Koonj Vichhadi' (separated crane) immortalized by Guru Nanak in the Guru Granth Sahib.

**Source**: *Punjabi Kavi Ate Kavitriyan (Marxist Critical Essays)* by Sant Singh Sekhon (1972, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, pp. 89–112). [Literary Criticism]

> "Sant Singh Sekhon termed Shiv the "Keats of Punjabi poetry" for his meter and folk metaphor, but charged him with escapism, arguing that romanticizing youth-suicide was reactionary."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਕੂੰਜਾਂ ਦੀ ਡਾਰ 'ਚੋਂ ਵਿਛੜੀ,  
> ਕੱਲੀ ਰਹਿ ਗਈ ਅੰਬਰਾਂ 'ਤੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਸਾਥੀ ਲੰਘ ਗਏ ਦੂਰ ਕਿਤੇ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਰੋਣਾ ਸੁਣੇ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਧਰਤਾਂ 'ਤੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں کونجاں دی ڈار چوں وچھڑی،  
> کلی رہ گئی عنبراں تے!  
> میرے ساتھی لنگھ گئے دور کتے،  
> میرا رونا سنے نہ کوئی دھرتاں تے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main koonjaan di daar chon vichhrhi,  
> Kalli reh gayi ambaraan te!  
> Mere saathi langh gaye door kite,  
> Mera rona sune na koyi dhartaan te!  

#### English Translation
> I was severed from the flying chevron of migratory cranes,  
> Left entirely stranded and solitary in the vast skies!  
> My lifelong companions have soared far beyond the distant mountains,  
> While not a living soul upon the earth pauses to hear my wailing!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The archetypal tragedy of exile from the collective flock.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਥੱਲੇ ਦਿਸਣ ਸ਼ਿਕਾਰੀ ਜਾਲ਼ ਵਿਛਾਈ,  
> ਉੱਤੇ ਬੱਦਲ ਗੱਜਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਖੰਭਾਂ 'ਚ ਹੁਣ ਜ਼ੋਰ ਰਿਹਾ ਨਾ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਸਾਹ ਵੀ ਰੱਜਦੇ ਨੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> تھلے دسن شکاری جال وچھائی،  
> اتے بدل گجدے نے!  
> میرے کھنبھاں چ ہن زور رہا نہ،  
> ساڈے ساہ وی رجدے نے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Thalle dissan shikaari jaal vichhaayi,  
> Utte baddal gajjde ne!  
> Mere khambhaan ch hun zor reha na,  
> Saade saah vi rajjde ne!  

#### English Translation
> Below me, ruthless hunters wait with wide nets spread across the reeds,  
> While above my head, terrifying stormclouds rumble!  
> All physical strength has deserted my exhausted wings,  
> And my very breaths prepare to surrender life!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Hunters below and thunder above; the trapped soul.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਹਾਲ ਵੀ ਇਸ ਕੂੰਜ ਜਿਹਾ,  
> ਜੋ ਮਿੱਟੀ 'ਤੇ ਡਿੱਗ ਪਊਗੀ!  
> ਪਰ ਉਸਦੀ ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਚੀਕ ਸੁਣ ਕੇ,  
> ਇਹ ਸਾਰੀ ਧਰਤ ਕੰਬ ਪਊਗੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو دا حال وی اس کونج جہیا،  
> جو مٹی تے ڈگ پؤگی!  
> پر اس دی آخری چیک سن کے،  
> ایہہ ساری دھرتی کنب پؤگی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv da haal vi iss koonj jiha,  
> Jo mitti te digg paugi!  
> Par usdi aakhri cheek sun ke,  
> Eh saari dhart kamb paugi!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv's tragic fate mirrors that of this abandoned crane,  
> Destined to plummet lifeless onto the indifferent soil!  
> Yet hearing the piercing shriek of its dying breath,  
> The entire width of this earth shall shudder to its core!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The final cry that shakes the moral foundation of the world.*
