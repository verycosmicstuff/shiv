# Kachhe Kothe Da Booha

### **ਕੱਚੇ ਕੋਠੇ ਦਾ ਬੂਹਾ** • **کچے کوٹھے دا بوہا**

#### *"Door of the Clay Hearth"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aate Diyan Chiriyaan (1962)](../03_aate_diyan_chiriyan_1962.md)
- **Original Title**: ਕੱਚੇ ਕੋਠੇ ਦਾ ਬੂਹਾ (کچے کوٹھے دا بوہا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1962
- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#ClayHome` `#Threshold` `#Poverty`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `village_monsoon`

**Synopsis**: The decaying timber door of a humble mud room sags on its hinges, creaking in the wind like an old man coughing in the dark.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's family lived in modest mud-plastered quarters in Batala after losing their prosperous ancestral lands in 1947.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Documents the impoverished architectural reality of post-Partition refugee settlements in rural Punjab.

**Source**: *Shiv Batalvi: A Solitary and Passionate Singer* by Dr. O.P. Sharma (1979, Sterling Publishers, New Delhi, pp. 12–45, 180–205). [Primary Memoir]

> "Authoritative academic biography documenting his childhood in Bara Pind Lohtian (now Pakistan), the trauma of the 1947 Partition flight, his clerkship at the State Bank of India Chandigarh, and his final collapse in Kir Mangyal."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕੱਚੇ ਕੋਠੇ ਦਾ ਬੂਹਾ ਸਾਡਾ,  
> ਚੀਕ ਰਿਹਾ ਏ ਰਾਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਵੇਂ ਕੋਈ ਬੁੱਢਾ ਹੌਕੇ ਭਰਦਾ,  
> ਯਾਦ ਕਰੇ ਮੁਲਾਕਾਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کچے کوٹھے دا بوہا ساڈا،  
> چیک رہا اے راتاں نوں نی!  
> جیویں کوئی بڈھا ہوکے بھردا،  
> یاد کرے ملاقاتاں نوں نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kachhe kothe da booha saada,  
> Cheek reha ae raataan nu ni!  
> Jiven koyi buddha hoke bharda,  
> Yaad kare mulaakaataan nu ni!  

#### English Translation
> The sagging door of our humble mud dwelling  
> Groans and creaks through the lonely winter nights!  
> Just like a decrepit old man heaving agonizing sighs  
> As he remembers the warm meetings of departed youth!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Animistic personification: the timber door takes on elderly human infirmity.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕੱਚੀਆਂ ਕੰਧਾਂ ਚੋ ਰਹੀਆਂ ਨੇ,  
> ਸਾਉਣ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਦੀਆਂ ਝੜੀਆਂ 'ਚ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਤਾਂ ਦਰਦ ਸੰਭਾਲ ਕੇ ਬੈਠੇ,  
> ਸੁੰਞੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੀਆਂ ਘੜੀਆਂ 'ਚ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کچیاں کندھاں چو رہیاں نے،  
> ساؤن مہینے دیاں جھڑیاں چ!  
> اسیں تاں درد سنبھال کے بیٹھے،  
> سنجے دل دیاں گھڑیاں چ!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kachhiyaan kandhaan cho rahiyaan ne,  
> Saun maheene diyaan jhariyaan ch!  
> Aseen taan dard sambhaal ke baithe,  
> Sunje dil diyaan ghariyaan ch!  

#### English Translation
> The unbaked mud walls leak muddy rainwater  
> Under the relentless downpours of the month of Saawan!  
> We sit huddled inside, nursing our immortal grief  
> Through the empty, ticking hours of a shattered heart!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Monsoon downpours (*jhariyaan*) dissolving mud walls mirror internal emotional dissolution.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇੱਕ ਦਿਨ ਇਹ ਕੋਠਾ ਢਹਿ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਮਿੱਟੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਮਿਲ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਜਿੰਦ ਵੀ ਮੁੱਕ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਤੇ ਦਰਦ ਦੀ ਅੱਗ ਬੁੱਝ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اک دن ایہ کوٹھا ڈھہہ جاوے گا،  
> مٹی مٹی وچ مل جاوے گی!  
> ساڈی جند وی مک جاوے گی،  
> تے درد دی اگ بجھ جاوے گی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Ikk din eh kotha dheh jaavega,  
> Mitti mitti vich mil jaavegi!  
> Saadi jind vi mukk jaavegi,  
> Te dard di agg bujh jaavegi!  

#### English Translation
> One fateful day this fragile room will collapse to the ground,  
> Clay returning to clay in quiet, final reunion!  
> Our weary breath shall cease its painful labor,  
> And the searing fire of this grief will finally be extinguished!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Peace found only in total return to dust.*
