# Kore Kaghaz Te Likh Bhejea

### **ਕੋਰੇ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ 'ਤੇ ਲਿਖ ਭੇਜਿਆ** • **کورے کاغذ تے لکھ بھیجیا**

#### *"I Inscribed Upon a Blank Sheet of Paper"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aate Diyan Chiriyaan (1962)](../03_aate_diyan_chiriyan_1962.md)
- **Original Title**: ਕੋਰੇ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ 'ਤੇ ਲਿਖ ਭੇਜਿਆ (کورے کاغذ تے لکھ بھیجیا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1962
- **Thematic Tags**: `#BlankPage` `#UnsentLetter` `#Ink` `#Silence`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `modernism` | **Landscape Biome**: `snowy_cabin_night`

**Synopsis**: Sending a completely blank sheet of paper to the beloved, because words cannot contain the magnitude of the wound.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala when Shiv found himself unable to write to his estranged muse.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Famous meta-poetic exploration of the limits of language, where silence says more than written words.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਕੋਰੇ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ 'ਤੇ ਲਿਖ ਭੇਜਿਆ,  
> ਤੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ ਨਾ ਆਇਆ ਮੁੱਖ 'ਤੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਮੈਂ ਸੀਨੇ 'ਚ ਸਾਂਭਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਡਿੱਗ ਪਿਆ ਸੁੱਕੇ ਰੁੱਖ 'ਤੇ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں کورے کاغذ تے لکھ بھیجیا،  
> تیرا نام نہ آیا مکھ تے نی!  
> جو درد میں سینے چ سانبھیا سی،  
> اوہ ڈگ پیا سکے رخ تے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main kore kaghaz te likh bhejea,  
> Tera naam na aaya mukkh te ni!  
> Jo dard main seene ch saambheya si,  
> Oh digg peya sukke rukkh te ni!  

#### English Translation
> I dispatched a message upon a blank white page,  
> Refusing to let your name pass upon my lips!  
> That immense agony I sheltered deep inside my chest  
> Has collapsed upon the branches of a withered tree!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The blank page as the highest form of articulate speech.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਸ ਕੋਰੇਪਨ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੀ ਸਭ ਕੁਝ ਸੀ,  
> ਜੋ ਲਫ਼ਜ਼ਾਂ 'ਚ ਨਾ ਆ ਸਕਿਆ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਜੋ ਤੂਫ਼ਾਨ ਸੀ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਸ਼ਾਇਰ ਨਾ ਗਾ ਸਕਿਆ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اس کورےپن دے وچ ہی سبھ کچھ سی،  
> جو لفظاں چ نہ آ سکیا!  
> میرے دل دا جو طوفان سی،  
> کوئی شاعر نہ گا سکیا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Iss korepan de vich hi sabh kujh si,  
> Jo lafzaan ch na aa sakeya!  
> Mere dil da jo toofaan si,  
> Koyi shaayar na gaa sakeya!  

#### English Translation
> Within this stark blankness resided everything  
> Which ordinary words could never dare utter!  
> That apocalyptic tempest raging in my breast  
> No living poet could ever encompass in song!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The inadequacy of conventional poetic vocabulary.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਇਹ ਕੋਰਾ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ ਹੀ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਖ਼ਤ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਪੜ੍ਹੇਗੀ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਰੋਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਇਹ ਸੱਚ ਦਾ ਪਰਬਤ ਹੋਵੇਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو دا ایہہ کورا کاغذ ہی،  
> میرا آخری خط ہووےگا!  
> جد پڑھوگی دنیا رووےگی،  
> ایہہ سچ دا پربت ہووےگا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv da eh kora kaghaz hi,  
> Mera aakhri khat hovega!  
> Jad parhegi duniya rovegi,  
> Eh sach da parbat hovega!  

#### English Translation
> This pristine blank parchment of Shiv's  
> Shall stand as my final testament and letter!  
> When future generations read its silence, they shall weep;  
> It shall stand enduring like an unyielding mountain of truth!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The silent letter outlasting vocal poetry.*
