# Chanan Di Toli

### **ਚਾਨਣ ਦੀ ਟੋਲੀ** • **چانن دی ٹولی**

#### *"Company of Morning Rays"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aate Diyan Chiriyaan (1962)](../03_aate_diyan_chiriyan_1962.md)
- **Original Title**: ਚਾਨਣ ਦੀ ਟੋਲੀ (چانن دی ٹولی)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1962
- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#Morning` `#Sunbeams` `#GriefasLight`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `village_monsoon`

**Synopsis**: The company of morning rays enters the dark chamber like an uninvited festive crowd, disturbing the sacred gloom of the grieving poet.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv lived largely by night; daylight felt like a harsh, uninvited interrogation of his private melancholy.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Explores Shiv's unique sensory synesthesia where light is experienced as physical weight and intrusion.

**Source**: *Punjabi Kavi Ate Kavitriyan (Marxist Critical Essays)* by Sant Singh Sekhon (1972, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, pp. 89–112). [Literary Criticism]

> "Sant Singh Sekhon termed Shiv the "Keats of Punjabi poetry" for his meter and folk metaphor, but charged him with "palayanwad" (reactionary escapism), arguing that romanticizing youth-suicide was reactionary in post-Partition agrarian Punjab."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਚਾਨਣ ਦੀ ਟੋਲੀ ਆ ਵੜੀ ਵਿਹੜੇ,  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਨੀਂਦ ਉਚਾਟਣ ਆ ਗਈ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਅਸੀਂ ਲੁਕਾ ਕੇ ਰੱਖਿਆ,  
> ਉਸ ਨੂੰ ਜੱਗ ਨੂੰ ਵਿਖਾਉਣ ਆ ਗਈ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> چانن دی ٹولی آ وڑی ویہڑے،  
> ساڈی نیند اچاٹن آ گئی نی!  
> جو درد اسیں لکا کے رکھیا،  
> اس نوں جگ نوں وکھاؤن آ گئی نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Chanan di toli aa varhi vehre,  
> Saadi neend uchaatan aa gayi ni!  
> Jo dard aseen lukaa ke rakkheya,  
> Uss nu jagg nu vikhaaon aa gayi ni!  

#### English Translation
> A festive company of morning rays burst into the courtyard,  
> Arriving solely to shatter the fragile sanctuary of our sleep!  
> That private wound which we had concealed so carefully  
> It comes to expose to the mocking gaze of the world!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Light seen as an intrusive invader violating the privacy of grief.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕਿਰਨਾਂ ਨੱਚਣ ਕੰਧਾਂ ਉੱਤੇ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਦਿਲ ਤੇ ਛੁਰੀਆਂ ਚੱਲਣ ਵੇ!  
> ਇਸ ਚਾਨਣ ਦੇ ਮੇਲੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਸਾਹ ਅੰਗਿਆਰਾਂ 'ਚ ਬਲਣ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کرناں نچن کندھاں اتے،  
> ساڈے دل تے چھریاں چلن وے!  
> اس چانن دے میلے وچ،  
> ساڈے ساہ انگیاڑاں چ بلن وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kirnaan nachan kandhaan utte,  
> Saade dil te chhuriyaan challan ve!  
> Iss chanan de mele vich,  
> Saade saah angiyaaraan ch balan ve!  

#### English Translation
> Sunbeams dance playfully upon the mud-plastered walls,  
> Yet their dance cuts like jagged daggers across our heart!  
> Amidst this joyous carnival of morning radiance,  
> Our breath smolders like embers in a deserted hearth!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Daggers of light (chhuriyaan) cutting across the mourning soul.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸਾਨੂੰ ਕਾਲੀ ਰਾਤ ਹੀ ਭਾਉਂਦੀ ਏ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਵਹਾ ਸਕਦੇ ਹਾਂ!  
> ਇਸ ਚਾਨਣ ਦੇ ਸ਼ੋਰ ਤੋਂ ਦੂਰ,  
> ਆਪਣਾ ਦਰਦ ਸੁਣਾ ਸਕਦੇ ਹਾਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> سانوں کالی رات ہی بھاؤندی اے،  
> جتھے ہنجھو وہا سکدے ہاں!  
> اس چانن دے شور توں دور،  
> اپنا درد سنا سکدے ہاں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Saanu kaali raat hi bhaaundi ae,  
> Jithe hanjhu vahaa sakde haan!  
> Iss chanan de shor ton door,  
> Apna dard sunaa sakde haan!  

#### English Translation
> Only the velvet sanctuary of pitch-black night pleases us,  
> Where unobserved we can pour forth our flowing tears!  
> Far removed from the blinding clamor of daylight,  
> Where in silence we may confess our mortal agony!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Nocturnal preference: night as the only compassionate mother.*
