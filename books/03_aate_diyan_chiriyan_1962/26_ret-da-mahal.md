# Ret Da Mahal

### **ਰੇਤ ਦਾ ਮਹਿਲ** • **ریت دا محل**

#### *"Castle Built of River Sand"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aate Diyan Chiriyaan (1962)](../03_aate_diyan_chiriyan_1962.md)
- **Original Title**: ਰੇਤ ਦਾ ਮਹਿਲ (ریت دا محل)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1962
- **Thematic Tags**: `#AatedianChiriyean` `#Sandcastle` `#FutileHope` `#Decay`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `village_monsoon`

**Synopsis**: The poet builds an opulent palace out of river sand, watching with tearful fascination as the first gentle wave dissolves its spires.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's awareness that his romantic ideals were too delicate to survive the harsh reality of feudal Punjab.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> A quintessential Shiv metaphor: creating magnificent architectural monuments out of perishable materials.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਰੇਤ ਦਾ ਮਹਿਲ ਬਣਾਇਆ ਅਸੀਂ,  
> ਦਰਿਆ ਦੇ ਕੰਢੇ ਤੇ ਬਹਿ ਕੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਸੋਚਿਆ ਸੀ ਇੱਥੇ ਵਸਾਂਗੇ ਦੋਵੇਂ,  
> ਸੁੱਖ ਦੇ ਪਲ ਕੁਝ ਕਹਿ ਕੇ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ریت دا محل بنایا اسیں،  
> دریا دے کنڈھے تے بہہ کے نی!  
> سوچیا سی ایتھے وساں گے دوویں،  
> سکھ دے پل کجھ کہہ کے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Ret da mahal banaaya aseen,  
> Dariya de kandhe te beh ke ni!  
> Sochiya si itthe vasaange dovein,  
> Sukh de pal kujh keh ke ni!  

#### English Translation
> We built a magnificent palace of glistening river sand,  
> Sitting together upon the lonely embankment of the stream!  
> We dreamed we would dwell within its sunlit chambers,  
> Sharing quiet words of tenderness through peaceful hours!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The sandcastle as the grand illusion of youthful love.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇੱਕ ਛੱਲ ਆਈ ਦਰਿਆ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ,  
> ਮਹਿਲ ਉਹ ਪਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੋੜ੍ਹ ਗਈ ਨੀ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਉਮਰਾਂ ਦੀ ਜੋ ਕਮਾਈ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹਨੂੰ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਮਰੋੜ ਗਈ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اک چھل آئی دریا وچوں،  
> محل اوہ پل وچ روڑھ گئی نی!  
> ساڈی عمراں دی جو کمائی سی،  
> اوہنوں مٹی وچ مروڑ گئی نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Ikk chhall aayi dariya vichon,  
> Mahal oh pal vich rorh gayi ni!  
> Saadi umraan di jo kamaayi si,  
> Ohnu mitti vich marorh gayi ni!  

#### English Translation
> A single cresting wave surged from the river's heart,  
> And swept our palace away in the fraction of an instant!  
> The entire accumulated treasure of our mortal lifetimes  
> Was crushed and mangled back into common, featureless mud!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The river wave (*chhall*) represents indifferent, unstoppable time.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਖੜ੍ਹੇ ਹਾਂ ਕੰਢੇ ਤੇ ਇਕੱਲੇ,  
> ਖ਼ਾਲੀ ਹੱਥ ਤੇ ਰੋਂਦੀਆਂ ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਰੇਤ ਦੇ ਘਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਬਣਦੇ ਹੁੰਦੇ,  
> ਇਹ ਗੱਲਾਂ ਜੱਗ ਨੇ ਸੱਚੀਆਂ ਆਖੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن کھڑے ہاں کنڈھے تے اکلی،  
> خالی ہتھ تے روندیاں اکھیاں نی!  
> ریت دے گھر نہیں بندے ہوندے،  
> ایہ گلاں جگ نے سچیاں آکھیاں نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun kharhe haan kandhe te ikalle,  
> Khaali hath te rondiyaan akhiyaan ni!  
> Ret de ghar nahin bande hunde,  
> Eh gallan jagg ne sacchiyaan aakhiyaan ni!  

#### English Translation
> Now we stand deserted upon the barren shoreline,  
> With empty hands and burning, weeping eyes!  
> Houses sculpted of sand can never withstand the storm—  
> Alas, how terribly true were the warnings of this callous world!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Belated vindication of cynical worldly wisdom over romantic passion.*
