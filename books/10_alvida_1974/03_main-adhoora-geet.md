# Main Adhoora Geet Haan

### **ਮੈਂ ਅਧੂਰਾ ਗੀਤ ਹਾਂ** • **میں ادھورا گیت ہاں**

#### *"I Am an Unfinished Melody"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Alvida (1974)](../10_alvida_1974.md)
- **Original Title**: ਮੈਂ ਅਧੂਰਾ ਗੀਤ ਹਾਂ (میں ادھورا گیت ہاں)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1974
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Unfinished` `#Fragment` `#Elegiac`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: Accepting that early death has left his life, his loves, and his poetic mission broken as an uncompleted fragment.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Kir Mangyal shortly before passing away at 36.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Included in the canonical Punjabi collection 'Alvida' (1974).

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> Autobiographical self-definition as an eternal fragment.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Broken, sighing, unresolved musical phrasing.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (4 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਅਧੂਰੇ ਗੀਤ ਦੀ ਇੱਕ ਸਤਰ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਜੋ ਗਾਈ ਨਾ ਗਈ ਪੂਰੀ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਹੋਠਾਂ 'ਤੇ ਹੀ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਈ,  
> ਇਹ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਦੀ ਦੂਰੀ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں ادھورے گیت دی اک سطر ہاں،  
> جو گائی نہ گئی پوری نی!  
> میرے ہوٹھاں تے ہی مک گئی،  
> ایہہ زندگی دی دوری نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main adhoore geet di ikk satar haan,  
> Jo gaayi na gayi poori ni!  
> Mere hothaan te hi mukk gayi,  
> Eh zindagi di doori ni!  

#### English Translation
> I am merely a single line of an unfinished melody  
> Which was never permitted to be sung to completion!  
> Upon my very lips it halted and dissolved;  
> Such was the tragic distance of my existence!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Life as an abruptly truncated verse.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕਿੰਨੇ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਅਧੂਰੇ ਰਹਿ ਗਏ,  
> ਕਿੰਨੇ ਗੀਤ ਅਣਗਾਏ ਮਰ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਰਾਜ਼ ਮੈਂ ਖੋਲ੍ਹਣੇ ਸੀ ਜੱਗ 'ਤੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਕਬਰਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੜ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کنے سپنے ادھورے رہ گئے،  
> کنے گیت انگائے مر گئے نی!  
> جو راز میں کھولنے سی جگ تے،  
> اوہ قبراں دے وچ گڑ گئے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kinne supne adhoore reh gaye,  
> Kinne geet an-gaaye mar gaye ni!  
> Jo raaz main kholhne si jagg te,  
> Oh qabraan de vich garh gaye ni!  

#### English Translation
> How many dreams remained broken and half-realized;  
> How many melodies perished before they could be sung!  
> Those profound mysteries I intended to reveal to the world  
> Have been swallowed into the darkness of the grave!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The unwritten library buried in the poet's mind.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਨਾ ਮੇਰਾ ਕੋਈ ਅੰਤ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਸ਼ੁਰੂਆਤ ਸੀ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਤਾਂ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੀ ਇੱਕ ਲੰਮੀ,  
> ਬੇ-ਚਿਰਾਗ਼ੀ ਰਾਤ ਸੀ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> نہ میرا کوئی انت ہویا،  
> نہ کوئی شروعات سی نی!  
> میں تاں برہا دی اک لمی،  
> بے-چراغی رات سی نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Na mera koyi ant hoya,  
> Na koyi shuru-aat si ni!  
> Main taan birha di ikk lammi,  
> Be-chiraaghi raat si ni!  

#### English Translation
> Neither did my journey find a rightful conclusion,  
> Nor was there any gentle beginning to my days!  
> I was merely an interminable, endless night  
> Bereft of a single lamp of separation!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Be-chiraaghi (lampless): utter lack of illumination.*

* * *

### Stanza 4

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਅਧੂਰਾ ਹੀ ਤੁਰ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਪਰ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਇਸਨੂੰ ਪੂਰਾ ਕਰੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਕੋਈ ਆਸ਼ਿਕ ਦਰਦ 'ਚ ਰੋਊਗਾ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਭਰੇਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو تاں ادھورا ہی تر گیا،  
> پر پنجاب اس نوں پورا کریگا!  
> جد کوئی عاشق درد چ روؤگا،  
> تاں میرے گیتاں نوں بھریگا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv taan adhoora hi tur gaya,  
> Par Punjab isnu poora karega!  
> Jad koyi aashiq dard ch rouga,  
> Taan mere geetan nu bharega!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv departed this earth incomplete and fragmented,  
> Yet the people of Punjab shall complete his verse!  
> Whenever any lover weeps in genuine agony,  
> Their living tears shall fill the unfinished spaces of my song!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The future readers of Punjab will complete the poem with their own lives.*
