# Lahu Di Chhitt

### **ਲਹੂ ਦੀ ਛਿੱਟ** • **لہو دی چھٹ**

#### *"The Splash of Blood: Staining the Pure Page"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Alvida (1974)](../10_alvida_1974.md)
- **Original Title**: ਲਹੂ ਦੀ ਛਿੱਟ (لہو دی چھٹ)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1974
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Posthumous` `#Hemoptysis` `#Suffering`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The poet sees his terminal internal hemorrhage not merely as medical pathology, but as his poems refusing to remain trapped inside his dying flesh.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Written following a violent episode of internal bleeding in Chandigarh before his final retreat to the village.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> In his final year, Shiv suffered esophageal varices and internal hemorrhages, coughing up blood that he grimly equated with his unfinished verses.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on cigarette packets and café napkins in Chandigarh, leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜਦ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ ਉੱਤੇ ਡਿੱਗੀ ਛਿੱਟ,  
> ਉਹ ਸਿਆਹੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਲਹੂ ਸੀ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਿਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੜਦੀ ਰਹੀ ਸਦਾ,  
> ਉਹ ਮੇਰੀ ਬਲਦੀ ਰੂਹ ਸੀ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جد کاغذ اتے ڈگی چھٹ،  
> اوہ سیاہی نہیں لہو سی نی!  
> جو دل وچ سڑدی رہی سدا،  
> اوہ میری بلدی روح سی نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Jad kaagaz utte diggi chhitt,  
> Oh siyahi nahin lahu si ni!  
> Jo dil vich sardi rahi sada,  
> Oh meri baldi rooh si ni!  

#### English Translation
> When the dark drop spattered across the white paper,  
> It was not ink at all, but the hot blood of my lungs!  
> That which smoldered constantly within my breast  
> Was nothing less than my own incandescent, burning soul!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Direct transubstantiation of bodily hemorrhage into verse ink.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਲੋਕਾਂ ਨੇ ਆਖਿਆ ਗੀਤ ਨਵਾਂ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਆਖਿਆ ਮੇਰੀ ਜਾਨ ਗਈ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਦਰਦ ਨੂੰ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਵੇਖਦੀ ਰਹੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਮੇਰੀ ਪਹਿਚਾਣ ਗਈ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> لوکاں نے آکھیا گیت نواں،  
> میں آکھیا میری جان گئی!  
> جس درد نوں دنیا ویکھدی رہی،  
> اوہ میری پہچان گئی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Lokaan ne aakheya geet nawaan,  
> Main aakheya meri jaan gayi!  
> Jis dard nu duniya vekhdi rahi,  
> Oh meri pehchaan gayi!  

#### English Translation
> The spectators cheered and cried, 'Behold, a novel song!'  
> While I whispered in terror, 'My mortal breath has fled!'  
> That agony which the audience enjoyed as mere spectacle  
> Has stolen away my very breath and human countenance!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Critique of voyeuristic audiences who romanticized Shiv's physical self-destruction.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਪੰਨੇ ਲਾਲ ਗੁਲਾਬ ਹੋਏ,  
> ਹਰ ਲਫ਼ਜ਼ ਅੰਦਰ ਅੰਗਾਰ ਬਲੇ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਆਪਣਾ ਲਹੂ ਵਹਾ ਕੇ ਵੀ,  
> ਇਸ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਦੇ ਰਾਹਾਂ ਉੱਤੇ ਚਲੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن پنے لال گلاب ہوئے،  
> ہر لفظ اندر انگار بلے!  
> شیو اپنا لہو وہا کے وی،  
> اس عشق دے راہاں اتے چلے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun panne laal gulaab hoye,  
> Har lafaz andar angaar bale!  
> Shiv apna lahu vahaa ke vi,  
> Iss ishq de raahaan utte chale!  

#### English Translation
> Now the blank leaves have bloomed into scarlet roses;  
> Inside every syllable, incandescent embers burn!  
> Even while bleeding his life away unto the unyielding dust,  
> Shiv continues his pilgrimage along the thorny tracks of love!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Unbroken aesthetic defiance even in the face of terminal hemorrhage.*
