# Chup Da Samundar

### **ਚੁੱਪ ਦਾ ਸਮੁੰਦਰ** • **چپ دا سمندر**

#### *"The Ocean of Silence: Beyond All Utterance"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Alvida (1974)](../10_alvida_1974.md)
- **Original Title**: ਚੁੱਪ ਦਾ ਸਮੁੰਦਰ (چپ دا سمندر)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1974
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Posthumous` `#Silence` `#Metaphysical`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `solitude` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: Silence is not the absence of sound, but an unfathomable ocean into which all ephemeral words ultimately drown.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's late metaphysical realization that the ultimate culmination of lyricism is absolute, sacred silence.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Written in early 1973 when Shiv experienced prolonged periods of complete muteness, staring silently for hours into space.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on cigarette packets and café napkins in Chandigarh, leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਲਫ਼ਜ਼ ਮੁੱਕੇ ਤੇ ਗੀਤ ਵੀ ਮੁੱਕੇ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਚੁੱਪ ਦਾ ਵੱਡਾ ਸਮੁੰਦਰ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਰੋਲਾ ਬਾਹਰ ਸੁਣਦਾ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਸ਼ਾਂਤ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ ਅੰਦਰ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> لفظ مکے تے گیت وی مکے،  
> ہن چپ دا وڈا سمندر اے!  
> جو رولا باہر سندا سی،  
> اوہ شانت ہو گیا اندر اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Lafaz mukke te geet vi mukke,  
> Hun chup da vadda samundar ae!  
> Jo rola baahar sunda si,  
> Oh shaant ho gaya andar ae!  

#### English Translation
> The syllables have vanished, and the melodies are spent;  
> Now only the immense, unfathomable ocean of silence remains!  
> All that frantic clamor that echoed across the outer world  
> Has sunk into absolute stillness within my deep soul!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Transition from sensory turmoil to inner oceanic quietude.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਸ ਚੁੱਪ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੋਈ ਤਰੰਗ ਨਹੀਂ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਹਵਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਕੋਈ ਝੱਖੜ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਦੁੱਖਾਂ ਦੀ ਸਾਰੀ ਉਮਰ ਦਾ,  
> ਟੁੱਟ ਗਿਆ ਹਰ ਇੱਕ ਪੱਖੜ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اس چپ وچ کوئی ترنگ نہیں،  
> کوئی ہوا نہیں کوئی جھکھڑ نی!  
> میرے دکھاں دی ساری عمر دا،  
> ٹٹ گیا ہر اک پکھڑ نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Iss chup vich koi tarang nahin,  
> Koi havaa nahin koi jhakkhar ni!  
> Mere dukkhaan di saari umar da,  
> Tutt gaya har ikk pakkhar ni!  

#### English Translation
> Within this fathomless silence, no restless ripple stirs;  
> No biting gale howls, no sudden hurricane strikes!  
> Every heavy chain and armored shackle of grief  
> Forged through my long lifetime has shattered into nothingness!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Silence as total emancipation (*tutt gaya har ikk pakkhar*).*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਡੁੱਬ ਜਾ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤੂੰ ਇਸ ਸਮੁੰਦਰ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਕਿਨਾਰੇ ਦੀ ਲੋੜ ਨਹੀਂ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਮਿਲ ਗਈ ਅਮਰ ਖ਼ਾਮੋਸ਼ੀ,  
> ਉੱਥੇ ਦਰਦਾਂ ਦੀ ਕੋਈ ਤੋੜ ਨਹੀਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ڈب جا شیو توں اس سمندر وچ،  
> ہن کنارے دی لوڑ نہیں!  
> جتھے مل گئی امر خاموشی،  
> او Rome درد دی کوئی توڑ نہیں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Dubb jaa Shiv tu iss samundar vich,  
> Hun kinaare di lorh nahin!  
> Jitthe mil gayi amar khamoshi,  
> Otthe dardaan di koi torh nahin!  

#### English Translation
> Submerge yourself, O Shiv, deep into this boundless ocean;  
> There is no longer any need to search for a distant shore!  
> Where eternal silence embraces the traveler at last,  
> No agony can ever break or ravage the spirit again!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Self-dissolution into the infinite void where pain loses all dominion.*
