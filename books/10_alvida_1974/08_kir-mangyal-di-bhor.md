# Kirmachi Bhor

### **ਕਿਰਮਚੀ ਭੋਰ** • **کرمچی بھور**

#### *"The Crimson Dawn at Kir Mangyal: The Final Morning"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Alvida (1974)](../10_alvida_1974.md)
- **Original Title**: ਕਿਰਮਚੀ ਭੋਰ (کرمچی بھور)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1974
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Posthumous` `#KirMangyal` `#FinalDay`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `ancestral_village`

**Synopsis**: A piercing depiction of the crimson twilight just before death, looking out from a village courtyard where silence descends.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Reflecting the serene yet heartbreaking geography of Kir Mangyal in Pathankot district where Shiv spent his final conscious hours.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Kir Mangyal was the village of Shiv's in-laws where he was taken in early May 1973 in his final critical state, passing away on May 6, 1973.

**Source**: *Shiv Batalvi: A Solitary and Passionate Singer* by Dr. O.P. Sharma (1979, Sterling Publishers, New Delhi, pp. 12–45, 180–205). [Primary Memoir]

> "Authoritative academic biography documenting his childhood in Bara Pind Lohtian (now Pakistan), the trauma of the 1947 Partition flight, his clerkship at the State Bank of India Chandigarh, and his final collapse in Kir Mangyal."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕਿਰਮਚੀ ਭੋਰ ਚੜ੍ਹੀ ਪਿੰਡ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਸੂਰਜ ਲਹੂ ਜਿਹਾ ਲਾਲ ਨੀ!  
> ਪਿੰਡੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਪੈ ਗਈ ਠੰਢਕ,  
> ਮੁੱਕ ਗਿਆ ਜੀਵਨ ਦਾ ਜੰਜਾਲ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کرمچی بھور چڑھی پنڈ اندر،  
> سورج لہو جیہا لال نی!  
> پنڈے اتے پے گئی ٹھنڈک،  
> مک گیا جیون دا جنجال نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kirmachi bhor charhi pind andar,  
> Suraj lahu jiha laal ni!  
> Pinde utte pai gayi thandhak,  
> Mukk gaya jeevan da janjaal ni!  

#### English Translation
> A crimson dawn broke over the quiet village,  
> The rising sun red as freshly spilled blood!  
> A deathly coolness settled deep upon my limbs,  
> And the tangled entrapments of living were dissolved forever!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The dawn sky mirroring somatic collapse (*pinde utte pai gayi thandhak*).*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕੱਚੀ ਕੰਧ ਨਾਲ਼ ਢੋਅ ਲਾਈ ਏ,  
> ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਨੀਂਦ ਉਡਾਈ ਏ!  
> ਮਿੱਟੀ ਨੇ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਨੂੰ ਸੱਦਿਆ,  
> ਜਿੰਦੜੀ ਆਪਣੀ ਭੇਟ ਚੜ੍ਹਾਈ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کچی کندھ نال ڈھو لائی اے،  
> اکھیاں وچوں نیند اڈائی اے!  
> مٹی نے مٹی نوں سدیا،  
> جندڑی اپنی بھیٹ چڑھائی اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kachhi kandh naal dho layi ae,  
> Akhiyaan vichon neend udayi ae!  
> Mitti ne mitti nu saddiya,  
> Jindri apni bhet charhayi ae!  

#### English Translation
> I leaned my weary back against the raw mud wall,  
> Sleep banished far from my restless, heavy eyes!  
> The ancient dust of the earth called out to its own dust,  
> And my trembling life was placed as a sacrifice upon the altar!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The earth returning to earth (*mitti ne mitti nu saddiya*), echo of primal dust.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਦਾਰੂ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਦੁਆ,  
> ਸਾਹਾਂ ਨੇ ਵੀ ਮੋੜਿਆ ਮੂੰਹ!  
> ਅੱਜ ਕਿਰ ਮੰਗਿਆਲ ਦੇ ਵਿਹੜੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਉੱਡ ਚੱਲੀ ਏ ਮੇਰੀ ਰੂਹ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> نہ کوئی دارو نہ کوئی دعا،  
> ساہاں نے وی موڑیا منہ!  
> اج کر منگیال دے ویہڑے وچ،  
> اڈ چلی اے میری روح!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Na koi daru na koi dua,  
> Saahaan ne vi mordiya moonh!  
> Ajj Kir Mangyal de vehre vich,  
> Udd challee ae meri rooh!  

#### English Translation
> No medicine can heal, no prayer can deliver;  
> Even the breath turns its cold face away!  
> Today in the modest courtyard of Kir Mangyal,  
> My sovereign soul takes flight into the infinite sky!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The exact geographical locus of death celebrated as spiritual liberation.*
