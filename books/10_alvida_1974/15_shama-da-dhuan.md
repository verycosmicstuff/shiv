# Shama Da Dhuan

### **ਸ਼ਮ੍ਹਾ ਦਾ ਧੂੰਆਂ** • **شمع دا دھواں**

#### *"The Smoke of the Candle: Tracing the Fading Soul"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Alvida (1974)](../10_alvida_1974.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸ਼ਮ੍ਹਾ ਦਾ ਧੂੰਆਂ (شمع دا دھواں)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1974
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Posthumous` `#ExtinguishedFlame` `#FadingLight`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `abandoned_haveli`

**Synopsis**: When the flame of life is snuffed out, only a fragile spiral of fragrant smoke lingers behind to mark where brightness once burned.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Chandigarh as Shiv watched evening gatherings disperse, feeling his life slipping away like a dying wick.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> The motif of the snuffed candle was a classical Urdu-Punjabi mushaira trope that Shiv radically personalized in his final hospital stays.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on cigarette packets and café napkins in Chandigarh, leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਮ੍ਹਾ ਤਾਂ ਬੁੱਝ ਗਈ ਰਾਤ ਪਈ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਧੂੰਆਂ ਉੱਠਦਾ ਕਾਲ਼ਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਚਾਨਣ ਪਿੱਛੇ ਜੱਗ ਭੱਜਿਆ,  
> ਉਸ ਚਾਨਣ ਦਾ ਮੁੱਕਿਆ ਪਿਆਲ਼ਾ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شمع تاں بجھ گئی رات پئی،  
> ہن دھواں اٹھدا کالا نی!  
> جس چانن پچھے جگ بھجیا،  
> اس چانن دا مکیا پیالا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shama taan bujjh gayi raat payi,  
> Hun dhuan uthda kaala ni!  
> Jis chanan pichhe jagg bhajeya,  
> Uss chanan da mukkeya pyala ni!  

#### English Translation
> The candle was snuffed out as the deep night fell;  
> Now only dark, spiraling smoke rises into the void!  
> That blazing brilliance which the hungry world once pursued—  
> The goblet of that radiance has been drained to its very dregs!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The extinguished candle (*shama*) as symbol of the poet's exhausted vitality.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਹ ਧੂੰਆਂ ਹਵਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਰਲ਼ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਿਸ਼ਾਨ ਨਾ ਰਹਿਣਾ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਨੇ ਹੰਢਾਇਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੁਣ ਸਹਿਣਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ایہ دھواں ہوا وچ رل جاوے گا،  
> کوئی نشان نہ رہنا اے!  
> جو درد شیو نے ہنڈھایا سی،  
> اوہ کسے نہیں ہن سہنا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Eh dhuan havaa vich ral jaavega,  
> Koi nishaan na rehna ae!  
> Jo dard Shiv ne handhaya si,  
> Oh kise nahin hun sehna ae!  

#### English Translation
> This fading smoke will dissolve effortlessly into the wind;  
> Not a single earthly footprint will remain behind!  
> That monumental grief which Shiv endured upon his brow  
> No other living soul shall ever have the courage to endure!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Incomparability of the poet's agony disappearing into cosmic air.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਪਰਵਾਨੇ ਸਾਰੇ ਉੱਡ ਗਏ ਨੇ,  
> ਮਹਿਫ਼ਿਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੁੰਞ ਪਸਰ ਗਈ ਏ!  
> ਸੜ ਕੇ ਜੋ ਰਾਖ਼ ਬਣੀ ਸੀ ਸ਼ਮ੍ਹਾ,  
> ਉਹ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਠਰ ਗਈ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> پروانے سارے اڈ گئے نے،  
> محفل وچ سنج پسر گئی اے!  
> سڑ کے جو راکھ بنی سی شمع،  
> اوہ مٹی دے وچ ٹھر گئی اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Parwaane saare udd gaye ne,  
> Mehfil vich sunj pasar gayi ae!  
> Sarh ke jo raakh bani si shama,  
> Oh mitti de vich thar gayi ae!  

#### English Translation
> The dazzled moths have all scattered and taken flight;  
> A harrowing desolation has blanketed the banquet hall!  
> The candle which burned itself down to cold, grey ash  
> Has frozen into eternal rest within the quiet clay!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The empty gathering hall (*mehfil*) abandoned by fair-weather admirers.*
