# Kabr Da Sirhana

### **ਕਬਰ ਦਾ ਸਿਰਹਾਣਾ** • **قبر دا سرہانا**

#### *"The Pillow of the Grave: Resting Upon Cold Earth"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Alvida (1974)](../10_alvida_1974.md)
- **Original Title**: ਕਬਰ ਦਾ ਸਿਰਹਾਣਾ (قبر دا سرہانا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1974
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Posthumous` `#Grave` `#EternalRest`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The grave is imagined not as a frightening subterranean dungeon, but as a soft earthen bolster offering eternal sleep after a life of severe insomnia.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv suffered from chronic, agonizing insomnia throughout the late 1960s and early 1970s; death was conceived primarily as sleep.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Though cremated according to Hindu rites, Shiv frequently invoked Islamic Sufi imagery of the grave (kabr, mazaar) in his poems as symbols of permanent physical rest.

**Source**: *Shiv Batalvi: A Solitary and Passionate Singer* by Dr. O.P. Sharma (1979, Sterling Publishers, New Delhi, pp. 12–45, 180–205). [Primary Memoir]

> "Authoritative academic biography documenting his childhood in Bara Pind Lohtian (now Pakistan), the trauma of the 1947 Partition flight, his clerkship at the State Bank of India Chandigarh, and his final collapse in Kir Mangyal."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸਾਰੀ ਉਮਰ ਮੈਂ ਜਾਗਦਾ ਰਿਹਾ,  
> ਨੀਂਦ ਨਾ ਆਈ ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਨੂੰ!  
> ਅੱਜ ਕਬਰ ਦੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਕਹਿੰਦੀ ਏ,  
> ਸਿਰਹਾਣਾ ਦੇਵਾਂ ਪੰਖੀਆਂ ਨੂੰ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ساری عمر میں جاگدا رہا،  
> نیند نہ آئی اکھیاں نوں!  
> اج قبر دی مٹی کہندی اے،  
> سرہانا دیواں پنکھیاں نوں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Saari umar main jaagda reha,  
> Neend na aayi akhiyaan nu!  
> Ajj kabr di mitti kehndi ae,  
> Sirhana devaan pankhiyaan nu!  

#### English Translation
> Throughout my entire youth I remained sleeplessly awake,  
> No soothing slumber ever touched these burning eyes!  
> Today the gentle earth of the grave whispers softly,  
> Offering its cool bolster to this weary, wounded bird!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The earth welcoming the chronic insomniac to sweet, unbroken rest.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਸ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦੇ ਸਿਰਹਾਣੇ ਉੱਤੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਸੁਪਨਾ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਡਰ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਈ ਭਟਕਣ ਸਾਰੀ,  
> ਉਹੀਓ ਬਣ ਗਿਆ ਮੇਰਾ ਘਰ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اس مٹی دے سرہانے اتے،  
> نہ کوئی سپنا نہ کوئی ڈر!  
> جتھے مک گئی بھٹکن ساری،  
> اوہیؤ بن گیا میرا گھر!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Iss mitti de sirhaane utte,  
> Na koi supna na koi darr!  
> Jitthe mukk gayi bhatkan saari,  
> Ohiyo ban gaya mera ghar!  

#### English Translation
> Upon this soft pillow sculpted of ancient soil,  
> No tormenting nightmares strike, no terrifying shadows loom!  
> Where all relentless wanderings of the soul come to an end,  
> That desolate haven alone has become my true, eternal home!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Resolution of existential wandering (*bhatkan*) into permanent homecoming.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜੇ ਕੋਈ ਲੰਘੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ਼ੋਂ,  
> ਹੌਲ਼ੀ ਕਦਮ ਧਰਾਵੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਕਿਤੇ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੀ ਗੂੜ੍ਹੀ ਨੀਂਦਰ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਖ਼ਲਲ ਨਾ ਪਾਵੇ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جے کوئی لنگھے میرے کولوں،  
> ہولی قدم دھراوے نی!  
> کتے شیو دی گوڑھی نیندڑ وچ،  
> کوئی خلل نہ پاوے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Je koi langhe mere kolon,  
> Hauli kadam dharaave ni!  
> Kite Shiv di goorhi neendar vich,  
> Koi khalal na paave ni!  

#### English Translation
> If any traveler should chance to walk past my resting place,  
> Let him tread softly with reverent, gentle steps!  
> Lest in the profound, sacred slumber of Shiv,  
> Any careless intruder should cast disturbance or noise!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Plea for absolute quietude over the resting place.*
