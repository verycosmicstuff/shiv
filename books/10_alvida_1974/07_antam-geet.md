# Antam Geet

### **ਅੰਤਮ ਗੀਤ** • **انتم گیت**

#### *"The Final Song: When the Throat Turns to Ash"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Alvida (1974)](../10_alvida_1974.md)
- **Original Title**: ਅੰਤਮ ਗੀਤ (انتم گیت)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1974
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Posthumous` `#FinalBreath` `#ElegiacSong`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The poet recognizes that the final song of life is not sung with melody, but with the rattling silence of an exhausted chest.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Shiv's last weeks when physical exhaustion had stolen his celebrated singing voice, leaving only pen and paper.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Discovered among Shiv's final handwritten loose sheets preserved by his wife Aruna after his death in Kir Mangyal in May 1973.

**Source**: *Shiv Batalvi: A Solitary and Passionate Singer* by Dr. O.P. Sharma (1979, Sterling Publishers, New Delhi, pp. 12–45, 180–205). [Primary Memoir]

> "Authoritative academic biography documenting his childhood in Bara Pind Lohtian (now Pakistan), the trauma of the 1947 Partition flight, his clerkship at the State Bank of India Chandigarh, and his final collapse in Kir Mangyal."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੇਰੇ ਗਲੇ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਗੀਤ ਨਹੀਂ ਨਿਕਲਦਾ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਤਾਂ ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਹਟਕੋਰੇ ਨੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਜੀਵਨ ਨੇ ਦਰਦ ਵੰਡਾਇਆ,  
> ਉਸ ਜੀਵਨ ਦੇ ਦਿਨ ਥੋੜ੍ਹੇ ਨੇ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میرے گلے وچوں گیت نہیں نکلدا،  
> ہن تاں صرف ہٹکورے نے نی!  
> جس جیون نے درد ونڈایا،  
> اس جیون دے دن تھوڑھے نے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Mere gale vichon geet nahin nikalda,  
> Hun taan sirf hatkore ne ni!  
> Jis jeevan ne dard vandaya,  
> Uss jeevan de din thodhe ne ni!  

#### English Translation
> No melody rises from my throat any longer,  
> Nothing remains now but ragged, sobbing gasps!  
> That life which distributed holy sorrow to the world  
> Has only a handful of fleeting hours left to breathe!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Loss of the physical singing voice (*hatkore*) as the mortal threshold approaches.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਸ਼ਬਦਾਂ ਦੀ ਚਿਖਾ ਬਣਾਈ,  
> ਹਰ ਹੰਝੂ ਨੂੰ ਅੱਗ ਦਿਖਾਈ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਿਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੀ ਦਰਦ ਛੁਪਾਇਆ,  
> ਉਹਨੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਉਮਰ ਮੁਕਾਈ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں شبداں دی چکھا بنائی،  
> ہر ہنجھو نوں اگ دکھائی!  
> جو دل وچ سی درد چھپایا،  
> اوہنے میری عمر مکائی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main shabdaan di chikha banayi,  
> Har hanjhu nu agg dikhayi!  
> Jo dil vich si dard chhupaya,  
> Ohne meri umar mukayi!  

#### English Translation
> I fashioned a funeral pyre out of my sacred words,  
> And showed the burning fire to every salted tear!  
> The quiet torment held captive deep inside my heart  
> Has consumed the entirety of my fleeting youth!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The metapoetic pyre (*shabdaan di chikha*) burning the poet's own grief.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਕਹਿਣ ਦੀ ਵੇਲ਼ਾ ਆਈ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਰੋਵੇ ਮੇਰੀ ਕਬਰ ਉੱਤੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤ ਹੀ ਮੇਰਾ ਕਫ਼ਨ ਬਣਨਗੇ,  
> ਸੌਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ ਮੈਂ ਧਰਤ ਉੱਤੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> الوداع کہن دی ویلا آئی،  
> کوئی نہ رووے میری قبر اتے!  
> میرے گیت ہی میرا کفن بنن گے،  
> سوں جاواں گا میں دھرتی اتے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Alvida kehan di vela aayi,  
> Koi na rove meri kabr utte!  
> Mere geet hi mera kafan banange,  
> Saun jaavanga main dharat utte!  

#### English Translation
> The solemn hour has struck to utter my farewell;  
> Let no stranger weep upon my solitary grave!  
> My surviving songs alone shall be stitched into my shroud  
> As I sleep forever upon the bosom of the earth!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Poetry transforming into the burial shroud (*kafan*) in perpetual union with the soil.*
