# Alvida (Aakhri Geet)

### **ਅਲਵਿਦਾ (ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਗੀਤ)** • **الوداع (آخری گیت)**

#### *"Farewell: The Ultimate Departure"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Alvida (1974)](../10_alvida_1974.md)
- **Original Title**: ਅਲਵਿਦਾ (ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਗੀਤ) (الوداع (آخری گیت))
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1974
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Farewell` `#Posthumous` `#Epitaph` `#Masterpiece`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The definitive posthumous title poem where the poet settles his earthly debt to Punjab, releasing his songs to the wind and bidding a final goodbye.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Found among the papers in his father-in-law's house in Kir Mangyal after Shiv passed away on May 6, 1973 at age 36.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Included in the canonical Punjabi collection 'Alvida' (1974).

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> The definitive epitaph of Shiv Kumar Batalvi's life and art.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (4 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਮੇਰੇ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦਿਓ ਲੋਕੋ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਜਾਣ ਦਾ ਵੇਲਾ ਆ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਦੀਵਾ ਬਾਲ਼ਿਆ ਸੀ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ,  
> ਉਹ ਬੁਝਣ ਕਿਨਾਰੇ ਆ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> الوداع میرے پنجاب دیو لوگو،  
> میرا جان دا ویلا آ گیا اے!  
> جو دیوا بالیا سی برہا دا،  
> اوہ بجھن کنارے آ گیا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Alvida mere Punjab diyo loko,  
> Mera jaan da vela aa gaya ae!  
> Jo deeva baaleya si birha da,  
> Oh bujhan kinaare aa gaya ae!  

#### English Translation
> Farewell, O people of my beloved Punjab,  
> The hour of my final departure has arrived!  
> That earthen lamp of separation which I illuminated  
> Has reached the very threshold of being extinguished!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The final address to the people of Punjab.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਛੱਤੀ ਵਰ੍ਹੇ ਹੀ ਜੀਵਿਆ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਪਰ ਸਦੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਦਰਦ ਹੰਢਾਇਆ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾ ਕਹਿਣਾ ਚਾਹਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਮੈਂ ਗੀਤਾਂ 'ਚ ਗਾਇਆ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں چھتی ورھے ہی جیویا ہاں،  
> پر صدیاں دا درد ہنڈھایا اے!  
> جو کسے نہ کہنا چاہیا سی،  
> اوہ میں گیتاں چ گایا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main chhatti varhe hi jeeveya haan,  
> Par sadiyaan da dard handaaya ae!  
> Jo kise na kehna chaaheya si,  
> Oh main geetan ch gaaya ae!  

#### English Translation
> I lived merely thirty-six brief mortal years,  
> Yet I endured and exhausted centuries of collective agony!  
> That which no mortal soul dared to whisper aloud,  
> I articulated boldly within the living body of my songs!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The 36 years encapsulating centuries of Punjabi historical trauma.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਨਾ ਰੋਇਓ ਮੇਰੀ ਕਬਰ ਉੱਤੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਫੁੱਲ ਚੜ੍ਹਾਇਓ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਦ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ ਕੋਈ ਪੰਛੀ ਕੂਕੇ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਮੇਰਾ ਗੀਤ ਸੁਣਾਇਓ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> نہ رویو میری قبر اتے،  
> نہ کوئی پھل چڑھایو نی!  
> جد برہا دا کوئی پنچھی کوکے،  
> تاں میرا گیت سنایو نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Na royeyo meri qabar utte,  
> Na koyi phull charhaayeo ni!  
> Jad birha da koyi panchhi kooke,  
> Taan mera geet sunaayeo ni!  

#### English Translation
> Weep not over my quiet earthen grave,  
> Nor lay cut flowers upon the mound!  
> Whenever the wild bird of separation calls across the twilight,  
> Sing my living verses aloud in communion!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Rejecting funerary rituals in favor of living poetic recitation.*

* * *

### Stanza 4

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਹਵਾ 'ਚ ਘੁਲ਼ ਗਿਆ ਏ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਇੱਕ ਸਾਹ 'ਚ ਵੱਸਾਂਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਪੰਜਾਬ 'ਚ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਰੋਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਹੰਝੂ ਬਣ ਕੇ ਵਰ੍ਹਾਂਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو تاں ہوا چ گھل گیا اے،  
> میں ہر اک ساہ چ وساں گا!  
> جد پنجاب چ عشق رویگا،  
> میں ہنجھو بن کے ورھانگا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv taan hawa ch ghull gaya ae,  
> Main har ikk saah ch vasaanga!  
> Jad Punjab ch ishq rovega,  
> Main hanjhu ban ke varhaanga!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv has dissolved forever into the ambient air of Punjab;  
> I shall dwell inside every breath drawn by future generations!  
> Whenever true love weeps anywhere in this land,  
> I shall descend as molten tears from the sky!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Immortal omnipresence: the poet becomes the very tears of Punjab.*
