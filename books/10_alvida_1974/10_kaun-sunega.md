# Kaun Sunega

### **ਕੌਣ ਸੁਣੇਗਾ** • **کون سنے گا**

#### *"Who Will Listen: The Unheard Cry in the Wilderness"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Alvida (1974)](../10_alvida_1974.md)
- **Original Title**: ਕੌਣ ਸੁਣੇਗਾ (کون سنے گا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1974
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Posthumous` `#Loneliness` `#UnheardVoice`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `solitude` | **Landscape Biome**: `barren_dune`

**Synopsis**: A profound meditation on the futility of singing to an unfeeling world that seeks amusement rather than truth.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's late reflection on the deep spiritual alienation between the solitary poet and the urban consumer of literature.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> In his final recorded interviews in early 1973, Shiv lamented that society applauded his voice but had gone stone deaf to the existential warning in his poetry.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar Batalvi: Jeevan Te Rachna (Life and Works)* by Dr. Satinder Singh (1983, Publication Bureau, Punjabi University, Patiala, pp. 45–78). [Literary Criticism]

> "Scholarly monograph evaluating Shiv Kumar’s lyrical prosody, the metaphysics of Birha, and the critical reception of his posthumous anthology Alvida."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜਦ ਮੈਂ ਨਾ ਰਿਹਾ ਇਸ ਜੱਗ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਦੱਸੋ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤ ਕੌਣ ਸੁਣੇਗਾ?  
> ਸੁੰਞੇ ਰਾਹਾਂ ਉੱਤੇ ਰੁਲਦੇ ਹੋਏ,  
> ਦਰਦਾਂ ਦੇ ਮੋਤੀ ਕੌਣ ਚੁਣੇਗਾ?  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جد میں نہ رہا اس جگ اندر،  
> دسو میرے گیت کون سنے گا؟  
> سنجے راہاں اتے رلدے ہوئے،  
> درداں دے موتی کون چنے گا؟  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Jad main na reha iss jagg andar,  
> Dasso mere geet kaun sunega?  
> Sunjhe raahaan utte rulde hoye,  
> Dardaan de moti kaun chunega?  

#### English Translation
> When I no longer dwell within this transient world,  
> Tell me, who will pause to listen to my lonely songs?  
> Trampled underfoot along the deserted wilderness paths,  
> Who will gather up these scattered pearls of suffering?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Fear of posthumous neglect and trivialization of authentic suffering.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਲੋਕਾਂ ਨੇ ਤਾਂ ਮੇਲਾ ਲਾਇਆ,  
> ਹੱਸ-ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਮਾਸ ਖ਼ਰੀਦਿਆ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਕੰਡੇ ਉੱਤੇ ਮੈਂ ਤੜਫਦਾ ਰਿਹਾ,  
> ਉਸ ਕੰਡੇ ਨੂੰ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾ ਦੀਦਿਆ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> لوکاں نے تاں میلہ لایا،  
> ہس ہس کے میرا ماس خریدیا!  
> جس کنڈے اتے میں تڑپھدا رہا،  
> اس کنڈے نوں کسے نہ دیدیا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Lokaan ne taan mela laaya,  
> Hass-hass ke mera maas khareediya!  
> Jis kande utte main taraphda reha,  
> Uss kande nu kise na deediya!  

#### English Translation
> The crowds assembled as if attending a carnival,  
> Laughing aloud as they purchased the very flesh of my soul!  
> Yet that piercing thorn upon which I writhed in agony—  
> Not a single eye among them possessed the sight to behold it!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Commercialization of grief where audiences consume the poet's vulnerability.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇੱਕ ਦਿਨ ਹਵਾਵਾਂ ਗਾਉਣਗੀਆਂ,  
> ਰੁੱਖਾਂ ਦੇ ਪੱਤੇ ਰੋਵਣਗੇ!  
> ਜਦ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੀ ਗ਼ੈਰ-ਹਾਜ਼ਰੀ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਸਭ ਲੋਕੀਂ ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਧੋਵਣਗੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اک دن ہواواں گاؤن گیاں،  
> رکھاں دے پتے روون گے!  
> جد شیو دی غیر حاضری وچ،  
> سب لوکی اکھیاں دھوون گے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Ikk din havaavaan gaaungiyaan,  
> Rukkhaan de patte rovange!  
> Jad Shiv di ghair-haazri vich,  
> Sabh lokin akhiyaan dhovange!  

#### English Translation
> One day the sweeping winds themselves will sing my verses,  
> The green leaves of ancient trees will weep in mourning!  
> When in the haunting absence of Shiv,  
> All the penitent crowds will finally wash their blind eyes with tears!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Nature adopting the orphan songs when human ears fail to understand.*
