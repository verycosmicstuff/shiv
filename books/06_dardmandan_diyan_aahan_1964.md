## ਦਰਦਮੰਦਾਂ ਦੀਆਂ ਆਹਾਂ • دردمنداں دیاں آہਾਂ
### *"Sighs of the Broken-Hearted"*

---

## 📚 Anthology Overview

- **Author**: Shiv Kumar Batalvi (ਸਿ਼ਵ ਕੁਮਾਰ ਬਟਾਲਵੀ / شیو کمار بٹالوی)
- **Publication Year**: 1964
- **Original Publisher**: Kasturi Lal & Sons, Amritsar
- **Total Master Poems in this Edition**: 15

### Literary & Historical Background
> Ghazal-form and classical metered compositions exploring the intoxication of grief and tragic romance. Includes 'Jaach Mainu Aa Gayi' and 'Mainu Tera Shabab Lai Baitha'.

**Critical Reception**: Demonstrated Shiv's effortless command over formal Punjabi ghazal meters alongside free-flowing song.

## 1. Jaach Mainu Aa Gayi Gham Khaan Di

### **ਜਾਚ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਆ ਗਈ ਗ਼ਮ ਖਾਣ ਦੀ** • **جاچ مینوں آ گئی غم کھان دی**

#### *"I Have Mastered the Art of Consuming Grief"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Ghazal` `#Jaach` `#Mastery` `#Grief` `#Irony`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: The poet declares that after years of torture, sorrow has become an internalized discipline—he has mastered the refined art of weeping in silence without asking for sympathy.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Chandigarh; recorded in multiple live mushairas.
- **Archival Context**: One of Shiv's most iconic and frequently quoted ghazals across India and Pakistan, celebrated for its razor-sharp psychological irony. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: One of his most quoted ghazals; celebrated for its psychological irony.
- **Musical Recitation**: Delicate, stoic, conversational ghazal cadence.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਾਚ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਆ ਗਈ ਗ਼ਮ ਖਾਣ ਦੀ,  
> ਹੌਲੀ ਹੌਲੀ ਰੋ ਕੇ ਜੀ ਪਰਚਾਣ ਦੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جاچ مینوں آ گئی غم کھان دی،  
> ہولی ہولی رو کے جی پرچان دی!  

**Romanized**:
> Jaach mainu aa gayi gham khaan di,  
> Hauli hauli ro ke jee parchaan di!  

**English Translation**:
> I have mastered the refined art of consuming grief,  
> And consoling my own desolate soul by weeping softly in secret!  

💡 *Commentary*: Grief turned from external affliction into an internal aesthetic discipline (jaach).

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਚੰਗਾ ਹੋਇਆ ਤੂੰ ਪਰਾਇਆ ਹੋ ਗਿਓਂ,  
> ਮੁੱਕ ਗਈ ਚਿੰਤਾ ਤੈਨੂੰ ਆਪਣਾਣ ਦੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> چنگا ہویا توں پرایا ہو گیوں،  
> مک گئی چنتا تینوں اپناون دی!  

**Romanized**:
> Changa hoya toon paraaya ho gayon,  
> Mukk gayi chinta tainu apnaan di!  

**English Translation**:
> It is well that you became a stranger to me—  
> For at last the agonizing burden of striving to make you mine has ended!  

💡 *Commentary*: Brilliant irony: relief in the finality of rejection.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮਰ ਤੇ ਜਾਂ ਪਰ ਡਰ ਹੈ ਦੰਮਾਂ ਵਾਲਿਓ,  
> ਧਰਤ ਵੀ ਵਿਕਦੀ ਹੈ ਮੁੱਲ ਸ਼ਮਸ਼ਾਨ ਦੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> مر تے جاں پر ڈر ہے دماں والیو،  
> دھرت وی وکدی ہے مل شمشان دی!  

**Romanized**:
> Mar te jaan par dar hai dammaan waleyo,  
> Dhart vi vikdi hai mull shamshaan di!  

**English Translation**:
> I would gladly die, O wealthy masters of gold,  
> Were I not afraid that even the soil of the cremation ground is sold for profit!  

💡 *Commentary*: Fierce social critique: capitalist greed monetizing even funeral dust.

##### Stanza 4

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਦਿਓ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਾਹ ਉਧਾਰੇ ਦੋਸਤੋ,  
> ਲੈ ਕੇ ਮੁੜ ਹਿੰਮਤ ਨਹੀਂ ਪਰਤਾਣ ਦੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ دیوو مینوں ساہ ادھارے دوستو،  
> لے کے مڑ ہمت نہیں پرتان دی!  

**Romanized**:
> Na deyo mainu saah udhaare dosto,  
> Lai ke murh himmat nahin partaan di!  

**English Translation**:
> Lend me not your borrowed breaths, dear friends,  
> For once taken, I have no strength left to repay such a debt!  

💡 *Commentary*: Refusal of pity and borrowed consolations.

##### Stanza 5

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਕਰੋ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੀ ਉਦਾਸੀ ਦਾ ਇਲਾਜ,  
> ਰੋਣ ਦੀ ਮਰਜ਼ੀ ਹੈ ਅੱਜ ਬੇ-ਈਮਾਨ ਦੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ کرو شیو دی اداسی دا علاج،  
> رون دی مرضی ہے اج بے-ایمان دی!  

**Romanized**:
> Na karo Shiv di udaasi da ilaaj,  
> Ron di marzi hai ajj be-eemaan di!  

**English Translation**:
> Seek not a medical cure for Shiv's profound melancholy—  
> For today, this faithless heart desires only to weep without restraint!  

💡 *Commentary*: The sovereign right to mourn: rejecting clinical cures for spiritual grief.

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਜਾਚ (Jaach)** | *Jaach* | Art / Manner / Expertise | The cultivated etiquette and disciplined mastery of a difficult craft. |

## 2. Mainu Tera Shabab Lai Baitha

### **ਮੈਨੂੰ ਤੇਰਾ ਸ਼ਬਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ** • **مینوں تیرا شباب لے بیٹھا**

#### *"The Splendor of Your Youth Ruined Me"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Ghazal` `#Shabab` `#Wine` `#Intoxication` `#Masterpiece`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: Classic ghazal charting the poet's complete self-surrender to the beloved's intoxicating beauty and his alcoholic descent in the taverns of Chandigarh.
- **Genesis**: Composed during Shiv's bohemian years in Sector 17, Chandigarh.
- **Archival Context**: Immortalized in legendary musical renditions across the subcontinent, capturing Shiv's tragic bohemian life in Chandigarh. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Universally celebrated as one of the finest romantic ghazals in modern Indian literature.
- **Musical Recitation**: Lyrical Ghazal rhythm with playful, tragic undertones.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਤੇਰਾ ਸ਼ਬਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ,  
> ਰੰਗ ਗੋਰਾ ਗੁਲਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> مینوں تیرا شباب لے بیٹھا،  
> رنگ گورا گلاب لے بیٹھا!  

**Romanized**:
> Mainu tera shabaab lai baitha,  
> Rang gora gulaab lai baitha!  

**English Translation**:
> The blinding splendor of your youth ruined me,  
> Your fair countenance, flushed like a wild rose, consumed me!  

💡 *Commentary*: The opening matla attributes self-ruin to the intoxicating beauty of the beloved.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਡਰ ਸੀ ਕਿਤੇ ਨਾ ਲੈ ਬੈਠੇ,  
> ਲੈ ਹੀ ਬੈਠਾ ਜਨਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> دل دا ڈر سی کتے نہ لے بیٹھے،  
> لے ہی بیٹھا جناب لے بیٹھا!  

**Romanized**:
> Dil da dar si kite na lai baithe,  
> Lai hi baitha janaab lai baitha!  

**English Translation**:
> The heart feared from the start that it would destroy me;  
> And in the end, honored sir, it destroyed me utterly!  

💡 *Commentary*: Playful irony: the inevitable catastrophe foretold by intuition.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਿੰਨੀ ਬੀਤੀ ਤੇ ਕਿੰਨੀ ਬਾਕੀ ਏ,  
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਏਹੋ ਹਿਸਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کنی بیتی تے کنی باقی اے،  
> مینوں ایہو حساب لے بیٹھا!  

**Romanized**:
> Kinni beeti te kinni baaki ae,  
> Mainu eho hisaab lai baitha!  

**English Translation**:
> How much of life has been squandered, and how much yet remains?  
> This relentless calculation alone has devoured my spirit!  

💡 *Commentary*: Existential dread of the ticking clock.

##### Stanza 4

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਨੂੰ ਇੱਕ ਗ਼ਮ 'ਤੇ ਹੀ ਭਰੋਸਾ ਸੀ,  
> ਗ਼ਮ ਤੋਂ ਕੋਰਾ ਜਵਾਬ ਲੈ ਬੈਠਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو نوں اک غم تے ہی بھروسہ سی،  
> غم توں کورا جواب لے بیٹھا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv nu ikk gham te hi bharosa si,  
> Gham ton kora jawaab lai baitha!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv placed his faith only in the loyalty of his sorrow;  
> Yet from that very sorrow he received a cold, absolute refusal!  

💡 *Commentary*: Ultimate metaphysical abandonment: even sorrow betrays the poet.

| **ਸ਼ਬਾਬ (Shabaab)** | *Sha-baab* | Youthful bloom / Radiance | The intoxicating zenith of biological and romantic vitality. |

## 3. Ishq Di Baazi

### **ਇਸ਼ਕ ਦੀ ਬਾਜ਼ੀ** • **عشق دی بازی**

#### *"The High Wager of Passion"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Ghazal` `#Ishq` `#Wager` `#Sacrifice` `#Martyrdom`
- **Theme / Biome**: `rebellion` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: The lover enters the arena of passion knowing that the wager requires the forfeit of life, yet plays with ecstatic abandon.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Chandigarh during a late-night poetic symposium.
- **Archival Context**: Employs the classical Urdu/Persian ghazal tradition of Ishq as a fatal tournament where the lover willingly bets his head. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਸ਼ਕ ਦੀ ਬਾਜ਼ੀ ਜਿੱਤ ਨਾ ਸਕੇ,  
> ਅਸਾਂ ਸਿਰ ਦਾ ਸੌਦਾ ਲਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ!  
> ਜੋ ਸਾਹ ਸੀ ਸਾਡੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਉਹ ਤੇਰੇ ਨਾਮ ਵਹਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> عشق دی بازی جت نہ سکے،  
> اساں سر دا سودا لا دتا!  
> جو ساہ سی ساڈے سینے وچ،  
> اوہ تیرے نام وہا دتا!  

**Romanized**:
> Ishq di baazi jitt na sake,  
> Asaan sir da sauda laa ditta!  
> Jo saah si saade seene vich,  
> Oh tere naam vahaa ditta!  

**English Translation**:
> We could not emerge victorious in the high wager of love,  
> Yet we staked our very severed head on the board!  
> Every living breath that pulsed inside our chest  
> We poured out as an unreturning offering in your name!  

💡 *Commentary*: Staking one's head (sir da sauda) as the Sufi price of divine love.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਦੁਨੀਆ ਨੇ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਕਾਫ਼ਰ ਕਿਹਾ,  
> ਜਦ ਮੰਦਰ-ਮਸਜਿਦ ਛੱਡ ਦਿੱਤੇ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਤੇਰੇ ਦਰ 'ਤੇ ਸਿਰ ਧਰ ਕੇ,  
> ਸਭ ਵਹਿਮ ਦਿਲੋਂ ਹੀ ਕੱਢ ਦਿੱਤੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> دنیا نے سانوں کافر کہیا،  
> جد مندر مسجد چھڈ دتے!  
> اسیں تیرے در تے سر دھر کے،  
> سبھ وہم دلوں ہی کڈھ دتے!  

**Romanized**:
> Duniya ne saanu kaafir keha,  
> Jad mandar-masjid chhadd ditte!  
> Aseen tere dar te sir dhar ke,  
> Sabh vahim dilon hi kaddh ditte!  

**English Translation**:
> The orthodox world branded us heretics and infidels  
> When we abandoned both the temple and the mosque!  
> Laying our forehead upon your sacred threshold,  
> We purged every ritual superstition from our spirit!  

💡 *Commentary*: Transcendence of formal religious orthodoxy through personal devotion.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਹਾਰ ਕੇ ਵੀ ਬਾਦਸ਼ਾਹ ਬਣਿਆ,  
> ਇਸ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਦੇ ਖ਼ਾਲੀ ਤਖ਼ਤ ਉੱਤੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਜਿੱਤ ਗਏ ਉਹ ਰੋਣਗੇ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਲਿਖੇ ਹੋਏ ਇਸ ਵਕਤ ਉੱਤੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو ہار کے وی بادشاہ بنیا،  
> اس عشق دے خالی تخت اتے!  
> جو جت گئے اوہ رونگے،  
> ساڈے لکھے ہوئے اس وقت اتے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv haar ke vi baadshah baneya,  
> Iss ishq de khaali takht utte!  
> Jo jitt gaye oh roange,  
> Saade likhe hoye iss vaqt utte!  

**English Translation**:
> Even in defeat, Shiv was crowned emperor  
> Upon this sovereign, empty throne of sacred love!  
> Those who supposedly won worldly triumphs shall weep in envy  
> Reading the immortal verses inscribed by his hand across time!  

💡 *Commentary*: The defeated lover crowned as sovereign of the poetic realm.

## 4. Dard Mandan Di Dastaan

### **ਦਰਦ-ਮੰਦਾਂ ਦੀ ਦਾਸਤਾਨ** • **دردمنداں دی داستان**

#### *"The Chronicle of the Suffering Wayfarers"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Dardmand` `#Chronicle` `#Sighs` `#Exile` `#Companions`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: The collective testament of those souls across Punjab who live solely through longing, finding solidarity in shared sighs.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Chandigarh after receiving letters from heartbroken admirers across the divided Punjab.
- **Archival Context**: The title inspiration for the collection, invoking the classical Persian tradition of 'Dardmand' (those burdened with holy pain). (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਸੀਂ ਦਰਦ-ਮੰਦਾਂ ਦੀ ਟੋਲੀ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਜੋ ਰਾਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਕੁਰਲਾਉਂਦੀ ਏ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਆਹਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਇੱਕ ਨਵੀਂ ਸਵੇਰ ਮੁਸਕਾਉਂਦੀ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اسیں دردمنداں دی ٹولی ہاں،  
> جو راتاں نوں کرلاؤندی اے!  
> ساڈی آہاں دے وچوں اڑیو،  
> اک نویں سویر مسکاوندی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Aseen dard-mandaan di toli haan,  
> Jo raataan nu kurlaaundi ae!  
> Saadi aahaan de vichon arhiyo,  
> Ikk naveen saver muskaaundi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> We belong to the fraternity of the suffering wayfarers,  
> Who wail into the silent expanses of the midnight!  
> Yet out of the burning furnace of our sighs, friends,  
> A radiant, newborn dawn begins to smile!  

💡 *Commentary*: Sighs of the suffering transmuting into cosmic dawn.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਸਾਡਾ ਕੋਈ ਘਰ-ਬਾਰ ਏ,  
> ਨਾ ਸਾਡੀ ਕੋਈ ਛੱਤ ਨੀ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਪਾਂਧੀ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਦਰਦ ਹੀ ਸਾਡੀ ਪੱਤ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ ساڈا کوئی گھر بار اے،  
> نہ ساڈی کوئی چھت نی!  
> اسیں برہا دے پاندھی ہاں،  
> ساڈا درد ہی ساڈی پت نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Na saada koyi ghar-baar ae,  
> Na saadi koyi chhatt ni!  
> Aseen birha de paandhi haan,  
> Saada dard hi saadi patt ni!  

**English Translation**:
> We possess neither home nor landed estate;  
> No solid ceiling shelters our vulnerable heads!  
> We are the eternal pilgrims of sacred separation;  
> Our wound alone constitutes our supreme honor and nobility!  

💡 *Commentary*: Suffering recognized as the only true spiritual aristocracy (patt).

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੇ ਗੀਤ ਸਾਡੀ ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਬਣੇ,  
> ਜੋ ਸਾਰੇ ਜੱਗ 'ਚ ਗੂੰਜਣਗੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਹੰਝੂ ਅਸਾਂ ਵਹਾਏ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਰੱਬ ਦੇ ਚਰਨਾਂ ਧੁੰਜਣਗੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دے گیت ساڈی آواز بنے،  
> جو سارے جگ چ گونجنگے!  
> جو ہنجھو اساں وہائے سی،  
> اوہ رب دے چرناں دھنجنگے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv de geet saadi aawaaz bane,  
> Jo saare jagg ch goonjange!  
> Jo hanjhu asaan vahaaye si,  
> Oh Rabb de charnaan dhunjange!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv's verses became our collective voice,  
> Destined to echo across the breadth of the world!  
> Those scalding tears we poured out in obscurity  
> Shall wash the feet of the Divine in the highest heavens!  

💡 *Commentary*: The collective tears sanctified as divine libation.

## 5. Dard Da Dariya

### **ਦਰਦ ਦਾ ਦਰਿਆ** • **درد دا دریا**

#### *"The Turbulent River of Sorrow"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#River` `#Flood` `#Drowning` `#Currents` `#Chenab`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `village_monsoon`
- **Synopsis**: Personal grief expands until it resembles the swollen monsoon river Chenab, sweeping away all earthly moorings.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala after visiting the riverbank of the Chenab near Akhnoor.
- **Archival Context**: Evokes the River Chenab of Sohni Mahiwal, transforming the river of love into an inescapable torrent of grief. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਡੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਵਗਦਾ ਦਰਦ ਦਾ ਦਰਿਆ,  
> ਜਿਸਦਾ ਕੋਈ ਕਿਨਾਰਾ ਨਹੀਂ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਡੁੱਬ ਚੱਲੇ ਇਸ ਵਹਿਣ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਕੋਈ ਸਹਾਰਾ ਨਹੀਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ساڈے سینے وگدا درد دا دریا،  
> جس دا کوئی کنارہ نہیں!  
> اسیں ڈب چلے اس وہین اندر،  
> ساڈا کوئی سہارا نہیں!  

**Romanized**:
> Saade seene vagda dard da dariya,  
> Jisda koyi kinaara nahin!  
> Aseen dubb challe iss vahan andar,  
> Saada koyi sahaara nahin!  

**English Translation**:
> Inside our chest surges a raging river of pain  
> Possessing no visible shores or tranquil banks!  
> We have begun to drown within this unsparing torrent,  
> With not a single earthly lifeline to hold onto!  

💡 *Commentary*: Boundless internal flood without shoreline or salvation.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸੋਹਣੀ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਕੱਚੇ ਘੜੇ 'ਤੇ ਤਰੇ,  
> ਪਰ ਘੜਾ ਪਾਣੀ 'ਚ ਖੁਰ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਪਾਰ ਮਿਲਣਾ ਸੀ ਯਾਰ ਸਾਨੂੰ,  
> ਉਹ ਦੇਸ ਹੀ ਦੂਰ ਤੁਰ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> سوہنی وانگوں کچے گھڑے تے ترے،  
> پر گھڑا پانی چ کھر گیا نی!  
> جس پار ملنا سی یار سانوں،  
> اوہ دیس ہی دور تر گیا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Sohni vaangu kachhe ghare te tare,  
> Par ghara paani ch khur gaya ni!  
> Jis paar milna si yaar saanu,  
> Oh des hi door tur gaya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Like Sohni we ventured onto the waves with an unbaked clay pot,  
> Yet the fragile pot dissolved in the rushing currents!  
> That distant shore where our beloved waited  
> Has drifted infinitely far beyond the reach of our sight!  

💡 *Commentary*: The dissolving clay pitcher (Kachha Ghara) of Sohni.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਪਰ ਇਹ ਡੁੱਬਣਾ ਵੀ,  
> ਇੱਕ ਅਮਰ ਕਹਾਣੀ ਬਣ ਜਾਊਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਰਾਵੀ-ਚਨਾਬ ਦਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਵਹੂ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਸਾਡਾ ਗੀਤ ਸੁਣਾਊਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو کہندا پر ایہہ ڈبنا وی،  
> اک امر کہانی بن جاؤگا!  
> جد راوی چناب دا پانی وہو،  
> تاں ساڈا گیت سناؤگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv kehnda par eh dubbna vi,  
> Ikk amar kahaani ban jaauga!  
> Jad Ravi-Chenab da paani vahu,  
> Taan saada geet sunaauga!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv declares: 'Even this drowning surrender  
> Shall crystallize into an immortal legend for all time!  
> As long as the holy waters of the Ravi and Chenab flow,  
> They shall sing the melody of our sacrifice to Punjab!'  

💡 *Commentary*: Drowning transformed into eternal mythic melody.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਚਨਾਬ (Chenab)** | *Che-naab* | The River of Passion / River Chenab | Sacred river of Punjabi romance associated with the tragic drownings of Sohni and Heer. |

## 6. Peedan Da Shehar

### **ਪੀੜਾਂ ਦਾ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ** • **پیڑاں دا شہر**

#### *"The Necropolis of Urban Sorrows"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#City` `#Chandigarh` `#Alienation` `#Modernity` `#Urban`
- **Theme / Biome**: `modernism` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: The planned modern city with its concrete grids is revealed as a cold necropolis where souls are compartmentalized and isolated.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Chandigarh during his first year working at the State Bank of India.
- **Archival Context**: Direct critique of Le Corbusier's modern grid city Chandigarh, contrasting concrete geometry with rural human warmth. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਹ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੀੜਾਂ ਦਾ ਜੰਗਲ ਏ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਪੱਥਰਾਂ ਦੇ ਦਿਲ ਵੱਸਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਕਿਸੇ ਦਾ ਦਰਦ ਨਾ ਜਾਣੇ ਇੱਥੇ,  
> ਸਭ ਵੇਖ ਕੇ ਪਰਾਇਆ ਹੱਸਦੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ایہہ شہر نہیں پیڑاں دا جنگل اے،  
> جتھے پتھراں دے دل وسدے نے!  
> کوئی کسے دا درد نہ جانے ایتھے،  
> سبھ ویکھ کے پرایا ہسدے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Eh shehar nahin peedan da jangal ae,  
> Jitthe patthran de dil vassde ne!  
> Koyi kise da dard na jaane itthe,  
> Sabh vekh ke paraaya hassde ne!  

**English Translation**:
> This is no civilized city—it is a concrete jungle of sorrows,  
> Where dwell citizens with hearts carved of unyielding stone!  
> Not a single soul comprehends another's wound here;  
> All laugh in cold derision at the suffering of strangers!  

💡 *Commentary*: Modern urban architecture alienating the human heart.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕੋਠੀਆਂ ਉੱਚੀਆਂ, ਸੜਕਾਂ ਚੌੜੀਆਂ,  
> ਪਰ ਰੂਹਾਂ ਤੰਗ ਹੋਈਆਂ ਨੇ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਪੈਸੇ ਦੀ ਹੀ ਪੂਜਾ ਹੁੰਦੀ,  
> ਉੱਥੇ ਆਸਾਂ ਬੇ-ਰੰਗ ਹੋਈਆਂ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کوٹھیاں اچیاں، سڑکاں چوڑیاں،  
> پر روحاں تنگ ہوئیاں نے!  
> جتھے پیسے دی ہی پوجا ہندی،  
> اوتھے آساں بے-رنگ ہوئیاں نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kotthiyan uchiyan, sarrkaan chaurhiyaan,  
> Par roohaan tang hoiyan ne!  
> Jitthe paise di hi pooja hundi,  
> Outhe aasaan be-rang hoiyan ne!  

**English Translation**:
> The residential mansions are towering, the boulevards are broad,  
> Yet human spirits have shriveled and suffocated in narrowness!  
> Where currency and capital alone are worshiped as gods,  
> All the vibrant hopes of youth have turned pale and bleached!  

💡 *Commentary*: Physical grandeur contrasting with spiritual claustrophobia.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਮੈਂ ਮੁੜ ਚੱਲਾਂਗਾ,  
> ਆਪਣੇ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੀਆਂ ਕੱਚੀਆਂ ਢਾਰਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਦਿਲ ਵਾਲ਼ੇ ਅਜੇ ਵੱਸਦੇ ਨੇ,  
> ਸਜਦਾ ਕਰਦੇ ਟੁੱਟਦੇ ਤਾਰਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو کہندا میں مڑ چلاںگا،  
> اپنے پنڈ دیاں کچیاں ڈھاریاں نوں!  
> جتھے دل والے اجے وسدے نے،  
> سجدہ کردے ٹٹدے تاریاں نوں!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv kehnda main murh challaanga,  
> Aapne pind diyan kachiyan dhaariyaan nu!  
> Jitthe dil waale aje vassde ne,  
> Sajda karde tuttde taariyan nu!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv declares: 'I shall pack my bag and return  
> To the unbaked mud huts of my ancestral village!  
> Where folk with beating hearts still reside,  
> Bowing their heads in reverence to falling shooting stars!'  

💡 *Commentary*: Return to the village as the only spiritual salvation.

|:---|:---|:---|:---|

## 7. Zindagi Ik Khed Hai

### **ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਇੱਕ ਖੇਡ ਹੈ** • **زندگی اک کھیل ہے**

#### *"Life is a Puppeteer's Theatrical Game"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Puppet` `#Drama` `#Illusion` `#Stage` `#Play`
- **Theme / Biome**: `modernism` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: Human beings are merely wooden puppets dancing to the invisible strings pulled by an unseen, indifferent puppeteer.
- **Genesis**: Composed after watching a street puppeteer perform in the Batala fairgrounds.
- **Archival Context**: Draws on traditional Rajasthani and Punjabi Kathputli (marionette) puppetry, exploring free will versus cosmic manipulation. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਸੀਂ ਕਾਠ ਦੀਆਂ ਪੁਤਲੀਆਂ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਡੋਰੀ 'ਤੇ ਨੱਚਦੇ ਹਾਂ!  
> ਜੋ ਨਚਾਉਣ ਵਾਲ਼ਾ ਚਾਹੁੰਦਾ ਏ,  
> ਉਸਦੇ ਇਸ਼ਾਰੇ 'ਤੇ ਮੱਚਦੇ ਹਾਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اسیں کاٹھ دیاں پتلیوں وانگوں،  
> کسے ڈوری تے نچدے ہاں!  
> جو نچاون والا چاہندا اے،  
> اس دے اشارے تے مچدے ہاں!  

**Romanized**:
> Aseen kaath diyan putliyaan vaangu,  
> Kise dori te nachde haan!  
> Jo nachaavan waala chaahnda ae,  
> Usde ishaare te machde haan!  

**English Translation**:
> Like marionettes carved of dead wood,  
> We dance suspended upon invisible strings!  
> Whatever the unseen puppeteer desires,  
> We leap and burn at the twitch of his fingers!  

💡 *Commentary*: The existential tragedy of human agency as an illusion.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਦ ਡੋਰੀ ਟੁੱਟਦੀ ਹੈ ਹੱਥੋਂ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਪੁਤਲੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ 'ਚ ਢਹਿੰਦੀ ਏ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਦਰਸ਼ਕ ਹੰਝੂ ਨਹੀਂ ਕੇਰਦਾ,  
> ਦੁਨੀਆ ਤਾੜੀਆਂ ਮਾਰ ਕੇ ਬਹਿੰਦੀ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جد ڈوری ٹٹدی ہے ہتھوں،  
> تاں پتلی مٹی چ ڈھہندی اے!  
> کوئی درشک ہنجھو نہیں کیردا،  
> دنیا تاڑیاں مار کے بہندی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Jad dori tutt-di hai hatthon,  
> Taan putli mitti ch dhehndi ae!  
> Koyi darshak hanjhu nahin kerda,  
> Duniya taariyan maar ke behndi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> When the thread snaps from the puppeteer's hand,  
> The wooden figure collapses lifeless in the dust!  
> Not a single spectator sheds a tear of sympathy;  
> The callous audience merely applauds and moves on!  

💡 *Commentary*: Applause at the spectacle of mortal collapse.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਨੇ ਇਸ ਖੇਲ ਦਾ ਭੇਤ ਪਾਇਆ,  
> ਤੇ ਡੋਰੀਆਂ ਆਪ ਹੀ ਕੱਟ ਲਈਆਂ!  
> ਆਪਣੀ ਰੂਹ ਦੀ ਆਜ਼ਾਦੀ ਖ਼ਾਤਰ,  
> ਉਸ ਮੌਤ ਨਾਲ਼ ਸਾਂਝਾਂ ਗੱਠ ਲਈਆਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو نے اس کھیل دا بھیت پایا،  
> تے ڈوریاں آپ ہی کٹ لئیاں!  
> اپنی روح دی آزادی خاطر،  
> اس موت نال سانجھاں گٹھ لئیاں!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv ne iss khel da bhet paaya,  
> Te doriyaan aap hi katt laiyan!  
> Aapni rooh di aazaadi khaatar,  
> Us maut naal saanjhaan gatth laiyan!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv deciphered the cruel secret of this puppet game,  
> And severed the manipulating strings with his own hands!  
> For the sovereign freedom of his unfettered spirit,  
> He forged a lifelong brotherhood with Death itself!  

💡 *Commentary*: Severing the puppet strings to attain absolute spiritual freedom.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਕਾਠ ਦੀ ਪੁਤਲੀ (Kaath Di Putli)** | *Kaath di Put-lee* | Wooden puppet / Marionette | Ancient folk symbol for mortal helplessness under cosmic predestination. |

## 8. Ghamman Da Parona

### **ਗ਼ਮਾਂ ਦਾ ਪ੍ਰਾਹੁਣਾ** • **غماں دا پروہنا**

#### *"The Uninvited Guest of Sorrow"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Guest` `#Hospitality` `#Sorrow` `#Nocturnal` `#Courtyard`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: Sorrow arrives at the door like a revered, uninvited guest at twilight, and the poet serves it with the fine wine of his tears.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala during solitary winter evenings.
- **Archival Context**: Transforms the sacred Punjabi tradition of unconditional hospitality (Pahunchaar) into an intimate banquet with Grief. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਗ਼ਮਾਂ ਦਾ ਪ੍ਰਾਹੁਣਾ ਆਇਆ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਬੂਹਾ ਖੋਲ੍ਹ ਕੇ ਬਹਿ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਨਾ ਉਹ ਉੱਠੇ, ਨਾ ਉਹ ਜਾਵੇ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਦਿਲ ਹੀ ਲੈ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> غماں دا پروہنا آیا اڑیو،  
> بوہا کھول کے بہہ گیا نی!  
> نہ اوہ اٹھے، نہ اوہ جاوے،  
> ساڈا دل ہی لے گیا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Ghamman da parona aaya arhiyo,  
> Booha kholh ke beh gaya ni!  
> Na oh utthe, na oh jaave,  
> Saada dil hi lai gaya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The guest of Sorrow has arrived at our threshold, friends,  
> Unbolting the door and settling firmly inside!  
> He neither arises nor takes his departure;  
> He has taken complete possession of our heart!  

💡 *Commentary*: Grief as the permanent guest occupying the house of life.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਸਾਂ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਦੀ ਚੂਰੀ ਕੁੱਟੀ,  
> ਦਰਦ ਦਾ ਪਾਣੀ ਪਿਲਾਇਆ ਏ!  
> ਇਸ ਅਣ-ਸੱਦੇ ਮਹਿਮਾਨ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਅਸਾਂ ਸੀਨੇ ਨਾਲ਼ ਲਗਾਇਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اساں ہنجھواں دی چوری کٹی،  
> درد دا پانی پلایا اے!  
> اس ان-سدے مہمان نوں،  
> اساں سینے نال لگایا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Asaan hanjuwan di choori kutti,  
> Dard da paani pilaaya ae!  
> Iss an-sadde mehmaan nu,  
> Asaan seene naal lagaaya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> We crumbled a sweet offering fashioned of our tears,  
> Serving him the bitter water of our own agony!  
> To this uninvited, nocturnal guest of destiny,  
> We extended our warmest, most intimate embrace!  

💡 *Commentary*: Treating sorrow with exquisite traditional hospitality.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਇਹ ਪ੍ਰਾਹੁਣਾ ਸਾਡਾ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਉਮਰਾਂ ਤਾਈਂ ਰਹੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਤੱਕ ਦੀਵਾ ਬਲੇਗਾ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਇਹ ਸਾਡੇ ਕੋਲ਼ ਹੀ ਬਹੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو کہندا ایہہ پروہنا ساڈا،  
> ہن عمراں تائیں رہےگا!  
> جد تک دیوا بلےگا گھر وچ،  
> ایہہ ساڈے کول ہی بہےگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv kehnda eh parona saada,  
> Hun umraan taayin rahega!  
> Jad takk deeva balega ghar vich,  
> Eh saade kol hi bahega!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv declares: 'This solemn guest of ours  
> Shall dwell with us through all our remaining lifetimes!  
> As long as the frail clay lamp flickers in this room,  
> He shall sit faithfully right beside our shoulder!'  

💡 *Commentary*: Sorrow as lifelong faithful companion.

## 9. Kore Pind Di Kudi

### **ਕੋਰੇ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੀ ਕੁੜੀ** • **کورے پنڈ دی کڑی**

#### *"The Maiden of the Pristine Village"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#VillageMaiden` `#Innocence` `#Purity` `#Nostalgia` `#Pastoral`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `grassy_hills_sunrise`
- **Synopsis**: A nostalgic tribute to the pure, uncorrupted village maiden whose unaffected smile remains the only holy thing in a cynical world.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Batala after visiting relatives in rural Gurdaspur.
- **Archival Context**: Dedicated to the innocent village girls of Majha who never knew the corrosive vanity of modern urban life. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਉਹ ਕੋਰੇ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੀ ਸਾਦੀ ਕੁੜੀ,  
> ਜਿਸਦੇ ਹਾਸੇ 'ਚ ਫੁੱਲ ਖਿੜਦੇ ਸੀ!  
> ਜਦੋਂ ਲੰਘਦੀ ਸੀ ਖੇਤਾਂ 'ਚੋਂ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਕਣਕਾਂ ਦੇ ਸਿੱਟੇ ਗਿੜਦੇ ਸੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اوہ کورے پنڈ دی سادی کڑی،  
> جس دے ہاسے چ پھل کھڑدے سی!  
> جدوں لنگھدی سی کھیتاں چوں،  
> تاں کنکاں دے سٹے گڑدے سی!  

**Romanized**:
> Oh kore pind di saadi kudi,  
> Jisde haase ch phull khirhde si!  
> Jadon langhdi si khetaan chon,  
> Taan kankan de sitte girhde si!  

**English Translation**:
> She was an innocent maiden of a pristine, untouched village,  
> In whose unforced laughter wild blossoms seemed to bloom!  
> Whenever she walked past the green agrarian fields,  
> The heavy golden ears of wheat bowed down in greeting!  

💡 *Commentary*: Nature recognizing and responding to innocent grace.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਉਹਨੂੰ ਸ਼ਹਿਰੀ ਰੰਗ ਨਾ ਚੜ੍ਹਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਨਾ ਝੂਠੇ ਬੋਲ ਬੋਲਦੀ ਸੀ!  
> ਆਪਣੇ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਪਾਕ ਸੁਪਨਿਆਂ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਉਹ ਮਿੱਠੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ 'ਚ ਘੋਲਦੀ ਸੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اوہنوں شہری رنگ نہ چڑھیا سی،  
> نہ جھوٹھے بول بولدی سی!  
> اپنے دل دے پاک سپنیاں نوں،  
> اوہ مٹھے گیتاں چ گھولدی سی!  

**Romanized**:
> Ohnu shehri rang na charheya si,  
> Na jhoothe bol boldi si!  
> Aapne dil de paak supniyaan nu,  
> Oh mitthe geetan ch gholdi si!  

**English Translation**:
> No artificial urban varnish had ever tarnished her spirit;  
> No calculating falsehood ever passed her lips!  
> The sacred, unblemished dreams of her tender heart  
> She dissolved into sweet, spontaneous folk songs!  

💡 *Commentary*: Purity of agrarian maidenhood contrasted with urban duplicity.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਉਹ ਵੀ ਵਿਆਹੀ ਗਈ ਦੂਰ ਕਿਤੇ,  
> ਪਿੰਡ ਸੁੰਞਾ ਕਰ ਗਈ ਏ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਦਿਲ ਪਰ ਉਸ ਕਲੀ ਲਈ,  
> ਸਦਾ ਬਿਰਹਾ 'ਚ ਭਰ ਗਈ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن اوہ وی ویاہی گئی دور کتے،  
> پنڈ سنجا کر گئی اے!  
> شیو دا دل پر اس کلی لئی،  
> سدا برہا چ بھر گئی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun oh vi vyaahi gayi door kite,  
> Pind sunjna kar gayi ae!  
> Shiv da dil par us kali layi,  
> Sada birha ch bhar gayi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Now she too has been wedded and carried far away,  
> Leaving the ancestral village hollow and deserted!  
> Yet Shiv's heart, for that unplucked wild blossom,  
> Shall remain filled forever with the holy perfume of longing!  

💡 *Commentary*: The inevitable loss of pastoral innocence to marital exile.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਸਿੱਟੇ (Sitte)** | *Sit-te* | Ears of ripening wheat / corn stalks | The supreme agricultural symbol of Punjabi fertility, bounty, and divine blessing. |

## 10. Maut Nu Aawaz

### **ਮੌਤ ਨੂੰ ਆਵਾਜ਼** • **موت نوں آواز**

#### *"Summoning Death into the Cold Night"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Death` `#Summons` `#Cold` `#Winter` `#Deliverance`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: Standing beneath the icy winter stars, the poet calls out to Death to end his unbearable physical and emotional weariness.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Chandigarh during a freezing December night in 1964.
- **Archival Context**: Closes Dardmandaan Dian Aahaan (1964), directly calling for physical death as an end to psychic disintegration. (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਠੰਢੀ ਰਾਤ 'ਚ ਖੜ੍ਹ ਕੇ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਮੌਤ ਨੂੰ ਆਵਾਜ਼ਾਂ ਮਾਰੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਸਾਹਾਂ ਦੀ ਡੋਰੀ ਮੁੱਕ ਚੱਲੀ,  
> ਮੇਰੀਆਂ ਸਭ ਆਸਾਂ ਹਾਰੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں ٹھنڈی رات چ کھڑ کے اڑیو،  
> موت نوں آوازاں ماریاں نی!  
> میرے ساہاں دی ڈوری مک چلی،  
> میریاں سبھ آساں ہاریاں نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Main thanddhi raat ch kharh ke arhiyo,  
> Maut nu aawaazaan maariyaan ni!  
> Mere saahaan di dori mukk challey,  
> Meriyaan sabh aasaan haariyaan ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Standing beneath the freezing winter midnight, friends,  
> I called out with open arms to Death itself!  
> The slender thread of my fluttering breaths has exhausted its span;  
> Every lingering hope of my youth has surrendered to defeat!  

💡 *Commentary*: The active invocation of death in physical winter.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਦਾਰੂ ਕੰਮ ਆਈ ਮੇਰੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਦੁਆ ਨੇ ਤਾਰਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਦਰਦ ਨੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਜਿਊਣਾ ਖੋਹਿਆ,  
> ਉਸਨੇ ਹੀ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਮਾਰਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ کوئی دارو کم آئی میرے،  
> نہ کوئی دعا نے تاریا نی!  
> جس درد نے میرا جیونا کھوہیا،  
> اس نے ہی مینوں ماریا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Na koyi daaru kamm aayi mere,  
> Na koyi dua ne taareya ni!  
> Jis dard ne mera jiyuna khoheya,  
> Usne hi mainu maareya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Not a single pharmaceutical medicine brought relief;  
> Not a single pious prayer could salvage my sinking boat!  
> That very agonizing passion which robbed me of the will to live  
> Has delivered the final, merciful stroke of death!  

💡 *Commentary*: Neither medicine nor prayer can cure metaphysical longing.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਆ ਜਾ ਮੌਤੇ ਗਲ਼ ਲਾ ਲੈ ਮੈਨੂੰ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਦੇਰ ਨਾ ਲਾਈਂ ਤੂੰ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਸਾਂਭ ਲੈ,  
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਅਮਰ ਬਣਾਈਂ ਤੂੰ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> آ جا موتے گل لا لے مینوں،  
> ہن دیر نہ لائیں توں!  
> شیو دے گیتاں نوں سانبھ لے،  
> مینوں امر بنائیں توں!  

**Romanized**:
> Aa jaa maute gal laa lai mainu,  
> Hun der na laayin toon!  
> Shiv de geetan nu saambh lai,  
> Mainu amar banaayin toon!  

**English Translation**:
> Come forth, O gentle Death, and fold me into your embrace;  
> Tarry no longer in the shadows!  
> Take under your eternal guardianship the orphaned verses of Shiv,  
> And grant him everlasting immortality beyond the grave!  

💡 *Commentary*: Death as the custodian who grants immortality to verse.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਦਾਰੂ (Daaru)** | *Daa-roo* | Medicine / Remedy (colloquially also liquor) | Dual meaning in Punjabi poetry: both healing medicine and intoxicating beverage used to forget pain. |

## 11. Peeti Te Kujh Na Keha

### **ਪੀਤੀ ਤੇ ਕੁਝ ਨਾ ਕਿਹਾ** • **پیتی تے کجھ نہ کہیا**

#### *"I Drank the Wine and Uttered No Cry"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#DardmandaanDianAahaan` `#Ghazal` `#Tavern` `#StoicPain`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: The poet drinks the bitter cup of unrequited grief in the tavern without raising a complaint, proving that true suffering is silent.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's nights in the rustic dhabas and taverns of Batala and Chandigarh.
- **Archival Context**: Showcases Shiv's mastery of the classical Punjabi ghazal meter, blending Persian tavern motifs with rural sensibility. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪੀਤੀ ਸ਼ਰਾਬ ਅਸੀਂ ਤੇ ਕੁਝ ਨਾ ਕਿਹਾ,  
> ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਦਾ ਘੁੱਟ ਵੀ ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਪੀ ਲਿਆ!  
> ਜੋ ਜ਼ੁਲਮ ਇਸ ਜਹਾਨ ਨੇ ਸਾਡੇ ਤੇ ਢਾਹਿਆ,  
> ਉਹਨੂੰ ਵੀ ਸੀਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੀ ਲਿਆ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پیتی شراب اسیں تے کجھ نہ کہیا،  
> زہر دا گھٹ وی ہس کے پی لیا!  
> جو ظلم اس جہان نے ساڈے تے ڈھایا،  
> اوہنوں وی سینے وچ سی لیا!  

**Romanized**:
> Peeti sharab aseen te kujh na keha,  
> Zeher da ghutt vi hass ke pee leya!  
> Jo zulm iss jahaan ne saade te dhaaya,  
> Ohnu vi seene vich see leya!  

**English Translation**:
> We drank the bitter tavern wine and uttered not a word of cry,  
> We drained the cup of fatal hemlock with an unyielding smile!  
> Whatever cruelty this merciless universe inflicted upon our head,  
> We stitched it deep into our breast and kept our silence!  

💡 *Commentary*: Stitching the wound (*seene vich see leya*): stoic internal containment of agony.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮਹਿਫ਼ਿਲ 'ਚ ਸਾਰੇ ਹੱਸਦੇ ਰਹੇ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਰੋਣਾ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾ ਡਿੱਠਾ ਵੇ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਦਰਦ ਨੂੰ ਆਪਣਾ ਰਹਿਬਰ ਮੰਨਿਆ,  
> ਜੋ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਵੀ ਮਿੱਠਾ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> محفل چ سارے ہسدے رہے،  
> ساڈا رونا کسے نہ ڈٹھا وے!  
> اسیں درد نوں اپنا رہبر منیا،  
> جو ہنجھواں وچ وی مٹھا وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Mehfil ch saare hasde rahe,  
> Saada rona kise na ditthe ve!  
> Aseen dard nu apna rehbar manneya,  
> Jo hanjhuwan vich vi mittha ve!  

**English Translation**:
> In the bustling tavern gathering the guests laughed in merriment,  
> While not a single eye perceived our inward, silent weeping!  
> We adopted Sorrow as our only spiritual guide upon the path,  
> Finding a holy sweetness even in our falling saltwater tears!  

💡 *Commentary*: Sorrow as spiritual master (*rehbar*): Sufi influence on ghazal poetry.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਹ ਜਾਮ ਵੀ ਖ਼ਾਲੀ ਹੋ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਜਦੋਂ ਰਾਤ ਦਾ ਅੰਤਮ ਪਹਿਰ ਹੋਊ!  
> ਫਿਰ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਨਾਂ ਕੋਈ ਨਹੀਂ ਲਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਜਦ ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਚਾਰੇ ਪਾਸੇ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਹੋਊ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ایہ جام وی خالی ہو جاوے گا،  
> جدوں رات دا انتم پہر ہوؤ!  
> پھر شیو دا ناں کوئی نہیں لوے گا،  
> جد موت دا چارے پاسے شہر ہوؤ!  

**Romanized**:
> Eh jaam vi khaali ho jaavega,  
> Jadon raat da antam pehar ho-oo!  
> Phir Shiv da naan koyi nahin lavega,  
> Jad maut da chaare paase shehar ho-oo!  

**English Translation**:
> This earthen wine goblet too will be drained to the bottom  
> When the final, darkest watch of midnight arrives!  
> And then no voice will utter the name of Shiv in this hall,  
> When the silent city of Death has enclosed us on every side!  

💡 *Commentary*: Classical ghazal maqta (signature line) invoking his own pen name (*Shiv*).

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਜਾਮ (Jaam)** | *Jaam* | Wine goblet / Cup | Classical Persian-Urdu metaphor for the vessel of ecstatic suffering or mortal transience. |

## 12. Shama Bujh Gayi

### **ਸ਼ਮਾ ਬੁੱਝ ਗਈ** • **شمع بجھ گئی**

#### *"The Candle Flickered Out in the Wind"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#DardmandaanDianAahaan` `#Ghazal` `#CandleandMoth` `#Shama`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: The candle that burned through the night flickers and dies as morning arrives; the moth has burnt to ash, and the gathering disperses.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's feeling of exhaustion after all-night mushairas when the crowds dispersed and left him alone in dark halls.
- **Archival Context**: Employs the classical Urdu-Persian *Shama-Parwana* (candle and moth) trope, rooted in Punjabi metric cadence. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਮਾ ਬੁੱਝ ਗਈ ਮਹਿਫ਼ਿਲ ਮੁੱਕੀ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਸਭ ਆਪੋ-ਆਪਣੇ ਘਰ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਬੱਸ ਪਰਵਾਨਾ ਸੜ ਕੇ ਰਾਖ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਜੋ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਦਾਅਵੇ ਕਰ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شمع بجھ گئی محفل مکی،  
> ہن سبھ آپو اپنے گھر گئے نی!  
> بس پروانہ سڑ کے راکھ ہویا،  
> جو دل دے دعوے کر گئے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shama bujh gayi mehfil mukki,  
> Hun sabh aapo-apne ghar gaye ni!  
> Bass parwaana sarh ke raakh hoyea,  
> Jo dil de daawe kar gaye ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The candle has flickered out and the gathering has dispersed,  
> All guests have returned to the shelter of their homes!  
> Only the devoted moth was incinerated to cold grey ash,  
> Having staked its immortal life upon the covenant of love!  

💡 *Commentary*: The classical *parwaana* (moth) sacrificed while spectators return home.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹਨੇਰਾ ਛਾ ਗਿਆ ਚਾਰੇ ਪਾਸੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਰੌਸ਼ਨੀ ਦੀ ਕਿਰਨ ਨਾ ਰਹੀ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਜਿੰਦ ਵੀ ਉਸੇ ਸ਼ਮਾ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਜਿਸ ਵਿੱਚ ਜਿਊਣ ਦੀ ਤਾਂਘ ਨਾ ਰਹੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہنیرا چھا گیا چارے پاسے،  
> کوئی روشنی دی کرن نہ رہی!  
> ساڈی جند وی اسے شمع وانگوں،  
> جس وچ جیون دی تانگھ نہ رہی!  

**Romanized**:
> Hanera chhaa gaya chaare paase,  
> Koyi raushni di kiran na rahi!  
> Saadi jind vi usse shama vaangoo,  
> Jis vich jiyun di taangh na rahi!  

**English Translation**:
> Suffocating darkness has descended upon the empty chamber,  
> Not a single ray of radiant light has survived!  
> Our fragile life was identical to that dying candle,  
> Within whose burnt wick no yearning for survival remained!  

💡 *Commentary*: Extinguished candle (*bujhi shama*) as image of suicidal exhaustion.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਦ ਸੂਰਜ ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਫਿਰ ਚੜ੍ਹੇਗਾ,  
> ਨਵੀਂ ਮਹਿਫ਼ਿਲ ਨਵੇਂ ਲੋਕ ਹੋਣਗੇ!  
> ਪਰ ਸਾਡੇ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਸੜਨ ਵਾਲੇ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਸੁੰਞੀ ਕਬਰ ਦੇ ਸੋਗ ਹੋਣਗੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جد سورج کلھ پھر چڑھے گا،  
> نوی محفل نویں لوک ہون گے!  
> پر ساڈے وانگوں سڑن والے،  
> کسے سنجی قبر دے سوگ ہون گے!  

**Romanized**:
> Jad suraj kallh phir charhega,  
> Naveen mehfil navein lok honge!  
> Par saade vaangoo sarhan vaale,  
> Kise sunji kabr de shok honge!  

**English Translation**:
> When tomorrow's sun rises upon this bustling city,  
> A fresh gathering with new laughing faces will assemble!  
> Yet those who burned to ash like ourselves  
> Will sleep as forgotten mourners within a deserted grave!  

💡 *Commentary*: The indifferent cycle of society replacing the dead artist.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਸ਼ਮਾ (Shama)** | *Sha-ma* | Candle / Flame of beauty | Classical Persian motif of the glowing candle around which the moth (*parwaana*) dances until consumed. |

## 13. Sharab Te Shikwa

### **ਸ਼ਰਾਬ ਤੇ ਸ਼ਿਕਵਾ** • **شراب تے شکوہ**

#### *"Liquor and Lamentation"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#DardmandaanDianAahaan` `#Ghazal` `#Liquor` `#Intoxication`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: The tavern wine fails to numb the agonizing memory of the beloved; the poet curses the bottle that cannot grant oblivion.
- **Genesis**: Shiv famously told Balwant Gargi: 'I drink not for intoxication, but to make the skin of my soul thick enough to endure the day.'
- **Archival Context**: Documents Shiv's complex relationship with alcohol as an anesthetic for his hypersensitive nervous system. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURME`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਰਾਬ ਨੇ ਵੀ ਕੋਈ ਅਸਰ ਨਾ ਕੀਤਾ,  
> ਦਰਦ ਅਜੇ ਵੀ ਜਾਗ ਰਿਹਾ ਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਯਾਦ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਭੁਲਾਉਣਾ ਚਾਹਿਆ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਦਿਲ ਨੂੰ ਡੰਗ ਰਿਹਾ ਏ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شراب نے وی کوئی اثر نہ کیتا،  
> درد اجے وی جاگ رہا اے نی!  
> جس یاد نوں میں بھلاؤنا چاہیا،  
> اوہی دل نوں ڈنگ رہا اے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Sharab ne vi koyi asar na keeta,  
> Dard aje vi jaag reha ae ni!  
> Jis yaad nu main bhulaaona chaaheya,  
> Uhi dil nu dang reha ae ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Even the strongest tavern liquor failed to dull the wound,  
> The sharp agony remains awake and pulsing in my breast!  
> That very memory which I sought to drown in oblivion  
> Stings like a horned viper at the core of my heart!  

💡 *Commentary*: Alcohol failing to numb the acute memory of love.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਬੋਤਲਾਂ ਖ਼ਾਲੀ ਹੋ ਗਈਆਂ ਸਾਰੀਆਂ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਨਸ਼ਾ ਪਰ ਦਰਦ ਦਾ ਏ ਵੇ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਦਵਾ ਨਾ ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਜੋ ਮੇਰੇ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ ਨੂੰ ਭਰਦਾ ਏ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> بوتلاں خالی ہو گئیاں ساریاں،  
> ساڈا نشہ پر درد دا اے وے!  
> کوئی دوا نہ اس دنیا وچ،  
> جو میرے زخم نوں بھردا اے وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Botlaan khaali ho gaiyaan saariyaan,  
> Saada nasha par dard da ae ve!  
> Koyi dawa na iss duniya vich,  
> Jo mere zakham nu bharda ae ve!  

**English Translation**:
> All the dark bottles on the tavern table lie emptied,  
> Yet our true intoxication is the drunkenness of pure grief!  
> There exists no medicinal balm in this mortal universe  
> Capable of sealing the open, bleeding chasm of our wound!  

💡 *Commentary*: Grief itself as the supreme, unyielding intoxicant (*nasha dard da*).

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਤਾਂ ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਜਾਮ ਹੀ ਪੀਣਾ ਏ,  
> ਜੋ ਸਭ ਨਸ਼ਿਆਂ ਤੋਂ ਵੱਡਾ ਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਨੂੰ ਪੀ ਕੇ ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਜਾਗੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਨਸ਼ਾ ਹੀ ਸਾਡਾ ਅੱਡਾ ਏ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن تاں موت دا جام ہی پینا اے،  
> جو سبھ نشیاں توں وڈا اے نی!  
> جس نوں پی کے کوئی نہ جاگے،  
> اوہ نشہ ہی ساڈا اڈا اے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun taan maut da jaam hi peena ae,  
> Jo sabh nashiyaan ton vadda ae ni!  
> Jis nu pee ke koyi na jaage,  
> Oh nasha hi saada adda ae ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Now only the final goblet of Death remains to be drained,  
> An intoxication vaster than all worldly wines combined!  
> Drinking which no slumbering traveler ever awakens,  
> That eternal oblivion is our true and final home!  

💡 *Commentary*: Death as the ultimate and only permanent anesthetic.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਸ਼ਰਾਬ (Sharab)** | *Sha-raab* | Wine / Liquor | Classical ghazal motif representing either mystical ecstasy or self-destructive despair. |

## 14. Zakham Te Marham

### **ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ ਤੇ ਮਰਹਮ** • **زخم تے مرہم**

#### *"Wounds That Spurn All Ointment"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#DardmandaanDianAahaan` `#Ghazal` `#WoundasJewel` `#Marham`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: The poet fiercely protects his wound from doctors and sympathizers, declaring that his grief is his only genuine title to nobility.
- **Genesis**: Shiv rejected well-meaning friends who advised him to marry respectably and stop agonizing over lost loves.
- **Archival Context**: Celebrates the aesthetic pride in one's wound (*zakham*), refusing pity or false healing. (*Citation: `PAASH-1973-SIARH`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੇਰੇ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ ਤੇ ਮਰਹਮ ਨਾ ਲਾਓ,  
> ਇਹ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ ਮੇਰੀ ਪਹਿਚਾਣ ਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸੱਜਣਾਂ ਨੇ ਬਖ਼ਸ਼ਿਆ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਮੇਰੀ ਜਿੰਦ ਤੇ ਜਾਨ ਏ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میرے زخم تے مرہم نہ لاؤ،  
> ایہ زخم میری پہچان اے نی!  
> جو درد مینوں سجناں نے بخشیا،  
> اوہی میری جند تے جان اے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Mere zakham te marham na laao,  
> Eh zakham meri pehchaan ae ni!  
> Jo dard mainu sajjnaan ne bakhshiya,  
> Uhi meri jind te jaan ae ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Do not apply soothing ointments to my bleeding wound,  
> For this very wound is my only true title of nobility!  
> That holy affliction which my beloved bestowed upon me  
> Is the breath, the soul, and the majesty of my life!  

💡 *Commentary*: Refusal of healing: the wound is identity itself.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜੇ ਇਹ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ ਭਰ ਗਿਆ ਤਾਂ,  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਦਾ ਕੀ ਬਣੇਗਾ ਵੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਅੱਗ ਮੇਰੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਲਦੀ,  
> ਉਸ ਬਿਨਾਂ ਕੌਣ ਜੀਵੇਗਾ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جے ایہ زخم بھر گیا تاں،  
> میرے گیتاں دا کی بنے گا وے!  
> جو اگ میرے سینے وچ بلدی،  
> اس بناں کون جیوے گا وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Je eh zakham bhar gaya taan,  
> Mere geetaan da ki banega ve!  
> Jo agg mere seene vich baldi,  
> Uss binaan kaun jeevega ve!  

**English Translation**:
> If ever this gaping wound should knit together and heal,  
> What would become of the incandescent beauty of my songs?  
> That wild fire which rages within my open breast,  
> Deprived of its fuel, what singer could draw breath?  

💡 *Commentary*: Creativity directly dependent upon keeping the injury unhealed.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਇਸ ਦਰਦ ਨੂੰ ਸੀਨੇ ਨਾਲ ਲਾ ਕੇ,  
> ਕਬਰਿਸਤਾਨ ਵੱਲ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੌਤ ਨੂੰ ਆਪਣਾ ਮਰਹਮ ਮੰਨ ਕੇ,  
> ਸਦਾ ਲਈ ਸੌਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗਾ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں اس درد نوں سینے نال لا کے،  
> قبرستان ول جاواں گا نی!  
> موت نوں اپنا مرہم من کے،  
> سدا لئی سون جاواں گا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Main iss dard nu seene naal laa ke,  
> Kabristaan vall jaawaanga ni!  
> Maut nu apna marham mann ke,  
> Sadaa layi saun jaawaanga ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Pressing this open agony against my trembling breast,  
> I shall march proudly towards the gates of the graveyard!  
> Accepting only Death as my true and final ointment,  
> I shall lay down to sleep in peace forevermore!  

💡 *Commentary*: Death as the only authentic ointment (*marham*).

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਮਰਹਮ (Marham)** | *Mar-ham* | Healing salve / Medicinal ointment | Arabic-Persian loan word for medicinal balm applied to sword or arrow wounds. |

## 15. Pyas Na Bujhi

### **ਪਿਆਸ ਨਾ ਬੁੱਝੀ** • **پیاس نہ بجھی**

#### *"The Thirst That No Wine Could Sate"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#DardmandaanDianAahaan` `#Ghazal` `#InsatiableThirst` `#Desert`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `tavern_midnight`
- **Synopsis**: Rivers, oceans, and taverns dry up before the poet's insatiable spiritual thirst, which can only be quenched by the dust of the grave.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's metaphysical thirst for absolute love in a world of compromises.
- **Archival Context**: Philosophical climax of Shiv's ghazals, expressing the insatiability of the romantic soul. (*Citation: `SEKHON-1972-PUNJABI-KAVI`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਦਰਿਆ ਵੀ ਪੀ ਲਏ ਅਸੀਂ,  
> ਪਰ ਪਿਆਸ ਨਾ ਬੁੱਝੀ ਦਿਲ ਦੀ ਨੀ!  
> ਸਮੁੰਦਰ ਵੀ ਸੁੱਕ ਗਏ ਸਾਹਮਣੇ ਸਾਡੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਛਾਂ ਨਾ ਮਿਲੀ ਮਹਿਲ ਦੀ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> دریا وی پی لئے اسیں،  
> پر پیاس نہ بجھی دل دی نی!  
> سمندر وی سک گئے ساہمنے ساڈے،  
> کوئی چھاں نہ ملی محل دی نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Dariya vi pee laye aseen,  
> Par pyaas na bujhi dil di ni!  
> Samundar vi sukk gaye saahmne saade,  
> Koyi chhaan na mili mehel di ni!  

**English Translation**:
> We drained entire rushing rivers to their dry bed,  
> Yet the burning thirst of our heart could not be quenched!  
> Mighty oceans dried to dust before our parched lips,  
> Nor did the marble palaces of this world grant us shade!  

💡 *Commentary*: Hyperbolic thirst (*pyaas*): the soul's infinite longing vs. finite world.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਰਾਬ ਵੀ ਪਾਣੀ ਬਣ ਕੇ ਰਹਿ ਗਈ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਸ਼ਾ ਨਾ ਸਾਡੇ ਕੰਮ ਆਇਆ ਵੇ!  
> ਇਸ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੀ ਤਪਦੀ ਧੁੱਪ ਨੇ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਅੰਦਰ-ਬਾਹਰ ਝੁਲਸਾਇਆ ਵੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شراب وی پانی بن کے رہ گئی،  
> کوئی نشہ نہ ساڈے کم آیا وے!  
> اس برہا دی تپدی دھپ نے،  
> ساڈا اندر باہر جھلسایا وے!  

**Romanized**:
> Sharab vi paani ban ke reh gayi,  
> Koyi nasha na saade kamm aaya ve!  
> Iss birha di tapdi dhup ne,  
> Saada andar-baahar jhulsaaya ve!  

**English Translation**:
> The strongest wine turned to common water in our glass,  
> Not a single earthly intoxicant could soothe our ache!  
> The blistering midday sun of this cosmic separation  
> Has scorched our inner spirit and outward flesh to cinder!  

💡 *Commentary*: Failure of all earthly intoxicants to dull the thirst.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਕਬਰ ਦੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਹੀ ਪਿਆਸ ਬੁਝਾਊ,  
> ਜਦੋਂ ਸਾਡੇ ਮੂੰਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਵੇਗੀ ਨੀ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੀ ਇਸ ਅਮਰ ਪਿਆਸ ਦੀ ਬਾਤ,  
> ਇਹ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਸਦਾ ਕਹੇਗੀ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن قبر دی مٹی ہی پیاس بجھاؤ،  
> جدوں ساڈے منہ وچ پوے گی نی!  
> شیو دی اس امر پیاس دی بات،  
> ایہ دنیا سدا کہے گی نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun kabr di mitti hi pyaas bujhaa-oo,  
> Jadon saade moonh vich pavegi ni!  
> Shiv di iss amar pyaas di baat,  
> Eh duniya sadaa kahegi ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Only the cool earth of the grave shall finally quench this thirst  
> When its quiet dust is placed upon our silent lips!  
> And the legend of Shiv's immortal, unslaked longing  
> Shall be repeated by this weeping world forevermore!  

💡 *Commentary*: Earth in the mouth (*kabr di mitti*) as the only quencher of existential thirst.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਪਿਆਸ (Pyaas)** | *Pyaas* | Thirst / Spiritual yearning | Central concept in Punjabi mysticism denoting the soul's unquenchable craving for reunion with the infinite. |
