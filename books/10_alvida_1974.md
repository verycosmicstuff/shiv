# Alvida (1974)

## ਅਲਵਿਦਾ • الوداع
### *"The Final Farewell (Posthumous Anthology)"*

---

## 📚 Anthology Overview

- **Author**: Shiv Kumar Batalvi (ਸਿ਼ਵ ਕੁਮਾਰ ਬਟਾਲਵੀ / شیو کمار بٹالوی)
- **Publication Year**: 1974
- **Original Publisher**: Navyug Publishers, Delhi (Posthumous)
- **Total Master Poems in this Edition**: 17

### Literary & Historical Background
> Published posthumously following Shiv's death on May 6, 1973 at age 36. Brings together his final verses written during his 1972 England tour, hospital notebooks, and terminal reflections on exile and return.

**Critical Reception**: Serves as the poignant epitaph to Punjab's most incandescent twentieth-century lyrical voice.

## 1. Alvida (Aakhri Geet)

### **ਅਲਵਿਦਾ (ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਗੀਤ)** • **الوداع (آخری گیت)**

#### *"Farewell: The Ultimate Departure"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Farewell` `#Posthumous` `#Epitaph` `#Masterpiece`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The definitive posthumous title poem where the poet settles his earthly debt to Punjab, releasing his songs to the wind and bidding a final goodbye.
- **Genesis**: Found among the papers in his father-in-law's house in Kir Mangyal after Shiv passed away on May 6, 1973 at age 36.
- **Archival Context**: Included in the canonical Punjabi collection 'Alvida' (1974). (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: The definitive epitaph of Shiv Kumar Batalvi's life and art.
- **Musical Recitation**: Whispered, serene, transcendent melody.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਮੇਰੇ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦਿਓ ਲੋਕੋ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਜਾਣ ਦਾ ਵੇਲਾ ਆ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਦੀਵਾ ਬਾਲ਼ਿਆ ਸੀ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ,  
> ਉਹ ਬੁਝਣ ਕਿਨਾਰੇ ਆ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> الوداع میرے پنجاب دیو لوگو،  
> میرا جان دا ویلا آ گیا اے!  
> جو دیوا بالیا سی برہا دا،  
> اوہ بجھن کنارے آ گیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Alvida mere Punjab diyo loko,  
> Mera jaan da vela aa gaya ae!  
> Jo deeva baaleya si birha da,  
> Oh bujhan kinaare aa gaya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Farewell, O people of my beloved Punjab,  
> The hour of my final departure has arrived!  
> That earthen lamp of separation which I illuminated  
> Has reached the very threshold of being extinguished!  

💡 *Commentary*: The final address to the people of Punjab.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਛੱਤੀ ਵਰ੍ਹੇ ਹੀ ਜੀਵਿਆ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਪਰ ਸਦੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਦਰਦ ਹੰਢਾਇਆ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾ ਕਹਿਣਾ ਚਾਹਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਮੈਂ ਗੀਤਾਂ 'ਚ ਗਾਇਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں چھتی ورھے ہی جیویا ہاں،  
> پر صدیاں دا درد ہنڈھایا اے!  
> جو کسے نہ کہنا چاہیا سی،  
> اوہ میں گیتاں چ گایا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Main chhatti varhe hi jeeveya haan,  
> Par sadiyaan da dard handaaya ae!  
> Jo kise na kehna chaaheya si,  
> Oh main geetan ch gaaya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> I lived merely thirty-six brief mortal years,  
> Yet I endured and exhausted centuries of collective agony!  
> That which no mortal soul dared to whisper aloud,  
> I articulated boldly within the living body of my songs!  

💡 *Commentary*: The 36 years encapsulating centuries of Punjabi historical trauma.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਰੋਇਓ ਮੇਰੀ ਕਬਰ ਉੱਤੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਫੁੱਲ ਚੜ੍ਹਾਇਓ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਦ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ ਕੋਈ ਪੰਛੀ ਕੂਕੇ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਮੇਰਾ ਗੀਤ ਸੁਣਾਇਓ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ رویو میری قبر اتے،  
> نہ کوئی پھل چڑھایو نی!  
> جد برہا دا کوئی پنچھی کوکے،  
> تاں میرا گیت سنایو نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Na royeyo meri qabar utte,  
> Na koyi phull charhaayeo ni!  
> Jad birha da koyi panchhi kooke,  
> Taan mera geet sunaayeo ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Weep not over my quiet earthen grave,  
> Nor lay cut flowers upon the mound!  
> Whenever the wild bird of separation calls across the twilight,  
> Sing my living verses aloud in communion!  

💡 *Commentary*: Rejecting funerary rituals in favor of living poetic recitation.

##### Stanza 4

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਹਵਾ 'ਚ ਘੁਲ਼ ਗਿਆ ਏ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਹਰ ਇੱਕ ਸਾਹ 'ਚ ਵੱਸਾਂਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਪੰਜਾਬ 'ਚ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਰੋਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਹੰਝੂ ਬਣ ਕੇ ਵਰ੍ਹਾਂਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو تاں ہوا چ گھل گیا اے،  
> میں ہر اک ساہ چ وساں گا!  
> جد پنجاب چ عشق رویگا،  
> میں ہنجھو بن کے ورھانگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv taan hawa ch ghull gaya ae,  
> Main har ikk saah ch vasaanga!  
> Jad Punjab ch ishq rovega,  
> Main hanjhu ban ke varhaanga!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv has dissolved forever into the ambient air of Punjab;  
> I shall dwell inside every breath drawn by future generations!  
> Whenever true love weeps anywhere in this land,  
> I shall descend as molten tears from the sky!  

💡 *Commentary*: Immortal omnipresence: the poet becomes the very tears of Punjab.

## 2. Chup Di Cheekh

### **ਚੁੱਪ ਦੀ ਚੀਕ** • **چپ دی چیک**

#### *"The Screaming Silence"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Silence` `#Scream` `#Mystery`
- **Theme / Biome**: `modernism` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: When language is exhausted and grief exceeds the capacity of words, silence becomes a deafening scream.
- **Genesis**: Posthumously recovered from his final notebook in Kir Mangyal.
- **Archival Context**: Included in the canonical Punjabi collection 'Alvida' (1974). (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Reaches the outer limits of linguistic expression.
- **Musical Recitation**: Whispered, stark, haunting tone.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੇਰੀ ਚੁੱਪ ਦੇ ਅੰਦਰ ਇੱਕ ਚੀਕ ਏ,  
> ਜੋ ਸਾਰੀ ਰਾਤ ਪੁਕਾਰਦੀ ਏ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਕੰਨ ਨਹੀਂ ਜੋ ਸੁਣ ਸਕੇ,  
> ਇਹ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਅੰਦਰੋਂ ਮਾਰਦੀ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میری چپ دے اندر اک چیک اے،  
> جو ساری رات پکاردی اے!  
> کوئی کن نہیں جو سن سکے،  
> ایہہ مینوں اندروں ماردی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Meri chup de andar ikk cheek ae,  
> Jo saari raat pukaardi ae!  
> Koyi kann nahin jo sun sake,  
> Eh mainu andron maardi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Inside my outward silence dwells a piercing scream  
> That shrieks without ceasing through the dead of night!  
> There are no human ears capable of hearing its sound;  
> It murders me silently from within!  

💡 *Commentary*: The sound of unutterable trauma.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਬਦ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਏ, ਗੀਤ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਏ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਸੁੰਨ ਪਸਰੀ ਏ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਦਰਦ ਨੂੰ ਮੈਂ ਗਾਉਂਦਾ ਸਾਂ,  
> ਉਹ ਹੁਣ ਹੱਡਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਠਰੀ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شبداں مک گئے، گیت مک گئے،  
> ہن صرف سن پسری اے!  
> جس درد نوں میں گاؤندا ساں،  
> اوہ ہن ہڈاں وچ ٹھری اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Shabad mukk gaye, geet mukk gaye,  
> Hun sirf sunn pasri ae!  
> Jis dard nu main gaaunda saan,  
> Oh hun haddaan vich thari ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Words have been exhausted, songs have reached their end;  
> Now only an absolute void blankets existence!  
> That vibrant agony which I once sang with passion  
> Has frozen into solid ice inside my bones!  

💡 *Commentary*: Transition from fiery lyricism to frozen silence.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਦ ਚੁੱਪ ਵੀ ਚੀਕਣ ਲੱਗ ਪਵੇ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਸਮਝੋ ਅੰਤ ਨੇੜੇ ਏ!  
> ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਦੇ ਪੁਆੜਿਆਂ 'ਚੋਂ,  
> ਮੁੱਕਣ ਵਾਲ਼ੇ ਸਭ ਝੇੜੇ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جد چپ وی چیکن لگ پوے،  
> تاں سمجھو انت نیڑے اے!  
> اس دنیا دے پواڑیاں چوں،  
> مکن والے سبھ جھیڑے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Jad chup vi cheekan lag pave,  
> Taan samjho ant nehre ae!  
> Is duniya de puaareyaan chon,  
> Mukkan waale sabh jherhe ne!  

**English Translation**:
> When silence itself begins to scream,  
> Know with certainty that the end is near!  
> Out of the exhausting entanglements of this world,  
> All futile disputes are on the verge of concluding!  

💡 *Commentary*: Silence signaling impending death.

##### Stanza 4

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਹੁਣ ਚੁੱਪ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ ਏ,  
> ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਮੁੜ ਕੇ ਬੋਲੇਗਾ!  
> ਜੋ ਭੇਤ ਸੀ ਉਸਦੇ ਸੀਨੇ 'ਚ,  
> ਕਦੇ ਜੱਗ ਅੱਗੇ ਨਾ ਖੋਲ੍ਹੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو تاں ہن چپ ہو گیا اے،  
> کدے نہ مڑ کے بولیگا!  
> جو بھیت سی اس دے سینے چ،  
> کدے جگ اگے نہ کھولیگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv taan hun chup ho gaya ae,  
> Kade na murh ke bolega!  
> Jo bhet si usde seene ch,  
> Kade jagg agge na kholega!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv has now fallen completely silent;  
> He shall never speak aloud in this mortal realm again!  
> That final, sacred mystery locked inside his chest  
> He shall never reveal to the eyes of this world!  

💡 *Commentary*: The final sealed secret of the poet.

## 3. Main Adhoora Geet Haan

### **ਮੈਂ ਅਧੂਰਾ ਗੀਤ ਹਾਂ** • **میں ادھورا گیت ہاں**

#### *"I Am an Unfinished Melody"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Unfinished` `#Fragment` `#Elegiac`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: Accepting that early death has left his life, his loves, and his poetic mission broken as an uncompleted fragment.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Kir Mangyal shortly before passing away at 36.
- **Archival Context**: Included in the canonical Punjabi collection 'Alvida' (1974). (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Autobiographical self-definition as an eternal fragment.
- **Musical Recitation**: Broken, sighing, unresolved musical phrasing.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਅਧੂਰੇ ਗੀਤ ਦੀ ਇੱਕ ਸਤਰ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਜੋ ਗਾਈ ਨਾ ਗਈ ਪੂਰੀ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਹੋਠਾਂ 'ਤੇ ਹੀ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਈ,  
> ਇਹ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਦੀ ਦੂਰੀ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں ادھورے گیت دی اک سطر ہاں،  
> جو گائی نہ گئی پوری نی!  
> میرے ہوٹھاں تے ہی مک گئی،  
> ایہہ زندگی دی دوری نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Main adhoore geet di ikk satar haan,  
> Jo gaayi na gayi poori ni!  
> Mere hothaan te hi mukk gayi,  
> Eh zindagi di doori ni!  

**English Translation**:
> I am merely a single line of an unfinished melody  
> Which was never permitted to be sung to completion!  
> Upon my very lips it halted and dissolved;  
> Such was the tragic distance of my existence!  

💡 *Commentary*: Life as an abruptly truncated verse.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਿੰਨੇ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਅਧੂਰੇ ਰਹਿ ਗਏ,  
> ਕਿੰਨੇ ਗੀਤ ਅਣਗਾਏ ਮਰ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਰਾਜ਼ ਮੈਂ ਖੋਲ੍ਹਣੇ ਸੀ ਜੱਗ 'ਤੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਕਬਰਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੜ ਗਏ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کنے سپنے ادھورے رہ گئے،  
> کنے گیت انگائے مر گئے نی!  
> جو راز میں کھولنے سی جگ تے،  
> اوہ قبراں دے وچ گڑ گئے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Kinne supne adhoore reh gaye,  
> Kinne geet an-gaaye mar gaye ni!  
> Jo raaz main kholhne si jagg te,  
> Oh qabraan de vich garh gaye ni!  

**English Translation**:
> How many dreams remained broken and half-realized;  
> How many melodies perished before they could be sung!  
> Those profound mysteries I intended to reveal to the world  
> Have been swallowed into the darkness of the grave!  

💡 *Commentary*: The unwritten library buried in the poet's mind.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਮੇਰਾ ਕੋਈ ਅੰਤ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਸ਼ੁਰੂਆਤ ਸੀ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਤਾਂ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੀ ਇੱਕ ਲੰਮੀ,  
> ਬੇ-ਚਿਰਾਗ਼ੀ ਰਾਤ ਸੀ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ میرا کوئی انت ہویا،  
> نہ کوئی شروعات سی نی!  
> میں تاں برہا دی اک لمی،  
> بے-چراغی رات سی نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Na mera koyi ant hoya,  
> Na koyi shuru-aat si ni!  
> Main taan birha di ikk lammi,  
> Be-chiraaghi raat si ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Neither did my journey find a rightful conclusion,  
> Nor was there any gentle beginning to my days!  
> I was merely an interminable, endless night  
> Bereft of a single lamp of separation!  

💡 *Commentary*: Be-chiraaghi (lampless): utter lack of illumination.

##### Stanza 4

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਅਧੂਰਾ ਹੀ ਤੁਰ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਪਰ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਇਸਨੂੰ ਪੂਰਾ ਕਰੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਕੋਈ ਆਸ਼ਿਕ ਦਰਦ 'ਚ ਰੋਊਗਾ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਭਰੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو تاں ادھورا ہی تر گیا،  
> پر پنجاب اس نوں پورا کریگا!  
> جد کوئی عاشق درد چ روؤگا،  
> تاں میرے گیتاں نوں بھریگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv taan adhoora hi tur gaya,  
> Par Punjab isnu poora karega!  
> Jad koyi aashiq dard ch rouga,  
> Taan mere geetan nu bharega!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv departed this earth incomplete and fragmented,  
> Yet the people of Punjab shall complete his verse!  
> Whenever any lover weeps in genuine agony,  
> Their living tears shall fill the unfinished spaces of my song!  

💡 *Commentary*: The future readers of Punjab will complete the poem with their own lives.

## 4. Qabar Te Deeva

### **ਕਬਰ 'ਤੇ ਦੀਵਾ** • **قبر تے دیوا**

#### *"An Earthen Lamp Upon My Tomb"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Tomb` `#EarthenLamp` `#Wanderer` `#Solitude`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: Asking who will remember to light an earthen lamp upon the lonely grave of the wanderer once the funeral crowd disperses.
- **Genesis**: Composed with a premonition of his lonely final days in Kir Mangyal.
- **Archival Context**: Included in the canonical Punjabi collection 'Alvida' (1974). (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Classic Sufi 'Mazaar' motif transposed into modernist solitude.
- **Musical Recitation**: Flickering, quiet, nocturnal melody.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੇਰੀ ਕਬਰ 'ਤੇ ਦੀਵਾ ਬਾਲ਼ਣ ਵਾਲ਼ਾ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਆਪਣਾ ਨਾ ਲੱਭਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਦੇ ਮੇਲੇ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਦਰਦ ਹੀ ਦੱਬਿਆ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میری قبر تے دیوا بالن والا،  
> کوئی اپنا نہ لبھیا نی!  
> اس دنیا دے میلے اندر،  
> ساڈا درد ہی دبیا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Meri qabar te deeva baalan waala,  
> Koyi apna na labbheya ni!  
> Is duniya de mele andar,  
> Saada dard hi dabbeya ni!  

**English Translation**:
> To light a single earthen lamp upon my lonely grave,  
> Not a single beloved soul was found!  
> Inside this bustling, indifferent carnival of the world,  
> Our profound agony was buried beneath the dust!  

💡 *Commentary*: The abandoned grave in twilight.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹਵਾਵਾਂ ਆਣ ਕੇ ਬੁਝਾ ਜਾਂਦੀਆਂ ਨੇ,  
> ਜੇ ਕੋਈ ਬਾਲ਼ਦਾ ਵੀ ਏ ਦੀਵਾ!  
> ਇੱਥੇ ਪੱਥਰਾਂ ਦੀ ਨਗਰੀ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਕੌਣ ਕਰੇ ਕਿਸੇ ਦਾ ਸੀਵਾ?  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہواواں آن کے بجھا جاندیاں نے،  
> جے کوئی بالدا وی اے دیوا!  
> ایتھے پتھراں دی نگری اندر،  
> کون کرے کسے دا سیوا؟  

**Romanized**:
> Havaavan aan ke bujhaa jaandiyan ne,  
> Je koyi baalda vi ae deeva!  
> Itthe pattharan di nagri andar,  
> Kaun kare kise da seeva?  

**English Translation**:
> The harsh night winds swoop down and snuff the flame  
> If ever any kind passerby lights a lamp!  
> Inside this stone-hearted metropolis of callousness,  
> Who cares to mend another's shattered spirit?  

💡 *Commentary*: Wind snuffing the flame: nature collaborating with oblivion.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਨੂੰ ਦੀਵੇ ਦੀ ਲੋੜ ਨਹੀਂ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤ ਹੀ ਮੇਰੀ ਲੋਅ ਨੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਬਲ਼ਦੇ ਰਹਿਣਗੇ ਯੁੱਗਾਂ ਤਾਈਂ,  
> ਉਹ ਮੇਰੀ ਅਮਰ ਛੋਹ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> مینوں دیوے دی لوڑ نہیں اڑیو،  
> میرے گیت ہی میری لوء نے!  
> جو بلدے رہنگے جگاں تائیں،  
> اوہ میری امر چھوہ نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Mainu deeve di lorh nahin arhiyo,  
> Mere geet hi meri loh ne!  
> Jo balde rehange yuggaan taayin,  
> Oh meri amar chhoh ne!  

**English Translation**:
> I possess no need for an earthen lamp, companions—  
> My own burning verses are my eternal flame!  
> Which shall blaze undiminished across eons and ages,  
> Standing as the immortal touch of my soul!  

💡 *Commentary*: Poetry as self-sustaining celestial light.

##### Stanza 4

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੀ ਕਬਰ 'ਤੇ ਘਾਹ ਉੱਗ ਆਵੇ,  
> ਪਰ ਨਾਮ ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਮਿਟੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਦ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਬੋਲੀ ਬੋਲੀ ਜਾਊਗੀ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਸਾਡਾ ਚਾਨਣ ਦਿਖੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو دی قبر تے گھاہ اگ آوے،  
> پر نام کدے نہ مٹیگا!  
> جد پنجابی بولی بولی جاؤگی،  
> تاں ساڈا چانن دکھیگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv di qabar te ghaah ugg aave,  
> Par naam kade na mitega!  
> Jad Punjabi boli boli jaaugi,  
> Taan saada chaanan dikhega!  

**English Translation**:
> Let wild grass sprout and cover Shiv's physical tomb,  
> Yet his name shall never be erased from time!  
> As long as the Punjabi language is spoken upon this earth,  
> His radiant light shall illuminate human hearts!  

💡 *Commentary*: Indestructible link with the survival of the Punjabi mother tongue.

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Deeva (ਦੀਵਾ)** | *Dee-vaa* | Earthen oil lamp | Placed on tombs on Thursday evenings (Jumeraat) in Punjabi folk tradition. |

## 5. Hanjhu Muk Gaye

### **ਹੰਝੂ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਏ** • **ہنجھو مک گئے**

#### *"The Tears Have Run Dry"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#DryTears` `#Terminal` `#Resignation`
- **Theme / Biome**: `birha` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: When terminal illness reaches its climax, even tears dry up, leaving a dry, stoic landscape where the poet simply waits for release.
- **Genesis**: Composed in early 1973 in Kir Mangyal during his final weeks.
- **Archival Context**: Included in the canonical Punjabi collection 'Alvida' (1974). (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: The cessation of weeping as the final stage of grief.
- **Musical Recitation**: Parched, dry, whispered tone.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅੱਖੀਆਂ 'ਚੋਂ ਹੰਝੂ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਏ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਰੋਇਆ ਵੀ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਾਂਦਾ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਪਥਰਾ ਗਿਆ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਉਹ ਕਿਸੇ ਨੂੰ ਦੱਸਿਆ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਾਂਦਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اکھیاں چوں ہنجھو مک گئے اڑیو،  
> ہن رویا وی نہیں جاندا!  
> جو درد پتھرا گیا اندر،  
> اوہ کسے نوں دسیا نہیں جاندا!  

**Romanized**:
> Akkhiyaan chon hanjhu mukk gaye arhiyo,  
> Hun royea vi nahin jaanda!  
> Jo dard pathraa gaya andar,  
> Oh kise nu dasseya nahin jaanda!  

**English Translation**:
> The tears have run completely dry within my eyes, companions;  
> Now I can no longer even weep!  
> That agony which has turned to petrified stone within  
> Can no longer be expressed to any mortal soul!  

💡 *Commentary*: Petrification (pathraana) of grief: tears dry into stone.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਦਰਿਆ ਸੁੱਕ ਗਿਆ ਏ,  
> ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਰੇਤ ਹੀ ਰੇਤ ਪਸਰੀ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਕਦੇ ਵਰ੍ਹਦਾ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹਦੀ ਬੂੰਦ ਬੂੰਦ ਹੁਣ ਮਰੀ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> دل دا دریا سک گیا اے،  
> صرف ریت ہی ریت پسری اے!  
> جو برہا کدے ورھدا سی،  
> اوہدی بوند بوند ہن مری اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Dil da dariya sukk gaya ae,  
> Sirf ret hi ret pasri ae!  
> Jo birha kade varhda si,  
> Ohdi boond boond hun mari ae!  

**English Translation**:
> The rushing river of the heart has dried to bone;  
> Only barren, blinding sand stretches across the bed!  
> That separation which once rained in torrential monsoons  
> Has had its every living drop extinguished into dust!  

💡 *Commentary*: The dried riverbed as the final landscape of terminal grief.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਤਾਂਘ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਆਸ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਮਿਲਣ ਦਾ ਚਾਅ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਤਾਂ ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਉਡੀਕਦਾ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਕਦ ਮੁੱਕੇਗਾ ਇਹ ਸਾਹ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن نہ کوئی تانگھ نہ کوئی آس،  
> نہ کوئی ملن دا چاء نی!  
> میں تاں صرف اڈیکدا ہاں،  
> کد مکیگا ایہہ ساہ نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun na koyi taangh na koyi aas,  
> Na koyi milan da chaa ni!  
> Main taan sirf udeekda haan,  
> Kad mukkega eh saah ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Now no yearning remains, nor any lingering hope;  
> Not a trace of desire for reunion stirs within!  
> I merely sit in silent patience waiting  
> For when this final, labored breath will cease!  

💡 *Commentary*: Total detachment and Buddhist-like peace before dying.

##### Stanza 4

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਪੱਥਰ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ ਏ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਕੋਈ ਤੀਰ ਨਾ ਚੁੱਭੇਗਾ!  
> ਜਿਸਨੇ ਸਾਰੀ ਉਮਰ ਹੰਝੂ ਪੀਤੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਹੁਣ ਮੌਤ 'ਚ ਹੀ ਡੁੱਬੇਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو تاں پتھر ہو گیا اے،  
> ہن کوئی تیر نہ چبھیگا!  
> جس نے ساری عمر ہنجھو پیئے،  
> اوہ ہن موت چ ہی ڈبیگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv taan patthar ho gaya ae,  
> Hun koyi teer na chubhega!  
> Jisne saari umar hanjhu peete,  
> Oh hun maut ch hi dubbega!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv has turned into invulnerable, unfeeling stone;  
> No arrow of earthly cruelty can pierce him now!  
> He who drank tears as his daily nourishment throughout life  
> Shall now submerge serenely into the ocean of death!  

💡 *Commentary*: Immunity achieved through total petrification.

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Pathraana (ਪਥਰਾਣਾ)** | *Path-raa-naa* | To turn to stone | State where the human capacity to weep is exhausted. |

## 6. Antim Suneha

### **ਅੰਤਿਮ ਸੁਨੇਹਾ** • **انتم سنیہا**

#### *"The Final Testament to Punjab"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Testament` `#Blessing` `#Peace` `#Reconciliation`
- **Theme / Biome**: `folklore` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: Shiv blesses both his passionate adorers and his ideological detractors with peace, releasing all grudges before closing his eyes forever.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's final dictated thoughts to his wife Aruna and close friends in Kir Mangyal.
- **Archival Context**: Included in the canonical Punjabi collection 'Alvida' (1974). (*Citation: `LAHORE-BOOKSHOP-1974`*)
- **Critical Debate**: Transcendent closure to Punjab's greatest modernist life.
- **Musical Recitation**: Solemn, benedictory, peace-filled cadence.

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਾਂਦੇ ਹੋਏ ਮੈਂ ਸਭ ਨੂੰ ਅਸੀਸ ਦੇਵਾਂ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਵੈਰੀ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਬੇਗ਼ਾਨਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਨੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਪਿਆਰਿਆ,  
> ਉਹਨਾਂ ਦਾ ਦਿਲ ਸ਼ਾਦਾਨਾ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جاندے ہوئے میں سبھ نوں اسیس دیواں،  
> کوئی ویری نہ کوئی بیگانہ نی!  
> جنہاں نے میرے گیتاں نوں پیاریا،  
> اوہناں دا دل شادانہ نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Jaande hoye main sabh nu asees devaan,  
> Koyi vairi na koyi begaana ni!  
> Jinhaan ne mere geetan nu pyaareya,  
> Ohnaan da dil shaadaana ni!  

**English Translation**:
> As I depart, I shower my unconditional blessing upon all humanity;  
> To me, there is no enemy, nor any stranger on this earth!  
> Those faithful souls who cherished and loved my songs,  
> May their hearts remain forever joyful and blessed!  

💡 *Commentary*: Universal benediction stripping away all enmity.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜਿਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਨੇ ਮੇਰੇ 'ਤੇ ਪੱਥਰ ਸੁੱਟੇ,  
> ਉਹਨਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਵੀ ਖਿਮਾ ਕਰਾਂ ਮੈਂ!  
> ਇਸ ਛੋਟੀ ਜਿਹੀ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਦਾ ਦਿਲ ਕਿਉਂ ਦੁਖਾਵਾਂ ਮੈਂ?  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جنہاں نے میرے تے پتھر سٹے،  
> اوہناں نوں وی کھما کراں میں!  
> اس چھوٹی جیہی زندگی اندر،  
> کسے دا دل کیوں دکھاواں میں؟  

**Romanized**:
> Jinhaan ne mere te patthar sutte,  
> Ohnaan nu vi khima karaan main!  
> Is chhoti jihi zindagi andar,  
> Kise da dil kyon dukhaavaan main?  

**English Translation**:
> Those critics who cast sharp stones at my name,  
> To them too I grant wholehearted forgiveness!  
> Inside this brief, fragile flicker of mortal existence,  
> Why should I cause sorrow to any human heart?  

💡 *Commentary*: Forgiveness of his Marxist detractors (Sekhon, Kishan Singh).

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੇਰੇ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਨੂੰ ਸਦਾ ਹੱਸਦਾ ਰੱਖਿਓ,  
> ਇੱਥੇ ਪਿਆਰ ਦੇ ਗੀਤ ਗਾਉਂਦੇ ਰਹਿਣਾ!  
> ਜੋ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਮੈਂ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਕਾਗ਼ਜ਼ਾਂ 'ਤੇ,  
> ਉਸਨੂੰ ਦਿਲਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਵਸਾਉਂਦੇ ਰਹਿਣਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میرے پنجاب نوں سدا ہسدا رکھیو،  
> ایتھے پیار دے گیت گاؤندے رہنا!  
> جو برہا میں لکھیا کاغذاں تے،  
> اس نوں دلاں وچ وساؤندے رہنا!  

**Romanized**:
> Mere Punjab nu sada hassda rakheyo,  
> Itthe pyaar de geet gaaunde rehna!  
> Jo birha main likheya kaagzaan te,  
> Usnu dilaan vich vasaaunde rehna!  

**English Translation**:
> Keep my beloved Punjab laughing and flourishing forever;  
> Continue to sing songs of sublime devotion across its fields!  
> That sacred separation which I inscribed upon fragile paper,  
> Keep it housed with tenderness inside your living hearts!  

💡 *Commentary*: Final charge to Punjab: nurture love and cherish the sacred sorrow of poetry.

##### Stanza 4

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਹੁਣ ਚੱਲਿਆ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦੀ ਗੋਦ 'ਚ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਸਦਾ ਲਈ ਚੁੱਪ ਹੋ ਜਾਊਗਾ!  
> ਪਰ ਜਦ ਵੀ ਕੋਈ ਬਿਰਹੋਂ ਰੋਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਫ਼ੇਰ ਜਿਊਂਦਾ ਹੋ ਜਾਊਗਾ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شیو ہن چلیا مٹی دی گود چ،  
> جتھے سدا لئی چپ ہو جاؤگا!  
> پر جد وی کوئی برہوں رویگا،  
> تاں شیو فیر جیوندا ہو جاؤگا!  

**Romanized**:
> Shiv hun challeya mitti di god ch,  
> Jithe sada layi chup ho jaauga!  
> Par jad vi koyi birhon rovega,  
> Taan Shiv fer jiyunda ho jaauga!  

**English Translation**:
> Shiv now rests his head into the maternal lap of earth,  
> Where he shall slumber in eternal silence!  
> Yet whenever any heart anywhere weeps in true separation,

##### Stanza 2

**English Translation**:
> I fashioned a funeral pyre out of my sacred words,  
> And showed the burning fire to every salted tear!  
> The quiet torment held captive deep inside my heart

💡 *Commentary*: The metapoetic pyre (*shabdaan di chikha*) burning the poet's own grief.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:

**English Translation**:

- **Synopsis**: The tamboora (tanpura), Shiv's faithful musical instrument, stands as a metaphor for his ruined nervous system and singing chest.
- **Genesis**: Composed when physical palsy and weakness prevented Shiv from tuning or strumming his instruments.
- **Archival Context**: Shiv was famous for singing his poetry with a tanpura or harmonium in mushairas; this late poem allegorizes his bodily collapse as broken strings. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURMA`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੇਰੇ ਤੰਬੂਰੇ ਦੀ ਤਾਰ ਟੁੱਟ ਗਈ,  
> ਸੁਰ ਹਵਾਵਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਖਿੱਲਰੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਸਾਜ਼ ਨੇ ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਰੁਆਈ ਸੀ,  
> ਅੱਜ ਉਸਦੇ ਸਾਰੇ ਸੁਰ ਬਿੱਲਰੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میرے تنبورے دی تار ٹٹ گئی،  
> سر ہواواں وچ کھلرے نی!  
> جس ساز نے دنیا روائی سی،  
> اج اسدے سارے سر بلرے نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Mere tamboore di taar tutt gayi,  
> Sur havaavaan vich khillre ni!  
> Jis saaz ne duniya ruwayi si,  
> Ajj uss de saare sur billre ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The resonant string of my tanpura has snapped in twain,  
> Its golden melodies scattered upon the indifferent winds!  
> That instrument which once brought the entire world to tears  
> Has today seen all its notes dissolve into chaotic despair!  

💡 *Commentary*: The snapped string (*taar tutt gayi*) representing sudden physiological collapse.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਕਾਠ ਦਾ ਤੂੰਬਾ ਸੁੱਕ ਗਿਆ ਏ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਹੱਥ ਨਾ ਇਸਨੂੰ ਛੂੰਹਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਸੁੰਨੀ ਰਾਤ ਦੇ ਹਨੇਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਇਹ ਰੋ-ਰੋ ਅੱਥਰੂ ਧੂੰਹਦਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> کاٹھ دا تومبا سک گیا اے،  
> کوئی ہتھ نہ اسنوں چھونہدا اے!  
> سنی رات دے ہنیرے وچ،  
> ایہ رو رو اتھرو دھونہدا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Kaath da toomba sukk gaya ae,  
> Koi hath na iss nu chhoonhda ae!  
> Sunni raat de hanere vich,  
> Eh ro-ro atthru dhoonhda ae!  

**English Translation**:
> The gourd of resonant wood has dried and cracked,  
> No living hand reaches out to touch its curving spine!  
> In the terrifying pitch-black darkness of the silent night,  
> It weeps alone, dragging its tears through the gathering dust!  

💡 *Commentary*: The dried hollow gourd (*toomba*) symbolizing an emptied chest cavity.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਨਾ ਛਿੜਨੇ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਰਾਗ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਗਾਵਣ ਵਾਲ਼ਾ ਏ!  
> ਤੰਬੂਰੇ ਦੀ ਹਿੱਕ ਤੇ ਸੌਂ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਜੋ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਦਾ ਰਖਵਾਲ਼ਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن نہ چھڑنے برہا دے راگ،  
> نہ کوئی گاون والا اے!  
> تنبورے دی ہک تے سوں گیا،  
> جو گیتاں دا رکھوالا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun na chhirne birha de raag,  
> Na koi gaavan waala ae!  
> Tamboore di hikk te saun gaya,  
> Jo geetaan da rakhwaala ae!  

**English Translation**:
> No longer shall the desolate rags of separation be sung;  
> No singer remains to awaken their weeping resonance!  
> Asleep forever upon the hollow chest of the tanpura rests he  
> Who was the faithful guardian and high priest of songs!  

💡 *Commentary*: The poet resting eternally upon his instrument in sacred silence.

## 12. Zindagi Alvida

### **ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਅਲਵਿਦਾ** • **زندگی الوداع**

#### *"Farewell to This Mortal Life: Severing the Last Cord"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Posthumous` `#Renunciation` `#FinalRelease`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: A dignified, solemn severance of ties with earthly existence, forgiving the world for its cruelty and choosing eternal quietude.
- **Genesis**: Representing the culmination of Shiv's lifelong preoccupation with early departure, executed with tender equanimity.
- **Archival Context**: The title poem sequence of the posthumous collection 'Alvida', which Guru Nanak Dev University scholar Satinder Singh cited as Shiv's conscious surrender to fate. (*Citation: `SINGH-1983-HERITAGE`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਤੈਨੂੰ ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਕਹਿੰਦੇ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਤੇਰੇ ਸ਼ਹਿਰੋਂ ਹੁਣ ਤੁਰ ਚੱਲੇ ਹਾਂ!  
> ਜਿੰਨੇ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ ਤੂੰ ਦਿੱਤੇ ਸੀ ਸਾਨੂੰ,  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਸੀਨੇ ਲਾ ਕੇ ਖੱਲੇ ਹਾਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> زندگی تینوں الوداع کہندے ہاں،  
> تیرے شہروں ہن تر چلے ہاں!  
> جنے زخم توں دتے سی سانوں،  
> اسیں سینے لا کے کھلے ہاں!  

**Romanized**:
> Zindagi tainu alvida kehnde haan,  
> Tere shehron hun tur challe haan!  
> Jinne zakham tu ditte si saanu,  
> Aseen seene laa ke khalle haan!  

**English Translation**:
> To you, mortal life, we bid our solemn farewell;  
> We pack our grief and depart from your cruel city!  
> Every deep wound that you mercilessly dealt against our soul  
> We have pressed to our breast and endured in lonely silence!  

💡 *Commentary*: Acceptance without bitterness; wounded pride transmuted into dignified farewell.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਸ਼ਿਕਵਾ ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਰੋਸਾ,  
> ਨਾ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾਲ਼ ਕੋਈ ਵੈਰ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਰਾਹ ਉੱਤੇ ਮੌਤ ਖਲੋਤੀ,  
> ਉਸ ਰਾਹ ਪਾ ਲਏ ਪੈਰ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> نہ کوئی شکوہ نہ کوئی روسا،  
> نہ کسے نال کوئی ویر نی!  
> جس راہ اتے موت کھلوتی،  
> اس راہ پا لئے پیر نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Na koi shikwa na koi rosa,  
> Na kise naal koi vair ni!  
> Jis raah utte maut khaloti,  
> Uss raah paa laye pair ni!  

**English Translation**:
> No grievance lingers in our heart, no sullen grudge remains;  
> Not a trace of enmity do we bear against any living soul!  
> Upon that path where Death stands waiting like a patient bride,  
> We have set our weary feet without hesitation or fear!  

💡 *Commentary*: Death framed not as an executioner, but as a peaceful sanctuary.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ ਰਹਿਣ ਇਹ ਵੱਸਦੇ ਲੋਕੀਂ,  
> ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ ਰਹਿਣ ਇਹ ਮਹਿਲਾਂ ਵਾਲ਼ੇ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਚੱਲਿਆ ਸਿਵਿਆਂ ਵੱਲ ਨੂੰ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਚਾਨਣ ਕਰਦੇ ਛਾਲ਼ੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> خوش رہن ایہ وسدے لوکی،  
> خوش رہن ایہ محلاں والے!  
> شیو تاں چلیا سویاں ول نوں،  
> جتھے چانن کردے چھالے!  

**Romanized**:
> Khush rehan eh vassde lokin,  
> Khush rehan eh mehlaan waale!  
> Shiv taan chaleya siveyaan vall nu,  
> Jitthe chanan karde chhaale!  

**English Translation**:
> May these thriving multitudes dwell forever in merriment!  
> May the dwellers of grand palaces flourish in prosperity!  
> As for Shiv, he has turned his face toward the cremation ground,  
> Where the blisters of his weeping heart provide their own radiant light!  

💡 *Commentary*: Ironic blessing for worldly vanity contrasted with the sacred light of the cremation ground (*siveyaan*).

## 13. Chup Da Samundar

### **ਚੁੱਪ ਦਾ ਸਮੁੰਦਰ** • **چپ دا سمندر**

#### *"The Ocean of Silence: Beyond All Utterance"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Posthumous` `#Silence` `#Metaphysical`
- **Theme / Biome**: `solitude` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: Silence is not the absence of sound, but an unfathomable ocean into which all ephemeral words ultimately drown.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's late metaphysical realization that the ultimate culmination of lyricism is absolute, sacred silence.
- **Archival Context**: Written in early 1973 when Shiv experienced prolonged periods of complete muteness, staring silently for hours into space. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURMA`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਲਫ਼ਜ਼ ਮੁੱਕੇ ਤੇ ਗੀਤ ਵੀ ਮੁੱਕੇ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਚੁੱਪ ਦਾ ਵੱਡਾ ਸਮੁੰਦਰ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਰੋਲਾ ਬਾਹਰ ਸੁਣਦਾ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਸ਼ਾਂਤ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ ਅੰਦਰ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> لفظ مکے تے گیت وی مکے،  
> ہن چپ دا وڈا سمندر اے!  
> جو رولا باہر سندا سی،  
> اوہ شانت ہو گیا اندر اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Lafaz mukke te geet vi mukke,  
> Hun chup da vadda samundar ae!  
> Jo rola baahar sunda si,  
> Oh shaant ho gaya andar ae!  

**English Translation**:
> The syllables have vanished, and the melodies are spent;  
> Now only the immense, unfathomable ocean of silence remains!  
> All that frantic clamor that echoed across the outer world  
> Has sunk into absolute stillness within my deep soul!  

💡 *Commentary*: Transition from sensory turmoil to inner oceanic quietude.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਸ ਚੁੱਪ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੋਈ ਤਰੰਗ ਨਹੀਂ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਹਵਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਕੋਈ ਝੱਖੜ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਦੁੱਖਾਂ ਦੀ ਸਾਰੀ ਉਮਰ ਦਾ,  
> ਟੁੱਟ ਗਿਆ ਹਰ ਇੱਕ ਪੱਖੜ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اس چپ وچ کوئی ترنگ نہیں،  
> کوئی ہوا نہیں کوئی جھکھڑ نی!  
> میرے دکھاں دی ساری عمر دا،  
> ٹٹ گیا ہر اک پکھڑ نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Iss chup vich koi tarang nahin,  
> Koi havaa nahin koi jhakkhar ni!  
> Mere dukkhaan di saari umar da,  
> Tutt gaya har ikk pakkhar ni!  

**English Translation**:
> Within this fathomless silence, no restless ripple stirs;  
> No biting gale howls, no sudden hurricane strikes!  
> Every heavy chain and armored shackle of grief  
> Forged through my long lifetime has shattered into nothingness!  

💡 *Commentary*: Silence as total emancipation (*tutt gaya har ikk pakkhar*).

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਡੁੱਬ ਜਾ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤੂੰ ਇਸ ਸਮੁੰਦਰ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਕਿਨਾਰੇ ਦੀ ਲੋੜ ਨਹੀਂ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਮਿਲ ਗਈ ਅਮਰ ਖ਼ਾਮੋਸ਼ੀ,  
> ਉੱਥੇ ਦਰਦਾਂ ਦੀ ਕੋਈ ਤੋੜ ਨਹੀਂ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ڈب جا شیو توں اس سمندر وچ،  
> ہن کنارے دی لوڑ نہیں!  
> جتھے مل گئی امر خاموشی،  
> او Rome درد دی کوئی توڑ نہیں!  

**Romanized**:
> Dubb jaa Shiv tu iss samundar vich,  
> Hun kinaare di lorh nahin!  
> Jitthe mil gayi amar khamoshi,  
> Otthe dardaan di koi torh nahin!  

**English Translation**:
> Submerge yourself, O Shiv, deep into this boundless ocean;  
> There is no longer any need to search for a distant shore!  
> Where eternal silence embraces the traveler at last,  
> No agony can ever break or ravage the spirit again!  

💡 *Commentary*: Self-dissolution into the infinite void where pain loses all dominion.

|:---|:---|:---|:---|

## 14. Kabr Da Sirhana

### **ਕਬਰ ਦਾ ਸਿਰਹਾਣਾ** • **قبر دا سرہانا**

- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: The grave is imagined not as a frightening subterranean dungeon, but as a soft earthen bolster offering eternal sleep after a life of severe insomnia.
- **Genesis**: Shiv suffered from chronic, agonizing insomnia throughout the late 1960s and early 1970s; death was conceived primarily as sleep.
- **Archival Context**: Though cremated according to Hindu rites, Shiv frequently invoked Islamic Sufi imagery of the grave (kabr, mazaar) in his poems as symbols of permanent physical rest. (*Citation: `SHARMA-1979-SOLITARY`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਸਾਰੀ ਉਮਰ ਮੈਂ ਜਾਗਦਾ ਰਿਹਾ,  
> ਨੀਂਦ ਨਾ ਆਈ ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਨੂੰ!  
> ਅੱਜ ਕਬਰ ਦੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਕਹਿੰਦੀ ਏ,  
> ਸਿਰਹਾਣਾ ਦੇਵਾਂ ਪੰਖੀਆਂ ਨੂੰ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ساری عمر میں جاگدا رہا،  
> نیند نہ آئی اکھیاں نوں!

> سرہانا دیواں پنکھیاں نوں!  

**Romanized**:
> Saari umar main jaagda reha,  
> Neend na aayi akhiyaan nu!  
> Ajj kabr di mitti kehndi ae,  
> Sirhana devaan pankhiyaan nu!  

**English Translation**:
> Throughout my entire youth I remained sleeplessly awake,  
> No soothing slumber ever touched these burning eyes!  
> Today the gentle earth of the grave whispers softly,  
> Offering its cool bolster to this weary, wounded bird!  

💡 *Commentary*: The earth welcoming the chronic insomniac to sweet, unbroken rest.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਸ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦੇ ਸਿਰਹਾਣੇ ਉੱਤੇ,

> ਉਹੀਓ ਬਣ ਗਿਆ ਮੇਰਾ ਘਰ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اس مٹی دے سرہانے اتے،  
> نہ کوئی سپنا نہ کوئی ڈر!  
> جتھے مک گئی بھٹکن ساری،  
> اوہیؤ بن گیا میرا گھر!  

**Romanized**:
> Iss mitti de sirhaane utte,  
> Na koi supna na koi darr!  
> Jitthe mukk gayi bhatkan saari,  
> Ohiyo ban gaya mera ghar!  

**English Translation**:
> Upon this soft pillow sculpted of ancient soil,  
> No tormenting nightmares strike, no terrifying shadows loom!  
> Where all relentless wanderings of the soul come to an end,  
> That desolate haven alone has become my true, eternal home!  

💡 *Commentary*: Resolution of existential wandering (*bhatkan*) into permanent homecoming.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਜੇ ਕੋਈ ਲੰਘੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ਼ੋਂ,  
> ਹੌਲ਼ੀ ਕਦਮ ਧਰਾਵੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਕਿਤੇ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੀ ਗੂੜ੍ਹੀ ਨੀਂਦਰ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਖ਼ਲਲ ਨਾ ਪਾਵੇ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> جے کوئی لنگھے میرے کولوں،  
> ہولی قدم دھراوے نی!  
> کتے شیو دی گوڑھی نیندڑ وچ،  
> کوئی خلل نہ پاوے نی!  

**Romanized**:

> Hauli kadam dharaave ni!  
> Kite Shiv di goorhi neendar vich,  
> Koi khalal na paave ni!  

**English Translation**:
> If any traveler should chance to walk past my resting place,  
> Let him tread softly with reverent, gentle steps!  
> Lest in the profound, sacred slumber of Shiv,  
> Any careless intruder should cast disturbance or noise!  

💡 *Commentary*: Plea for absolute quietude over the resting place.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |

- **Archival Context**: The motif of the snuffed candle was a classical Urdu-Punjabi mushaira trope that Shiv radically personalized in his final hospital stays. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURMA`*)

##### Stanza 1

> ਹੁਣ ਧੂੰਆਂ ਉੱਠਦਾ ਕਾਲ਼ਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਚਾਨਣ ਪਿੱਛੇ ਜੱਗ ਭੱਜਿਆ,  
> ਉਸ ਚਾਨਣ ਦਾ ਮੁੱਕਿਆ ਪਿਆਲ਼ਾ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> شمع تاں بجھ گئی رات پئی،  
> ہن دھواں اٹھدا کالا نی!  
> جس چانن پچھے جگ بھجیا،  
> اس چانن دا مکیا پیالا نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Shama taan bujjh gayi raat payi,  
> Hun dhuan uthda kaala ni!  
> Jis chanan pichhe jagg bhajeya,  
> Uss chanan da mukkeya pyala ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The candle was snuffed out as the deep night fell;  
> Now only dark, spiraling smoke rises into the void!  
> That blazing brilliance which the hungry world once pursued—  
> The goblet of that radiance has been drained to its very dregs!  

💡 *Commentary*: The extinguished candle (*shama*) as symbol of the poet's exhausted vitality.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਇਹ ਧੂੰਆਂ ਹਵਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਰਲ਼ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਿਸ਼ਾਨ ਨਾ ਰਹਿਣਾ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਨੇ ਹੰਢਾਇਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੁਣ ਸਹਿਣਾ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ایہ دھواں ہوا وچ رل جاوے گا،  
> کوئی نشان نہ رہنا اے!  
> جو درد شیو نے ہنڈھایا سی،  
> اوہ کسے نہیں ہن سہنا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Eh dhuan havaa vich ral jaavega,  
> Koi nishaan na rehna ae!  
> Jo dard Shiv ne handhaya si,  
> Oh kise nahin hun sehna ae!  

**English Translation**:
> This fading smoke will dissolve effortlessly into the wind;  
> Not a single earthly footprint will remain behind!  
> That monumental grief which Shiv endured upon his brow  
> No other living soul shall ever have the courage to endure!  

💡 *Commentary*: Incomparability of the poet's agony disappearing into cosmic air.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਪਰਵਾਨੇ ਸਾਰੇ ਉੱਡ ਗਏ ਨੇ,  
> ਮਹਿਫ਼ਿਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੁੰਞ ਪਸਰ ਗਈ ਏ!  
> ਸੜ ਕੇ ਜੋ ਰਾਖ਼ ਬਣੀ ਸੀ ਸ਼ਮ੍ਹਾ,  
> ਉਹ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਠਰ ਗਈ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> پروانے سارے اڈ گئے نے،  
> محفل وچ سنج پسر گئی اے!  
> سڑ کے جو راکھ بنی سی شمع،  
> اوہ مٹی دے وچ ٹھر گئی اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Parwaane saare udd gaye ne,  
> Mehfil vich sunj pasar gayi ae!  
> Sarh ke jo raakh bani si shama,  
> Oh mitti de vich thar gayi ae!  

**English Translation**:
> The dazzled moths have all scattered and taken flight;  
> A harrowing desolation has blanketed the banquet hall!  
> The candle which burned itself down to cold, grey ash  
> Has frozen into eternal rest within the quiet clay!  

💡 *Commentary*: The empty gathering hall (*mehfil*) abandoned by fair-weather admirers.

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਸ਼ਮ੍ਹਾ (Shama)** | *Sha-maa* | Candle / Lamp flame | Classical Persian/Urdu metaphor for the blazing, self-sacrificing center of romantic devotion. |
| **ਪਰਵਾਨੇ (Parwaane)** | *Par-vaa-ney* | Moths | The devoted lovers drawn irresistibly to the candle flame, courting annihilation. |

## 16. Mushaira Mukkeya

### **ਮੁਸ਼ਾਇਰਾ ਮੁੱਕਿਆ** • **مشاعرہ مکیا**

#### *"The Mushaira Has Concluded: The Lights Turned Down"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Posthumous` `#Mushaira` `#FameandEmptiness`
- **Theme / Biome**: `solitude` / `cremation_dusk`
- **Synopsis**: An incisive look at the chilling aftermath of a poetic gathering: when the thunderous applause ceases, the poet walks back alone into the dark.
- **Genesis**: Shiv's poignant deconstruction of fame and the loneliness that followed his triumphant public recitations.
- **Archival Context**: Shiv was the undisputed rock star of Punjabi mushairas, drawing audiences of thousands who would stay until 4 AM just to hear his singing. (*Citation: `GARGI-1979-SURMA`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਤਾੜੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਰੋਲਾ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਖ਼ਾਲੀ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ ਪੰਡਾਲ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਲੋਕ ਵਾਹ-ਵਾਹ ਕਰਦੇ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹਨਾਂ ਪੁੱਛਿਆ ਨਾ ਮੇਰਾ ਹਾਲ ਨੀ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> تاڑیاں دا رولا مک گیا،  
> خالی ہو گیا پنڈال نی!  
> جو لوگ واہ واہ کردے سی،  
> اوہناں پچھیا نہ میرا حال نی!  

**Romanized**:
> Taariyaan da rola mukk gaya,  
> Khaali ho gaya pandaal ni!  
> Jo lok vaah-vaah karde si,  
> Ohnaan puchheya na mera haal ni!  

**English Translation**:
> The thunder of applause has died into total silence;  
> The vast festival tent stands hollow and deserted!  
> Those very spectators who shouted 'Wah! Wah!' in ecstasy  
> Did not pause to ask how my wounded heart was faring!  

💡 *Commentary*: Contrast between external applause (*vaah-vaah*) and internal ruin.

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮਾਈਕ ਵੀ ਬੰਦ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ ਏ,  
> ਬੱਤੀਆਂ ਵੀ ਬੁੱਝ ਗਈਆਂ ਨੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਪੈੜਾਂ ਮੇਰੇ ਪਿੱਛੇ ਤੁਰੀਆਂ,  
> ਉਹ ਹਨੇਰੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੁਲ਼ ਗਈਆਂ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> مائیک وی بند ہو گیا اے،  
> بتیاں وی بجھ گئیاں نے!  
> جو پیڑاں میرے پچھے تریاں،  
> اوہ ہنیرے وچ رل گئیاں نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Mic vi band ho gaya ae,  
> Battiyaan vi bujjh gayiaan ne!  
> Jo pairhaan mere pichhe turiyaan,  
> Oh hanere vich rul gayiaan ne!  

**English Translation**:
> The microphone has been switched off and unplugged;  
> The stage footlights have been extinguished one by one!  
> Those fleeting footprints that followed eagerly behind me  
> Have disappeared into the swallowing, merciless dark!  

💡 *Commentary*: De-electrification of the modern stage revealing primal isolation.

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣੀ ਇਕੱਲ ਚੁੱਕ ਕੇ ਤੁਰਿਆ,  
> ਸੁੰਨਸਾਨ ਸੜਕ ਦੇ ਰਾਹੇ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਨੇ ਤਾਂ ਗੀਤ ਵੇਚੇ ਸੀ,  
> ਪਰ ਦਰਦ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾ ਲਾਹੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> میں اپنی اکل چک کے تریا،  
> سنسان سڑک دے راہے!  
> شیو نے تاں گیت ویچے سی،  
> پر درد کسے نہ لاہے!  

**Romanized**:
> Main apni ikkall chukk ke tureya,  
> Sunsaan sarak de raahe!  
> Shiv ne taan geet veche si,  
> Par dard kise na laahe!  

**English Translation**:
> I lifted my heavy solitude and stepped into the night,  
> Walking alone along the deserted, midnight road!  
> Shiv had indeed sold his golden songs to the crowd,  
> Yet not a single soul could lift the agony from his shoulders!  

💡 *Commentary*: The solitary walk down the road bearing solitude (*ikkall*).

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਪੰਡਾਲ (Pandaal)** | *Pun-daal* | Marquee / Festival tent | The large fabric pavilion erected for grand Punjabi kavi darbars and mushairas. |
| **ਵਾਹ-ਵਾਹ (Vaah-vaah)** | *Waah-waah* | Bravo! / Acclamation | The traditional vocal exclamation of aesthetic ecstasy uttered by mushaira audiences. |

## 17. Hanjhu Di Akhiri Boond

### **ਹੰਝੂ ਦੀ ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਬੂੰਦ** • **ہنجھو دی آخری بوند**

#### *"The Final Drop of Tear: When Weeping Ceases"*

- **Thematic Tags**: `#Alvida` `#Posthumous` `#Desiccation` `#DryEyes`
- **Theme / Biome**: `mortality` / `barren_dune`
- **Synopsis**: The ultimate threshold of suffering is reached not when one weeps uncontrollably, but when the wells of tears themselves dry into salted stone.
- **Genesis**: Composed in Shiv's final week, capturing the frightening somatic state where grief exhausts the tear glands.
- **Archival Context**: In his final days at Kir Mangyal, Shiv told his close friends that his eyes had dried up completely and could no longer produce tears. (*Citation: `SHARMA-1979-SOLITARY`*)

#### Complete Verses & Translations

##### Stanza 1

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਦੇ ਖੂਹ ਸੁੱਕ ਗਏ ਨੇ,  
> ਹੰਝੂ ਵੀ ਹੁਣ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਏ ਨੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਕਦੇ ਛਲਕਦੇ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਪੱਥਰ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਰੁੱਕ ਗਏ ਨੇ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> اکھیاں دے کھوہ سک گئے نے،  
> ہنجھو وی ہن مک گئے نے!  
> جو درد کدے چھلکدے سی،  
> اوہ پتھر وانگوں رک گئے نے!  

**Romanized**:
> Akhiyaan de khooh sukk gaye ne,  
> Hanjhu vi hun mukk gaye ne!  
> Jo dard kade chhalkade si,  
> Oh patthar vaangoon rukk gaye ne!  

**English Translation**:
> The deep wells of my eyes have parched and run dry;  
> Even the salted tears have exhausted their final drop!  
> Those torrents of grief that once spilled over in deluge  
> Have turned to silent, unyielding stone inside my chest!  

💡 *Commentary*: Exhaustion of somatic weeping (*akhiyaan de khooh sukk gaye*).

##### Stanza 2

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਬੂੰਦ ਵੀ ਡਿੱਗ ਪਈ ਅੱਜ,  
> ਮਿੱਟੀ ਨੇ ਉਸਨੂੰ ਪੀ ਲਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਸਾਹ ਬਾਕੀ ਸੀ ਸੀਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਉਹ ਮੌਤ ਨੇ ਹੁਣ ਸੀ ਲਿਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> آخری بوند وی ڈگ پئی اج،  
> مٹی نے اسنوں پی لیا اے!  
> جو ساہ باقی سی سینے وچ،  
> اوہ موت نے ہن سی لیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Akhiri boond vi digg payi ajj,  
> Mitti ne uss nu pee leya ae!  
> Jo saah baaki si seene vich,  
> Oh maut ne hun see leya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> Even the very last drop fell to the earth today;  
> The thirsty dust absorbed it into its ancient bosom!  
> Whatever remnant of breath lingered in this hollow chest  
> Has now been stitched shut forever by the needle of Death!  

💡 *Commentary*: Death as the tailor stitching the chest closed (*maut ne hun see leya ae*).

##### Stanza 3

**Gurmukhi**:
> ਹੁਣ ਰੋਵਣ ਦੀ ਕੋਈ ਲੋੜ ਨਹੀਂ,  
> ਇਹ ਸਫ਼ਰ ਮੁਕੰਮਲ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ ਸੁਲਤਾਨ ਅੱਜ,  
> ਚਿਰ-ਨਿੰਦਰਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੋ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  

**Shahmukhi**:
> ہن روون دی کوئی لوڑ نہیں،  
> ایہ سفر مکمل ہو گیا اے!  
> شیو برہا دا سلطان اج،  
> چر نندرا وچ سو گیا اے!  

**Romanized**:
> Hun rovan di koi lorh nahin,  
> Eh safar mukammal ho gaya ae!  
> Shiv birha da sultan ajj,  
> Chir-nindra vich so gaya ae!  

**English Translation**:
> There is no longer any need for weeping or lamentation;  
> This arduous pilgrimage has reached its sacred completion!  
> Shiv, the sovereign Sultan of Separation,  
> Has fallen asleep forever into eternal, undisturbed slumber!  

💡 *Commentary*: Grand poetic apotheosis: the Sultan of Birha crowned in eternal sleep (*chir-nindra*).

##### Cultural Glossary

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਚਿਰ-ਨਿੰਦਰਾ (Chir-nindra)** | *Chir-nin-dra* | Eternal sleep / Deep slumber | The unbroken sleep of death from which no worldly distress can awaken the sleeper. |
| **ਸੁਲਤਾਨ (Sultan)** | *Sul-taan* | King / Emperor | Title bestowed upon Shiv ('Birha da Sultan' / King of Separation) for reigning over the kingdom of longing. |
