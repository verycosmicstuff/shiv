# Bhull Bakhshao

### **ਭੁੱਲ ਬਖ਼ਸ਼ਾਓ** • **بھل بخشاؤ**

#### *"Forgive My Wayward Trespasses"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aarti (1971)](../09_aarti_1971.md)
- **Original Title**: ਭੁੱਲ ਬਖ਼ਸ਼ਾਓ (بھل بخشاؤ)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1971
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Aarti` `#Repentance` `#Forgiveness` `#Supplication`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The dying poet asks forgiveness from his parents, lovers, and friends for all the sorrow his chaotic life caused them.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's tender, guilt-ridden reconciliation with his father Pandit Krishan Gopal before his health collapsed entirely.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Traditional Punjabi supplication (*Bhull Chuk Maaf*), written by Shiv as an apology to his parents and friends.

**Source**: *Nagmani (Special Memorial Edition on Shiv Kumar)* by Amrita Pritam (1973, Nagmani Press, New Delhi, Vol. 85, June 1973). [Primary Memoir]

> "Amrita Pritam writes: "Death did not steal Shiv; Shiv courted death as his final bride." She catalogs his uncollected verses and reflects on the intellectual hostility he faced from dogmatic critics."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੇਰੀਆਂ ਭੁੱਲਾਂ ਬਖ਼ਸ਼ ਦਿਓ ਲੋਕੋ,  
> ਜੇ ਮੈਂ ਕਿਸੇ ਦਾ ਦਿਲ ਦੁਖਾਇਆ ਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਤਾਂ ਆਪਣੇ ਹੀ ਦਰਦ 'ਚ ਸੜਿਆ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਵੈਰ ਨਾ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾਲ ਪਾਇਆ ਏ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میریاں بھلاں بخش دیو لوگو،  
> جے میں کسے دا دل دکھایا اے نی!  
> میں تاں اپنے ہی درد چ سڑیا،  
> کوئی ویر نہ کسے نال پایا اے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Meriyaan bhullaan bakhsh diyo loko,  
> Je main kise da dil dukhaaya ae ni!  
> Main taan apne hi dard ch sarheya,  
> Koyi vair na kise naal paaya ae ni!  

#### English Translation
> Forgive all my wayward trespasses, O people of the world,  
> If ever inadvertently I caused injury to a human heart!  
> I was consumed solely by the raging fire of my own agony,  
> Never did I harbor malice or enmity against any living soul!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Plea for forgiveness: the poet was absorbed in his own fire, not malice.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮਾਂ ਮੇਰੀਏ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਮੁਆਫ਼ ਕਰੀਂ ਤੂੰ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਤੇਰਾ ਕਹਿਣਾ ਨਾ ਮੰਨਿਆ ਵੇ!  
> ਮੈਂ ਤਾਂ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ ਰੋਗੀ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਇਸ ਇਸ਼ਕ ਨੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਛੰਨਿਆ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ماں میریئے مینوں معاف کریں توں،  
> میں تیرا کہنا نہ منیا وے!  
> میں تاں برہا دا روگی ہویا،  
> اس عشق نے مینوں چھنیا وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Maan meriye mainu muaaf kareen tu,  
> Main tera kehna na manneya ve!  
> Main taan birha da rogi hoyea,  
> Iss ishq ne mainu chhanneya ve!  

#### English Translation
> O my beloved mother, forgive your wayward son,  
> For I failed to obey your gentle maternal counsels!  
> I was struck down by the incurable malady of Separation,  
> And this tragic passion sifted my flesh like fine grain!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Heartbreaking confession to his mother Shanti Devi.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਮੈਂ ਤੁਰ ਚੱਲਿਆ ਹਾਂ ਦੋਸਤੋ,  
> ਹੱਥ ਜੋੜ ਕੇ ਅਲਵਿਦਾ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਹਾਂ!  
> ਜੋ ਗੀਤ ਮੇਰੇ ਸੀਨੇ 'ਚ ਬਚੇ ਨੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਤੁਹਾਡੀ ਝੋਲੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਾਉਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن میں تر چلیا ہاں دوستو،  
> ہتھ جوڑ کے الوداع کہندا ہاں!  
> جو گیت میرے سینے چ بچے نے،  
> اوہ تہاڈی جھولی وچ پاؤندا ہاں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun main tur challeya haan dosto,  
> Hath jorh ke alvida kehnda haan!  
> Jo geet mere seene ch bache ne,  
> Oh tuhaadi jholi vich paaunda haan!  

#### English Translation
> Now I take my final departure, O companions of my youth,  
> With folded palms I utter my eternal, peaceful Adieu!  
> Those sacred songs which still remain within my dying breast,  
> I place with reverence into the open lap of Punjab!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Final benediction and bequest of poetry to Punjab.*
