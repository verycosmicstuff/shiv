# Daru Di Botal

### **ਦਾਰੂ ਦੀ ਬੋਤਲ** • **دارو دی بوتل**

#### *"The Bottle of Liquor: Embalming My Torment"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aarti (1971)](../09_aarti_1971.md)
- **Original Title**: ਦਾਰੂ ਦੀ ਬੋਤਲ (دارو دی بوتل)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1971
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Aarti` `#Alcoholism` `#ChandigarhLatePeriod` `#SlowPoison`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The glass bottle is addressed not as an indulgence, but as an embalming fluid keeping a living corpse standing.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's tragic late poem written when doctors at PGI Chandigarh warned him that his liver was fatally damaged.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Written during Shiv's severe health decline in Chandigarh in 1971, confronting his chemical dependence as slow suicide.

**Source**: *Shiv Batalvi: A Solitary and Passionate Singer* by Dr. O.P. Sharma (1979, Sterling Publishers, New Delhi, pp. 12–45, 180–205). [Primary Memoir]

> "Authoritative academic biography documenting his childhood in Bara Pind Lohtian (now Pakistan), the trauma of the 1947 Partition flight, his clerkship at the State Bank of India Chandigarh, and his final collapse in Kir Mangyal."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਦਾਰੂ ਦੀ ਬੋਤਲ ਸਾਹਮਣੇ ਪਈ ਏ,  
> ਕੱਚ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਲਦੀ ਮੇਰੀ ਜਾਨ ਨੀ!  
> ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਨੇ ਤਾਂ ਧੋਖਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ,  
> ਇਹਨੇ ਦਿੱਤਾ ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਦਾਨ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> دارو دی بوتل ساہمنے پئی اے،  
> کچ وچ بلدی میری جان نی!  
> زندگی نے تاں دھوکھا دتا،  
> ایہنے دتا موت دا دان نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Daru di botal saahmne payi ae,  
> Kach vich baldi meri jaan ni!  
> Zindagi ne taan dhokha ditta,  
> Ehne ditta maut da daan ni!  

#### English Translation
> The dark bottle of liquor rests before me on the table,  
> My very life-force burning captive within its glass!  
> Life itself gave only deceit and betrayal,  
> While this amber liquid offered the holy alms of death!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Alcohol viewed as the only faithful companion that delivers death's mercy.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹਰ ਘੁੱਟ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਲੰਘਦਾ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ,  
> ਰਗ-ਰਗ ਨੂੰ ਠੰਢਾ ਕਰਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਇਸ ਜੱਗ ਨੇ ਦਿੱਤਾ ਏ,  
> ਉਸ ਨੂੰ ਪਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਰਦਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہر گھٹ وچوں لنگھدا زہر،  
> رگ رگ نوں ٹھنڈا کردا اے!  
> جو درد اس جگ نے دتا اے،  
> اس نوں پل وچ ہردا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Har ghutt vichon langhda zeher,  
> Rag-rag nu thandha karda ae!  
> Jo dard iss jagg ne ditta ae,  
> Uss nu pal vich harda ae!  

#### English Translation
> With every burning swallow passes the poison,  
> Freezing the boiling blood within my weary veins!  
> That colossal agony which this callous world inflicted,  
> It snatches away for one brief, blessed instant!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Chemical numbing (*thandha karda*) of somatic pain.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇੱਕ ਦਿਨ ਇਹ ਬੋਤਲ ਮੁੱਕ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਤੇ ਨਾਲੇ ਸਾਹ ਵੀ ਮੁੱਕਣਗੇ!  
> ਫਿਰ ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੇ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਗੀਤ,  
> ਲੋਕਾਂ ਦੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਫੁੱਟਣਗੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اک دن ایہ بوتل مک جاوے گی،  
> تے نالے ساہ وی مکن گے!  
> پھر شیو دے برہا دے گیت،  
> لوکاں دے سینے پھٹن گے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Ikk din eh botal mukk jaavegi,  
> Te naale saah vi mukkange!  
> Phir Shiv de birha de geet,  
> Lokaan de seene phuttange!  

#### English Translation
> One day this bottle will be drained to its final drop,  
> And simultaneously my mortal breath will cease!  
> And then the sovereign separation songs of Shiv  
> Will erupt like volcanoes within the breast of humanity!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Prophecy of post-mortem resonance erupting from the ashes of addiction.*
