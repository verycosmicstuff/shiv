# Soorma Sukkeya

### **ਸੁਰਮਾ ਸੁੱਕਿਆ** • **سورمہ سکیا**

#### *"Dried Kohl in Death"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aarti (1971)](../09_aarti_1971.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸੁਰਮਾ ਸੁੱਕਿਆ (سورمہ سکیا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1971
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Aarti` `#Kohl` `#Petrification` `#TerminalEyes`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: Tears cease flowing as the eyes turn to cold stone; the kohl dries into an indelible black line marking the end of longing.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's poetic contemplation of the corpse's peaceful, impassive face.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Traditional belief that the eyes of the dying petrify and dried kohl remains like an unwashed line of ink.

**Source**: *Nagmani (Special Memorial Edition on Shiv Kumar)* by Amrita Pritam (1973, Nagmani Press, New Delhi, Vol. 85, June 1973). [Primary Memoir]

> "Amrita Pritam writes: "Death did not steal Shiv; Shiv courted death as his final bride." She catalogs his uncollected verses and reflects on the intellectual hostility he faced from dogmatic critics."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸੁਰਮਾ ਸੁੱਕ ਗਿਆ ਨੈਣਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਦਾ ਵਹਿਣ ਥੰਮ੍ਹ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਕਦੇ ਸੀ ਅੱਗ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਉਹ ਬਰਫ਼ ਬਣ ਕੇ ਜੰਮ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> سورمہ سک گیا نیناں دے وچ،  
> ہنجھواں دا وہن تھمھ گیا نی!  
> جو درد کدے سی اگ وانگوں،  
> اوہ برف بن کے جم گیا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Soorma sukk gaya nainaan de vich,  
> Hanjhuwan da vahan thammh gaya ni!  
> Jo dard kade si agg vaangoo,  
> Oh baraf ban ke jamm gaya ni!  

#### English Translation
> The black kohl has dried forever within our eyes,  
> The torrential flood of tears has frozen into stone!  
> That agony which once raged like an untamed furnace  
> Has turned to motionless, petrified winter ice!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Transition from active fire to frozen stillness in death.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਪੱਥਰ ਹੋ ਗਈਆਂ ਸਾਡੀਆਂ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਰੂਪ ਨਾ ਹੁਣ ਦਿਸਦਾ ਏ ਵੇ!  
> ਇਸ ਚੁੱਪ ਦੀ ਡੂੰਘੀ ਖਾਈ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਭੇਦ ਨਾ ਹੁਣ ਰਿਸਦਾ ਏ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اکھیاں پتھر ہو گئیاں ساڈیاں،  
> کوئی روپ نہ ہن دسدا اے وے!  
> اس چپ دی ڈونگھی کھائی وچ،  
> کوئی بھید نہ ہن رسدا اے وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Akhiyaan patthar ho gaiyaan saadiyaan,  
> Koyi roop na hun disda ae ve!  
> Iss chup di doonghi khaayi vich,  
> Koyi bhed na hun risda ae ve!  

#### English Translation
> Our weeping eyes have turned to solid marble stone,  
> No earthly beauty can register upon their glazed surface!  
> Within this bottomless, sacred abyss of silence,  
> No secret drop of blood oozes from our wounds!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Petrification of the senses: immunity from further pain.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੌਤ ਨੇ ਆ ਕੇ ਪੱਲਕਾਂ ਢਕੀਆਂ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਸੁਪਨਾ ਨਾ ਹੁਣ ਆਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦਾ ਅੰਤਮ ਸ਼ਾਇਰ,  
> ਸਦਾ ਲਈ ਸੌਂ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> موت نے آ کے پلکاں ڈھکیاں،  
> کوئی سپنا نہ ہن آوے گا!  
> ساڈے برہا دا انتم شاعر،  
> سدا لئی سون جاوے گا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Maut ne aa ke pallkaan dhakkiyaan,  
> Koyi supna na hun aavega!  
> Saade birha da antam shaayar,  
> Sadaa layi saun jaavega!  

#### English Translation
> Death stepped forward and closed our heavy eyelids,  
> No tormenting dream shall ever disturb our rest!  
> And Punjab's final, supreme poet of Separation  
> Has laid down his head to sleep for all eternity!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The sleep of the ultimate poet of Birha.*
