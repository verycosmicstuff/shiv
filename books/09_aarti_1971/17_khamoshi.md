# Khamoshi Da Shor

### **ਖ਼ਾਮੋਸ਼ੀ ਦਾ ਸ਼ੋਰ** • **خاموشی دا شور**

#### *"Tumult of Total Silence"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aarti (1971)](../09_aarti_1971.md)
- **Original Title**: ਖ਼ਾਮੋਸ਼ੀ ਦਾ ਸ਼ੋਰ (خاموشی دا شور)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1971
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Aarti` `#Khamoshi` `#InternalVoid` `#ModernistDespair`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: Total silence creates a clamor vaster than thunder, exposing the terrifying void at the heart of human existence.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's confrontations with the existential void during his solitary convalescence in 1971.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Reflects the modernist existential themes developed in 'Main Te Main' and carried into his late 'Aarti' poems.

**Source**: *Punjabi Kavi Ate Kavitriyan (Marxist Critical Essays)* by Sant Singh Sekhon (1972, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, pp. 89–112). [Literary Criticism]

> "Sant Singh Sekhon termed Shiv the "Keats of Punjabi poetry" for his meter and folk metaphor, but charged him with "palayanwad" (reactionary escapism), arguing that romanticizing youth-suicide was reactionary in post-Partition agrarian Punjab."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਖ਼ਾਮੋਸ਼ੀ ਦਾ ਇਹ ਸ਼ੋਰ ਭਿਆਨਕ,  
> ਕੰਨਾਂ ਦੇ ਪਰਦੇ ਪਾੜਦਾ ਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਇਸ ਸੁੰਞੇ ਸੰਸਾਰ ਦੇ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਵਜੂਦ ਉਜਾੜਦਾ ਏ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> خاموشی دا ایہ شور بھیانک،  
> کناں دے پردے پاڑدا اے نی!  
> اس سنجے سنسار دے اندر،  
> ساڈا وجود اجاڑدا اے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Khamoshi da eh shor bhayaanak,  
> Kannaan de parde paarda ae ni!  
> Iss sunje sansaar de andar,  
> Saada wajood ujaarda ae ni!  

#### English Translation
> This terrifying, deafening clamor of absolute Silence  
> Rips through the delicate membranes of our ears!  
> Within the hollow chambers of this abandoned universe,  
> It reduces our very identity to wasteland and dust!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Existential horror of silence dismantling identity (*wajood ujaarna*).*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕੋਈ ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਨਾ ਇਸ ਧਰਤ ਤੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਅੰਬਰੋਂ ਤਾਰਾ ਬੋਲੇ ਵੇ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਆਪਣੇ ਹੀ ਸਾਹਾਂ ਦੇ ਸ਼ੋਰ ਤੋਂ,  
> ਡਰ ਕੇ ਅੰਦਰ ਦਿਲ ਨੂੰ ਖੋਲ੍ਹੇ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کوئی آواز نہ اس دھرتی تے،  
> نہ کوئی انبروں تارا بولے وے!  
> اسیں اپنے ہی ساہاں دے شور توں،  
> ڈر کے اندر دل نوں کھولھے وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Koyi aawaaz na iss dhart te,  
> Na koyi ambron taara bole ve!  
> Aseen apne hi saahaan de shor ton,  
> Dar ke andar dil nu khole ve!  

#### English Translation
> Not a single living voice whispers upon this earth,  
> Nor does any star utter comfort from the heavens!  
> Trembling before the monstrous roar of our own breath,  
> We recoil in terror from our own beating heart!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *One's own heartbeat experienced as monstrous noise in absolute isolation.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਹ ਖ਼ਾਮੋਸ਼ੀ ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਦੂਤ ਏ,  
> ਜੋ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਲੈਣ ਆਈ ਏ ਨੀ!  
> ਇਸ ਸ਼ੋਰ ਤੋਂ ਛੁਟਕਾਰਾ ਪਾ ਕੇ,  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਸੌਣ ਦੀ ਸੇਜ ਸਜਾਈ ਏ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ایہ خاموشی موت دا دوت اے،  
> جو سانوں لین آئی اے نی!  
> اس شور توں چھٹکارا پا کے،  
> اسیں سون دی سیج سجائی اے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Eh khamoshi maut da doot ae,  
> Jo saanu lain aayi ae ni!  
> Iss shor ton chhutkaara paa ke,  
> Aseen saun di sej sajaayi ae ni!  

#### English Translation
> This cosmic silence is the solemn emissary of Death,  
> Arriving to escort our trembling spirit home!  
> To find release from this terrifying inner clamor,  
> We have laid out our bed to sleep forevermore!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Death as the only deliverer from the roar of existence.*
