# Chup Di Awaaz

### **ਚੁੱਪ ਦੀ ਆਵਾਜ਼** • **چپ دی آواز**

#### *"The Screaming Voice of Silence"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aarti (1971)](../09_aarti_1971.md)
- **Original Title**: ਚੁੱਪ ਦੀ ਆਵਾਜ਼ (چپ دی آواز)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1971
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Aarti` `#Silence` `#InternalScream` `#Alienation`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: Silence is not the absence of sound, but an internal scream deafening the poet from within while the world hears nothing.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's late psychological state in Chandigarh where he felt screaming internally while maintaining outward quiet.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Features in Shiv's late recitations where he transitioned from singing to harsh, whispering free verse.

**Source**: *All India Radio (AIR Jalandhar & Delhi) Station Retention Protocols* by Akashvani Archives Committee (1975, Directorate General, All India Radio, New Delhi, Spool Audit Report 1971–1975). [Institutional Record]

> "Historical administrative audit revealing that chronic shortages of 2-inch quadruplex magnetic video and 1/4-inch audio spools mandated routine bulk-erasure and re-use of tapes for daily bulletins, inadvertently destroying dozens of Batalvi’s live mushaira recordings."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਚੁੱਪ ਦੀ ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਬੜੀ ਡਰਾਉਣੀ ਏ,  
> ਕੰਨਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਚੀਕਾਂ ਪਾਉਂਦੀ ਏ!  
> ਬਾਹਰੋਂ ਤਾਂ ਸਭ ਸ਼ਾਂਤ ਦਿਸਦਾ,  
> ਅੰਦਰੋਂ ਦਿਲ ਨੂੰ ਸਾੜਦੀ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> چپ دی آواز بڑی ڈراؤنی اے،  
> کناں وچ چیکاں پاؤندی اے!  
> باہروں تاں سبھ شانت دسدا،  
> اندروں دل نوں ساڑدی اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Chup di aawaaz barhi daraaoni ae,  
> Kannaan vich cheekaan paaundi ae!  
> Baahron taan sabh shaant disda,  
> Andron dil nu saardi ae!  

#### English Translation
> Terrifying beyond measure is the screaming voice of Silence,  
> Pouring deafening shrieks into my ringing ears!  
> Outwardly all appears serene and motionless to the world,  
> Yet within, it consumes the heart with living fire!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The paradox of deafening silence (*chup di cheekh*).*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕੋਈ ਸ਼ਬਦ ਨਾ ਸਾਥੋਂ ਬੋਲਿਆ ਜਾਵੇ,  
> ਜੀਭ ਵੀ ਪੱਥਰ ਹੋ ਗਈ ਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਸੀ ਸਾਡੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ ਦਾ,  
> ਉਹਦੀ ਰੂਹ ਹੀ ਗੁਆਚ ਗਈ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کوئی شبد نہ ساتھوں بولیا جاوے،  
> جینبھ وی پتھر ہو گئی اے!  
> جو درد سی ساڈے گیتاں دا،  
> اوہدی روح ہی گواچ گئی اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Koyi shabad na saathon boleya jaave,  
> Jeebh vi patthar ho gayi ae!  
> Jo dard si saade geetaan da,  
> Ohdi rooh hi guaach gayi ae!  

#### English Translation
> Not a single spoken word can cross our lips anymore,  
> Our tongue has calcified into unyielding granite!  
> That holy grief which once inspired our singing melodies  
> Has had its very soul extinguished in the dark!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Tongue turned to stone: loss of lyric capacity.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਤਾਂ ਮੌਤ ਹੀ ਆ ਕੇ ਤੋੜੂ,  
> ਇਸ ਚੁੱਪ ਦਾ ਭਿਆਨਕ ਘੇਰਾ ਵੇ!  
> ਜਦ ਕਬਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੌਂ ਜਾਵਾਂਗੇ,  
> ਮਿਟ ਜਾਊ ਇਹ ਸ਼ੋਰ ਬਥੇਰਾ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن تاں موت ہی آ کے توڑو،  
> اس چپ دا بھیانک گھیرا وے!  
> جد قبر وچ سون جاواں گے،  
> مٹ جاؤ ایہ شور بتھیرا وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun taan maut hi aa ke torh-oo,  
> Iss chup da bhayaanak ghera ve!  
> Jad kabr vich saun jaawaange,  
> Mitt jaaoo eh shor batheera ve!  

#### English Translation
> Now only Death will possess the strength to break  
> This terrifying, suffocating perimeter of silence!  
> When we lay our head to rest within the quiet grave,  
> This unbearable internal clamor will finally be extinguished!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The grave as the only true quiet that can silence the internal scream.*
