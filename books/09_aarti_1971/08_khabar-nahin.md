# Khabar Nahin Ki Hoyea

### **ਖ਼ਬਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਕਿ ਹੋਇਆ** • **خبر نہیں کہ ہویا**

#### *"I Know Not What Transpired"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aarti (1971)](../09_aarti_1971.md)
- **Original Title**: ਖ਼ਬਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਕਿ ਹੋਇਆ (خبر نہیں کہ ہویا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1971
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Aarti` `#AmnesiaofTrauma` `#Disillusionment` `#Void`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The poet awakens from youth's delirium to find that everything he loved has vanished, without knowing when or how it slipped away.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Written during a hospital stay when Shiv felt time accelerating irreversibly toward the end.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Documents Shiv's late existential bewilderment, feeling that his entire life slipped through his fingers without explanation.

**Source**: *Punjabi Kavi Ate Kavitriyan (Marxist Critical Essays)* by Sant Singh Sekhon (1972, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, pp. 89–112). [Literary Criticism]

> "Sant Singh Sekhon termed Shiv the "Keats of Punjabi poetry" for his meter and folk metaphor, but charged him with "palayanwad" (reactionary escapism), arguing that romanticizing youth-suicide was reactionary in post-Partition agrarian Punjab."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਖ਼ਬਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਕਿ ਹੋਇਆ ਸਾਨੂੰ,  
> ਕਿੱਥੇ ਗੁਆਚ ਗਈ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਨੀ!  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਤਾਂ ਤੁਰੇ ਸੀ ਮੰਜ਼ਿਲ ਵੱਲ,  
> ਪਰ ਰਾਹਾਂ ਨੇ ਖੋਹ ਲਈ ਬੰਦਗੀ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> خبر نہیں کہ ہویا سانوں،  
> کتھے گواچ گئی زندگی نی!  
> اسیں تاں ترے سی منزل ول،  
> پر راہاں نے کھوہ لئی بندگی نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Khabar nahin ki hoyea saanu,  
> Kitthe guaach gayi zindagi ni!  
> Aseen taan ture si manzil vall,  
> Par raahaan ne khoh layi bandagi ni!  

#### English Translation
> I know not what strange calamity befell us,  
> Where has our precious life evaporated into mist?  
> We had set forth bravely towards the radiant summit,  
> Yet the winding crossroads plundered our sacred devotion!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Existential amnesia: waking up to realize life was lost along the way.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਯਾਰ ਵੀ ਛੁੱਟ ਗਏ ਹਾਣੀ ਵੀ ਮੁੱਕੇ,  
> ਸੁੰਞਾ ਪਿਆ ਏ ਚੌਰਾਹਾ ਵੇ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਪੁੱਛੇ ਸਾਡਾ ਹਾਲ ਹੁਣ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਮਾਹਰਾ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> یار وی چھٹ گئے ہانی وی مکے،  
> سنجا پیا اے چوراہا وے!  
> کوئی نہ پچھے ساڈا حال ہن،  
> نہ کوئی دل دا ماہرا وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Yaar vi chhutt gaye haani vi mukke,  
> Sunja peya ae chauraaha ve!  
> Koyi na puchhe saada haal hun,  
> Na koyi dil da maahra ve!  

#### English Translation
> Friends of my youth have vanished and companions have expired,  
> The four-way crossroad lies deserted under the wind!  
> Not a single living soul inquires of our condition now,  
> Nor exists any physician wise enough to understand our heart!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The crossroads (*chauraaha*) as the site of total abandonment.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਬੱਸ ਇੱਕ ਹੌਕਾ ਬਾਕੀ ਰਹਿ ਗਿਆ,  
> ਜੋ ਸੀਨੇ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਉੱਠਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਮੌਤ ਹੀ ਦੱਸੂ ਭੇਦ ਇਹ ਸਾਰਾ,  
> ਜਦ ਸਾਹਾਂ ਦਾ ਧਾਗਾ ਟੁੱਟਦਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> بس اک ہوکا باقی رہ گیا،  
> جو سینے وچوں اٹھدا اے!  
> موت ہی دسو بھید ایہ سارا،  
> جد ساہاں دا دھاگا ٹٹدا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Bass ikk hoka baaki reh gaya,  
> Jo seene vichon utthda ae!  
> Maut hi dassoo bhed eh saara,  
> Jad saahaan da dhaaga tuttda ae!  

#### English Translation
> Only a solitary, shuddering sigh remains behind,  
> Rising like smoke from the ruins of my breast!  
> Only Death in her final revelation will unlock this riddle,  
> When the fragile thread of breathing snaps forever!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Death as the only philosopher who can explain the tragedy of life.*
