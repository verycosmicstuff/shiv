# Chiraag Bujh Challeya

### **ਚਿਰਾਗ਼ ਬੁੱਝ ਚੱਲਿਆ** • **چراغ بجھ چلیا**

#### *"The Oil Lamp Dies Down"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aarti (1971)](../09_aarti_1971.md)
- **Original Title**: ਚਿਰਾਗ਼ ਬੁੱਝ ਚੱਲਿਆ (چراغ بجھ چلیا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1971
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Aarti` `#FlickeringLamp` `#DyingBreath` `#Oil`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The oil in the lamp is exhausted; the wick gutters, flares one final time, and dies into smoke.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's physical collapse in late 1971 when his doctors told him his vital organs were giving out.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> A meditation on physical exhaustion, written after Shiv collapsed on stage in Delhi in late 1971.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਚਿਰਾਗ਼ ਬੁੱਝ ਚੱਲਿਆ ਸਾਡੀ ਜਿੰਦ ਦਾ,  
> ਤੇਲ ਮੁੱਕ ਗਿਆ ਕਟੋਰੇ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਲੌਅ ਕੰਬਦੀ ਏ ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਵਾਰੀ,  
> ਇਸ ਹਨੇਰੇ ਦੇ ਘੋਰੇ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> چراغ بجھ چلیا ساڈی جند دا،  
> تیل مک گیا کٹورے وچوں نی!  
> لوء کنبدی اے آخری واری،  
> اس ہنیرے دے گھورے وچوں نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Chiraag bujh challeya saadi jind da,  
> Tel mukk gaya katore vichon ni!  
> Lau kambda ae aakhiri vaari,  
> Iss hanere de ghore vichon ni!  

#### English Translation
> The oil lamp of our mortal existence is dying down,  
> The last drop of oil has vanished from its earthenware bowl!  
> The failing flame trembles for one final, desperate instant  
> Against the rising tide of absolute, encroaching dark!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Oil lamp as ancient symbol of the mortal body.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਧੂੰਆਂ ਉੱਠਿਆ ਚਿੱਟਾ-ਸਿਆਹ,  
> ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੜਕਣ ਲੱਗਾ ਵੇ!  
> ਸਾਹਾਂ ਦੀ ਡੋਰੀ ਟੁੱਟ ਰਹੀ ਏ,  
> ਦਿਲ ਵੀ ਮੱਠਾ ਧੜਕਣ ਲੱਗਾ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> دھواں اٹھیا چٹا سیاہ،  
> اکھیاں وچ رڑکਣ لگا وے!  
> ساہاں دی ڈوری ٹٹ رہی اے،  
> دل وی مٹھا دھڑکݨ لگا وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Dhuan uttheya chitta-siyaah,  
> Akhiyaan vich radkan lagga ve!  
> Saahaan di dori tutt rahi ae,  
> Dil vi mattha dharakan lagga ve!  

#### English Translation
> Pale grey smoke curls upward from the blackened wick,  
> Stinging our watering eyes with the smell of mortality!  
> The cord of breathing is snapping strand by delicate strand,  
> While the tired pulse of the heart slows to stillness!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Slowing heart (*mattha dharakan*) described with diagnostic accuracy.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਹਨੇਰਾ ਹੀ ਸਾਡਾ ਸਾਥੀ ਏ,  
> ਜੋ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਗਲ ਨਾਲ ਲਾਵੇਗਾ!  
> ਇਸ ਚਿਰਾਗ਼ ਦੇ ਬੁੱਝਣ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਵਾਂ ਸੂਰਜ ਨਾ ਆਵੇਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن ہنیرا ہی ساڈا ساتھی اے،  
> جو سانوں گل نال لاوے گا!  
> اس چراغ دے بجھن توں بعد،  
> کوئی نواں سورج نہ آوے گا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun hanera hi saada saathi ae,  
> Jo saanu gal naal laavega!  
> Iss chiraag de bujhan ton baad,  
> Koyi nawaan suraj na aavega!  

#### English Translation
> Now absolute darkness is our only faithful companion,  
> Waiting to fold our tired spirit into its boundless peace!  
> And after the final extinction of this frail earthly lamp,  
> No fresh morning sun shall ever disturb our slumber!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Rejection of rebirth or false hopes: peaceful extinction.*
