# Lahoo Da Dariya

### **ਲਹੂ ਦਾ ਦਰਿਆ** • **لہو دا دریا**

#### *"The River of Blood"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aarti (1971)](../09_aarti_1971.md)
- **Original Title**: ਲਹੂ ਦਾ ਦਰਿਆ (لہو دا دریا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1971
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Blood` `#Partition` `#War` `#Rivers`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The five rivers of Punjab turning into a singular river of blood through generational warfare, Partition, and communal violence.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed around the 1971 Indo-Pak war when Punjab borders flared up once again.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Included in the canonical Punjabi collection 'Aarti' (1971).

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> One of Shiv's fiercest anti-war and political poems.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Deep, thunderous, tragic cadence.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (4 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਹ ਪੰਜ ਦਰਿਆਵਾਂ ਦੀ ਧਰਤੀ ਸੀ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਦੁੱਧ ਤੇ ਘਿਓ ਦੇ ਵਹਿਣ ਵਗਦੇ!  
> ਅੱਜ ਵੇਖੋ ਇਸ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦੇ ਅੰਦਰ,  
> ਲਹੂ ਦੇ ਦਰਿਆ ਨੇ ਵਗਦੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ایہہ پنج دریاواں دی دھرتی سی،  
> جتھے ددھ تے گھئو دے وہن وگدے!  
> اج ویکھو اس پنجاب دے اندر،  
> لہو دے دریا نے وگدے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Eh panj daryavaan di dharti si,  
> Jithe dudh te ghio de vehn vagde!  
> Ajj vekho is Punjab de andar,  
> Lahoo de dariya ne vagde!  

#### English Translation
> This was once the blessed land of the Five Rivers,  
> Where torrents of milk and honey flowed in abundance!  
> Behold today inside this truncated Punjab:  
> Only rivers of human blood surge across the plains!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The pastoral legend of Punjab contrasted with contemporary bloodshed.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸੰਤਾਲੀ 'ਚ ਲੱਖਾਂ ਕਤਲ ਹੋਏ,  
> ਹੁਣ ਫ਼ੇਰ ਸਰਹੱਦਾਂ ਬਲ਼ਦੀਆਂ ਨੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਭਰਾ ਸੀ ਕਦੇ ਇੱਕੋ ਕੁੱਖ ਦੇ,  
> ਉਹਨਾਂ 'ਤੇ ਤੋਪਾਂ ਚੱਲਦੀਆਂ ਨੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> سنیتالی چ لکھاں قتل ہوئے،  
> ہن فیر سرحداں بلدیاں نے!  
> جو بھرا سی کدے اکو ککھ دے،  
> اوہناں تے توپاں چلدیاں نے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Sentaali ch lakkhan qatl hoye,  
> Hun fer sarhaddan baldiyaan ne!  
> Jo bhra si kade ikko kukkh de,  
> Ohnaan te topaan chaldiyaan ne!  

#### English Translation
> In 1947 hundreds of thousands were slaughtered,  
> And today once again the barbed borders blaze with war!  
> Brothers born of the very same maternal soil  
> Hurl artillery shells across the trenches at each other!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Direct condemnation of brother-against-brother warfare across the border.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕਿਸ ਲਈ ਇਹ ਲਹੂ ਵਹਾਉਂਦੇ ਹੋ,  
> ਕਿਸ ਦੇਸ਼ ਦੀ ਖ਼ਾਤਰ ਲੜਦੇ ਹੋ?  
> ਜਦ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦੋਹੀਂ ਪਾਸੀਂ ਇੱਕ ਏ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਕਿਉਂ ਨਫ਼ਰਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੜਦੇ ਹੋ?  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کس لئی ایہہ لہو وہاؤندے ہو،  
> کس دیس دی خاطر لڑدے ہو؟  
> جد مٹی دوہیں پاسیں اک اے،  
> تاں کیوں نفرت وچ سڑدے ہو؟  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kis layi eh lahoo vahaaunde ho,  
> Kis desh di khaatar lard-de ho?  
> Jad mitti doheen paaseen ikk ae,  
> Taan kyon nafrat vich sarhde ho?  

#### English Translation
> For whose benefit do you spill this innocent blood?  
> For what fabricated nation do you slaughter one another?  
> When the soil on both sides of the fence is one and the same,  
> Why do you burn in the inferno of communal hatred?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Radical questioning of nationalism that divides identical soil.*

* * *

### Stanza 4

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਆਖੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਗੀਤ ਲਹੂ ਵਿੱਚ ਡੁੱਬੇ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਕੀ ਗਾਵਾਂ, ਕੀ ਸੁਣਾਵਾਂ ਨੀ?  
> ਜਦ ਆਪਣਾ ਹੀ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਸੜਦਾ ਹੋਵੇ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਮੈਂ ਕਿਹੜੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਵਹਾਵਾਂ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو آکھے میرے گیت لہو وچ ڈبے،  
> میں کی گاواں، کی سناواں نی؟  
> جد اپنا ہی پنجاب سڑدا ہووے،  
> تاں میں کہڑے ہنجھو وہاواں نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv aakhe mere geet lahoo vich dubbe,  
> Main ki gaavaan, ki sunaavaan ni?  
> Jad apna hi Punjab sarhda hove,  
> Taan main kehre hanjhu vahaavaan ni!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv proclaims: my songs are drowned in living blood—  
> What melody can I sing, what verse can I recite?  
> When my own beloved Punjab burns in flames,  
> What tears are sufficient to mourn its destruction?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The silence of the poet when the homeland is engulfed in fire.*
