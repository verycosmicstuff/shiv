# Be-Vatan

### **ਬੇਵਤਨ** • **بے وطن**

#### *"The Stateless Wanderer"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Aarti (1971)](../09_aarti_1971.md)
- **Original Title**: ਬੇਵਤਨ (بے وطن)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1971
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Aarti` `#Homelessness` `#PartitionTrauma` `#Exile`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: The refugee poet realizes that after 1947, he was permanently stripped of a homeland; everywhere in India he remains an exile.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's enduring rootlessness following Partition, never feeling at home in Indian Punjab.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Written after Shiv realized he could never return to his birthplace Bara Pind Lohtian across the Pakistani border.

**Source**: *Shiv Batalvi: A Solitary and Passionate Singer* by Dr. O.P. Sharma (1979, Sterling Publishers, New Delhi, pp. 12–45, 180–205). [Primary Memoir]

> "Authoritative academic biography documenting his childhood in Bara Pind Lohtian (now Pakistan), the trauma of the 1947 Partition flight, his clerkship at the State Bank of India Chandigarh, and his final collapse in Kir Mangyal."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਸੀਂ ਬੇਵਤਨ ਮੁਸਾਫ਼ਿਰ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਕੋਈ ਦੇਸ ਨਾ ਰਿਹਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਜਨਮ ਲਿਆ ਸੀ ਬਚਪਨ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਉਹ ਪਿੰਡ ਵੀ ਪਰਦੇਸ ਹੋ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اسیں بےوطن مسافر ہاں،  
> ساڈا کوئی دیس نہ رہا نی!  
> جتھے جنم لیا سی بچپن وچ،  
> اوہ پنڈ وی پردیس ہو گیا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Aseen be-vatan musaafir haan,  
> Saada koyi des na reha ni!  
> Jithe janam leya si bachpan vich,  
> Oh pind vi parades ho gaya ni!  

#### English Translation
> We are stateless wanderers without an earthly home,  
> No territory on this map belongs to our soul!  
> That beloved village where our innocent childhood played  
> Has become an alien, forbidden country across barbed wire!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Bara Pind Lohtian turned into foreign soil by political partition.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਸ ਨਵੇਂ ਵਤਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਅਸੀਂ,  
> ਸਦਾ ਪਰਾਏ ਹੀ ਰਹੇ ਹਾਂ ਵੇ!  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਨੇ ਗਲ ਨਾਲ ਨਾ ਲਾਇਆ,  
> ਅਸੀਂ ਦਰਦ ਹੀ ਸਹੇ ਹਾਂ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اس نویں وطن وچ اسیں،  
> سدا پرائے ہی رہے ہاں وے!  
> کسے نے گل نال نہ لایا،  
> اسیں درد ہی سہے ہاں وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Iss navein vatan vich aseen,  
> Sadaa paraaye hi rahe haan ve!  
> Kise ne gal naal na laaya,  
> Aseen dard hi sahe haan ve!  

#### English Translation
> Within this adopted homeland of independent India  
> We remained uninvited strangers to the very end!  
> Not a single institution folded us into its embrace,  
> Leaving us only the solitary dignity of our wounds!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Alienation within the post-Partition Indian state.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਮੌਤ ਹੀ ਸਾਡਾ ਵਤਨ ਬਣੂ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਕੋਈ ਸਰਹੱਦ ਨਾ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ!  
> ਉੱਥੇ ਜਾ ਕੇ ਵਸਾਂਗੇ ਅਸੀਂ,  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੀ ਕੈਦ ਨਾ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن موت ہی ساڈا وطن بنو،  
> جتھے کوئی سرحد نہ ہووے گی!  
> ایتھے جا کے وساں گے اسیں،  
> جتھے برہا دی قید نہ ہووے گی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun maut hi saada vatan ban-oo,  
> Jithe koyi sarhadd na hovegi!  
> Utthe jaa ke vasaange aseen,  
> Jithe birha di kaid na hovegi!  

#### English Translation
> Now Death alone shall become our eternal homeland,  
> Where no barbed wire nor border checkposts exist!  
> In that boundless country we shall dwell in peace,  
> Where the cruel prison of separation cannot follow!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Death as the borderless homeland (*be-sarhadd vatan*).*
