# Doli Turi Kehraan Di

### **ਡੋਲੀ ਤੁਰੀ ਕਹਿਰਾਂ ਦੀ** • **ڈولی تری قہراں دی**

#### *"The Tragic Palanquin Moves Away"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਡੋਲੀ ਤੁਰੀ ਕਹਿਰਾਂ ਦੀ (ڈولی تری قہراں دی)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Doli` `#Palanquin` `#Betrothal` `#TragicDeparture` `#Majha`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `cremation_dusk`

**Synopsis**: Watching the bridal palanquin carry away the beloved while the lover stands by the village crossroads like a living corpse.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed after the wedding of the Gurdaspur magistrate's daughter, whom Shiv loved deeply.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Documents the definitive trauma of Shiv's life: the marriage of his first love to a district official, sealing his lifelong vow of mourning.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਡੋਲੀ ਤੁਰੀ ਕਹਿਰਾਂ ਦੀ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਜਨਾਜ਼ਾ ਨਿਕਲ ਗਿਆ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਮੁੱਖ ਨੂੰ ਪੂਜਿਆ ਕਰਦੇ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਗ਼ੈਰਾਂ ਦੇ ਘਰ ਚੱਲ ਗਿਆ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ڈولی تری قہراں دی اڑیو،  
> ساڈا جنازہ نکل گیا!  
> جس مکھ نوں پوجیا کردے سی،  
> اوہ غیراں دے گھر چل گیا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Doli turi kehraan di arhiyo,  
> Saada janaaza nikal gaya!  
> Jis mukkh nu poojeya karde si,  
> Oh gairaan de ghar chal gaya!  

#### English Translation
> The bridal palanquin has departed in devastating wrath, friends;  
> It is our funeral bier that has taken to the road!  
> That sacred face which we worshiped like a deity  
> Has crossed over into the threshold of strangers!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The bridal doli equated to a funeral bier (janaaza).*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕਹਾਰਾਂ ਨੇ ਚੁੱਕਿਆ ਕਾਠ ਦਾ ਡੋਲਾ,  
> ਪਰ ਸਾਡੇ ਸਾਹ ਘੁੱਟ ਦਿੱਤੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਫੁੱਲ ਸੀ ਸਾਡੇ ਪਿਆਰ ਦੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਰਾਹਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੁੱਟ ਦਿੱਤੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کہاراں نے چکیا کاٹھ دا ڈولا،  
> پر ساڈے ساہ گھٹ دتے!  
> جو پھل سی ساڈے پیار دے،  
> اوہ راہاں دے وچ سٹ دتے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kahaaraan ne chukkeya kaath da dola,  
> Par saade saah ghutt ditte!  
> Jo phull si saade pyaar de,  
> Oh raahaan de vich sutt ditte!  

#### English Translation
> The four bearers hoisted the wooden litter upon their shoulders,  
> Yet in doing so, they strangled the breath from our lungs!  
> Those fragile blossoms of our devotion  
> Were cast down and trampled in the roadside dust!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Palanquin bearers acting as unintentional executioners.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਪਿੰਡ ਦੀ ਜੂਹ 'ਚ ਖੜ੍ਹ ਕੇ ਸ਼ਿਵ,  
> ਕਿਸਨੂੰ ਆਵਾਜ਼ਾਂ ਮਾਰੇਗਾ?  
> ਜੋ ਤੁਰ ਗਿਆ ਉਹ ਮੁੜਨਾ ਨਹੀਂ,  
> ਇਹ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਗੁਜ਼ਾਰੇਗਾ?  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن پنڈ دی جوہ چ کھڑ کے شیو،  
> کس نوں آوازاں مارےگا؟  
> جو تر گیا اوہ مڑنا نہیں،  
> ایہہ زندگی کویں گزارےگا؟  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun pind di jooh ch kharh ke Shiv,  
> Kisnu aawaazaan maarega?  
> Jo tur gaya oh murhna nahin,  
> Eh zindagi kiven guzaarega?  

#### English Translation
> Standing alone upon the outer boundary of the village, Shiv,  
> Whom will you ever summon with your desperate cries?  
> She who has departed shall never return;  
> How will you ever survive the remainder of this hollow life?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The village border (jooh) as the limit of existence.*

## 🌾 Cultural Glossary & Regional Idioms

| Term | Pronunciation | Literal Meaning | Cultural & Poetic Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **ਡੋਲੀ (Doli)** | *Do-lee* | Bridal palanquin | Covered wooden litter in which a bride was carried to her in-laws' home, symbol of irreversible separation from paternal home. |
| **ਜੂਹ (Jooh)** | *Jooh* | Village pasture boundary | The geographical limit of the village beyond which lies the unknown outside world. |
