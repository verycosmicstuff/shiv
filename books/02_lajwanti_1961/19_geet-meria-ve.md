# Geet Meria Ve

### **ਗੀਤ ਮੇਰਿਆ ਵੇ** • **گیت میریا وے**

#### *"O My Song, Fly to Her Courtyard"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਗੀਤ ਮੇਰਿਆ ਵੇ (گیت میریا وے)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#PoeticMuse` `#Messenger` `#Trinjan`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: The poet commands his sorrowful song to take wing like an evening swallow and settle gently in the distant courtyard of the beloved.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv viewed his verses as living emissaries carrying physical pain across physical borders.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Often sung by Shiv in his signature high-pitched tarannum during all-night kavi sammelans in Delhi and Jalandhar.

**Source**: *All India Radio (AIR Jalandhar & Delhi) Station Retention Protocols* by Akashvani Archives Committee (1975, Directorate General, All India Radio, New Delhi, Spool Audit Report 1971–1975). [Institutional Record]

> "Historical administrative audit revealing that chronic shortages of 2-inch quadruplex magnetic video and 1/4-inch audio spools mandated routine bulk-erasure and re-use of tapes for daily bulletins, inadvertently destroying dozens of Batalvi’s live mushaira recordings."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਗੀਤ ਮੇਰਿਆ ਵੇ ਉੱਡ ਕੇ ਜਾਹ ਤੂੰ,  
> ਸੱਜਣਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿਹੜੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਹਿ ਜਾਹ ਵੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਦਰਦ ਮੇਰੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਲਦਾ,  
> ਉਹਦੇ ਕੰਨੀਂ ਹੌਲੀ ਦੇਣੇ ਕਹਿ ਜਾਹ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> گیت میریا وے اڈ کے جاہ توں،  
> سجناں دے ویہڑے وچ بہہ جاہ وے!  
> جو درد میرے سینے وچ بلدا،  
> اوہدے کنیں ہولی دینے کہہ جاہ وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Geet meria ve udd ke jaah tu,  
> Sajjnaan de vehre vich beh jaah ve!  
> Jo dard mere seene vich balda,  
> Ohde kanneen hauli dene keh jaah ve!  

#### English Translation
> O my song, spread your wings and take flight,  
> Descend softly into the courtyard of my beloved!  
> That burning agony which consumes my breast,  
> Whisper it tenderly into her listening ear!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The poem as an animistic, flying messenger bridging geographic estrangement.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜੇ ਉਹ ਪੁੱਛੇ ਮੇਰਾ ਹਾਲ ਕਦੇ,  
> ਤੂੰ ਆਖੀਂ ਉਹ ਤਾਂ ਜਿਊਂਦਾ ਏ!  
> ਬੱਸ ਤੇਰੇ ਦਿੱਤੇ ਗ਼ਮਾਂ ਦੀ ਮਾਲਾ,  
> ਹਰ ਪਲ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਨਾਲ ਪਰੋਂਦਾ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جے اوہ پچھے میرا حال کدے،  
> توں آکھیں اوہ تاں جیوندا اے!  
> بس تیرے دتے غماں دی مالا،  
> ہر پل ہنجھواں نال پروندا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Je oh puchhe mera haal kade,  
> Tu aakheen oh taan jiyunda ae!  
> Bass tere ditte ghamaan di maala,  
> Har pal hanjhuwan naal paronda ae!  

#### English Translation
> If ever she should inquire of my condition,  
> Answer her gently that he still draws breath!  
> Only that with the dark rosary of sorrows she gifted,  
> He threads unceasing tears with every passing heartbeat!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Rosary of sorrows (ghamaan di maala) blends Sufi remembrance with romantic devotion.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜੇ ਉਹ ਸੁਣ ਕੇ ਰੋ ਪਵੇ ਤਾਂ,  
> ਉਹਦੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਚੁੰਮ ਕੇ ਮੁੜ ਆਵੀਂ!  
> ਮੇਰੀ ਮੌਤ ਦਾ ਆਖ਼ਰੀ ਸਨੇਹਾ,  
> ਉਹਦੀ ਝੋਲੀ ਪਾ ਕੇ ਮੁੜ ਆਵੀਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جے اوہ سن کے رو پوے تاں،  
> اوہدے ہنجھو چم کے مڑ آویں!  
> میری موت دا آخری سنیہا،  
> اوہدی جھولی پا کے مڑ آویں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Je oh sun ke ro pave taan,  
> Ohde hanjhu chumm ke murh aaveen!  
> Meri maut da aakhiri suneha,  
> Ohdi jholi paa ke murh aaveen!  

#### English Translation
> And if she should weep upon hearing your melody,  
> Kiss her falling tears and return swiftly to me!  
> Placing the final testament of my impending death  
> Reverently into her lap before you take your leave!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Placing grief in the lap (*jholi*) evokes ancient Punjabi blessings and sacrifices.*
