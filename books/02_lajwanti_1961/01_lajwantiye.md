# Lajwantiye (Ni Lajwantiye)

### **ਲਾਜਵੰਤੀਏ (ਨੀ ਲਾਜਵੰਤੀਏ)** • **لاجونتیئے (نی لاجونتیئے)**

#### *"O Sensitive Mimosa Plant"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਲਾਜਵੰਤੀਏ (ਨੀ ਲਾਜਵੰਤੀਏ) (لاجونتیئے (نی لاجونتیئے))
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#Fragility` `#Touch` `#Modesty`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: The touch-me-not plant (mimosa) becomes a metaphor for female vulnerability and bruised modesty in an ungentle world.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala, dedicated to the fragile innocence of village maidens facing patriarchal scrutiny.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Title track of Shiv's second collection, published in 1961, which cemented his mastery of botanical symbolism.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> Praised for introducing botanical imagery into romantic Punjabi verse.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Tender, whisper-like tarannum cadence.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (4 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਨੀ ਲਾਜਵੰਤੀਏ ਨੀ ਛੋਹ ਨਾ ਸਹਾਰੀਏ,  
> ਤੈਨੂੰ ਪੌਣ ਵੀ ਛੂਹੇ ਤਾਂ ਕੁਮਲਾਵੇਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਕਿਹੜੀ ਰੁੱਤੇ ਤੂੰ ਜੰਮੀ ਏਂ ਧਰਤ 'ਤੇ,  
> ਜੋ ਹਰ ਝੋਕੇ ਤੋਂ ਡਰਦੀ ਜਾਵੇਂ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> نی لاجونتیئے نی چھوہ نہ سہاریئے،  
> تینوں پون وی چھوئے تاں کملاویں نی!  
> کہڑی رتے توں جمی ایں دھرتی تے،  
> جو ہر جھوکے توں ڈردی جاویں نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Ni Lajwantiye ni chhoh na sahaariye,  
> Tainu paun vi chhoohe taan kumlaaven ni!  
> Kehri rutte toon jammi ain dhart te,  
> Jo har jhoke ton dardi jaaven ni!  

#### English Translation
> O tender Mimosa, unable to bear even the lightest touch,  
> You wither if even the gentle breeze brushes your leaves!  
> In what cursed season were you born upon this earth,  
> That you tremble in fear before every passing gust?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The mimosa plant (touch-me-not) represents hyper-sensitive innocence in an abrasive world.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਤੇਰੇ ਪੱਤਿਆਂ 'ਚ ਲਿਪੀ ਸ਼ਰਮ ਏਨੀ,  
> ਕਿ ਸੂਰਜ ਵੀ ਵੇਖ ਸ਼ਰਮਾਵੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਦ ਰਾਤ ਪਵੇ ਤੇਰੇ ਵਿਹੜੇ 'ਚ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਚੰਨ ਵੀ ਪੈਰ ਦਬਾਵੇ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> تیرے پتیاں چ لپی شرم اینی،  
> کہ سورج وی ویکھ شرماوے نی!  
> جد رات پوے تیرے ویہڑے چ،  
> تاں چن وی پیر دباوے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Tere pattiyan ch lippi sharam aini,  
> Ki sooraj vi vekh sharmaave ni!  
> Jad raat pave tere vehre ch,  
> Taan chann vi pair dabaave ni!  

#### English Translation
> So deep is the bashfulness folded within your petals,  
> That even the burning sun turns away in shame!  
> When night descends upon your secluded courtyard,  
> Even the silver moon steps lightly upon tiptoe!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Personification of celestial bodies respecting her inviolate modesty.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਪਰ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਹੈ ਪੱਥਰਾਂ ਦੀ ਨਗਰੀ,  
> ਇੱਥੇ ਕੰਡਿਆਂ ਦਾ ਹੀ ਰਾਜ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿੱਥੇ ਫੁੱਲ ਵਾਂਗੂ ਖਿੜਨਾ ਮਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਏ,  
> ਉੱਥੇ ਕੌਣ ਸੁਣੇ ਤੇਰੀ ਆਵਾਜ਼ ਨੀ?  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> پر دنیا ہے پتھراں دی نگری،  
> ایتھے کنڈیاں دا ہی راج نی!  
> جتھے پھل وانگو کھڑنا منع اے،  
> اوتھے کون سنے تیری آواز نی؟  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Par duniya hai pattharan di nagri,  
> Itthe kandiyan da hi raaj ni!  
> Jithe phull vaangu khirhna manaan ae,  
> Outhe kaun sune teri aavaaz ni?  

#### English Translation
> Yet this mortal world is a city carved of stone,  
> Where thorns and briars alone hold dominion!  
> Where blooming like a tender flower is forbidden,  
> Who will pause to hear your fragile voice?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The poet laments the harsh, unfeeling reality of human society towards vulnerable souls.*

* * *

### Stanza 4

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਆਖੇ ਨੀ ਮੇਰੀਏ ਕਲੀਏ,  
> ਤੂੰ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਘੁੱਟ ਪੀ ਲੈ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੇ ਜੀਣਾ ਹੈ ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆ 'ਚ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਅੱਥਰੂ ਪੀ ਕੇ ਜੀ ਲੈ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو آکھے نی میریئے کلیئے،  
> توں برہا دے گھٹ پی لے نی!  
> جے جینا ہے اس دنیا چ،  
> تاں اتھرو پی کے جی لے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv aakhe ni meriye kaliye,  
> Toon birha de ghutt pee lai ni!  
> Je jeena hai is duniya ch,  
> Taan atthru pee ke jee lai ni!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv says to you, O my fragile blossom:  
> Drink down the bitter sips of separation!  
> If you must survive in this unsparing world,  
> You must learn to swallow your own tears and endure!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The conclusion offers the only survival strategy for the sensitive poet: inner containment of grief.*
