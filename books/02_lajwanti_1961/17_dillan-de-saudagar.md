# Dillan De Saudagar

### **ਦਿਲਾਂ ਦੇ ਸੌਦਾਗਰ** • **دلاں دے سوداگر**

#### *"The Merchants of Broken Hearts"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਦਿਲਾਂ ਦੇ ਸੌਦਾਗਰ (دلاں دے سوداگر)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Merchants` `#Marketplace` `#LoveasCommodity` `#City` `#Modernity`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `modernism` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: In the cynical bazaar of modern society, love and bleeding hearts are weighed and traded like ordinary merchandise.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala as publishers and agents began profiting off Shiv's early verses while leaving him penniless.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Reflects Shiv's first encounters with the commercial publishing world of Lahore and Jalandhar, where poetry was traded as merchandise.

**Source**: *Punjabi Kavi Ate Kavitriyan (Marxist Critical Essays)* by Sant Singh Sekhon (1972, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, pp. 89–112). [Literary Criticism]

> "Sant Singh Sekhon termed Shiv the "Keats of Punjabi poetry" for his meter and folk metaphor, but charged him with escapism, arguing that romanticizing youth-suicide was reactionary."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਸ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ 'ਚ ਵੱਸਦੇ ਨੇ ਸੌਦਾਗਰ,  
> ਜੋ ਦਿਲਾਂ ਦਾ ਸੌਦਾ ਕਰਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਦੇ ਹੰਝੂ ਤੋਲ ਕੇ ਉਹ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਗ਼ਮ ਦੀਆਂ ਕੀਮਤਾਂ ਧਰਦੇ ਨੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اس شہر چ وسدے نے سوداگر،  
> جو دلاں دا سودا کردے نے!  
> اکھیاں دے ہنجھو تول کے اوہ،  
> ساڈے غم دیاں قیمتاں دھردے نے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Iss shehar ch vassde ne saudagar,  
> Jo dillan da sauda karde ne!  
> Akhiyan de hanjhu tol ke oh,  
> Saade gham diyan keemataan dharde ne!  

#### English Translation
> In this modern city dwell cold merchants  
> Who trade in living, beating human hearts!  
> Weighing the tears of our eyes on commercial scales,  
> They affix cynical price tags upon our sacred grief!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The commodification of artistic suffering in the capitalist city.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਸੀਂ ਆਪਣਾ ਆਪ ਲੁਟਾ ਬੈਠੇ,  
> ਇਸ ਝੂਠੇ ਕਾਰੋਬਾਰ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ!  
> ਸਾਨੂੰ ਕੱਖੋਂ ਹੌਲੇ ਕਰ ਦਿੱਤਾ,  
> ਇਸ ਮਤਲਬੀ ਸੰਸਾਰ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اسیں اپنا آپ لٹا بیٹھے،  
> اس جھوٹھے کاروبار دے وچ!  
> سانوں ککھوں ہولے کر دتا،  
> اس مطلبی سنسار دے وچ!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Assin aapna aap lutaa baithe,  
> Iss jhoothe kaarobaar de vich!  
> Saanu kakhon haule kar ditta,  
> Iss matlabi sansaar de vich!  

#### English Translation
> We squandered our entire being and identity  
> Within the gears of this fraudulent marketplace!  
> They rendered us lighter than dry straw  
> Inside this ruthless world of selfish exploitation!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Total spiritual bankruptcy resulting from uneven commercial exchange.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਕਹਿੰਦਾ ਪਰ ਇਹ ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਮੁੱਲ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਦੌਲਤ ਦੇ ਨਾ ਪਾਊਗੀ!  
> ਜਦ ਕਲਮ ਚੱਲੇਗੀ ਸ਼ਾਇਰ ਦੀ,  
> ਤਾਂ ਸਾਰੀ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਗਾਊਗੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو کہندا پر ایہہ دل دا مل،  
> کوئی دولت دے نہ پاؤگی!  
> جد قلم چلےگی شاعر دی،  
> تاں ساری دنیا گاؤگی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv kehnda par eh dil da mull,  
> Koyi daulat de na paaugi!  
> Jad kalam challegi shaayar di,  
> Taan saari duniya gaaugi!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv declares: 'No mountain of worldly wealth  
> Can ever purchase the true worth of this heart!  
> When the pen of the poet begins its fiery dance,  
> The whole world shall rise to sing these verses!'  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Art's transcendent victory over market commercialism.*
