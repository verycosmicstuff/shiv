# Kothe Te Kankan

### **ਕੋਠੇ ਤੇ ਕਣਕਾਂ** • **کوٹھے تے کنکاں**

#### *"Wheat Drying on the Mud Rooftop"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਕੋਠੇ ਤੇ ਕਣਕਾਂ (کوٹھے تے کنکاں)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#AgrarianDomestic` `#Rooftop` `#Harvest`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: A young bride spreads wheat grains upon the mud rooftop under the midday sun, weeping as pigeons peck away her youth.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv observed village women singing mournful verses on rooftops in the afternoon quiet.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Depicts the domestic chore of village women spreading harvested wheat to dry before milling.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕੋਠੇ ਤੇ ਕਣਕਾਂ ਸੁੱਕਣੇ ਪਾਈਆਂ,  
> ਅੱਖੀਆਂ ਵਿੱਚੋਂ ਝੜੀਆਂ ਕਣੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਕਿਸ ਨੂੰ ਦੱਸਾਂ ਆਪਣੇ ਦਿਲ ਦਾ ਦੁੱਖੜਾ,  
> ਜੋ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਿਰ ਤੇ ਬਣੀਆਂ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کوٹھے تے کنکاں سکنے پائیاں،  
> اکھیاں وچوں جھڑیاں کنیاں نی!  
> کس نوں دساں اپنے دل دا دکھڑا،  
> جو میرے سر تے بنیاں نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kothe te kankan sukkne paaiyaan,  
> Akhiyaan vichon jharhiyaan kaniyaan ni!  
> Kis nu dassaan apne dil da dukhrha,  
> Jo mere sir te baniyaan ni!  

#### English Translation
> I spread the golden wheat upon the flat mud roof to dry,  
> While from my brimming eyes fell drops like bitter rain!  
> To whom can I confess the affliction of my wounded heart,  
> And the heavy trials that have gathered upon my head?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Wheat drying in the sun contrasted with tears raining down.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਚਿੜੀਆਂ ਆਈਆਂ ਚੋਗ ਚੁਗਣ ਲਈ,  
> ਮੇਰਾ ਜੋਬਨ ਖਾ ਗਈਆਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਹਾਸਿਆਂ ਨਾਲ ਸੀ ਵਿਹੜਾ ਗੂੰਜਦਾ,  
> ਉਹ ਸਭ ਰੋਣੇ ਪਾ ਗਈਆਂ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> چڑیاں آئیاں چوگ چگن لئی،  
> میرا جوبن کھا گئیاں نی!  
> جنہاں ہاسیاں نال سی ویہڑا گونجدا،  
> اوہ سبھ رونے پا گئیاں نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Chiriyaan aaiyaan chog chugan layi,  
> Mera joban khaa gaiyaan ni!  
> Jinhaan haasiyaan naal si vehra goonjda,  
> Oh sabh rone paa gaiyaan ni!  

#### English Translation
> Flocks of hungry sparrows arrived to peck the grain,  
> And pecked away the prime of my blooming youth!  
> Those sweet laughter that once resonated across the courtyard  
> Have dissolved into unceasing funeral wails!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Grain pecking becomes an animistic metaphor for youth being devoured by time.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਾਮ ਢਲੀ ਤੇ ਕਣਕ ਸਮੇਟੀ,  
> ਦਰਦ ਨਾ ਪਰ ਸਮੇਟਿਆ ਜਾਵੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਇਸ ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੀ ਅੱਗ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੜਦੀ ਜਿੰਦ,  
> ਕਦੇ ਨਾ ਬੁਝਣੇ ਪਾਵੇ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شام ڈھلی تے کنک سمیٹی،  
> درد نہ پر سمیٹیا جاوے نی!  
> اس برہا دی اگ وچ سڑدی جند،  
> کدے نہ بجھنے پاوے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shaam dhali te kanak sameti,  
> Dard na par sametiya jaave ni!  
> Iss birha di agg vich sarhdi jind,  
> Kade na bujhne paave ni!  

#### English Translation
> Dusk descended and I gathered the dried wheat in my skirt,  
> Yet the boundless agony of my heart could not be gathered!  
> This fragile life burning in the furnace of separation  
> Finds no water to quench its consuming flame!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Domestic gathering (sametna) contrasted with uncontainable grief.*
