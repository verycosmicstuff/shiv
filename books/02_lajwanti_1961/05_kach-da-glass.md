# Kach Da Glass

### **ਕੱਚ ਦਾ ਗਲਾਸ** • **کچ دا گلاس**

#### *"The Tumbler of Brittle Glass"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਕੱਚ ਦਾ ਗਲਾਸ (کچ دا گلاس)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Glass` `#Fragility` `#Heart` `#Modernity`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `modernism` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: Comparing the human soul to a fragile drinking glass that shatters into unrepairable shards.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Penned in a local Batala tea stall after observing a glass shatter on the stone floor.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Marked Shiv's introduction of everyday industrial/domestic objects into modern Punjabi lyrics.

**Source**: *Punjabi Kavi Ate Kavitriyan (Marxist Critical Essays)* by Sant Singh Sekhon (1972, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, pp. 89–112). [Literary Criticism]

> "Sant Singh Sekhon termed Shiv the "Keats of Punjabi poetry" for his meter and folk metaphor, but charged him with escapism, arguing that romanticizing youth-suicide was reactionary."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> Demonstrates modern domestic objects replacing classical Sufi symbols.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Brittle, staccato cadence.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸਾਡਾ ਦਿਲ ਸੀ ਕੱਚ ਦਾ ਗਲਾਸ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਜੋ ਠੋਕਰ ਖਾ ਕੇ ਟੁੱਟ ਗਿਆ!  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਨੇ ਚੁੱਕ ਕੇ ਜੋੜਿਆ ਨਾ,  
> ਸਭ ਕੁਝ ਪਲਾਂ 'ਚ ਲੁੱਟ ਗਿਆ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ساڈا دل سی کچ دا گلاس اڑیو،  
> جو ٹھوکر کھا کے ٹٹ گیا!  
> کسے نے چک کے جوڑیا نہ،  
> سبھ کچھ پلاں چ لٹ گیا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Saada dil si kach da glass arhiyo,  
> Jo thokar khaa ke tutt gaya!  
> Kise ne chukk ke jorheya na,  
> Sabh kujh palaan ch lutt gaya!  

#### English Translation
> Our heart was like a tumbler of fragile glass, friends,  
> Which was kicked by careless feet and shattered!  
> Not a soul bent down to gather and mend the pieces,  
> And in a matter of seconds, our whole world was plundered!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The modern glass object signifies the fragility of urban emotional bonds.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕਿਰਚਾਂ ਖਿਲਰੀਆਂ ਰਾਹਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਲੰਘਣ ਵਾਲ਼ੇ ਲਤਾੜ ਗਏ!  
> ਜੋ ਆਏ ਸੀ ਮੱਲ੍ਹਮ ਲਾਉਣ ਬਹਾਨੇ,  
> ਉਹ ਹੋਰ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਸਾੜ ਗਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کرچاں کھلرویاں راہاں دے وچ،  
> لنگھن والے لتاڑ گئے!  
> جو آئے سی ملہم لاون بہانے،  
> اوہ ہور زخماں نوں ساڑ گئے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kirchaan khilariyaan raahaan de vich,  
> Langhan waale lataarh gaye!  
> Jo aaye si mallham laaun bahaane,  
> Oh hor zakhmaan nu saarh gaye!  

#### English Translation
> The sharp shards were scattered across the open road,  
> Trampled underfoot by indifferent passersby!  
> Those who arrived under the pretext of bringing healing salve  
> Only poured burning acid into the fresh lacerations!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *False sympathy proved more destructive than direct violence.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਕੱਚ ਦੇ ਟੁਕੜੇ ਚਮਕਣ ਪਏ,  
> ਲੋਕ ਕਹਿੰਦੇ ਇਹ ਹੀਰੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਆਖੇ ਮੇਰੇ ਟੁੱਟੇ ਸੁਪਨੇ,  
> ਸਦਾ ਰਹਿੰਦੇ ਦਿਲਗੀਰੇ ਨੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن کچ دے ٹکڑے چمکن پئے،  
> لوک کہندے ایہہ ہیرے نے!  
> شیو آکھے میرے ٹٹے سپنے،  
> سدا رہندے دلگیرے نے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun kach de tukre chamkan paye,  
> Lok kehnde eh heere ne!  
> Shiv aakhe mere tutte supne,  
> Sada rehnde dilgeere ne!  

#### English Translation
> Now the glittering shards catch the light and gleam,  
> And shallow people proclaim: 'Behold, these are diamonds!'  
> Shiv says: 'These are merely the fragments of my shattered dreams,  
> Eternally drenched in sorrow and tears!'  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Society mistakes the sparkling wreckage of a poet's life for decorative entertainment.*
