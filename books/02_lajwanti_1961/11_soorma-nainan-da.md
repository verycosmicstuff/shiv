# Soorma Nainan Da

### **ਸੂਰਮਾ ਨੈਣਾਂ ਦਾ** • **سورما نیناں دا**

#### *"The Kohl Lining Her Weeping Eyes"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸੂਰਮਾ ਨੈਣਾਂ ਦਾ (سورما نیناں دا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Kohl` `#Tears` `#Eyes` `#Beauty` `#Mourning`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: The black kohl applied to beautify maidenly eyes is melted and washed away by nocturnal tears of longing.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Written during the period when Shiv's muse was married into a wealthy household, leaving her in gilded sorrow.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> A quintessential example of Shiv subverting traditional Sringara (romantic adornment) into Karuna (tragic mourning).

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਤੇਰੇ ਨੈਣਾਂ ਦਾ ਸੂਰਮਾ ਅੜੀਏ,  
> ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਵਹਿ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਸੁਪਨਾ ਅਸੀਂ ਵੇਖਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹ ਅਧੂਰਾ ਹੀ ਰਹਿ ਗਿਆ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> تیرے نیناں دا سورما اڑیئے،  
> ہنجھواں دے وچ وہہ گیا نی!  
> جو سپنا اسیں ویکھیا سی،  
> اوہ ادھورا ہی رہ گیا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Tere nainan da soorma arhiye,  
> Hanjuwan de vich veh gaya ni!  
> Jo supna aseen vekheya si,  
> Oh adhoora hi reh gaya ni!  

#### English Translation
> The dark kohl lining your tender eyes, beloved,  
> Has washed away in torrential cascades of tears!  
> That luminous dream we dared to envision together  
> Remains forever shattered and unfulfilled!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Cosmetic beauty undone by genuine human agony.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਕਾਲ਼ੀ ਰਾਤ ਵੀ ਰੋ ਪਈ ਵੇਖ ਕੇ,  
> ਤੇਰੀ ਅੱਖ ਦੀ ਲਾਲੀ ਨੂੰ!  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਨੇ ਪੁੱਛਿਆ ਨਾ ਮਾਲਣ ਤੋਂ,  
> ਇਸ ਸੁੱਕੀ ਹੋਈ ਡਾਲੀ ਨੂੰ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> کالی رات وی رو پئی ویکھ کے،  
> تیری اکھ دی لالی نوں!  
> کسے نے پچھیا نہ مالن توں،  
> اس سکی ہوئی ڈالی نوں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Kaali raat vi ro payi vekh ke,  
> Teri akh di laali nu!  
> Kise ne puchheya na maalan ton,  
> Iss sukki hoyi daali nu!  

#### English Translation
> Even the pitch-black midnight broke into tears  
> Witnessing the bloodshot inflammation of your weeping eyes!  
> Not a single patron asked of the gardener woman  
> Concerning this withered, flowerless branch!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The bloodshot eye as evidence of sleepless lamentation.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਸੂਰਮਾ ਪਾ ਕੇ ਕੀ ਕਰਨਾ,  
> ਜਦ ਵੇਖਣ ਵਾਲ਼ਾ ਤੁਰ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਦਿਲ ਤਾਂ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਹੋਇਆ,  
> ਤੇਰਾ ਗ਼ਮ ਸੀਨੇ 'ਚ ਖੁਰ ਗਿਆ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن سورما پا کے کی کرنا،  
> جد ویکھن والا تر گیا اے!  
> شیو دا دل تاں مٹی ہویا،  
> تیرا غم سینے چ کھر گیا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun soorma paa ke ki karna,  
> Jad vekhan waala tur gaya ae!  
> Shiv da dil taan mitti hoya,  
> Tera gham seene ch khur gaya ae!  

#### English Translation
> What purpose remains in adorning the eyes with kohl  
> When he who loved to gaze into them has departed forever?  
> Shiv's heart has dissolved into anonymous earth,  
> And your sorrow has melted eternally into his breast!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Adornment becomes meaningless in the beloved's absence.*
