# Sohne Rang Da Geet

### **ਸੋਹਣੇ ਰੰਗ ਦਾ ਗੀਤ** • **سوہنے رنگ دا گیت**

#### *"Song of Beautiful Hues"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸੋਹਣੇ ਰੰਗ ਦਾ ਗੀਤ (سوہنے رنگ دا گیت)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#LyricMelody` `#FolkSinger` `#TragicSong`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: The poet weaves a song of breathtaking musical color, only to discover that the truest dye is the dark crimson of blood.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv's meditation on his own craft: that great lyrical beauty requires the sacrifice of the singer's vitality.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Shiv composed this after listening to wandering Mirasi folk bards singing Heer at a Batala shrine.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸੋਹਣੇ ਰੰਗ ਦਾ ਗੀਤ ਬਣਾਇਆ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਆਪਣੇ ਲਹੂ ਨੂੰ ਘੋਲ ਕੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਦੁਨੀਆਂ ਸਮਝੇ ਇਹ ਤਾਂ ਮੌਜ ਦੀ ਗੱਲ ਏ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਗਾਇਆ ਦਿਲ ਨੂੰ ਫੋਲ ਕੇ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> سوہنے رنگ دا گیت بنایا،  
> میں اپنے لہو نوں گھول کے نی!  
> دنیا سمجھے ایہ تاں موج دی گل اے،  
> میں گایا دل نوں پھول کے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Sohne rang da geet banaaya,  
> Main apne lahoo nu ghol ke ni!  
> Duniya samjhe eh taan mauj di gall ae,  
> Main gaaya dil nu fole ke ni!  

#### English Translation
> I crafted a song of breathtaking, radiant color  
> By dissolving my own warm blood into the verse!  
> The callous world assumed it was a merry pastime,  
> While I sang having flayed open the depths of my soul!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Metaphor of dissolving blood (lahoo ghol ke) to create art.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸੁਰਾਂ ਤਾਂ ਸਭ ਨੇ ਸੁਣ ਲਈਆਂ ਨੇ,  
> ਪਰ ਦਰਦ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾ ਵੇਖਿਆ ਵੇ!  
> ਜੋ ਅੱਗ ਮੇਰੇ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਬਲਦੀ,  
> ਉਸ ਨੂੰ ਕਿਸੇ ਨਾ ਸੇਕਿਆ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> سراں تاں سبھ نے سن لئیاں نے،  
> پر درد کسے نہ ویکھیا وے!  
> جو اگ میرے ہنجھواں وچ بلدی،  
> اس نوں کسے نہ سیکیا وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Suraan taan sabh ne sun laiyaan ne,  
> Par dard kise na vekheya ve!  
> Jo agg mere hanjhuwan vich baldi,  
> Uss nu kise na sekeya ve!  

#### English Translation
> All ears readily drunk the intoxicating melody,  
> Yet not a single soul perceived the underlying agony!  
> The wild fire that raged within my weeping tears  
> Remained unacknowledged by the cheering listeners!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The tragedy of the popular singer: audiences applaud the beauty of the wound.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਹ ਗੀਤ ਮੇਰੇ ਮਰਨ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਵੀ,  
> ਹਵਾਵਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਗੂੰਜਣਗੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਜੋ ਅੱਜ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਕਮਲਾ ਆਖਦੇ ਨੇ,  
> ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੇਰੀ ਕਬਰ ਨੂੰ ਚੁੰਮਣਗੇ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ایہ گیت میرے مرن توں بعد وی،  
> ہواواں وچ گونجن گے نی!  
> جو اج مینوں کملا آکھدے نے،  
> کلھ میری قبر نوں چمن گے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Eh geet mere maran ton baad vi,  
> Hawaawan vich goonjange ni!  
> Jo ajj mainu kamla aakhde ne,  
> Kallh meri kabr nu chummange ni!  

#### English Translation
> These songs long after my physical departure  
> Shall continue to reverberate through the winds of Punjab!  
> Those who mock me today as a madman lost in despair  
> Will tomorrow kneel to kiss the soil of my silent grave!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Prophetic declaration of post-mortem vindication.*
