# Chambe Di Booti

### **ਚੰਬੇ ਦੀ ਬੂਟੀ** • **چنبے دی بوٹی**

#### *"Jasmine Plant in My Breast"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਚੰਬੇ ਦੀ ਬੂਟੀ (چنبے دی بوٹی)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#SufiSymbolism` `#SultanBahu` `#Chamba`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: The plant of jasmine (white unrequited love) rooted deep within the poet's breast grows fragrant on the nourishment of tears.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv merges medieval Sufi allegory with twentieth-century romantic lyricism.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Direct homage to Sufi master Sultan Bahu's famous Abyat: 'Alif Allah chambe di booti murshid mann vich laai hoo'.

**Source**: *Punjabi Kavi Ate Kavitriyan (Marxist Critical Essays)* by Sant Singh Sekhon (1972, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, pp. 89–112). [Literary Criticism]

> "Sant Singh Sekhon termed Shiv the "Keats of Punjabi poetry" for his meter and folk metaphor, but charged him with "palayanwad" (reactionary escapism), arguing that romanticizing youth-suicide was reactionary in post-Partition agrarian Punjab."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੇਰੇ ਸੀਨੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਚੰਬੇ ਦੀ ਬੂਟੀ,  
> ਇਸ਼ਕ ਤੇਰੇ ਨੇ ਲਾਈ ਵੇ!  
> ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਦਾ ਮੈਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਪਾਇਆ,  
> ਜਦੋਂ ਖ਼ਿਜ਼ਾਂ ਦੀ ਰੁੱਤ ਆਈ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میرے سینے وچ چنبے دی بوٹی،  
> عشق تیرے نے لائی وے!  
> ہنجھواں دا میں پانی پایا،  
> جدوں خزاں دی رت آئی وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Mere seene vich chambe di booti,  
> Ishq tere ne laayi ve!  
> Hanjhuwan da main paani paaya,  
> Jadon khizaan di rutt aayi ve!  

#### English Translation
> A fragile plant of white jasmine  
> Your unsparing love planted within my breast!  
> With the salt water of my weeping eyes I nourished its root  
> When the chilling season of autumn stripped the boughs!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Direct dialogue with Sultan Bahu's Sufi imagery of the spiritual plant.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਇਸ ਬੂਟੀ ਨੂੰ ਫੁੱਲ ਜੋ ਲੱਗੇ,  
> ਬਿਰਹਾ ਦੇ ਰੰਗ ਵਿੱਚ ਰੰਗੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਜਾਣੇ ਇਹ ਮਹਿਕ ਉਦਾਸੀ,  
> ਜੋ ਦਿਲ ਦੇ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮਾਂ ਤੋਂ ਮੰਗੇ ਨੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اس بوٹی نوں پھل جو لگے،  
> برہا دے رنگ وچ رنگے نے!  
> کوئی نہ جانے ایہ مہک اداسی،  
> جو دل دے زخماں توں منگے نے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Iss booti nu phull jo lagge,  
> Birha de rang vich range ne!  
> Koyi na jaane eh mehek udaasi,  
> Jo dil de zakhmaan ton mange ne!  

#### English Translation
> The delicate blossoms that sprouted upon this stem  
> Are dyed in the deep, somber hues of separation!  
> None in this indifferent world discerns that sad perfume  
> Extracted from the open wounds of my broken heart!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Fragrance of jasmine equated with distilled grief.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜਦ ਮੇਰੀ ਜਿੰਦ ਮੁੱਕ ਜਾਵੇਗੀ,  
> ਇਹ ਬੂਟੀ ਕਬਰ ਤੇ ਖਿੜੇਗੀ ਵੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਮਰਨ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਵੀ ਸੱਜਣਾ,  
> ਤੇਰੇ ਪੈਰਾਂ ਹੇਠਾਂ ਵਿਛੇਗੀ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جد میری جند مک جاوے گی،  
> ایہ بوٹی قبر تے کھڑے گی وے!  
> میرے مرن توں بعد وی سجنا،  
> تیرے پیراں ہیٹھاں وچھے گی وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Jad meri jind mukk jaavegi,  
> Eh booti kabr te khirhegi ve!  
> Mere maran ton baad vi sajjna,  
> Tere pairaan hethaan vichhegi ve!  

#### English Translation
> When this weary thread of life snaps at last,  
> This jasmine plant shall blossom above my quiet tomb!  
> Even long after I am dust, O unyielding beloved,  
> Its fallen petals will carpet the path beneath your feet!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Post-mortem devotion: unrequited love extending past cremation into vegetative rebirth.*
