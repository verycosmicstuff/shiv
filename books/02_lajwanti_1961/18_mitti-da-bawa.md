# Mitti Da Bawa

### **ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦਾ ਬਾਵਾ** • **مٹی دا باوا**

#### *"The Clay Idol That Will Not Speak"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦਾ ਬਾਵਾ (مٹی دا باوا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Lajwanti` `#FolkMyth` `#ClayDoll` `#MittidaBawa`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: The beloved is likened to an unhearing, mute clay doll (mitti da bawa) who remains impassive despite tearful prayers and devotion.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Shiv took popular folk refrains sung by village women at spinning bees and gave them dark psychological resonance.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Direct adaptation of the ancient Punjabi folk song 'Mitti da bawa nayin bolda', transformed into a modernist meditation on unresponsive love.

**Source**: *Nagmani (Special Memorial Edition on Shiv Kumar)* by Amrita Pritam (1973, Nagmani Press, New Delhi, Vol. 85, June 1973). [Primary Memoir]

> "Amrita Pritam writes: "Death did not steal Shiv; Shiv courted death as his final bride." She catalogs his uncollected verses and reflects on the intellectual hostility he faced from dogmatic critics."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦਾ ਬਾਵਾ ਨਈਂ ਬੋਲਦਾ,  
> ਮੈਂ ਲੱਖ ਵਾਰੀ ਸਦਕੇ ਜਾਵਾਂ ਨੀ!  
> ਸੀਨੇ ਨਾਲ ਲਾ ਕੇ ਰੋਂਦੀ ਰਵਾਂ,  
> ਕਿਵੇਂ ਆਪਣਾ ਹਾਲ ਸੁਣਾਵਾਂ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> مٹی دا باوا نہیں بولدا،  
> میں لکھ واری صدقے جاواں نی!  
> سینے نال لا کے روندی رواں،  
> کویں اپنا حال سناواں نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Mitti da bawa nayin bolda,  
> Main lakkh vaari sadke jaawan ni!  
> Seene naal laa ke rondi rawaan,  
> Kiven apna haal sunaawan ni!  

#### English Translation
> This idol of clay will not utter a single word,  
> Though a hundred thousand times I sacrifice my soul!  
> Pressing it tightly against my weeping breast,  
> How can I speak the anguish that consumes my life?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Reframes the folk lyric of maternal loss into a metaphor for emotionally deadened romantic devotion.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਨਾ ਇਹ ਹੱਸੇ ਨਾ ਇਹ ਰੋਵੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਗੱਲ ਦਿਲ ਦੀ ਫੋਲੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਪੱਥਰਾਂ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ ਚੁੱਪ ਸਾਧੀ ਏ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਭੇਦ ਅੰਦਰ ਦਾ ਨਾ ਖੋਲ੍ਹੇ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> نہ ایہ ہسے نہ ایہ رووے،  
> نہ کوئی گل دل دی پھولے نی!  
> پتھراں وانگوں چپ سادھی اے،  
> کوئی بھید اندر دا نہ کھولھے نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Na eh hasse na eh rove,  
> Na koyi gall dil di fole ni!  
> Patthraan vaangoo chup saadhi ae,  
> Koyi bhed andar da na khole ni!  

#### English Translation
> It neither smiles in affection nor weeps in pity,  
> Nor shares the hidden secrets of its silent heart!  
> Maintaining a grave silence like cold river granite,  
> It refuses to unlock the mystery of its soul!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The unresponsive lover rendered as an inanimate pagan icon.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜੇ ਮੀਂਹ ਵਰ੍ਹਿਆ ਤਾਂ ਖੁਰ ਜਾਵੇਗਾ,  
> ਇਹ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਦਾ ਪਰਾਹੁਣਾ ਵੇ!  
> ਮੇਰੇ ਨੈਣਾਂ ਦੇ ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਇਹਨੇ ਇੱਕ ਦਿਨ ਰੁੜ੍ਹ ਜਾਣਾ ਵੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جے مینہہ ورھیا تاں کھر جاوے گا،  
> ایہ مٹی دا پرہونا وے!  
> میرੇ نیناں دے ہنجھواں وچ،  
> ایہنے اک دن رڑھ جانا وے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Je meenh varheya taan khur jaavega,  
> Eh mitti da parahuna ve!  
> Mere nainaan de hanjhuwan vich,  
> Ehne ikk din rurh jaana ve!  

#### English Translation
> When the heavy monsoon rain falls upon the courtyard,  
> This guest of unbaked clay will dissolve into mud!  
> In the torrential flood of my own streaming tears,  
> It is destined one day to wash away without trace!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Fragility of the clay figure: human emotion dissolves the very object it adores.*
