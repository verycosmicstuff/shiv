# Chamba Khidheya

### **ਚੰਬਾ ਖਿੜਿਆ** • **چنبا کھڑیا**

#### *"The White Jasmine has Blossomed"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਚੰਬਾ ਖਿੜਿਆ (چنبا کھڑیا)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Jasmine` `#Beauty` `#Memory` `#Aroma`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: The scent of white jasmine revives the painful memory of a touch that cannot return.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Jasmine bushes lined the courtyards of Batala where Shiv spent his early twenties.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Celebrates the fragrant memory of Shiv's lost love, contrasting blooming nature with decaying inner hope.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> Celebrated for olfactory imagery.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Melodic, intoxicating tune.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਚੰਬਾ ਖਿੜਿਆ ਵਿਹੜੇ ਮੇਰੇ,  
> ਤੇਰੀ ਯਾਦ ਲੈ ਕੇ ਆਇਆ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ਬੋ ਨੂੰ ਭੁੱਲ ਗਿਆ ਸਾਂ,  
> ਉਸ ਨੇ ਫ਼ੇਰ ਜਗਾਇਆ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> چنبا کھڑیا ویہڑے میرے،  
> تیری یاد لے کے آیا نی!  
> جس خوشبو نوں بھل گیا ساں،  
> اس نے فیر جگایا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Chamba khirheya vehre mere,  
> Teri yaad lai ke aaya ni!  
> Jis khushboo nu bhull gaya saan,  
> Us ne fer jagaaya ni!  

#### English Translation
> The white jasmine has blossomed in my courtyard,  
> Bringing the sudden, intoxicating remembrance of you!  
> That fragrance which I strove so hard to forget  
> Has once again awakened my sleeping wounds!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Involuntary memory stimulated by nature's scent.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਫੁੱਲਾਂ ਦੀ ਹਰ ਪੱਤੀ ਉੱਤੇ,  
> ਤੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ ਲਿਖਿਆ ਜਾਪੇ ਨੀ!  
> ਹਵਾ ਵਗੇ ਤਾਂ ਐਵੇਂ ਲੱਗਦਾ,  
> ਜਿਉਂ ਤੂੰ ਹੌਲੀ ਜਿਹੇ ਆਖੇਂ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> پھلاں دی ہر پتی اتے،  
> تیرا نام لکھیا جاپے نی!  
> ہوا وگے تاں ایویں لگدا،  
> جیوں توں ہولی جہے آکھیں نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Phullaan di har patti utte,  
> Tera naam likhiya jaape ni!  
> Hawa vage taan aiven lagda,  
> Jiyon toon hauli jihe aakhen ni!  

#### English Translation
> Upon every delicate petal of these blossoms,  
> Your name appears visibly inscribed!  
> When the gentle evening wind stirs the leaves,  
> It feels as if you whisper softly into my ear!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Nature becomes a canvas completely saturated by the beloved's presence.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਪਰ ਇਹ ਚੰਬਾ ਛੇਤੀ ਮੁਰਝਾਊ,  
> ਧੁੱਪ ਪੈਣ 'ਤੇ ਝੜ ਜਾਊਗਾ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਦਿਲ ਪਰ ਉਮਰਾਂ ਤਾਈਂ,  
> ਤੇਰੇ ਗ਼ਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੜ ਜਾਊਗਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> پر ایہہ چنبا چھیتی مرجھاؤ،  
> دھپ پین تے جھڑ جاؤگا!  
> شیو دا دل پر عمراں تائیں،  
> تیرے غم وچ سڑ جاؤگا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Par eh chamba cheti murjhaau,  
> Dhupp pain te jharh jaauga!  
> Shiv da dil par umraan taayin,  
> Tere gham vich sarh jaauga!  

#### English Translation
> Yet this white jasmine will quickly wither,  
> Shedding its petals when the fierce sun rises!  
> Yet Shiv's heart, across all lifetimes,  
> Shall continue to burn inside the kiln of your sorrow!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The flower's brief lifespan contrasts with the permanence of the poet's grief.*
