# Kachhi Kandh

### **ਕੱਚੀ ਕੰਧ** • **کچی کندھ**

#### *"The Unbaked Mud Wall"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਕੱਚੀ ਕੰਧ (کچی کندھ)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#MudWall` `#Erosion` `#Fragility` `#Monsoon` `#Promises`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `village_monsoon`

**Synopsis**: Human promises are compared to an unbaked village mud wall that melts and crumbles during the first heavy shower.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Inspired by the collapsed mud houses of displaced refugees in post-Partition Batala.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> A potent architectural metaphor from rural Majha homes, symbolizing the fragility of unwritten human vows.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸਾਡਾ ਪਿਆਰ ਸੀ ਕੱਚੀ ਕੰਧ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਜੋ ਪਹਿਲੇ ਮੀਂਹ ਵਿੱਚ ਢਹਿ ਗਈ ਏ!  
> ਕੋਈ ਇੱਟ ਨਾ ਬਚੀ ਸਲਾਮਤ ਹੁਣ,  
> ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਹੀ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਰਹਿ ਗਈ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ساڈا پیار سی کچی کندھ وانگوں،  
> جو پہلے مینہہ وچ ڈھہہ گئی اے!  
> کوئی اٹ نہ بچی سلامت ہن،  
> صرف مٹی ہی مٹی رہ گئی اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Saada pyaar si kachhi kandh vaangu,  
> Jo pehle meenh vich dheh gayi ae!  
> Koyi itt na bachi salaamat hun,  
> Sirf mitti hi mitti reh gayi ae!  

#### English Translation
> Our love was like an unbaked mud wall,  
> Collapsing in ruins beneath the very first summer shower!  
> Not a single brick remains intact or unbroken;  
> Nothing but desolate mud and wet clay is left behind!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The erosion of vulnerable rural relationships under environmental stress.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜਿਸ ਕੰਧ ਦੇ ਉਹਲੇ ਬਹਿੰਦੇ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਕੰਧ ਸਾਡੇ 'ਤੇ ਡਿੱਗ ਪਈ ਨੀ!  
> ਜਿਸ ਛਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਆਪਣਾ ਸਮਝਿਆ ਸੀ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਛਾਂ ਸਾਡਾ ਸਾਹ ਖੋਹ ਗਈ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جس کندھ دے اوہلے بہندے سی،  
> اوہی کندھ ساڈے تے ڈگ پئی نی!  
> جس چھاں نوں اپنا سمجھیا سی،  
> اوہی چھاں ساڈا ساہ کھوہ گئی نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Jis kandh de ohle behnde si,  
> Uhi kandh saade te digg payi ni!  
> Jis chhaan nu apna samjheya si,  
> Uhi chhaan saada saah khoh gayi ni!  

#### English Translation
> That very wall beneath whose shelter we once sat  
> Has fallen directly upon our own shoulders!  
> That protective shade which we claimed as our own sanctuary  
> Has crushed the living breath from our body!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The refuge transforms into the instrument of destruction.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਹੁਣ ਮਿੱਟੀ ਨੂੰ ਕੀ ਰੋਣਾ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਜੋ ਧਰਤ 'ਚ ਜਾ ਕੇ ਰਲ਼ ਗਈ ਏ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦੀ ਜ਼ਿੰਦਗੀ ਵੀ ਇਸ ਕੰਧ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਹੰਝੂਆਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਹੀ ਢਲ਼ ਗਈ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ہن مٹی نوں کی رونا اڑیو،  
> جو دھرتی چ جا کے رل گئی اے!  
> شیو دی زندگی وی اس کندھ وانگوں،  
> ہنجھواں دے وچ ہی ڈھل گئی اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Hun mitti nu ki rona arhiyo,  
> Jo dhart ch jaa ke ral gayi ae!  
> Shiv di zindagi vi iss kandh vaangu,  
> Hanjuwan de vich hi dhal gayi ae!  

#### English Translation
> Why weep over the fallen clay now, dear friends,  
> When it has already dissolved back into the mother earth?  
> Shiv's life, too, like this frail mud wall,  
> Has melted away completely into an ocean of tears!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Organic dissolution into the native soil.*
