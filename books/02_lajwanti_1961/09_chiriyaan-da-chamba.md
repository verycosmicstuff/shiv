# Saada Chiriyaan Da Chamba Ve

### **ਸਾਡਾ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਚੰਬਾ ਵੇ** • **ساڈا چڑیاں دا چنبا وے**

#### *"Our Flock of Little Sparrows"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸਾਡਾ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਚੰਬਾ ਵੇ (ساڈا چڑیاں دا چنبا وے)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Daughters` `#BridalDeparture` `#Babul` `#Courtyard` `#Exile`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `folklore` | **Landscape Biome**: `village_monsoon`

**Synopsis**: Daughters compared to a transient flock of sparrows in their father's courtyard, destined to fly to distant lands without return.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed in Batala as Shiv reflected on the migration of families and the inevitable separation of daughters.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Shiv's literary reinvention of the ancient Punjabi wedding folk song, infusing it with existential grief and Partition memory.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸਾਡਾ ਚਿੜੀਆਂ ਦਾ ਚੰਬਾ ਵੇ ਬਾਬਲ,  
> ਅਸਾਂ ਉੱਡ ਵੇ ਜਾਣਾ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਲੰਮੀ ਉਡਾਰੀ ਵੇ ਬਾਬਲ,  
> ਕਿਸੇ ਦੇਸ ਨਾ ਜਾਣਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> ساڈا چڑیاں دا چنبا وے بابل،  
> اساں اڈ وے جانا!  
> ساڈی لمبی اڈاری وے بابل،  
> کسے دیس نہ جانا!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Saada chiriyaan da chamba ve baabul,  
> Asaan udd ve jaana!  
> Saadi lammi udaari ve baabul,  
> Kise des na jaana!  

#### English Translation
> We are merely a flock of little sparrows, O father,  
> And our wings are poised for flight!  
> Ours is a long and unreturning voyage across the horizons,  
> To a foreign country where our destiny remains unknown!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The archetypal farewell of daughters in patriarchal Punjab.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਤੇਰੇ ਮਹਿਲਾਂ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਵਿੱਚ ਵੇ ਬਾਬਲ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਚਰਖਾ ਪਿਆ ਏ!  
> ਸਾਡੀ ਗੁੱਡੀਆਂ ਦੀ ਖੇਡ ਮੁੱਕੀ,  
> ਸਾਡਾ ਦਿਲ ਰੋ ਪਿਆ ਏ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> تیرے محلاں دے وچ وچ وے بابل،  
> ساڈا چرخا پیا اے!  
> ساڈی گڈیاں دی کھیڈ مکی،  
> ساڈا دل رو پیا اے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Tere mehlaan de vich vich ve baabul,  
> Saada charkha peya ae!  
> Saadi guddiyan di khed mukki,  
> Saada dil ro peya ae!  

#### English Translation
> Within the safe corridors of your home, O father,  
> Our spinning wheel lies abandoned and silent!  
> The innocent childhood games of cloth dolls have ended,  
> And our hearts have broken into weeping!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The charkha (spinning wheel) and dolls represent innocent girlhood.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਜਿਸ ਵਿਹੜੇ 'ਚ ਖੇਡੀਆਂ ਬਾਬਲ,  
> ਉਹੀ ਵਿਹੜਾ ਹੋਇਆ ਬਿਗਾਨਾ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਆਖੇ ਇਸ ਦੁਨੀਆ ਦੇ ਵਿੱਚ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਕਿਸੇ ਦਾ ਨਾ ਠਿਕਾਣਾ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> جس ویہڑے چ کھیڈیاں بابل،  
> اوہی ویہڑا ہویا بیگانہ!  
> شیو آکھے اس دنیا دے وچ،  
> کوئی کسے دا نہ ٹھکانہ!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Jis vehre ch khediyaan baabul,  
> Uhi vehra hoya bigaana!  
> Shiv aakhe iss duniya de vich,  
> Koyi kise da na thikaana!  

#### English Translation
> That very courtyard where we played as children, father,  
> Has suddenly become an alien, forbidden land!  
> Shiv says: 'In this vast and rootless world,  
> No living soul possesses a permanent dwelling place!'  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Folk bridal exile expanded into universal existential homelessness.*
