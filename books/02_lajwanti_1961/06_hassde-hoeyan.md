# Hassde Hoeyan

### **ਹੱਸਦੇ ਹੋਇਆਂ** • **ہسدے ہویاں**

#### *"With Laughter on My Lips"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਹੱਸਦੇ ਹੋਇਆਂ (ہسدے ہویاں)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Smile` `#Mask` `#Stoicism` `#Fame`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `birha` | **Landscape Biome**: `tavern_midnight`

**Synopsis**: How the poet conceals lethal suffering beneath a bright, theatrical smile so the world does not gloat.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Composed when Shiv was performing at literary mushairas while battling inner depression.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Recited at Kavi Sammelans to huge ovations, hiding the poet's deepening physical breakdown.

**Source**: *Surme Wali Akh (The Kohl-Lined Eye)* by Balwant Gargi (1979, Navyug Publishers, Delhi, pp. 142–168). [Primary Memoir]

> "Balwant Gargi documents Shiv’s chaotic writing habits: composing complete poems on Scissors and Gold Flake cigarette foil packets, café napkins in the Indian Coffee House Chandigarh, and tavern chits, frequently leaving drafts on tables or giving them away to casual admirers."

### ⚖️ Critical Reception & Ideological Debate
> Reveals the performative persona of the modern bohemian poet.

### 🎶 Tarannum & Musical Recitation Note
> Upbeat folk rhythm concealing tragic lyrics.

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਅਸੀਂ ਹੱਸਦੇ ਹੋਇਆਂ ਜ਼ਹਿਰ ਪੀਤਾ,  
> ਜੱਗ ਨੂੰ ਭੇਤ ਨਾ ਦਿੱਤਾ ਨੀ!  
> ਅੱਖੀਆਂ 'ਚੋਂ ਹੰਝੂ ਡਿੱਗਣ ਨਾ ਦਿੱਤੇ,  
> ਹੋਂਠਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਸੀਅ ਲਿੱਤਾ ਨੀ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> اسیں ہسدے ہویاں زہر پیتا،  
> جگ نوں بھیت نہ دتا نی!  
> اکھیاں چوں ہنجھو ڈگن نہ دتے،  
> ہونٹھاں نوں سی لتا نی!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Assin hassde hoeyan zehar peeta,  
> Jagg nu bhet na ditta ni!  
> Akkhiyaan chon hanjhu diggan na ditte,  
> Hothaan nu see litta ni!  

#### English Translation
> With laughter upon our lips we drank lethal poison,  
> Refusing to grant the callous world a glimpse of our secret!  
> We permitted not a single tear to fall from our eyes,  
> Stitching our lips firmly into silence!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The heroic refusal to let detractors witness his tears.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਲੋਕ ਸਮਝਦੇ ਅਸੀਂ ਬੜੇ ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ ਹਾਂ,  
> ਸਾਡੇ ਗੀਤਾਂ 'ਤੇ ਤਾੜੀਆਂ ਮਾਰਦੇ ਨੇ!  
> ਉਹ ਕੀ ਜਾਨਣ ਅੰਦਰ ਸਾਡੇ,  
> ਰੋਜ਼ ਕਿੰਨੇ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਹਾਰਦੇ ਨੇ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> لوک سمجھدے اسیں بڑے خوش ہاں،  
> ساڈے گیتاں تے تاڑیاں ماردے نے!  
> اوہ کی جانن اندر ساڈے،  
> روز کنے سپنے ہاردے نے!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Lok samajhde assin barhe khush haan,  
> Saade geetan te taariyan maarde ne!  
> Oh ki jaanan andar saade,  
> Roz kinne supne haarde ne!  

#### English Translation
> The crowd imagines we dwell in perpetual joy,  
> Clapping their hands in rapturous applause at our songs!  
> How could they ever know that inside our breast,  
> Hundreds of dreams concede defeat and perish each day?  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The disconnect between stage fame and private torment.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਤਾਂ ਹੱਸ ਕੇ ਵਿਦਾ ਹੋਊਗਾ,  
> ਇਸ ਜੱਗ ਦੇ ਮੇਲੇ ਤੋਂ!  
> ਪਿੱਛੋਂ ਲੋਕੀਂ ਯਾਦ ਕਰਨਗੇ,  
> ਉਸ ਦਰਦੀਲੇ ਚੇਲੇ ਤੋਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> شیو تاں ہس کے وداع ہوؤگا،  
> اس جگ دے میلے توں!  
> پچھوں لوکی یاد کرنگے،  
> اس دردیلے چیلے توں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Shiv taan hass ke vida houga,  
> Is jagg de mele ton!  
> Pichhon loki yaad karange,  
> Us dardeele chele ton!  

#### English Translation
> Shiv shall take his final departure with a smile  
> From this crowded carnival of the mortal world!  
> Only after he is gone will the people truly understand  
> The fathomless agony of this faithful disciple of love!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Prophetic announcement of his graceful exit from life's fair.*
