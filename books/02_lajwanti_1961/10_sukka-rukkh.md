# Sukka Rukkh

### **ਸੁੱਕਾ ਰੁੱਖ** • **سکا رخ**

#### *"The Withered Tree of the Barren Field"*

---

## 📜 Archival Metadata

- **Anthology**: [Lajwanti (1961)](../02_lajwanti_1961.md)
- **Original Title**: ਸੁੱਕਾ ਰੁੱਖ (سکا رخ)
- **Year of Composition / Publication**: 1961
- **Thematic Tags**: `#Tree` `#Drought` `#Barren` `#Solitude` `#Defiance`
- **Philosophical Theme**: `mortality` | **Landscape Biome**: `grassy_hills_sunrise`

**Synopsis**: A solitary withered tree stands in the midst of parched soil, stripped of leaves and bird nests, yet refusing to collapse.

**Genesis & Backstory**: Inspired by an ancient dead Shisham tree that stood along the path between Batala and Dera Baba Nanak.

### 🏛️ Historical & Biographical Context
> Direct predecessor to his famous poem 'Kujh Rukh' in Mainu Vida Karo (1963), pioneering arborial imagery in Punjabi literature.

**Source**: *Shiv Kumar: Sampooran Kaav Sangreh (Complete Poetical Works)* by Mohan Bhandari & Jiwan Singh (Eds.) (1974, Lahore Book Shop, Ludhiana, Editor Foreword, pp. 5–18). [Literary Criticism]

> "The standard definitive Punjabi anthology gathering 211 authenticated surviving lyric poems, songs, and ghazals across his nine lifetime and posthumous collections, plus Loona and Main Te Main."

## 📖 Verses & Translations (3 Stanzas)

### Stanza 1

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਮੈਂ ਇੱਕ ਸੁੱਕਾ ਰੁੱਖ ਹਾਂ ਅੜੀਓ,  
> ਜਿਸ 'ਤੇ ਪੰਛੀ ਬਹਿੰਦੇ ਨਹੀਂ!  
> ਜਿਸਦੀ ਛਾਂ 'ਤੇ ਰਾਹੀ ਬਹਿ ਕੇ,  
> ਸੁੱਖ ਦਾ ਸਾਹ ਹੁਣ ਲੈਂਦੇ ਨਹੀਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> میں اک سکا رخ ہاں اڑیو،  
> جس تے پنچھی بہندے نہیں!  
> جس دی چھاں تے راہی بہہ کے،  
> سکھ دا ساہ ہن لیندے نہیں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Main ikk sukka rukkh haan arhiyo,  
> Jis te panchhi behnde nahin!  
> Jisdi chhaan te raahi beh ke,  
> Sukkh da saah hun lainde nahin!  

#### English Translation
> I am a withered and dead tree, companions,  
> Upon whose dry branches no singing birds will alight!  
> Beneath whose shade no weary traveler will pause  
> To draw a soothing breath of relief from the road!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *The total exhaustion of the poet's capacity to provide solace.*

* * *

### Stanza 2

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਨਾ ਮੇਰੇ 'ਤੇ ਹਰਿਆਵਲ ਆਵੇ,  
> ਨਾ ਕੋਈ ਫੁੱਲ ਖਿੜਾਵੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ!  
> ਬੱਦਲ ਆ ਕੇ ਲੰਘ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਨੇ,  
> ਕੋਈ ਨਾ ਬੂੰਦ ਪਿਲਾਵੇ ਮੈਨੂੰ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> نہ میرے تے ہریاول آوے،  
> نہ کوئی پھل کھڑاوے مینوں!  
> بدل آ کے لنگھ جاندے نے،  
> کوئی نہ بوند پلاوے مینوں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Na mere te haryaawal aave,  
> Na koyi phull khirhaave mainu!  
> Baddal aa ke langh jaande ne,  
> Koyi na boond pilaave mainu!  

#### English Translation
> No emerald green renewal visits my limbs,  
> Nor does any wild blossom unfold upon my bark!  
> The rainclouds gather and drift indifferently overhead,  
> Offering not a single drop of moisture to soothe my thirst!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Nature's cruelty towards that which is already drying.*

* * *

### Stanza 3

#### Gurmukhi (ਗੁਰਮੁਖੀ)
> ਪਰ ਮੈਂ ਖੜ੍ਹਾ ਹਾਂ ਸੀਨਾ ਤਾਣ ਕੇ,  
> ਝੱਖੜਾਂ ਅੱਗੇ ਝੁਕਿਆ ਨਹੀਂ!  
> ਸ਼ਿਵ ਦਾ ਗੀਤ ਵੀ ਏਸੇ ਰੁੱਖ ਵਾਂਗੂੰ,  
> ਮਰ ਕੇ ਵੀ ਕਦੇ ਮੁੱਕਿਆ ਨਹੀਂ!  

#### Shahmukhi (شاہ مکھی)
> پر میں کھڑھا ہاں سینہ تان کے،  
> جھکھڑاں اگے جھکیا نہیں!  
> شیو دا گیت وی ایسے رخ وانگوں،  
> مر کے وی کدے مکیا نہیں!  

#### Roman Transliteration (Phonetic)
> Par main kharha haan seena taan ke,  
> Jhakhrhaan agge jhukeya nahin!  
> Shiv da geet vi aese rukkh vaangu,  
> Mar ke vi kade mukkeya nahin!  

#### English Translation
> Yet I stand upright with defiant chest bare,  
> Refusing to bend before the howling dust-storms!  
> Shiv's song too is like this withered tree—  
> Even having perished, it shall never be extinguished!  

💡 **Lyrical Commentary**: *Defiance in defeat; the withered timber endures.*
